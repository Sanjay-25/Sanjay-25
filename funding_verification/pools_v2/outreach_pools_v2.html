Fast Code Outreach Pools

The 1454 YC companies from any batch that raised beyond YC's investment since October 2021 with total funding under $200M, grouped into 11 pools by Fast Code AI proof point. Physical AI is split four ways with ordered rules (first match wins): data and evals, autonomous driving, robots and autonomous machines, then vision on cameras and sensors. 49 voice AI companies are excluded from outreach. Everyone in a pool gets the same proof sentence; only the opener changes.
