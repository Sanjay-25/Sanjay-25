Litmus fits at whatever stage you need a high-signal read on how someone actually builds. Two common ways teams slot it in: up front, for a trustworthy measure of technical ability before synchronous interviews, or in later rounds, to capture process signals like AI prompt usage and coding behavior alongside raw output. Book a demo and we’ll find your fit.. . . . Can candidates use AI? Yes, and we encourage it. Candidates use the same AI tools they’d have on the job, and Litmus tracks every prompt, and analyzes how they reasoned, prompted, and refined: signal you can’t get from output alone.. . . . How does grading work? Every submission runs against a rigorous deterministic harness, specific to your assessment, in a live sandbox for correctness. Configurable LLM graders then evaluate qualitative dimensions like architecture and process, and every judgment is tagged to the file and line that backs it up.. . . . Can I import my existing assessments? Yes. Bring your own take-home or coding problem and we’ll host and grade it, or let Litmus generate one from your codebase, tickets, and job descriptions. Either way you get the same evidence-based report.. . . . What if we can’t share our code or tickets? That’s fine. Connecting your codebase and tickets makes assessments sharper, but none of it is required. Litmus is fully configurable: share as much or as little as you want, and we can build a relevant assessment from public inputs alone, like your job descriptions. Either way, we’ll work with you to shape it to your team’s context.. . . . Does Litmus work with my ATS? Yes. Candidates sync to and from Ashby, Greenhouse, and Lever automatically, or you can manage them end to end on Litmus. Nothing about your pipeline has to change.. . . . How do you know the work is really theirs? Litmus tracks file iteration, commit history, and AI prompts across the whole build, and every candidate records a short walkthrough explaining their decisions. That makes an assessment far harder to game than a standard take-home.. . . . How is Litmus priced? We price custom contracts according to your hiring needs and volume.
