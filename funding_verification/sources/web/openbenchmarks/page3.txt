Best company funding data providers. The company funding data landscape splits into two groups: 21 programmatic endpoints measured through APIs or structured web research, plus Crunchbase and Harmonic exports measured on the same cohort. PitchBook and Tracxn are private-market investor-research products, including PE workflows, rather than self-serve GTM sales enrichment APIs. This page covers what each vendor exposes and how it is reached, and why the excluded incumbents are not ranked. Scores live on the benchmark itself.. Vendor material tends to cover coverage and skip lag. Both boards are below: one on rounds that had already settled when the vendor ran, one on rounds announced inside the last month. Vendors that look interchangeable on the first can separate by a wide margin on the second, which is usually the more useful thing to know when choosing between them.. The 17 measured providers are Apollo, CompanyEnrich, Crunchbase, Crustdata, Exa, Explorium, Fiber, Firecrawl, Fundable, Harmonic, Nimble, Ocean.io, Parallel, People Data Labs, PredictLeads, Seltz, and ZoomInfo. [04] faq. Common questions about the funding data vendors. . What separates a funding data provider from a funding API?. The provider is the company selling the data. The API is the endpoint you call with a company domain to get it back. Some vendors here sell both, some sell a searchable product where funding is one view, and two supply the data as a reviewed export with no endpoint at all. The table above shows which is which.. . Why do some vendors appear once here but several times on the benchmark?. This page lists one endpoint per vendor, because it is about the vendor landscape. The benchmark scores endpoints, and four vendors expose more than one, so they take a row each there. An endpoint is the honest unit for a score because two endpoints from the same vendor can differ sharply in price and speed.. . Can a vendor supplying an export be compared with one supplying an API?. On correctness, yes. Both answer the same question about the same companies under the same judging policy. On latency and cost, no, and those columns are left blank rather than estimated. An export also has no query moment relative to an announcement, so it cannot be scored on how quickly a round was picked up.. . Which vendors are deliberately left out?. Products built for investor research rather than per-domain enrichment, covered in the access context below. The test is whether a GTM system can ask about one company and get a structured answer back. A platform you browse rather than call does not fit that shape, however good its data.. . . .
