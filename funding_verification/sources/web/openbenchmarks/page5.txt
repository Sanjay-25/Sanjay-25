Crunchbase alternatives for funding data. Crunchbase's funding API is gated to paid Business and Enterprise plans. If you want self-serve startup funding data through an API , the question becomes which of the reachable providers is actually correct. Every provider here was called on the same companies and graded against source-verified rounds.. 23 measured endpoints received the same company domains and were scored on correct latest funding stage against source-verified funding events. That includes Crunchbase, measured from an exported dataset, alongside APIs and structured web research providers. Crunchbase's API remains sales-licensed; Harmonic is included from an audited supplied export. PitchBook and Tracxn serve private-market investor research, including PE workflows, rather than comparable self-serve GTM sales enrichment.. Moving off Crunchbase means replacing two things. The historical record you already query, and the rounds announced since you last looked. Both boards are below so a candidate can be checked on both before you commit, because strength on one does not carry over to the other.. Incumbent-specific breakdowns: Harmonic alternatives . . [05] faq. Common questions about moving off Crunchbase. . What makes an alternative a genuine replacement for Crunchbase funding data?. It has to answer the same question about the same company and be reachable the way your system works. That means a documented endpoint, a structured latest-stage answer, and access you can arrange yourself. The table above lists the endpoint and access terms for each option so you can rule out the ones that do not fit before testing.. . Why is Crunchbase measured from an export rather than its API?. Its funding API sits behind a sales-licensed plan, so it was scored from a self-serve plan export matched to the same company identities. That keeps the correctness comparison fair. It also means Crunchbase carries no latency or cost figure, because nothing was timed or billed.. . Does replacing Crunchbase mean losing coverage?. It depends which field you rely on. The benchmark judges the latest funding stage and separately records whether the date, amount, total raised and round count came back. A provider can name the right stage and return nothing else, which is fine for routing and not enough for writing a CRM record.. . How does PitchBook compare for this job?. It is built for private-market research rather than per-domain enrichment, so it is out of scope here for the same reason Tracxn is. That is a statement about shape, and about whether a GTM system can call it per company, rather than about the quality of its data.. . . .
