We are powering the next generation of product experiences.. Y Backed by Y Combinator . . . . . . . How Karumi started. Our story. . . Toni and Pablo found that the bottleneck in their previous company was product communication. The team was developing so many great features, so fast, that no one in GTM could keep up.. That meant losing a competitive edge against their competitors, and tons of inefficiencies: re-recording product videos, having engineers sit in demo calls, re-writing documentation, not being able to demo the product to key prospects on time.. They realized browser automation was the way to understand a software product deeply, creating a knowledge brain that could then be used for multiple use cases..
