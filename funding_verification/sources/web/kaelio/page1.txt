Cohort retention heatmap. . . . Show more use cases . . . . FAQ. Common questions. . . What is the Kaelio Data Agent? The Kaelio Data Agent is a managed AI data agent for business teams. Kaelio connects to your tools, runs the managed ingestion and modeling layer, uses ktx to keep the context layer governed, and delivers sourced answers, digests, and alerts through the interfaces your team already uses. . . . How is the Kaelio Data Agent different from the Kaelio Context Layer? ktx is the context engine inside the product: it keeps definitions, relationships, permissions, and source evidence governed. The Kaelio Data Agent is the managed product around it: connectors, ingestion, modeling, monitoring workflows, and the business-user interface for asking questions and receiving digests. . . . Do I need the Context Layer to use the Kaelio Data Agent? No separate context-layer project is required. The Kaelio Data Agent includes ktx -governed context as part of the managed service, so every answer is grounded in approved definitions, access rules, and source evidence instead of raw schema guesses. . . . What can the Kaelio Data Agent do? The Kaelio Data Agent can answer ad hoc analytics questions, generate recurring business digests, monitor accounts or metrics, prepare briefs, and surface cross-tool signals. It is designed for business-user workflows where teams need plain-English answers with governed definitions and source evidence, not another dashboard queue.. . . Where does the Kaelio Data Agent deliver answers? Kaelio can deliver answers and digests where teams already work: the Kaelio web interface, Slack, email, MCP-compatible tools, and internal applications. The same ktx -governed context can also support custom agents or API-based workflows, so teams do not need to rebuild definitions for each interface. . . . Can business users ask data questions without SQL? Yes. Business users can ask questions in plain English and receive answers grounded in governed metrics, permissions, and source evidence. The Data Agent handles ingestion, modeling, context lookup, and query execution behind the scenes, so users do not need to know table names, joins, or SQL syntax.. . . How long does it take to set up the Kaelio Data Agent? Most teams can get the Kaelio Data Agent set up within the same day. Kaelio handles managed connector setup, source mapping, and workflow configuration so your team does not need to build pipelines, models, or agent context from scratch.. . . Can I customize what the Kaelio Data Agent monitors? Yes. Tell the Kaelio Data Agent what to watch and how to respond in plain language, or start from a managed workflow pattern. You can adjust thresholds, signal weights, digest schedules, and the types of actions it recommends. The Data Agent also learns from team feedback, so recommendations get sharper over time.. . . Can the Kaelio Data Agent replace dashboards or BI? Not necessarily. The Kaelio Data Agent includes its own dashboards for the workflows it manages, plus conversation, digests, and alerts for business users. Many teams still keep BI for recurring reporting and deeper exploration while using Kaelio for governed, operational answers.. . . Can I trust the answers from the Kaelio Data Agent? Does it hallucinate? Every answer from the Kaelio Data Agent is grounded in ktx -governed context and cites its sources: which tool the data came from, when it was last synced, and the definitions or records behind it. Your team can verify answers quickly. No black boxes, no guessing. . . . Can we control what data the Kaelio Data Agent accesses? Yes. During connector setup, you control exactly which objects and fields Kaelio can read. Sensitive fields can be excluded, access rules can be mirrored in the governed context, and permissions can be reviewed at any time. Your data, your rules.. . . Do I need to replace my existing tools to use Kaelio? No. Kaelio connects to your existing stack through 900+ managed integrations, including Salesforce, Gainsight, Mixpanel, Zendesk, and many more. The Data Agent models data from your tools, correlates signals across them, and delivers intelligence where your team already works. Nothing to migrate, nothing to rip out.. . . Is my data secure with Kaelio? Yes. Kaelio is SOC 2 and HIPAA compliant, and uses 256-bit encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is never used to train models or shared across customers. Kaelio offers SSO, role-based access, and audit logs for enterprise deployments. . . . . . . . . Get Started. Give every team a managed data agent grounded in governed context..
