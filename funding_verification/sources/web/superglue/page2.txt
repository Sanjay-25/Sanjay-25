Dorina Döring . 3 min read . superglue Brings AI-powered Implementation Automation to Sage Intacct. Munich and San Francisco, August 25, 2026: Today, superglue is announcing that it is available on the Sage Intacct Marketplace as a Sage technology partner. Implementation consultants can now use superglue's AI agents to configure Sage instances and migrate client data from source systems, with a consultant signing off at every step.. Sage is a leader in accounting, financial, HR, and payroll technology for small and mid-sized businesses. The Sage Intacct Marketplace gives customers and partners access to a growing ecosystem of integrated solutions that extend the platform and cover a broad range of business needs.. When a consultant has gathered client requirements, superglue turns them into an implementation plan, connects to any source system, and configures the Sage instance accordingly. It reads the source data, maps it to the target structure, and surfaces the inconsistent or messy records for the consultant to resolve rather than guessing at them.. Approval gates sit between the steps. The consultant reviews what the agents propose, gets client sign-off where it is needed, and the outcome feeds back into updates to the statement of work.. Consultants can run several projects at once, and superglue keeps a full audit trail so every action can be traced and rolled back. Firms can also turn their approach into repeatable playbooks by implementation type, industry, or client profile.. Availability. The superglue connector is available to Sage implementation partners in the United States and Canada through the Sage Intacct Marketplace . It supports a self-hosted deployment model , so firms that need client data to stay inside their own environment can keep it there.. "Implementing new financial systems can be a time-intensive part of a digital transformation project. By helping automate data migration and configuration workflows, superglue gives Sage implementation partners an opportunity to spend less time on manual tasks and more time helping add value for the customer. We're happy to welcome superglue to the Sage Intacct Marketplace as part of our commitment to expanding the power of AI-based solutions to our partner ecosystem and customers.". Pat Schlight, Senior Director, Partner Programs at Sage. . "Implementation consultants lose weeks on implementation grunt work," said Adina Goerres, Founder and CEO of superglue . "With superglue, that work happens in minutes, and consultants get time back to work on strategy, advisory, and building strong client relationships. Consultants define requirements with their clients, and superglue does the heavy lifting: configuring the Sage instance, reading the data, mapping and moving it.". . superglue also supports operational integrations that implementation partners run to keep Sage Intacct connected to the other systems a business uses after go-live.. VARs and implementation partners delivering Sage Intacct can find superglue on the Sage Intacct Marketplace or book a demo to see it run against one of your own projects..
