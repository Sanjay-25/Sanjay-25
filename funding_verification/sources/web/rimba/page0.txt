Reach out to us at start@rimba.ai. We are here to help.. start@rimba.ai . . . FAQ / 01 What is Rimba? . Rimba is a specialized AI compliance and operations data platform for heavy industries, starting with low-carbon fuels compliance. It connects to the systems you already run — SCADA, ERP, document stores, lab systems — and turns meter reads, bills of lading, scale tickets, utility statements, and sustainability declarations into audit-ready reports.. . . . . . . FAQ / 02 Who is Rimba for? . Compliance and operations teams across the low-carbon fuel value chain: renewable fuel producers, feedstock collectors and traders, logistics providers, blenders, offtakers, and project developers. Rimba also serves the parties around them — consultants, auditors, and verifiers — in the same workspace. Customers range from small to Fortune 500 companies.. . . . . . . FAQ / 03 Which fuels and industries does Rimba cover? . Rimba is built for energy and industrial companies, and our focus starts with low-carbon fuels compliance: Renewable Natural Gas (RNG), biodiesel, renewable diesel (RD), sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), ethanol, and methanol.. . . . . . . FAQ / 04 What types of data does Rimba process? . Both unstructured and structured operational data:. Documents: PDFs, images, delivery notes, bills of lading, scale tickets, invoices, lab reports, utility statements, supplier and sustainability declarations, emails. Instrument and time-series data: flow meters, chromatographs, sensor and process data from SCADA-connected systems. System data: ERP records, inventory systems, custom databases, SharePoint. . . . . . . FAQ / 05 Which regulatory programs does Rimba support? . LCFS, RFS, ISCC, 45Z, CORSIA, RSB, Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations (CFR), EU RED III, RTCs/RECs, Prevailing Wage & Apprenticeship, and GHG/Scope 1–2–3 carbon accounting.. . . . . . . FAQ / 06 How does Rimba help with audits? . This is the part most teams find very helpful. Rimba maintains a shared audit workspace where your compliance team, consultant, and verifier work off the same dataset. Every reported figure traces back to its source document or meter read, and every formula is validated so an auditor can follow and easily verify. Reports are generated continuously, not assembled in a scramble before each audit deadline.. . . . . . . FAQ / 07 How does Rimba ensure data accuracy? . Extraction is only the first step. Rimba uses OCR and large language models to pull data from documents, then continuously reconciles volumes across sources and flags gaps, duplicates, and inconsistencies before submission — for example, catching a missing meter read before it turns into an invalid RIN. Where a program permits data substitution, Rimba applies the approved method and documents the derivation.. . . . . . . FAQ / 08 Can Rimba handle multiple certifications at once? . Yes. Rimba maintains one dataset that serves every program you're certified for, so a shipment recorded once feeds multiple programs/certification of your choosing (e.g. LCFS, RFS, and ISCC) without being re-entered. This minimizes data discrepancies, eliminates risk of double counting, and resources needed to maintain compliance, not to mention the time savings aspect.. . . . . . . FAQ / 09 Is Rimba secure? . Yes. Rimba is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified, with encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and audit logging throughout. Data stays yours — Rimba processes it on your behalf and does not use customer data to train models for other customers.. . . . . . . FAQ / 10 Who owns the data in Rimba? . You do. Your data remains your property, and it's exportable at any time.. . . . . . . FAQ / 11 Can our auditor, verifier, or consultant use Rimba? . Yes, and this is deliberate. Rimba is multi-party by design: producers, offtakers, consultants, and verifiers can each be given scoped access to the same workspace. Verifiers review evidence in place instead of receiving an export.. . . . . . . FAQ / 12 Does Rimba handle mass balance and chain of custody? . Yes. Rimba tracks chain of custody from feedstock origin through collection, production, trading, and delivery, maintaining mass balance and allocation across the chain, and validating fuel pathways against program requirements.. . . . . . . .
