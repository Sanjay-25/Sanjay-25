We're building persistent customer knowledge teams can actually inspect . One system that brings together what your customers have told you—and stays honest about what the evidence cannot answer.. . . . 01. The problem we kept seeing. Companies already capture useful evidence in sales calls, support tickets, CRM notes, surveys, and Slack threads—scattered across different tools and owners. Decisions then lean on whoever remembers loudest, and the same customer problem can be rediscovered every quarter.. . 02. What we built. Poth is a persistent knowledge layer for customer data. It connects the sources you already have and organizes accounts, people, features, and issues into an evidence map where every relationship traces back to the source behind it.. . 03. Why it's different. A general model can produce a fluent answer even when the supplied data is incomplete. Poth is built around the evidence: answers cite their sources, confidence uses retrieval signals, and contradictions and coverage gaps are surfaced instead of smoothed over.. . . . . Founding team. .
