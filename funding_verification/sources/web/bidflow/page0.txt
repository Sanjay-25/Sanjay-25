What's still not supported? + − Bidflow can't do conduit and wire lengths, feeders, panel schedules, security and fire alarm, or niche symbols in power (HVAC power) or lighting (LED tape lengths).. How accurate is the AI takeoff? + − Bidflow's AI counts typically land at 95–98% accuracy against a manual takeoff. Every run still goes through a human review step before you export.. What file types does Bidflow support? + − Upload your electrical plan sets as PDFs. Bidflow reads schedules, symbols, and legends directly from the sheets — no pre-processing required.. How long does a takeoff take? + − The AI runs on average between 1-3 minutes per drawing. The time to review the counts depends on the drawing.. Do you support both lighting and power takeoffs? + − Yes. Bidflow counts lighting fixtures and controls as well as power devices, from the same plan set.. What if the AI misses something? + − Every takeoff goes through review before you export. Missed or miscounted devices are easy to fix — you can add, move, or remove the color-coded count boxes on the plan so the totals match what’s actually there.. Is my data secure? + − Your plan sets and takeoff data are yours. We don't share project data, counts, or drawings to anyone else.
