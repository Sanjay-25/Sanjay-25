From the matter Generate Documents tab, produce the full set after intake is complete. Batch download when you are ready for review.. 4. Handle exceptions with AI Studio and Word. When a gift pattern is unusual:. Ask the matter AI Assistant with vault context. Draft from firm blueprints in AI Studio . Polish in Microsoft Word with the Relaw add-in. Keep unusual language in attorney-reviewed output, not in uncontrolled chat paste.. 5. Close the loop on funding. Trusts that are never funded are marketing failures. Use Relaw to:.
