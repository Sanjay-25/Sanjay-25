What is Getcho . The reliability platform for your existing carriers. . Getcho sits on top of the carriers you already use. We monitor every delivery your carriers make in real time, catch the failures they don't report, and hold them accountable — so you get more out of the carriers you already have instead of switching to new ones. . Built for brands, retailers and 3PLs running first-, middle- and last-mile delivery through third-party carriers. . . What that means in practice . Address intelligence The access point and the process for an address — which entrance or dock, whether the buzzer works, when reception closes — known before dispatch, sent to the driver, and used to route around the carriers that fail there. . Delivery Forensics Every proof-of-delivery photo scored against the address, the drop notes and past deliveries to that building. . Claims Filed with the carrier on your behalf with the evidence attached, tracked in one place across every carrier. . AI driver contact Multilingual voice and message, so a wrong drop gets corrected while the driver is still there. . First mile, middle mile, last mile. Works with your existing carrier accounts. . .
