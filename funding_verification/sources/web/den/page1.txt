About · Den . Back About Den. Den builds digital clones. A clone is a single AI agent that belongs to one person, learns from their conversations, and stays reachable by text or phone even when they can't answer themselves.. The idea is simple: everyone should have a version of themselves that remembers their life and can speak for them when they're busy, asleep, or just don't feel like typing. Den is what we're building toward that, one conversation at a time.. We're a small team based in San Francisco, backed by Y Combinator.. Legal. Den is operated by Den Inc., a Delaware corporation..
