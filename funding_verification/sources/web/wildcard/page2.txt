Wildcard helps ecommerce and retail teams understand how products appear in AI shopping, decide what to improve, and connect approved work to commercial outcomes.. . . . . Our purpose. Give commerce teams a clear way to operate as shopping becomes a conversation.. . Shoppers increasingly ask an AI system to compare products, interpret constraints, and make a recommendation. That changes how a product is discovered before it ever reaches a cart.. Ecommerce teams need to know what an agent sees, which sources it relies on, and where product information is incomplete or unclear.. They also need a practical path from that evidence to catalog work, review, publishing, and measurement. Wildcard is built around that full loop.. . . . . The company. Founded around a new operating problem in retail.. . Wildcard was founded to help brands and retailers work through the shift from link-based discovery to AI-mediated product discovery.. The company is based in San Francisco and is part of Y Combinator's W25 batch. We are building alongside commerce teams while the workflows, standards, and buyer habits around AI shopping are still taking shape.. Our story is the work itself: understand the market carefully, build with real operating constraints, and be precise about what the product can do today.. . . . . The product operating loop. Move from an AI answer to the next accountable action.. The loop keeps market observation, product work, review, and outcomes connected.. . 01 Observe. Track the prompts shoppers use, the products agents recommend, and the sources that shape each answer.. . 02 Understand. Connect changes in visibility to product facts, catalog gaps, citations, competitors, and market context.. . 03 Act. Turn evidence into scoped catalog and content work, with people reviewing what should move forward.. . 04 Measure. Read visibility, publishing activity, referral traffic, orders, and revenue in the same commercial context.. . . . How we work. Principles for a market still being defined.. . 01 Start with the product. A recommendation is only useful when it leads back to a real product, a clear fact, and a decision a commerce team can make.. . 02 Keep evidence attached. Teams should be able to inspect why work was proposed, what source supports it, and what changed after it shipped.. . 03 Protect human judgment. Wildcard can organize and draft work. The people responsible for the catalog and brand decide what is approved.. . 04 Close the commercial loop. Visibility is a signal.
