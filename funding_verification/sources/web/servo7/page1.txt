We’re a small team, building robots to automate how the world's goods are moved. We love the craft of building a real physical product from nothing, and seeing it doing useful work.. A robot that works is the only kind worth building.. . . . Getting them in the field. There's a lot happening in robotics right now. What is often forgotten, is to have the robots out in the real world, every day, doing the job for a paying customer. But that is the whole thing!. So we deploy, we watch, we fix, and deploy again. The whole loop, often within a single day.. . . . Where we go. Almost everything you own arrived in a shipping container that someone emptied by hand, in brutal heat or freezing cold. It's the most demanding job in logistics, and it's the entry point of the entire supply chain. We're here to automate it, and to build the company that ends up handling most of the goods on earth..
