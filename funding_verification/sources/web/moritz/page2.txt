We’ve watched the same pattern from the inside: in-house legal teams are constrained on time, resources, and jurisdictions. Outside counsel is expensive and slow. Founders end up signing things they shouldn’t, and attorneys spend nights on work software could do in minutes.. So we built the alternative. Moritz combines senior attorneys from firms like Fenwick, Cooley, and Goodwin with software purpose-built for legal work - not retrofitted onto it. We charge fixed fees per matter. We turn work around in hours, not weeks. And we take accountability for every word, redline, and piece of advice.. .
