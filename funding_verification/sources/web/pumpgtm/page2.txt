About PumpGTM: who builds it and why Skip to content ← Back to Home . About PumpGTM. PumpGTM finds the companies that need your product right now, messages the right people on LinkedIn from your own account, and learns from every reply to sharpen who it reaches next.. Why we built it. Most outbound tools hand you a database and a sequence editor, then leave the hard part to you: deciding who is actually worth a message this week. Founders and small sales teams do not have time for that. PumpGTM does the targeting, the writing, and the follow-up, and it shows you the conversations that came out of it.. The signals it watches are the ones that mean a company is ready to buy: hiring for the role you sell to, a fresh funding round, a tooling change, or people engaging with a LinkedIn post about the problem you solve.. Who is behind it. PumpGTM is built by Giga Next Inc., a US company founded by Namanyay Goel. The same team builds Gigacatalyst, an AI analytics product, and PumpGTM started as the system we used to find our own customers before we opened it up.. Early customers include founders at Workers.io, Supermemory, Harbor, Datoric and Smol Machines. Most of them started on a trial from this site and kept it running because the conversations kept coming.. Get in touch. Questions, press, partnerships: contact us or email hello@pumpgtm.com .
