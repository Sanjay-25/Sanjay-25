We believe e-commerce logistics should work like your brain invisible, adaptive, and constantly optimizing for outcomes you care about.. A brand should wake up and have their entire logistics operation shipping, tracking, returns, checkout optimization running as a single, intelligent system. One platform. One source of truth. OUR STORY. . Backed by. Y Combinator . . . . . . . . . . . . . The Beginning. . A journey of innovation. We are a team of serial entrepreneurs, scientists, and principal engineers.. We did not start with a product. We started with a conviction: logistics is not a back-office cost center, it is a growth lever that shapes conversion, retention, and margin. We spoke with 100+ e-commerce merchants to pressure test that belief, and the same pattern kept showing up across brands: fragmented tools, manual work, and slow decision-making.. Before writing code, we secured customers willing to pay for a unified platform, because the pain was already budgeted and urgent. Many global brands reached out to join us the mission.. The direction has stayed consistent since day one. We are building one operating layer for shipping, tracking, returns, and logistics workflows. So teams can improve outcomes weekly instead of rebuilding systems quarterly. The bet is simple: if logistics decisions become faster, measurable, and automated, e-commerce teams can scale into new markets without adding operational complexity at the same rate.. . . . . . . . .
