Frequently Asked Questions. Everything you need to know about Pango's unified e-commerce logistics platform.. . What is Pango? Pango is the next-generation Unified Operating System for e-commerce logistics. We bring together everything from checkout optimization and delivery tracking to returns management and customer experience—all powered by intelligent AI—into one seamless platform. Unlike fragmented solutions that force you to juggle multiple disconnected tools, Pango gives your team a single command center to manage the entire post-purchase journey, empowering you to move faster, reduce costs, and deliver exceptional customer experiences.. . . . . Who is Pango designed for? Pango is built for ambitious B2C e-commerce companies—from fast-growing mid-market merchants to established brands processing thousands of orders monthly. Our ideal customers are those who feel the pain of disconnected tech stacks and want to compete with the logistics sophistication of the world's largest retailers. Whether you're in fashion, apparel, footwear, or accessories, if you're ready to transform logistics from a cost center into a competitive advantage, Pango was designed with you in mind.. . . . . What makes Pango different from competitors like Aftership, Loop, or Ingrid? While traditional solutions force you to pay for separate tools for tracking, returns, and support—each operating in isolation—Pango unifies everything into a single, intelligent platform with shared data and coordinated AI driven workflows. Our competitors are built on rigid legacy architectures that can't deliver individual-level personalization or customization. Pango's AI-first design allows you to create the workflow you want for deliveries and returns and analyzes each customer's unique data in real-time to generate personalized delivery and return policies, driving higher conversions and lower costs—something no competitor offers today.. . . . . How quickly can we get started with Pango? Getting started with Pango is remarkably fast. The technical integration typically requires less than one hour of implementation time, with most configuration handled through intuitive web-based interfaces that require no programming expertise. Our software reads your existing data structure, delivery policies, and return rules, then crafts automated workflows tailored precisely to your operations. Your team will be up and running with a fully customized logistics backbone in days, not months.. . . . . Does Pango integrate with our existing tech stack? Absolutely. Pango's API-first architecture seamlessly integrates with the platforms you already use—including Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom-built solutions. We connect to your Warehouse Management System, customer experience tools like Gorgias and Klaviyo, and over 100 shipping carriers worldwide including DHL, FedEx, UPS, and regional providers. Our carrier integration layer abstracts away the complexity of multiple shipping APIs, presenting your team with a unified interface for managing all logistics operations.. . . . . Beyond these metrics, our AI-first design has reduced manual operational workload from 50% to just 5%—a ten-fold improvement that frees your team to focus on growth rather than logistics firefighting.
