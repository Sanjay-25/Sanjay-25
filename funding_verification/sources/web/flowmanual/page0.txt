How drawings, quantities, and pricing work together in FlowManual.. . What is FlowManual? FlowManual is construction takeoff and estimating software for general contractors and trade subcontractors. Count and measure from PDF drawings, build an estimate with your own pricing, and keep the supporting specs, quotes, and contract review in the same project.. What types of takeoff can I do? FlowManual supports mechanical equipment counts and piping measurements, ductwork lengths by size, concrete areas and volumes, and general counting for custom items such as doors, lights, and fixtures. Review the quantities on your drawings, supply the dimensions each calculation needs, and bring the results into your estimate.. How does drawing takeoff work? Upload your PDF drawings, choose the sheets, and count items or trace lengths and areas. Set and verify the drawing scale for measurements, then review the quantities against the sheet before using them in your estimate. You can add, edit, accept, or reject counts as you go.. How do takeoff quantities become a priced estimate? Bring accepted counts and measured quantities into an estimate workbook. Add your material costs, labor rates, and markup, then use formulas and reusable assemblies to build the bid. Linked rows let you return to the source drawing to check a quantity.. Can I use my existing spreadsheets and pricing? Yes. Import an Excel workbook, use your own rate library, and edit quantities, rates, and formulas in FlowManual. Export estimates to Excel or CSV, or download a proposal PDF. Your team supplies and reviews the pricing used in the bid.. What happens when the drawings change? Compare drawing revisions and review changes in accepted takeoff quantities. Linked estimate rows can refresh from the takeoff while keeping manually edited or locked quantities under your control. Check the changed scope and pricing before updating your bid.. Does FlowManual also review specs, quotes, and contracts? Yes. Document review supports your estimate by highlighting scope, costs, deadlines, and exclusions in PDFs. Compare related documents to check for scope and price changes. Your team reviews the findings and decides what belongs in the bid.. Can I try FlowManual for free? Yes. Create a free account with no credit card and use every feature with a daily usage allowance.
