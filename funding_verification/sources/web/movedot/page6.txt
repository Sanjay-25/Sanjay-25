DTC that fires torque_sensor_drift (both A and B channels simultaneously) . Source fix Fix 2 – drift monitor threshold lowered from 0.15 → 0.06 N·m . Physical cause At 120 kph, combined tire-road coupling noise (5–70 Hz) produces torque feedback peaks exceeding 0.06 N·m for sustained periods (>25 ms), tripping the debounce window. At 50/5 kph, amplitude is well below threshold. . Real fault present? No. These are fault-free baseline runs. The alarm is false. . . Why this matters . A human cannot compare 384 test pairs by hand. These 4 false alarms hide inside thousands of passing results. They land on the golden runs – the most stable tests in the whole campaign. Automated paired-run diffing catches it in minutes..
