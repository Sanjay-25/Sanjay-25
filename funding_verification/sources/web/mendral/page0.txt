We have spent more than a decade in the trenches of modern software delivery — first at Docker, where we helped define what container-based development looks like, then at Dagger, where we tried to make CI programmable instead of declarative. Along the way we watched the same pattern repeat at every company we worked with.. Engineering teams pour an enormous amount of human attention into the mechanics of shipping: chasing flaky tests, triaging CI failures, fighting build slowdowns, reading the same CVE advisories every week, and reviewing the same kinds of mistakes in pull requests. None of that work is what anyone signed up for. It is also exactly the kind of work that modern AI agents are good at, when they are given enough context.. Mendral is our attempt to take that work off engineers' plates. Not as a copilot that suggests fixes for a human to accept, but as an AI DevOps Engineer that does the job end to end: diagnoses, fixes, opens the pull request, and learns from what happened. Three always-on agents — Security, Reliability, and Performance — run from day one. The Security Agent reviews dependency PRs, pins safe versions, and surfaces only the CVEs actually exploitable in your code; it also catches compromised dependencies, malicious actions, and leaked secrets before they hit production. The Reliability Agent diagnoses CI failures and fixes flaky tests. The Performance Agent cuts build time via caching, parallelism, and slow-test pruning. On top of that, customers can add custom automations triggered from CI/CD events, infra alerts (Datadog, Sentry, cloud deploys), schedules, Slack, Linear, or webhooks. Mendral plugs into CI/CD, infrastructure, and notifications, then learns your stack from every run, every deploy, and every commit.. . Which CI providers are supported?. GitHub Actions today.
