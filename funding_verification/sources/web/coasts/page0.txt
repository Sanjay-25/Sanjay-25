Worktrees isolate code, not runtimes.. Your agents work in parallel, but your machine doesn't. Every worktree still runs on the same local environment — causing potential migration conflicts, port collisions, and competition for shared resources. So you validate sequentially, spin up cloud resources, or buy another laptop — while the bulk of your hardware sits idle.. Coasts are the local-first, free and open source solution to spawning parallel runtimes for your agents. A coast is a full runtime — its own ports, its own network, its own services. Run each stack in full isolation, or choose which resources to share across your coasts. Coasts work with any agentic coding solution — all you need is git worktrees.. . . . . . // how it works.
