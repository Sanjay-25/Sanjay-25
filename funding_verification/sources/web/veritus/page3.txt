Consumer lending . Where lending funnels really leak. An applicant checks their rate, gets qualified, and stalls. It happens across the industry: most approved applicants never accept their loan offers, and a significant percentage drop out before funding. Uncertainty about rates and terms, friction in uploading documents or verifying identity, or simply getting distracted. Each abandoned application is wasted marketing spend and missed revenue.. This case study breaks down how one digital-first lending platform plugged the leak, with an AI agent that now converts at the level of its most advanced human strategies.. . . About Splash Financial . A digital-first lender in a race to fund every qualified borrower. Splash Financial is a remote-first company based in Cleveland, Ohio, that operates a consumer lending platform connecting borrowers with a network of banks and credit unions for personal loans and refinancing. Its customers arrive digitally and want to finish digitally.. Mostly. Between “I've seen my rate” and “my loan is funded” sits the hard part: income documentation and identity verification. That's where qualified borrowers stall, and where a well-timed phone call changes the outcome.. . . The challenge . A race you lose by staying quiet. Many of the borrowers using Splash's platform arrive through personal finance sites like Credit Karma, and many are applying with several lenders at once. By the time someone pre-qualifies, Splash has already spent the marketing dollars to win them and wants to fund the loan. The race is to close it before a competitor does.. Outreach decides that race. The borrower is choosing between offers, from brands they may not be familiar with. A call that explains the product and clears a stuck document upload is often the reason a loan funds with you instead of someone else.. Splash knew what those calls were worth. Its most advanced calling strategies, built on real-time data, converted meaningfully better than the standard approach. The catch: that tier takes real staffing and constant effort, and it can't cover the whole funnel.. Before Sofia, the rest of the funnel got a mix of ringless voicemail drops layered with inconsistent human outreach. No conversation, and no way to help a self-employed borrower figure out which documents to upload.. Why Splash picked a lending specialist over horizontal AI. Splash evaluated a number of horizontal AI vendors alongside Veritus. They chose Veritus for one primary reason: the platform is 100% focused on the lending industry.. That focus showed up everywhere Splash looked. The compliance engine ships with TCPA enforcement and state-by-state calling rules already built in. The agents already understood income verification and the edge cases of a lending funnel. And the team behind them had spent years inside consumer lending operations.. Horizontal platforms offered a toolkit. Veritus offered a trained employee.. . . . . . The gap Sofia closes . 01 Applicant checks their rate . Qualified — and already paid for. . . 02 Income docs & identity check . Where applications stall. . . 03 Loan funded . Revenue. . . Every qualified applicant who stalls at that middle step is marketing spend already spent. Sofia calls them. . . . . The solution . Sofia, Splash's Veritus voice agent. Veritus built Sofia, an outbound voice agent for Splash's post-qualification funnel. She launched in March 2026, calling borrowers who had seen their rate but hadn't finished their application.. On every call, Sofia can:. Verify the borrower's identity before discussing any account details. Answer the questions that stall applications: which documents prove self-employment income, how multiple income sources like a pension, a W2, and Social Security work together, and what happens after submission. Walk borrowers through document upload and identity verification, including live troubleshooting. Recognize when a borrower is busy or reluctant and offer a callback instead of pushing. Escalate to a human agent the moment one is requested. And Splash initially ran her with a handicap. Sofia worked from batch data files with a 24-hour delay and made fewer contact attempts than the human benchmark allowed. She beat the baseline anyway.. . . The results . What happens when someone answers the phone. Across April, May, and June 2026, Sofia converted post-qualification applicants at a rate more than 20% above Splash's human baseline. That put her at the level Splash had only reached with its advanced human strategies — strategies that had real-time data and outreach freedom Sofia didn't get.*. Containment came in at 74% against a 70% target. Most borrowers who reached Sofia had their question resolved on the call itself, and the small share who asked for a human were transferred with no friction.. Complaints stayed rare, and none were about Sofia. The ones that did come in were about the same things borrowers complained about before Sofia existed.. What do borrowers ask Sofia? Nearly half of her conversations were general loan questions and income documentation: loan status, requesting a different amount, what a self-employed borrower needs to upload, how multiple income sources work. The unglamorous, knowledge-heavy questions that kill applications when nobody answers the phone.. * Methodology: period-over-period comparison of Sofia-only months (April–June 2026) against Splash's human-only baseline periods, excluding known origination outliers. Not a concurrent A/B test.. . . . . . “We looked at the horizontal AI vendors and went with Veritus because they're built for lending, not retrofitted for it. Their agents work across our funnel — turning applications that would otherwise stall into funded loans — capacity we could never have hired for.” Vaunte Epps. Director, Member Experience, Splash Financial. . . . Why Sofia works . What makes her different. She knows lending. Her knowledge base covers the edge cases that break generic bots: self-employment documentation, retirement and military income, Socure troubleshooting, even pronouncing “1099” and lender names correctly. A borrower with three income sources gets a real answer, not a transfer.. She has judgment. Sofia recognizes reluctance and busy signals and offers callbacks rather than plowing ahead. She doesn't repeat verification prompts or pad calls with redundant closings. Borrowers get a call that respects their time.. She's compliant by construction. She works from Splash's approved scripts and disclosures, and her guardrails enforce them on every call — Fair Lending and ECOA, TILA, FCRA, GLBA, and UDAAP. She identifies herself as an AI agent. She doesn't make credit decisions.. . . Under the hood . How Sofia handles the hard stuff. Outbound identity verification. Sofia confirms she's speaking with the right borrower before any account detail is discussed, keeping every conversation inside Splash's privacy and compliance requirements.. Real answers on income and documents. The knowledge base was tuned call by call through the pilot: income verification guidance for retirement and military borrowers, self-employment documentation, and troubleshooting steps for Socure identity checks. Each fix shipped within days.. Compliance engine and 100% QA coverage. The platform is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, with real-time conversation guardrails and monthly case-law review with Veritus's counsel. Automated QA evaluates every single call, human and AI alike. Splash's compliance team sees everything.. Smart escalation. Sofia transfers to Splash's team on request and flags accounts that need follow-up. Containment is the outcome of serving borrowers well, never the goal in itself.. . . . . . Take it with you.
