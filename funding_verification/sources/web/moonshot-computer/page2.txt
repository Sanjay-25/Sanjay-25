How Moonshot works and how to get early access moonshot home About Moonshot. We want to bring AI agents to a billion people.. To do this, we believe it needs to be 1. proactive and 2. personal. A billion people already carry the right device, but none of them have the right connector.. Moonshot is personal intelligence that lives on your iPhone and listens to you 24/7. It continuously builds up a greater understanding of you through constantly listening and building context. It just pays deep attention, the way a good friend does. Through this, Moonshot monitors your conscious and subconscious life, figuring out what you need before you know, reminding you about open loops, identifying life and emotional trends before you recognize it, and covering a lot more of what we gloss over in daily life.. For fifty years, software has waited to be asked. We think tools are just good enough to flip this, and the next great software will provide value before asking for something first. We're doing it through Moonshot.. Our thesis is simple: you're only living half your life. The half that you actively remember. The other half just evaporates into your subconscious: what you said, felt, promised, and forgot. It's the more interesting half, and until now nothing could catch it, making it not actionable.. Moonshot catches your subconscious half and understands you well enough to take actions on your behalf.. Take life personally.. Access. Moonshot is in a founding beta with 100 seats for exceptional people. Sign in with Google on the homepage and the door reads your world and decides. The Google sign-in is the path into early access.. Moonshot is built by a small team in San Francisco, backed by Y Combinator. Product, press, partnership, and data questions reach us through the Contact page..
