Sparkles is a safe online development environment that lets everyone on your team, not just engineers, take part in shipping software. Agents work on your existing repositories in isolated sandboxes, and every change comes back as a pull request.. . . . svg]:rotate-180 text-foreground min-h-14 items-center py-3 text-lg leading-tight font-medium hover:no-underline sm:text-xl" id="bits-s13" aria-expanded="false" aria-disabled="false" data-state="closed" data-orientation="vertical" data-accordion-trigger="" tabindex="0"> Can Sparkles break production? . No. Agents work in disposable cloud sandboxes and can only propose changes as GitHub pull requests. Your branch protections, reviews, and CI apply exactly as they do today.. . . . svg]:rotate-180 text-foreground min-h-14 items-center py-3 text-lg leading-tight font-medium hover:no-underline sm:text-xl" id="bits-s17" aria-expanded="false" aria-disabled="false" data-state="closed" data-orientation="vertical" data-accordion-trigger="" tabindex="0"> How is this different from Lovable or Cursor? . Lovable builds new apps from scratch; Cursor is an IDE for one engineer. Sparkles works on the repositories you already have and is shared with your whole organization. Every session and every proposed change is visible to the team..
