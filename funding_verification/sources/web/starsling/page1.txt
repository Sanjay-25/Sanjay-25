What makes StarSling different. . 01 Faster runs and ongoing improvements. StarSling combines runner infrastructure with agents that improve the workflow itself. At Mastra , the measured test suite went from 29m 56s to 5m 06s at wall p95 after the runner migration and optimization work. . . 02 Changes your engineers can review. Optimization arrives as a pull request with changes and an explanation. Your engineers review and merge improvements through their existing GitHub process; see real agent-authored PRs . . . 03 Usage-based compute pricing. StarSling's 4 vCPU / 16 GB Linux runner costs $0.008 per minute, compared with $0.012 for GitHub's 4-core Linux x64 larger runner . You pay only for the minutes your jobs run. . . 04 Your existing GitHub Actions workflows. For supported Ubuntu jobs, migration starts by installing the GitHub App and changing the runner label. Your team continues working with GitHub Actions workflows and pull-request checks; follow the quickstart . . . 05 Code review configured in your repository. Review Runners , available through early access, let teams version their model configuration, review skills, scripts, and reviewer responsibilities alongside their code. You pay StarSling for runner usage and your chosen provider for model tokens. . . . . . Who uses StarSling. . Software teams running build and test workflows on GitHub Actions..
