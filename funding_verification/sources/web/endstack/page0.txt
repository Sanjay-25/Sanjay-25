Endstack is the Superagent that handles all of your work — code, ops, marketing, and more — on a collaborative desktop workspace.. It gives you a real workspace on the web, mobile, and desktop — backed by Linux in the cloud — where your files, documents, code, browser tabs, apps, and agent all live. Your workspace desktop runs 24/7 and is accessible from anywhere.. How Endstack is structured. Every user in a workspace gets a full cloud desktop running in the cloud. When you sign in, you get your own persistent environment with a filesystem, a browser, a terminal, a code editor, and more. All workspaces come with a team desktop that is shared by all users and channels, a way for you to collaborate with your agent and other team members.. One agent per desktop. Each desktop comes with a built-in AI agent that is integrated at the operating system level. The agent can see your files, run terminal commands, browse the web, and use every app on your desktop — just like a colleague sitting at the same computer.. Individual and team desktops. Individual plans give you your own private desktop and agent.. Team plans give your team a shared desktop with shared files, shared agent context, and user isolation.. Your data stays on your machine. All your files, conversations, settings, and agent memory live on your container's filesystem — not on external databases.. What makes Endstack different. Most agent platforms are assembled from separate parts — a model, a browser tool, a sandbox, a scheduler, and several dashboards. Endstack takes a different approach: the operating system is the interface.. Your agent works inside the same familiar desktop you do. There is no setup and no learning curve.
