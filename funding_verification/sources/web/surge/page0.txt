For SMS, test with a demo number before setting up production registration. . . . . . The team behind Surge. Surge started with a practical problem. In early 2024, Dennis Beatty was building a CRM for small gyms and a communications tool for hearing implant specialists. Both needed messaging, and his earlier work with SMS had shown him how difficult it was to add well.. On a drive from California to Utah, Dennis had the idea for embeddable messaging for vertical SaaS. When another platform offered to pay him to build an SMS inbox, he persuaded them to become Surge’s first customer instead. With a paying customer in place, Brett Beatty left his job building VoIP systems for small businesses to join Dennis as co-founder.. The founders joined Y Combinator’s Fall 2024 batch . There, they saw a broader need: developers everywhere were struggling with SMS setup and carrier registration. They expanded Surge into a developer-focused SMS and voice platform while continuing to serve vertical SaaS companies. Today, the growing team works together in Lehi, Utah..
