The idea is pretty simple. Your dev box lives in the cloud instead of on your laptop, and it stays on when you don't, so your agents can keep working after you close the lid. Step away and it suspends. Come back and everything is right where you left it.. Renting a machine in the cloud was never the hard part. Making one feel like your own laptop is, and that's where most of our time goes. Files sync both ways in about 100 ms, .git included. A server on the box shows up on your localhost a few seconds later. Sessions stay alive, so you can detach on the train, reattach at your desk, and pick up mid-thought.. Thanks to everyone building with us. There's plenty left to do, so we'll get back to it.. . - Hudson Griffith & Marinos Eliades, cofounders of Prized. . . The dev box that feels like localhost.. .
