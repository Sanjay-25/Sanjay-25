F R E Q U E N T L Y A S K E D Q U E S T I O N S . Everything you need to know about your EGO and AI Passport. . . What does the AI Passport actually do? It is a permission-based context and identity layer for AI. Instead of reintroducing yourself to every assistant and app, your passport carries verified context like your preferences, goals, and connected apps, and shares it only when you approve.. . . . . . What can apps actually see? Only the slice you grant. An app asks for a pass to a specific category, you approve or deny it, and every access shows up in your receipts. You can cut an app off at any time.. . . . . . Where does my data live? In your passport, not scattered across apps. You feed it once and it travels with you. Remove something and it is gone at the source.. . . . . . Is this another social network? No. Your ego page is a page you can share, but the passport itself is plumbing. It works quietly underneath the AI tools you already use.. . . . . . What does it cost? Free for life for individuals. We are still experimenting with pricing for shared things, like group connectors and memory you share with other people. Business and enterprise pricing is separate.. . . . . . Can my app or company use this? Yes. Apps request a pass to the slices they need, people approve, and every access is logged. If you are building with context, start on our developer page . Early-stage startups can start free on the startup offer . . . . . . . Where does the AI run? On machines we control. Your questions are answered by an AI we run ourselves, so no other company ever sees what you ask. Questions you mark private are answered and then forgotten.. . . . . . . .
