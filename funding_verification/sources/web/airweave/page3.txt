We're proud to announce that we've raised a $6M seed round led by FCVC, with participation from LUX Capital, Y Combinator, Orange Collective, Pioneer Fund, Shay Banon (Founder of Elastic), and a group of phenomenal investors and angels.. Airweave started as a solution to our own frustration: agents couldn’t meaningfully search or understand our apps, limiting their potential to do useful work. After our initial release, we quickly realized how fundamental this problem was in the agent ecosystem. Enabling agents to deeply understand our systems is crucial to turning AI’s potential into real productivity.. This funding accelerates our mission to empower agents with critical knowledge and unlock the agent economy’s full potential. Now, we are building a small team of exceptional engineers to define the future of agent knowledge.
