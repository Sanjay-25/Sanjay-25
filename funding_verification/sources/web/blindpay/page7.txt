Go Back Yield and ACH pull in private beta, Tempo and Arc networks, prompt library. . Eric Viana, Bernardo Simonassi, André Mayer, Rafael Thayto . • September 18, 2026 . . . Two private betas this month: Yield, so managed wallets earn on the USDC they hold, and ACH pull, so a payin can debit a customer's bank account directly. Also new: Tempo and Arc networks, and a prompt library for AI coding agents. . TL;DR . Yield (private beta) : turn it on for a managed wallet and the idle USDC balance starts earning . ACH pull (private beta) : customers connect their bank account once and payins pull the funds, no manual transfer . Tempo and Arc : two new networks for wallets, payins, payouts and transfers . Prompt library : ready-to-paste prompts that build a full BlindPay integration with your AI coding agent . Yield (private beta) . Managed wallets can now earn on the stablecoins they hold. Turn yield on once per wallet and BlindPay moves the idle USDC into a lending vault, keeps it earning, and pulls it back automatically whenever a payout or transfer needs the funds. Turn it off and the full position returns to the wallet. There is no fee on the balance, only a performance fee on gains, and the APY we report is already net of it. . Yield is in private beta. Reach out to your BlindPay contact to enable it on your instance. Read the Yield guide . . ACH pull (private beta) . Payins in the US no longer depend on the customer sending a transfer. The customer connects their bank account once through a hosted page, and from then on a payin with the new ach_pull payment method debits that account directly. Connected accounts are verified as first party before they can be used. . ACH pull is in private beta. Reach out to your BlindPay contact to enable it on your instance. Read the bank accounts guide . . Tempo and Arc networks . Tempo and Arc are live for managed wallets, offramp wallets, payins, payouts and transfers. Tempo supports USDC.e and USDT0, Arc supports USDC. Both have a matching testnet on development instances. See the supported networks . . Prompt library . We published a library of prompts that walk an AI coding agent through a complete BlindPay integration, from a payin or payout quickstart to payables and wallets, with the API gotchas already encoded.
