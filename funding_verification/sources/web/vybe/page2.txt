Vybe raises $10M to make vibe-coding work within companies. Look inside every fast-growing company, and you'll see internal operations glued together with rigid SaaS, old school spreadsheets, and manual hacks. From admin consoles to onboarding workflows, and performance reviews to content calendars, these fragile tools slow teams down, break easily, and are hard to evolve. Meanwhile, engineering teams are stretched thin, so building or maintaining internal tools never makes it to the top of the backlog.. Vibe-coding is changing the game: using natural language and AI to build software has emerged as a powerful new way to create software faster, even by non-developers. But so far, vibe-coding has mostly proven useful for prototyping, landing pages, or small games. It falls short when you want to build secure, data-connected, team-ready apps that can actually run your business processes. We built Vybe to change that.. Now you can vibe-code secure, connected internal apps. Vybe lets anyone inside a company build secure internal apps connected to their real data — no engineering time required.. The simplicity of ChatGPT : Describe what you want in natural language, and Vybe generates real production-grade software.. Data-connected : Securely hook into Postgres, Salesforce, Jira, Redshift, MySQL, and many more.. Team-ready : Auth, roles, and deployments are all baked in from the start.. Secure : Our security layer is not vibe-coded and can't be modified by AI.. Templates : Remix apps based on the best practices of top operators.. The result is that operations teams, PMs, RevOps, HR, and other non-technical builders can finally ship the tools they need — securely, collaboratively, and without waiting in line.. Over the last few months, we've been in closed waitlist mode with over 1,000 sign-ups and hand-onboarded over a dozen teams to pressure-test Vybe on real, production-grade apps:. One CEO told us that running his whole customer success operation through Vybe is saving him ~2 days/week.. . Another ingested millions of rows from their warehouse to build BI-like internal views, something that would've broken typical vibe-coding platforms.. . A company replaced their BI tool (Metabase and Looker) altogether with Vybe. They plugged their redshift data warehouse and just started prompting to see important charts like MAU, DAU, drop-off funnel etc.. . Today we're opening sign-ups. No more waitlist. .
