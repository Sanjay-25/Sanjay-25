Harbor is electronic data capture software that helps medical device, biotech, and pharma companies set up studies and collect high-quality clinical data. While "agentic" has taken on a new meaning in the AI era, we define it simply: we turn the EDC from a passive database for data collection into an active worker in your clinical studies. Our software reads and interprets your study protocol, parses through messy source documents and enters data into CRFs for coordinators, and constantly looks through your data to identify any potential issues. As a result, we give lean clinical teams the capacity to run ambitious studies, while preserving the data quality and traceability regulated research demands.. . What Harbor does.
