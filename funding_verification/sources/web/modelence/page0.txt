Modelence is an AI-powered app builder that lets you create production-ready, full-stack web applications by describing what you want in plain language. Every app comes with authentication, a scalable database, monitoring, and one-click deployment built in - so you can go from idea to live app in minutes, not months.. Do I need coding experience to build an app with Modelence? No. Modelence is designed so anyone can build a full-stack application without writing code. Describe what you want in your own words, and the AI generates the frontend, backend, and database for you. If you do know how to code, you can access and extend the generated code at any time.. How is Modelence different from other AI app builders like Bolt.new or Lovable? Most AI app builders generate frontend prototypes that you then need to stitch together with separate backend, database, and hosting services. Modelence is different because it generates a complete, production-grade app on a framework purpose-built for AI, with authentication, database, monitoring, and deployment included out of the box. Your apps are ready to serve real user traffic, not just demos.. Can Modelence apps handle real production traffic? Yes. Unlike tools that produce throwaway prototypes, Modelence apps are built on a production-grade framework with secure authentication, enterprise-scale MongoDB databases, and managed cloud infrastructure. Apps scale automatically and include built-in monitoring, logging, and AI-powered alerting so you can serve real users from day one.. Do I own the code and data from apps built on Modelence? Yes, you own everything. There is no vendor lock-in. You can export your code to GitHub, run it on your own machine, or deploy it anywhere you choose. Your data stays yours and you can leave anytime.. What kinds of apps can I build with Modelence? You can build any web application: SaaS products, CRMs, internal tools, dashboards, booking platforms, marketplaces, project management tools, and more. Every app gets a complete full-stack setup with frontend, backend, database, and user authentication wired together out of the box.. Can I connect my own custom domain? Yes. You can point your own domain to your Modelence app with a few clicks. SSL certificates are provisioned automatically, so your app is secure and professional from the start.. Is Modelence free to use? Yes, you can start building for free. Modelence offers a free tier that includes everything you need to build and deploy your first app. Paid plans are available for teams and projects that need additional resources, higher usage limits, or priority support.. . . .
