What does Boom actually do? + – Boom runs real conversations with your customers on the channels they already use. It asks the follow-up, acts on the answer (collects payments, wins customers back, qualifies leads) and turns every conversation into data you can report on.. Which channels does it work on? + – WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, SMS, email and voice. One agent and one memory across all of them, so a customer who switches channels stays in one conversation.. How is this different from sending a survey? + – A survey stops at the answer. Boom asks the next question. “Too expensive” becomes which plan they would have kept. Interview depth, survey scale.. Does it replace my customer team? + – No. It takes the volume your team never reaches, and hands off the moment a person should step in. Your team sees every thread.. How long does it take to get running? + – Days. Connect a channel, pick who to reach, and review the first conversations before going to full volume.. How do you handle our customer data? + – Every conversation is scoped to your organization, with access control and a full audit trail.
