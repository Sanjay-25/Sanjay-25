What Caretta does. What Caretta does: one AI for before, during and after every sales call. . . People buy from people. Before features or pricing, the question is “Do I want to do business with this person?”. The best salespeople already know this.. Caretta gives reps complete knowledge of their product and their sales process, live, so they can spend the call on the human connection that actually sells. Most sales tools work on the simpler problems before or after the call. We built the fast, large intelligence needed to work inside it.. . . The part that matters.. Realtime intelligence for the moments that decide whether the deal moves forward.. . . Before the call. Caretta builds a brief and a game plan for every meeting from your CRM, past calls and documents. Reps walk in knowing the account story instead of researching it.. Before the call . During the call. Caretta listens to the live conversation and surfaces answers, objection handling and next steps as the prospect speaks. Reps handle deep product and technical questions without an engineer on the line.. During the call . After the call. Transcripts, notes, next steps and clean CRM fields are written for you, and the summary lands in Slack before you stand up. Follow-up stops being homework.. After the call . Caretta Metrics. Every call is scored against your sales methodology: discovery questions asked, next steps set, objections handled, pricing discussed. Leaders see what separates the best calls from the rest.. Metrics . Brain. Websites, docs, Slack, apps and every past call are connected into one organisational brain that stays current. It is the knowledge Caretta draws on live.. Brain . MCP server and webhooks. An MCP server with OAuth gives AI agents scoped access to calls, transcripts and todos. Signed webhooks deliver notes and metrics to your own systems after every call..
