Cyber Essentials Plus. Avoice Lab holds Cyber Essentials Plus, the UK Government-backed scheme, with the whole organisation in scope. Every device our team uses was audited by an IASME-licensed certification body, not self-declared. The certificate is available on request.. . . Independent penetration testing. The Avoice application was penetration tested by Casco in February 2026, with remediation independently verified in March 2026. Every finding was fixed; none were risk-accepted. A summary report is available under NDA.. . . UK data residency. Project data is stored in Amazon Web Services' UK region. UK residency is standard on every plan, including pilots, not an enterprise add-on.. . . Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Connections to Avoice use TLS, and stored files, specifications, and registers are encrypted on disk.. . . Backups and recovery. Project data is backed up continuously to UK storage. Enterprise agreements include a 99.5% uptime commitment with service credits.. . . Restricted internal access. Production access is limited to named engineers, protected by single sign-on and multi-factor authentication, and revoked within two hours of anyone leaving. Our Google Workspace integration has passed Google's CASA security assessment.. . . . . . 02. AI that works on your data, not with it. . Practices rightly ask where their specifications go when AI drafts them. The answer is short.. . . Zero retention. The AI model providers Avoice uses operate under zero-data-retention terms. Prompts and outputs are not stored by them once a request completes.. . . . No training on your projects. Third-party model providers do not train on your specifications, drawings, or correspondence. Your practice's clause library and masters stay private to your practice.. . . . Insured for AI work. Avoice carries professional indemnity, cyber, and AI liability cover. The insurance certificate is part of our standard terms pack.. . . . . . . 03. Custom permissions. Set exactly who can see and edit each project, down to the individual specification.. . . Project visibility. Open a project to the whole practice, or limit it to named members only.. . . . Access per specification. Set edit, view, or no access for each person and each specification. Give someone edit rights to the building fabric spec, read-only access to structure, and nothing else.. . . . Free viewer role. Consultants, contractors, and clients join as viewers at no cost and only see what they are given. Issued revisions go out from the Publication Library, with an optional password on the link.. . . .
