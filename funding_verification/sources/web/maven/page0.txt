No. Card data never touches your servers. Maven is PCI compliant and handles all card collection and processing. All you do is create a session and transfer the call. Maven handles the rest.. . . . . What does the caller actually hear? . A natural-sounding AI voice greets the caller and walks them through entering their card details. They can speak their card number or type it on their keypad. You can customize the voice, greeting, and input mode.. . . . . How long does integration take? . Most teams are live in under 10 minutes. For VAPI, you connect in the dashboard with one click. For Retell, paste a webhook URL and copy our JSON schemas. For direct API usage, it's a single POST to create a session.. . . . . Can I collect payments in chat, not just on calls? . Yes. The Maven chat widget renders a secure card form inline in your chatbot or web app. Your server creates a widget session, your page mounts the widget with one call, and you get the same webhooks as voice. Card data goes straight from the customer's browser to Maven.. . . . . What payment gateways do you support? . Twenty-five gateways: Stripe, Adyen, Fiserv, J.P. Morgan, Worldpay, Global Payments, Braintree, Authorize.net, Cybersource, Checkout.com, Shift4, NMI, Clover, Paysafe, CardConnect, Moneris, PayPal Payflow, Elavon, USAePay, Qualpay, Fortis, QuickBooks Payments, Trust Payments, Fat Zebra, and Ecommpay. Connect your gateway credentials and Maven processes payments through it on your behalf. If you use a processor we don't list, Maven can forward the collected card to your own PCI-compliant endpoint.. . . . . Can I preauthorize cards without charging? . Yes. Set the mode to tokenize and Maven collects and stores the card details on your gateway without charging. Useful for saving a card on file for future billing.. . . . . What happens if the payment fails? . Maven has built-in smart retry logic. If a card is declined, a digit is missed, or a fake card number is detected, it handles the conversation gracefully and re-prompts the caller. No awkward silences, no dropped calls.. . . . . . . .
