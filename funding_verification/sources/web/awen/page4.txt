You can now use Seedance 2.5 in awen to create clips up to 30 seconds long in one pass , with sound and up to 30 reference images to keep characters, styles, products, and locations consistent.. A whole scene in one generation. Most video models in awen generate clips up to 15 seconds long. Seedance 2.5 doubles that limit, giving you enough time for a complete scene or multi-shot sequence without stitching shorter clips together.. Keep more of your world consistent. Seedance 2.5 accepts up to 30 reference images , up from nine. Give awen several views of a character, a mood board, a product, or a location, and it can carry those details across the full clip.. It also follows framing, composition, and staging more closely than Seedance 2.0, giving you more control over specific shots.. Sound and resolution. Sound is on by default at no extra generation cost, with native speech across 11 languages. You can still ask for a silent clip.. Seedance 2.5 is available at 720p and 480p . Use 720p for its best output quality or 480p for lower-cost exploration. For final work at 1080p or 4K, use Seedance 2.0 or upscale the finished video..
