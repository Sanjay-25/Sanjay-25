At Y Combinator we built Koliseum to grade models against reality itself. We started in financial work, where we knew the cost of getting it wrong.. . . We learned to trust live results over historical fit. Kimpton brings that discipline to AI.. . . . . How the trading loop maps to AI systems.. We used backtests to develop strategies. We used live markets to find out whether they worked. A backtest can reward contamination or historical fit. A live test forces a model to act on data it has never seen.. Kimpton applies that discipline to AI systems: models, agents, predictions, actions, and the results they produce. Every run in an environment is recorded as a trajectory, so evaluation and training read from the same record.. . . Koliseum. Observe . Act in environment . Record trajectory . Evaluate . Train . Forward test . . . Observe . Act in environment . Record trajectory . Evaluate . Train . Forward test . Repeat from observe.. . . Stage Quantitative trading AI systems . Observe. Capture market data, positions, orders, risk, and outcomes.. Capture inputs, tool calls, and the state of the environment.. . Act in environment. Run the strategy live or in the simulator and let it place orders.. Run the model in the environment and let it decide and act.. . Record trajectory. Keep the full record of orders, fills, and marks for every session.. Store observations, actions, rewards, and state transitions for every run.. . Evaluate. Replay the record against point-in-time data and a disclosed baseline.. Verify the result, the resulting state, and the constraints against a disclosed baseline.. . Train. Change signals, parameters, portfolio rules, or execution.. The trainer updates the model, prompt, tools, or policy from the trajectories.. . Forward test. Freeze the strategy and run it on market data it has never seen.. Freeze the checkpoint and run it on tasks and data created after training.. . . . . Historical episodes are the backtests. Live environments then capture new data in real time. After training, the system encounters work and data it could not have seen before.
