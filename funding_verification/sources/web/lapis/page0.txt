Lapis is the operating system for paid growth. ChatSense helps brands advertise inside AI conversations. OmniSense turns briefs into channel-ready creative and experiments. RapidDomain gives each campaign a matched landing page. Together, they connect the work before the click, the experience after it, and the learning that shapes the next campaign.. . . . . Can I use Lapis myself? Yes. Self-serve plans give your team the brand, creative, audience, forecasting, and iteration tools to build and run campaigns in the app. Capabilities and supported channels vary by plan.. . . . . What does managed mean? On managed plans, Lapis agents and a dedicated strategist operate the campaign loop with you. They structure experiments, prepare creative, support launch and optimization, analyze results, and recommend what to test or fund next within agreed guardrails.. . . . . How does Lapis learn my brand? Paste your website URL and Lapis extracts a reviewable starting point for your logo, colors, visual style, products, and voice. Add references and brand rules to shape what the system creates.. . . . . Which advertising channels does Lapis support? Self-serve plans support Meta, with Google included on Pro. Managed plans cover Google, Meta, Reddit, and LinkedIn. ChatGPT and emerging LLM placements are available through the ChatGPT + LLM Ads managed plan.. . . . . Does Lapis create ads, run campaigns, or both? Both, depending on how you work with Lapis. Self-serve customers use the platform to create, forecast, and launch their own campaigns. Managed customers work with Lapis agents and a dedicated strategist across creative, experiments, launch, reporting, and the next optimization decision.. . . . . How do landing pages fit into the system? RapidDomain builds on-brand pages matched to each ad's message and conversion goal. It tests full-page directions and uses post-click behavior to inform what the team launches next. Paired with ChatSense and OmniSense, it closes the loop from creative to click to conversion.. . . . . How reliable are Lapis performance forecasts? Forecasts are directional planning signals, not guaranteed outcomes. Use them to compare creative before launch, then use live campaign results as the source of truth.. . . . . Does Lapis move my budget automatically? Managed workflows surface budget recommendations within agreed guardrails. Your team keeps control of approvals, limits, and the decisions that matter.. . . . . How much does Lapis cost? Self-serve plans start at $99 per month for Basic and $599 per month for Pro. Managed Traditional Ads and ChatGPT + LLM Ads are scoped to your channels, campaign needs, and operating model.. . . . . . . . . . . Your ads should get smarter every day.
