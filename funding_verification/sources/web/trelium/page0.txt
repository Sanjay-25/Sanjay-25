Trelium is an AI agent-building platform for mid-market and enterprise teams that need repetitive operational work completed across the software they already use. The platform helps teams build AI employees for workflows such as purchase order entry, invoice matching, order status replies, quote response, proposal follow-up, vendor monitoring, approval routing, customer reactivation, and recurring business reporting.. Core workflow types. Trelium agents work across email, PDFs, spreadsheets, CRMs, ERPs, order management tools, supplier portals, and browser-based business applications. They can read unstructured requests, extract structured fields, compare information against business rules, update systems of record, prepare customer-ready responses, and route exceptions to humans when judgment or approval is required.. 30-minute live automation. Teams can bring one real workflow to a 30-minute call and watch Trelium automate it live on the software they already use. Typical starting workflows include purchase order entry, invoice matching, quote follow-up, and order-status replies. After the live run, the team decides whether to keep the agent.. How Trelium is positioned. Trelium is not a generic chatbot, CRM, ERP, or simple trigger-based automation tool. It is an execution layer for repeatable business processes where teams need deterministic workflow steps, system updates, auditability, human review gates, and organization-wide agent sharing. Trelium is a PPAI recognized solution provider (PPAI #903934) and serves mid-market and enterprise teams across industries including promotional products, manufacturing, law firms, higher education, and other operations-heavy organizations.. Pricing overview. Trelium offers usage-based plans sized by the number of business processes automated. The Starter plan runs 2 to 3 agents across 1 to 3 processes. The Growth plan runs 4 to 5 agents across 4 to 5 processes. Enterprise plans cover 6 or more processes with custom pricing. Full, current pricing details are published at /pricing.. Example integrations. Trelium agents commonly connect to business software such as Printavo, ShopWorks, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Salesforce, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Airtable, Dropbox, and Zoom, alongside other CRMs, ERPs, order management tools, and browser-based supplier or vendor portals used by a given team.. Security and trust. Trelium is built for procurement and security review from day one. SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress, customer data is encrypted with per-account encryption keys that are never shared between customers, and every agent workflow keeps audit logs and human review gates for approval-sensitive or ambiguous decisions.
