Product compliance shouldn't be a patchwork of spreadsheets, PDFs, and supplier emails. We help product teams reduce risk, cut delays, and get products to market faster.. . . . Our story Why we started Complir. While e-commerce, logistics, and sourcing have been transformed by modern software, product compliance is still stuck in the past. Most teams rely on legacy ERPs, spreadsheets, email threads, and manual work just to keep products legally sellable. As companies scale, compliance turns into a hidden bottleneck - slowing launches, creating risk, and pushing revenue down the line.. Built side by side with compliance teams. Just three weeks after founding Complir, we moved into Flying Tiger's HQ to sit alongside their product compliance team. Not to pitch - to observe. We mapped real workflows, decisions, and bottlenecks. We didn't build based on assumptions; we built based on what we saw and heard directly from the people doing the work. Today, those same teams use Complir tailored to their needs.. . . . . What we believe Principles that guide us. . . Build with users, not for them. We listen before we launch - and follow up to keep improving.. . . A repetitive task is a signal to automate. When manual work shows up again and again, it’s time to make it smarter.. . .
