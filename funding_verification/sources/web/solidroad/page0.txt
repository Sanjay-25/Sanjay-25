Careers. . . An open letter from our founders. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Careers. . . An open letter from our founders. . . . . . . . . . This is not a normal startup. . . There's a wall in our office covered with rejection emails from investors who passed on us. Next to them hangs a hand-drawn revenue chart that starts at $1,000 in monthly revenue and climbs into the billions.. We made it when things weren't going well. Mark's calendar was empty. Our Stripe dashboard was empty. Our ideas list wasn't looking much better. We had left good jobs to try this, and there were plenty of moments when that decision looked questionable. But there was something in both of us that refused to buckle. We wanted to build a genuinely valuable business. In software, revenue is the clearest scoreboard we know. Customers don't pay you because you worked hard or had a clever idea. They pay you because you made something useful enough to be worth their money. The incentives line up: we only really win when our customers do.. The line has moved a long way since those empty-calendar days. Today, large companies use Solidroad to train and improve their human and AI agents. Relative to what we want to build, we are still near the bottom of the chart.. . . . . Solidroad Tower. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . We have another poster on our wall. It shows an updated Google Maps listing for the tower formerly known as Salesforce Tower. In our version, it has been renamed Solidroad Tower after we acquired the naming rights as part of becoming the anchor tenant.. Next to it is a fake headline announcing Solidroad's IPO in 2032. Salesforce doesn't own Salesforce Tower. It became the anchor tenant, and the naming rights came with the lease. Our plan is to do the same.. . That's the scale of our ambition. We want to build an enduring technology company large enough to put its name on the San Francisco skyline.. There are a lot of steps between here and there. Thousands of customers to win. Difficult problems to solve. Products to build. Mistakes to make and learn from. And, most importantly, a company to build with exceptional people who want to do the best work of their careers.. If that sounds like an exciting way to spend a meaningful chunk of your career, keep reading.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . See the Solidroad journey. . . . . . . . . . Our philosophy. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . A job and a hobby. . Nobody clocks in and out of an obsession. We want people drawn to work that's hard and rewarding enough that they'd put real hours into it because they want to, the kind of work that expands your life rather than just filling your calendar. As someone here put it, working at Solidroad is like signing up for a job and an Ironman.. . . An elite team, not a happy family. . We hold a high standard for everyone, and we earn our spots through the work rather than through tenure or loyalty. Performance is what counts, and we give each other direct, honest feedback in service of it. That means no coasting, and no avoiding hard conversations.. . . Capitalism is a contact sport. . There are no participation trophies. We're in a daily competition to earn our customers' business, against every other company and every other solution that wants it too. Our job is to be better than all of them, and then to earn that position again the next day.. . . Be so good they can't ignore you. . You can win by narrowly edging out the competition, or you can win by a margin wide enough that the contest isn't close. We aim for the second. Everything gets easier when you win decisively.. . . Name the elephant. . If something's broken, we say so. If a plan isn't working, we flag it. We don't dance around uncomfortable truths or wait for someone else to raise them, because the fastest way to fix a problem is to admit it exists.. . . . . . . . . . . . . .
