Hexa is an AI workflow automation platform built for mid-market industrial distributors and manufacturers that handle a high volume of RFQs and purchase orders for standard, high-volume products. Hexa's AI agents understand emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, and ERP data, and execute complete workflows across sales (RFQ processing, quoting, order entry), procurement (supplier RFQs, price comparison, PO creation, replenishment), and finance (invoice processing, matching, approvals, and following up on late payments). Rather than selling self-serve software, Hexa works as an operations partner: forward-deployed engineers design, implement, and adapt automations to each company's specific systems. An integration layer connects fragmented industrial environments — ERPs, email, CRMs, and databases. Hexa is backed by OpenAI and Y Combinator.. . . What Hexa does. Sales. Human approval loops keep your team in control: agents prepare and recommend, and your people approve exceptions and the decisions that matter.. Best-fit companies handle high RFQ and PO volume with repetitive workflows and data spread across ERP, email, PDFs, and spreadsheets.. . How long does implementation take?. Implementation typically takes 2-6 weeks depending on your company's size and workflow complexity. A forward-deployed engineer works directly with your team to map and build your workflows. Hexa is usually fully integrated within about 3 weeks, and accuracy keeps improving as the agents learn your processes and are corrected over time.. . When does a customer query get escalated to a human?. Hexa answers routine customer queries — order status, delivery updates, spec questions, and returns — automatically using live ERP data. It escalates to a human when a request needs judgment, falls outside its confidence threshold, involves a complaint or exception, or requires data it can't verify. Your team only sees what genuinely needs them.. . Which ERPs does Hexa integrate with?. Hexa integrates with major ERP and CRM platforms, including SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, and Epicor, Prophet 21, Quickbooks (ERP), and Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, and Pipedrive (CRM), with bidirectional sync. Hexa also connects to email, spreadsheets, and databases, and can integrate with custom platforms.. . How does Hexa decide when and what to reorder?. Hexa continuously tracks inventory across your ERP — stock levels, open orders, supplier lead times, and historical demand — reconciling it even when the data is scattered across systems, spreadsheets, and PDFs. It weighs each item against your reorder logic to catch shortages early, then compares qualified suppliers on price, lead time, and availability to recommend the right purchase for your approval.. .
