A few useful details.. What is Mochi, and what is Verbiflow? + Mochi is your AI coworker for GTM engineering and operations. Work with Mochi in Slack to prepare for calls, follow up on deals, review opportunities, and build pipeline. Verbiflow is the platform underneath, with the data tools, sending infrastructure, sequences, and shared inbox to put that work into action.. What can I ask Mochi to do? + Ask Mochi to prepare a meeting brief, draft a deal follow-up, investigate a stalled opportunity, spot expansion, or research a new audience. Turn work you want repeated into a scheduled Play. Mochi can work with company and contact data, connected tools, and web research, then deliver files and results back to your team.. How do I get started in Slack? + Create a Verbiflow workspace, connect Mochi from your workspace's Slack integration settings, and give it a task in Slack. Connect the accounts and data sources your work needs. Your Verbiflow workspace is where you manage billing, senders, sequences, and replies.. What is a Play? + A Play is a repeatable GTM workflow. It can research new accounts, enrich and qualify them, produce a report, or prepare outreach. Run it for a specific job or set a schedule so the work happens again with fresh data.. Can your team build and run this for us? + Yes. The FDE offering pairs Mochi with a forward-deployed engineer. We scope the work with you, set up data and sending infrastructure, build custom Plays, and help operate and improve them. Pricing is custom, based on your company and the work involved. A minimum commitment applies, with scope and term agreed before kickoff.. Do I have to replace my existing tools? + Keep the tools your team uses. Mochi can work with connected CRM records, Slack conversations, documents, files, and APIs. Verbiflow also connects Gmail, Microsoft, and LinkedIn sending accounts. The context and actions available depend on the connections and access you provide.. How does pricing work? + Self-serve plans start at $50 per month and include monthly credits and connected sending accounts. Credits cover metered work, with consumption depending on the task. Additional usage, sending accounts, and domains are billed separately. FDE engagements have a custom scope and minimum commitment.
