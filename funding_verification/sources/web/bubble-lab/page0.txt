Here is what operators ask us first.. . What is Bubble Lab? Bubble Lab is the intelligence layer for communities and events. It sits across the tools an operator already runs, such as Luma, Slack and Stripe, and turns what happens in them into something knowable: a profile for every person who registers or joins, a live view of who is engaged and who is drifting, a decision about who should meet whom, and a record of every introduction and every night that carries into the next one.. Who is Bubble Lab for? Two kinds of operator. The first runs gatherings: a dinner series, a workshop night, a summit. They want to know whether anyone actually met, and they may have no community, no Slack and no membership at all. The second runs a paid professional community: founder networks, membership clubs, career communities, investor groups and alumni networks. Bubble Lab is built for the person running the room, not only the people in it.. Do I need an existing community to use Bubble Lab? No. Many operators arrive running events and nothing else. Bubble Lab makes the room legible starting with the next event: who is coming, what they want, who they should meet, and what happened after. Building a membership on top of that is a choice available later, not a requirement to start.. How is Bubble Lab different from Luma or Eventbrite? Luma tells you who registered. Bubble Lab tells you who should meet, makes the introduction, and remembers all of it next time. Registration platforms end at the guest list, so the network that formed in the room evaporates when the event does. Bubble Lab keeps the attendees, the enrichment, the introductions and the attendance as permanent data, which means the second event starts with everything the first one learned.. How does attendee enrichment work? Every registrant arrives as a profile rather than an email address. Bubble Lab enriches them from LinkedIn and the public web before doors open, covering role, company, stage and what they are looking for, and adds their history with the community: events attended, people met, what they asked for last time. Organizers search the room in plain language, for example “founders raising a seed in climate”, and see who is engaged, who is drifting and who keeps bringing friends.. How does the matchmaking work? Before each gathering, every guest gets one or more matches worth knowing. Each carries a specific reason drawn from both profiles and their registration answers, plus icebreaker questions written for that pair. Introductions are delivered at the event and followed up afterwards. The quality of the reason is the product: weighting is tuned per event, and the judgment about what makes a good introduction is built from thousands of real ones.. What happens after an event? The morning after, every guest gets a personal recap sent as the organizer, with their own photos from the night, their match again, and the date of the next one. The organizer gets the other side: who came, who connected, who joined. That is what turns a good night into a member, and a member into a renewal.. What do members get between events? A member-facing dashboard carrying the community's own name: a searchable directory of everyone who has ever attended or joined, answerable in plain English; the events calendar and replay library; the job board and member perks; and the chat archive, searchable back to the day Bubble Lab connected. Most communities lose everything between two events to a Slack channel nobody can search. This is that, made durable.. Which tools does Bubble Lab replace? Communities arrive on stacks of separate tools for applications, payments, the directory, email, events and automations. Memberful, Notion, Flodesk, Wix, Disco, Zapier, Trova, Meetwaves and Airtable are the ones we see most. One login replaces all of them. The problem is rarely the total cost; it is that each tool holds one fragment, so nobody can answer whether any of it is working.. Does Bubble Lab work with Luma, Slack and Stripe? Yes. Events sync from Luma, so every RSVP arrives as an enriched profile. Slack is where members are invited, introductions post, reminders go out and the archive is indexed. Stripe handles checkout and dues. Bubble Lab also connects to Discord, WhatsApp, Google Workspace and LinkedIn enrichment.. . . . . . Infrastructure for human connection. . .
