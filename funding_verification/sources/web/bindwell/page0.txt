We’re Tyler Rose (Wolfram Research) and Navvye Anand (Caltech), a scrappy duo of engineers who met at the Wolfram Research Program in June 2023. We're from China and India, respectively, and both close to farmlands in our countries. United by our passion for tackling hard problems, we dropped out of school and started Bindwell to solve molecular discovery.. We're strong believers in Richard Sutton's The Bitter Lesson : general methods that scale with computation and data beat methods built on human knowledge, every time. We don't hand-design pesticides, we build wet labs that produce data at scale and neural networks that learn from it.
