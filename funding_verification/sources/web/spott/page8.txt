Spott raises $3.2M to launch first AI-native platform for recruitment firms. Key points:. Spott, founded in November 2024, has raised $3.2M to build the AI-native ATS/CRM for recruitment firms. The funding round is led by Base10 with participation from Y Combinator, True Equity and Fortino Ventures.. Spott’s vision is to empower professional recruiters with the all-in-one operating software that not just stores data, but works for you. With AI agents embedded in the platform, Spott lets recruiters focus on what really matters and close significantly more placements.. The funds will be used to accelerate the execution of the product roadmap and expand operations, focusing on further scaling in the U.S. and Europe.. For too long, recruitment firms have relied on outdated software to manage daily operations. These systems track candidate data, but fail their users in one crucial task: making their lives easier. The rise of AI presents an opportunity for a fundamental shift in how recruiting technology should work. It enables recruiters to shift their focus from manual, repetitive tasks to building stronger relationships and prioritize revenue-driving activities instead.. Since inception, Spott has been automating time-consuming recruitment workflows, such as the writing of candidate presentation reports, and has recently started paid trials with industry leaders such as Stanton Chase. Now, the platform is evolving further by bringing together everything recruiting firms need — from AI agents, to a full ATS & CRM and analytics — into one single, AI-native platform. Spott has already generated over 1,000 candidate reports for its growing list of customers.. From experiencing the problem, to building the solution. While working for McKinsey, BCG, and Bain, Spott’s co-founders experienced firsthand how recruiters wrestled with outdated, clunky systems. They realized that talent moves through conversations, not spreadsheets, yet recruiters were stuck logging conversations and updating records instead of driving results.. Realizing how AI could finally bring change, they teamed up to build Spott.. Discover the recruitment platform of the future. Over the past two years, AI tools have flooded the market, each solving a single problem but creating a bigger one - fragmentation.. The most common question we’ve heard since starting Spott is “Is it integrated? “From day one, we’ve felt the frustration of poorly connected software and the adoption challenges of stand-alone AI tools, and we are looking to change that.. Instead of juggling separate tools, recruiters get one powerful, integrated platform built around AI to focus on the most important parts of their jobs. By eliminating redundant single point solutions, Spott reduces costs, saves time switching between systems, enhances data operability and enables AI-driven automation of end-to-end workflows.. Spott accompanies recruiters across their entire workflow. It matches candidates to vacancies, automates outreach, schedules interviews, processes transcripts, manages pipelines, compiles reports, and supports business development—all within a single AI-native platform.. How much funding has Spott raised and who are the investors?. . . Spott has raised $24.2 million in total. The $3.2 million seed round was led by Base10 Partners, with participation from Y Combinator, True Equity and Fortino Ventures; the $21 million Series A was led by Balderton Capital, with Base10 Partners, Y Combinator and Fortino Ventures participating. The company was founded in November 2024 by former McKinsey, BCG, and Bain consultants who saw firsthand how recruiters struggled with fragmented, outdated software. Spott now serves recruitment firms across five continents from offices in Leuven, New York and Sydney, and the Series A funds deeper product development and growth in the U.S., European and APAC markets.. ‍. . . . . . . What does it mean that Spott is an AI-native ATS/CRM platform?. . . AI-native means Spott was architected from the ground up with AI at the core, not bolted on as an afterthought to a legacy database. This architecture enables AI agents embedded throughout every workflow, from candidate matching and semantic search to automated outreach and interview transcript processing. Legacy ATS platforms retrofit AI features onto relational databases that were never designed for them, which limits what the AI can actually do. Spott's approach eliminates software fragmentation by consolidating separate point solutions into one integrated system.. . . . . . . What problem does Spott solve for recruitment agencies?. . . Spott addresses the software fragmentation that forces recruiters to juggle multiple disconnected tools for sourcing, outreach, scheduling, reporting, and CRM. This fragmentation wastes time on administrative work and prevents agencies from leveraging AI effectively across their full workflow. Spott consolidates ATS, CRM, candidate matching, multi-channel outreach, interview scheduling, pipeline management, and analytics into one platform. The result is that recruiters spend more time building relationships and closing placements instead of switching between tools and manually entering data.. . . . . . . What features does Spott's AI-native recruitment platform include?. . . Spott combines full ATS and CRM functionality with AI-powered candidate matching, semantic search, automated multi-channel outreach with personalization, interview scheduling, and transcript processing. It also includes pipeline management, candidate report compilation, business development support, data enrichment, and conversation intelligence. The platform captures context from calls, messages, and notes rather than just storing static data. This integrated approach means agencies replace multiple point solutions with a single system that actively works for recruiters throughout every stage of the hiring process.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
