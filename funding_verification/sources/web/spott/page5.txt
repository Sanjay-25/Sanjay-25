Recruitment is one of the largest professional services markets in the world, yet agencies still run on software from decades ago, relying on the recruiter to act as the integration layer. The best recruiters earn their fee on judgment - knowing who fits where, and convincing both sides. Today, that represents only 20% of the job. Agencies have layered on separate point solutions to plug the gap, none of which talk to each other. The trade-off is the same inside every agency we have worked with - top billers don't waste time updating the CRM, so their knowledge stays in their head; the diligent recruiter does, and loses hours per week to admin. Either way, output scales only with headcount.. . Before: the same record, typed into five tools.. . . . . The rise of the AI-native ATS/CRM.
