How does Gecko work? Gecko uses an AI native engine to build a semantic understanding of your application. It links together context from your code, infrastructure and documentation to trace how data flows and trust boundaries occur. By threat modelling targeted attack scenarios, Gecko surfaces multi step and business logic vulnerabilities that pattern matching tools overlook.. . . . . How is Gecko different to other tools? Rather than using brittle AST parsing or call graph analysis, Gecko relies on semantic name bindings similar to a language server protocol. This makes its analysis more accurate, especially for microservice architecture, because it truly understands the meaning of your code and can parse dynamically typed languages. This approach leads to a lower false positive rate, of around 20 per cent based on customer testimonials and industry benchmarks, and prioritisation of remotely exploitable issues.. . . . .
