Frequently Asked Questions. What is people sourcing? People sourcing is finding the right humans for whatever you're working on — candidates, experts, leads, decision-makers — based on a description of who they are rather than a list of filters. Clodo searches the open web and proprietary datasets in real time, qualifies every match, and returns enriched profiles with verified contact info.. . . What can I use it for? Anything that involves finding people. Expert sourcing, recruiting, candidate sourcing, sales prospecting, deal sourcing, market mapping, account research, compliance. If you're looking for humans, Clodo's looking too.. . . How does it work? You describe who you're looking for in plain English. Clodo searches the live web plus hundreds of proprietary datasets, qualifies each match against your description, and returns a ranked list with verified emails, phones, and the signals it picked up along the way. No filters, no boolean strings, no spreadsheets.. . . Can I use Clodo for expert sourcing? Yes. Expert networks and research teams use Clodo to source sector veterans, niche specialists, and key decision-makers for expert calls, primary research, diligence, and surveys. Describe the expertise you need — "high-mix CNC manufacturing veterans" or "former GLP-1 commercial leads" — and Clodo surfaces qualified experts with verified contact info in minutes, not days.. . . Can I use Clodo for recruiting? Yes. Describe the candidate you need in plain language — "senior ML engineers with autonomous vehicle experience open to relocating" — and Clodo surfaces matching people with full profiles and verified contact details. Recruiting teams use Clodo to fill the hard-to-fill roles that boolean strings and job boards struggle with, then push candidates straight into their ATS.. . . Can I use it for lead gen? Yes. Describe your ICP in plain English — vertical, role, signals, geography, intent — and Clodo searches the live web and hundreds of proprietary datasets to surface qualified leads, enriches them with verified email and phone, and ranks them by fit. Keep the list, push it to your CRM, or hand it to Clodo for outbound.. . . Can Clodo do contact enrichment? Yes. Clodo enriches every person it finds — and any list you upload — with verified work email, mobile phone, LinkedIn URL, current title, company metadata, and buying signals. Enrichment runs across hundreds of sources in parallel so the data is fresh, not pulled from a stale snapshot.. . . Does Clodo integrate with my systems? Yes. Clodo plugs into your CRM, ATS, or internal database so the people it finds land where your team already works. Native integrations for Salesforce, Dynamics, HubSpot, Bullhorn, Zoho, and more, with an API for anything custom.. . . Can my agents use Clodo? Yes.
