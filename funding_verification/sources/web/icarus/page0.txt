From a barn in upstate New York, Henry Kwan founded Icarus in 2023 after building autonomous drones at NASA and designing spacecraft to build factories in space. There, he saw firsthand how Space is constrained by orbital mechanics. . He started from first principles, sketching systems, running the numbers, and designing a new kind of platform: a solar-powered aircraft that could fly for months at the edge of space. Then he packed up his 1996 Ford Bronco, drove to California, and assembled a team to build it. . In 2024, that vision became APOLLO, our first fully solar-powered, autonomous aircraft. It flew. It worked. No hype, just hardware in the sky. . Now we’re scaling the team, the systems, and the mission.
