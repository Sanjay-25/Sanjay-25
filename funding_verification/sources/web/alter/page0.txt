Credentials live in a dedicated secret store, encrypted at rest, with keys managed outside the application database. Fail-closed authorization: if a policy cannot be evaluated, the request is denied. Zero-trust ownership: a key confers no capability without an explicit record naming it. Postgres holds metadata and vault references; provider tokens and credentials live only in the vault. Per-key isolation, plaintext credentials never in logs, and proxy-only keys that cannot retrieve a credential at all. Compliance & audit . Controls monitored continuously. SOC 2 in progress .
