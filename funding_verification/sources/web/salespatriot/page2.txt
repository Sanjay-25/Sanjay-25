SalesPatriot Announces Strategic Partnership with Maven Engineering Corporation . . . . . . By. . Maciej Szymczyk. . . . . AI platform to modernize and accelerate Maven’s parts distribution business. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . SalesPatriot , the AI-powered operating system for defense and aerospace distributors and OEMs, today announced a strategic partnership with Maven Engineering Corporation , a manufacturer and distributor of precision machined parts that has supplied the defense and rail industries since 1946. Under the partnership, Maven will adopt SalesPatriot across its distribution business to quote faster, pursue more opportunities, and serve its customers with greater speed and accuracy.. . “. . “SalesPatriot lets us bring that same reliability to many more opportunities, far more quickly. It’s a real force multiplier for our distribution business.”. . . . Kavita Dawson. . President of Maven Engineering Corporation. . . . . . . . Maven’s distribution team manages a high volume of complex, made-to-spec parts across a broad base of customers and suppliers. With SalesPatriot, much of the manual work behind sourcing, quoting, and order processing is automated — allowing a lean, experienced team to handle more volume, shorten turnaround times, and focus on customer relationships and growth rather than paperwork.. “Companies like Maven are the backbone of the defense industrial base, and they’ve been underserved by software for far too long,” said Nelson Ray, Co-Founder and CEO of SalesPatriot. “We built SalesPatriot to give distributors a modern operating system — one that lets a lean, experienced team move faster and win more business. We’re proud to partner with a company that has Maven’s reputation and track record.”. “For 80 years, our name has stood for getting customers the right part, on time,” said Kavita Dawson, President of Maven Engineering Corporation. “SalesPatriot lets us bring that same reliability to many more opportunities, far more quickly. It’s a real force multiplier for our distribution business.”. The companies will begin rolling out SalesPatriot across Maven’s distribution operations immediately.. . . . About SalesPatriot. . SalesPatriot is the AI-powered operating system for defense, aerospace, industrial and electronics distributors and OEMs. The company is based in San Francisco, CA and is backed by Silicon Valley’s leading investors, including Y Combinator, CRV and Pear VC. Learn more at salespatriot.com.. . . . About Maven Engineering Corporation. . Founded in 1946 and headquartered in Rockville, Maryland, Maven Engineering Corporation develops, manufactures, sources, and distributes complex machined parts for the defense, rail, and heavy-equipment industries.. . . .
