Who we are. . . . . Why we are building SalesPatriot. . . . Supply Chain hasn’t kept up with the world it serves.. . Supply chain and distribution still run on email threads, PDFs, and legacy systems stitched together over decades, even as the scale, speed, and complexity of modern industries accelerate. The gap isn't theoretical. It's operational, and widening.. . SalesPatriot exists to close that gap. We build the AI operating system for complex procurement and distribution across defense, aerospace, and industrial sectors, ingesting fragmented data and turning manual workflows into semi-autonomous processes.. . . . . . . . Our mission. . . . . End-to-end operations streamlined with AI. . . From reactive chaos to continuous execution.. . Quoting, sourcing and procuring parts today still means navigating RFQs, emails, disconnected systems, and days of manual coordination. What should take hours takes weeks, limiting throughput and slowing the entire supply chain.. . SalesPatriot replaces that friction with a continuous, streamlined system that surfaces the right opportunities, prepares RFQs, and drives execution, while keeping decisions in your team's hands. The result: higher win rates, faster cycles, and lower costs.. . . . . . . . Our approach. . . . . Platform + embedded engineers. . . Software alone doesn’t fix your friction.. . The real problem is orchestration — across systems, teams, and decisions. Tools add surface area, but they rarely change outcomes without deep integration into how work actually gets done.. . SalesPatriot pairs an AI-native platform with forward-deployed engineers who embed directly with your team and leadership, architecting and building solutions to your toughest operational problems. They work onsite until it's solved. Manual load drops, timelines compress, and transactions move at the speed the mission demands.. . . . . . . . . . What drives us. . . . . Our values.. . . .
