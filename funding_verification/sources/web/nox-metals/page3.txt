Merchandise . . . Instant Quote . Quote . Cart Log in . . . About Nox Metals. Nox Metals is building America's first automated aluminum plate cutting operation. We are based in the Detroit area (Ferndale, MI) and we cut 6061, 7075, 7050, and 5000-series aluminum plate to size for aerospace, defense, and industrial manufacturing customers across the United States.. We built Nox because buying aluminum plate is slow. The traditional process involves emailing a sales rep, waiting hours or days for a quote, going back and forth on a PO, then waiting again for cutting and shipping. We replaced that with instant online quoting that shows your ship date before you order, and automated cutting.. How it works. You select your alloy, enter your dimensions, and get a price in seconds. When you place an order, it goes directly to our cutting queue. Your ship date is shown with your instant quote, before you order. Material is cut, inspected, and shipped with mill certs. Every customer gets portal access for real-time order tracking, cert downloads, and reordering past configurations at today's market price.. What we stock. We carry aluminum plate in four alloy families: 6061-T651 , 7075-T651 , 7050-T7451 , and cast mold aluminum plate (5000-series cast). We also stock 6061 round bar and flat bar, cast tool and jig plate, and stainless steel round bar in 304/304L, 316/316L, 17-4 PH and 416. DFARS compliant domestic melt material is available across all alloys.. Where we are. Our facility is at 1350 Jarvis St, Ferndale, MI 48220. We ship nationwide.. Who we serve. Our customers are machine shops, fabricators, and procurement teams building parts for aerospace, defense, space, medical, and industrial applications. We serve everyone from one-person job shops to publicly traded defense primes.. The team. Nox was founded by Zane Hengsperger. We are a Y Combinator company (W25) and have been covered by Forbes, American Bazaar, and Michigan Enjoyer. We are building from Detroit because this is where American manufacturing lives.. .
