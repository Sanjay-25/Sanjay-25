[MISSION]. Compute was built for humans. Agents need something else.. . Cloud infrastructure was designed for one app at a time. Agents need isolated environments to reason, code, and act, by the millions, and every result has to render instantly for the humans and agents waiting on it. We are building the runtime that makes that normal.. Inference belongs next to that runtime.
