We're building the product that improves itself: one snippet to collect everything, AI to understand it, and agents that fix and ship. Backed by $5M from General Catalyst, Paul Graham, Vercel, and Y Combinator.. . Our Plan. We believe the product that improves itself starts with one thing done well: a feedback loop built on real user behavior.. Human Behavior started as a simple AI that watched session replays, pulled out intent signals and bugs, and summarized them. That worked, and it turned into the rest of this.. Collect — You install one snippet and we instrument everything: session replays, errors, funnels, revenue. No dashboard, no UI. It runs headlessly, because if a company is going to run itself, no human should be clicking through screens to make that happen. One install beats wiring up a dozen MCPs — agents get the whole picture in one place instead of a thin, disconnected slice from each server you have to babysit.. Down the line, own more of what tells you where to go — voice of customer, Zendesk and support tickets, app store reviews. Everything that dictates what gets built next should come from installing Human Behavior and nothing else.. . Understand — AI watches the replays and structures what's hard to structure. Our NeurIPS 2025 work ranks which bugs actually matter.. Down the line, much better intent filtering. Knowing what a user was trying to do, not just what they clicked.. . Act — Agents run on automations and triggers today. Something breaks, they find it, fix it, and open the pull request. Nothing ships without a human merging it.. Down the line, agents that go looking on their own — reading the data, forming their own ideas about what to build, running the experiment, and validating it through A/B tests. No trigger, no prompt.. . And that's just the obvious path. Full product understanding unlocks a lot more. AI QA that actually uses your product the way a person would. A live helper that sees where a user is stuck and drives a second cursor to guide them — or does it for them. Computer-use agents trained on the mountain of session replay data we already have — real humans, real flows, real edge cases. There's a bajillion things you can build once you genuinely understand a product, and almost nobody has the data to try.. . . Culture . Roles.
