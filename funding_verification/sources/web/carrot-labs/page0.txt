Coding ROI for unlimited people . Talk to us . . . Compare plans in detail → . . . FAQ. What is SuperPenguin?. SuperPenguin is an AI spend intelligence platform: it turns what you spend across every AI provider and coding tool into cost you can attribute, understand, and control. It lets teams see what each customer, feature, team, or pull request actually costs, catch spikes before they reach the invoice, and forecast where the month is heading, without ever sitting in the path of a request.. . . . How do I try SuperPenguin?. Start free, no credit card. Pick the entry point that fits:. Attribution: add our Python or TypeScript SDK to break API spend down by customer, feature, and team. . Coding ROI: connect GitHub and have each engineer install the free Mac app to see cost per pull request across Cursor, Claude Code, and Codex. . One View: connect a provider's admin key to pull every AI bill into a single total. . . . . Do you store my prompts or responses?. By default, no. SuperPenguin records cost metadata only: token counts, model, latency, and the tags you set, never your prompt or response text. Content capture is off unless your organization turns it on in Settings, and even then it is sampled rather than complete, text-only with images and audio stripped, redacted by default, and encrypted at rest. An owner can delete captured content at any time.. . . . Can I use this with my team?. Yes. SuperPenguin is a shared workspace, not a personal account. Connect your providers and SDK once, invite your teammates, and manage AI spend together, with roles that control who can add connections or change billing.. . . . How is this different from my provider's dashboard?. Your provider's dashboard shows you their bill, by their project, their model, and their API keys. It cannot show you the other providers you pay, cannot group by your customer or your feature, and cannot tell you what a pull request cost. SuperPenguin puts every provider in one total and lets you slice it by dimensions you define.. . . . Will the SDK slow down my API calls?. No. Your calls go straight to the provider; SuperPenguin is not a proxy and is not in your request path. Telemetry is sent after your response has already returned, which adds under 10ms on non-streaming requests and under 5ms to time-to-first-token on streaming ones.. . . . How can I track OpenAI or Anthropic spend by customer?. Install the SuperPenguin Python or TypeScript SDK, wrap your provider client, and pass a customer id in the metadata on each call. Your spend is then broken down by customer, in dollars, down to the request. You can also track by feature, team, environment, and prompt version the same way.. . . . How do I catch an unexpected spike in my AI bill?. Set spend alerts and get notified in Slack, Discord, or email. You can set a daily threshold, a spike detector that compares against yesterday, or a monthly budget cap. Alerts fire within minutes of a breach, not at the end of the month.. . . . Can I see how much each pull request cost in AI spend?. Yes. Connect GitHub so pull requests can be matched, and have each engineer install the free Mac app. Cursor, Claude Code, and Codex all keep the per-session detail that ties spend to a pull request on the developer's machine, so the app is what syncs it. Coding ROI then prices each merged pull request and rolls the spend up by repository.. . . . Which providers are supported?. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, AWS Bedrock, Azure, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Together AI, Fireworks AI, Modal, OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway, LiteLLM, and Cursor, with more added regularly. Per-request attribution through the SDK reaches 100+ more via gateways like OpenRouter and LiteLLM.. . . . . . . . . . Give every AI dollar a name.. Across every provider you already pay..
