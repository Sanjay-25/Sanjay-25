Focus AI pentesting and cloud attack paths . Backed by Y Combinator . . Why Trident exists . Security teams rarely lack findings. They lack a reliable way to see which findings connect. A web authorization weakness, an exposed cloud workload, an assumable role, and a sensitive data store may live in four different tools even when they form one route an attacker could follow.. Trident brings those relationships into one reviewable context. The goal is not to manufacture a larger alert count. It is to help teams understand the path, preserve the evidence, identify the control that breaks the route, and verify the fix.. Our public security guides explain the methods behind that work, while the product documentation covers implementation and integrations.. . . . . How we approach the problem . . 01 Prove before prioritizing. Suspected issues and validated behavior are different states. Evidence should make that distinction visible.. . 02 Preserve the path. A finding is more useful when it includes the identities, access relationships, and data boundary that turn it into impact.. . 03 Retest the fix.
