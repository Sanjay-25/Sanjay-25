San Francisco, CA. . . . Founding story. Helonic was born from a simple observation: construction teams spend thousands of hours manually reviewing drawings, yet preventable errors still cause $31 billion in annual rework across the U.S. industry. The founding team saw that AI could fundamentally change how drawing review works, not by replacing human expertise, but by giving reviewers an AI-powered first pass that catches the quantitative, repetitive issues across every page.. The team invested in building a proprietary AI model trained specifically on construction drawings, learning the patterns, terminology, and cross-discipline relationships unique to construction documents. This domain-specific approach proved dramatically more reliable than applying general-purpose AI to construction, and became the foundation of the Helonic platform.. . . Brand assets. . Helonic logo.
