Construction is 13% of global GDP and one of the least digitized industries on the planet. Every year the US loses $31B to rework , most of it from mistakes that could have been caught during plan review.. We believe AI can fundamentally change this equation. Not by replacing experienced professionals, but by giving them superpowers: reviewing drawings faster, catching more issues, and focusing expertise where it matters most.. Our goal is to make AI-powered drawing analysis the standard in construction, as essential as Procore for project management or Bluebeam for PDF markup. Is Helonic backed by Y Combinator? + Yes. Helonic is a Y Combinator company, the accelerator behind Airbnb, Stripe, DoorDash, and more than 4,000 other startups.. What does Helonic actually do? + Helonic is an AI platform that reviews construction drawings and flags coordination conflicts, code compliance issues, missing information, and constructability problems before they reach the field. It reads the 2D PDF drawing sets teams already have, so it does not require a 3D BIM model to run.. How is Helonic different from BIM clash detection tools like Navisworks or Solibri? + BIM clash detection requires a coordinated 3D model, which many projects never build or build too late to prevent RFIs. Helonic works directly on the 2D PDF drawing set, so it catches issues during plan review even when no model exists. It complements BIM workflows rather than replacing them.. Does Helonic replace professional plan review? + No. Helonic is built to support licensed professionals, not replace them. It surfaces likely issues with page references, severity, and recommended actions so reviewers can verify and act quickly, keeping the engineer or project manager in control of every decision.. Who does Helonic build for? + Helonic serves teams across the project lifecycle, including general contractors, architects, MEP and structural engineers, estimators, owners and developers, plan reviewers, and construction managers.
