Still have questions?. Request a demo → . What is Hypercubic?. Hypercubic is an agentic AI platform for mainframe operations and modernization, serving enterprises and governments that run the world's most critical mainframe infrastructure. Hypercubic helps organizations understand, operate and transform that infrastructure at a fraction of the timeline and cost compared to traditional modernization methods.. . . . . Who uses Hypercubic?. Today's top enterprises and governments trust Hypercubic to transform their critical mainframe infrastructure. Hypercubic serves organizations across financial services, insurance, government, manufacturing, defense, airlines, telecommunications, healthcare, oil and gas, logistics, retail and utilities.. . . . . What does the Hypercubic platform include?. The Hypercubic platform is an end-to-end ecosystem for industrial-scale mainframe operations and modernization, covering four stages: discover, preserve, operate and transform. HyperDocs turns COBOL codebases into living, searchable documentation, HyperTwin captures tribal knowledge to debug and operate legacy systems, Hopper provides an agentic mainframe terminal for the TN3270 and HyperLoop architects legacy systems into modern architectures.. . . . . Why can't standard AI tools modernize mainframes?. Standard AI tools can generate code, but mainframe modernization requires much more than translation. Legacy systems often contain decades of undocumented business rules, tightly coupled batch jobs, data dependencies, terminal workflows, and edge cases that must continue to behave exactly as before.. Hypercubic is built for that environment. It combines code and system understanding, expert knowledge capture, mainframe operations support, and verification-driven transformation so teams can modernize critical systems with traceability and confidence, not just generate code translations.. . . . . How does Hypercubic handle data security and sovereignty?. Hypercubic runs entirely inside a customer's VPC or on-premise environment. Hypercubic operates with zero data egress, immutable audit trails and no model training on customer data, using the customer's own model and keys.. . . . . Which cloud platforms does Hypercubic support?. Hypercubic deploys to AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle and IBM cloud platforms. Hypercubic can be deployed in customer-controlled VPC or on-premise environments.. . . . . What mainframe use cases does Hypercubic address?. Hypercubic addresses six mainframe use cases across enterprises and governments: tribal knowledge preservation, verified mainframe transformation, legacy system understanding, business logic extraction, mainframe operations continuity and expert knowledge capture.. . . . . How does Hypercubic approach institutional knowledge preservation?. Hypercubic preserves institutional knowledge by combining what exists in the codebase with what only experienced operators know.. HyperDocs analyzes COBOL and related mainframe assets to create searchable documentation of programs, data flows, dependencies, and business logic. HyperTwin captures subject-matter expert knowledge through guided interviews and operational workflows, including how teams diagnose failures, run critical processes, and handle exceptions.. Together, they turn undocumented code and retiring-expert knowledge into a durable, searchable system of record that can support operations, onboarding, and modernization.. . . . . . . . .
