When should we start?. . . As early as possible. Immigration can affect start dates, hiring plans, relocation, and long-term retention. Starting earlier gives your team more room to assess the right pathway, collect documents, and avoid last-minute filing pressure.. . We are a startup. Can we actually sponsor someone?. . . In many cases, startups can sponsor employees, but the right pathway depends on the company, the role, the candidate’s background, and the available evidence. LegalOS helps teams understand what is realistic before they commit to a path.. . . Can a founder use their own startup for immigration?. . . Sometimes, but founder cases need to be structured carefully. LegalOS can help assess whether a founder’s company, role, ownership structure, achievements, and documentation support a viable immigration strategy.. . . We are not sure if this should be H-1B, O-1, EB-1, or EB-2 NIW. Can LegalOS help us figure that out?. . . Yes. LegalOS helps evaluate the person’s background, role, company needs, timeline, and evidence to identify the strongest available pathway.. . . What if the candidate has strong experience but does not look like a typical immigration case?. . . That is common for founders, engineers, researchers, and technical leaders. LegalOS helps organize achievements, company traction, publications, awards, press, recommendations, role evidence, and other supporting materials into a clearer case strategy.. . . What if we get an RFE?. . . A Request for Evidence does not automatically mean the case will be denied. It means the government wants more information before making a decision. LegalOS helps review the request, identify what evidence is needed, and prepare a structured response with attorney guidance.. . . Can premium processing solve our timeline issue?. . . Premium processing can speed up the government response for certain eligible filings, but it does not replace case preparation and does not guarantee approval. The case still needs to be complete, accurate, and well-supported before filing.. . . What documents should we prepare before starting?. . . The exact list depends on the pathway, but helpful materials usually include company information, role details, candidate background, immigration history, prior approvals if any, resume or CV, employment documents, achievements, and supporting evidence.. . . We promised sponsorship to a candidate. What should we check before moving forward?. . . Before making firm commitments, it is important to understand the candidate’s current status, deadlines, visa history, role requirements, pathway options, cost, timing, and potential risks. LegalOS helps teams get clarity before the process becomes urgent.. . . Can LegalOS help if we are managing several immigration cases at once?. . . Yes. LegalOS can support teams handling multiple candidates, employees, or case types by creating a more structured process for documents, timelines, next steps, and attorney review.. . . Does starting the process mean we are ready to file immediately?. . . Not always. Some cases may be filing-ready quickly, while others need stronger evidence, cleaner documentation, or additional review. LegalOS helps determine what is needed before moving forward.. . . Does LegalOS guarantee approval?. . . No immigration provider can guarantee approval. Final decisions are made by U.S. immigration authorities. LegalOS helps prepare stronger, more organized cases with experienced attorney review, but outcomes depend on the facts of each case and the applicable requirements.. . . . . . . .
