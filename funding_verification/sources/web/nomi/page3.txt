If you've been a customer, partner, or follower of our journey, you've probably noticed something: everyone already calls us Nomi . The "Hey" was there from the beginning as a friendly greeting, but as we've grown, we realized the best brand names are the ones people naturally use. So we're making it official.. What is Nomi? . Nomi is the AI copilot that coaches your revenue team in real time. We're the platform that sits alongside your sales reps, account executives, and customer success managers during live calls—surfacing the right playbook, the perfect objection response, or the competitive insight they need, exactly when they need it.. Nomi isn't a conversational AI chatbot. Nomi isn't a virtual companion. Nomi is your sales team's unfair advantage. . Built for revenue teams who refuse to let winnable deals slip away, Nomi combines real-time conversation intelligence with battle-tested sales frameworks to turn every rep into a top performer. From discovery calls to objection handling, from value selling to closing—Nomi is there, guiding your team to better outcomes.. Why Now? . This rebrand isn't just about dropping three letters. It reflects where Nomi is headed:. Deeper Product Integration . The future of sales coaching isn't a separate app you check between calls. It's woven into your workflow—your CRM, your dialers, your video platforms. Nomi lives where your team works, and our roadmap doubles down on that vision. A simpler name signals our focus on seamless integration.. Expanding Beyond Coaching . While real-time coaching remains our core, we're building capabilities that go further: automated follow-ups, deal health scoring, competitive battle cards that update themselves, and playbooks that evolve based on what's actually working in the field. Nomi will soon be the operating system for your entire revenue motion.. Global Growth . As we expand internationally, "Nomi" is clean, memorable, and easy to pronounce in every language.
