Frequently asked questions. . . . What does Infinite do?. Infinite is the global financial network built on a compliance system of intelligence; the more companies that connect, the faster and cheaper every dollar moves. One API provides real bank accounts , payments across SWIFT, US rails, stablecoins, and FX , and compliance, with first-time counterparty review dropping from ~30 days to 1–2 days on the network.. . . Does Infinite hold customer funds itself?. No. Funds sit in dedicated accounts with unique routing numbers at chartered, FDIC-insured partner banks, custody inside the bank perimeter. Infinite Agents, Inc. is a financial technology company, not an FDIC-insured bank; banking services are provided by partner banks. Infinite operates the network, the API, and the compliance program.. . . Who founded Infinite?. Infinite was founded by Nikhil Srinivasan (CEO) and Raj Lad (CTO), with Krisan Nichani as Chief Compliance Officer. The leadership team built payments and compliance products at scale at Coinbase , Sardine , and Socure , and the company is based in the Presidio in San Francisco .. . . Why does a shared compliance network make payments faster?. Because the bottleneck is compliance, not the rails. Money can settle in seconds, but counterparty review traditionally takes around 30 days, and every institution repeats the same checks on the same companies. On a shared network , a counterparty reviewed once is reviewed for everyone, moving review time from ~30 days to 1–2.. . . Who backs Infinite?. Infinite is backed by Bessemer Venture Partners, Y Combinator, 468 Capital, Rebel Fund, Coinbase Ventures, Trousdale Ventures, and Orrick, alongside the angels listed on the careers page .
