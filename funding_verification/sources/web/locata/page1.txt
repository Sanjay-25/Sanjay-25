We started with a simple belief: gaps in care should never happen. Referrals are a critical part of healthcare, yet too often patients fall through the cracks, missing the care they need. We're on a mission to close the loop.. Today’s referral processes rely on fragmented systems and heavy administrative work. The burden falls on staff to manually coordinate every step, leading to delays, bottlenecks, and overwhelmed operations. With modern AI, that no longer has to be the case. . Backed by leading Silicon Valley investors, we’re building Locata to solve healthcare’s most complex operational challenges. We envision a world where patients receive timely, effective care without friction. Where staff can focus on assisting patients, not filling out forms. And where providers have full visibility into each patient’s care journey..
