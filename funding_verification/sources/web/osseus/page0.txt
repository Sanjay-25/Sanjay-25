Osseus: Building medical superintelligence . Skip to content Backed by Y Combinator . Research Get in touch We’re building medical superintelligence.. We’re starting with medical and biological datasets, evaluations and reinforcement learning environments that help frontier labs train more capable, aligned and safe models.. . . . What we offer. Off-the-shelf and custom datasets spanning patient histories, clinician–patient conversations, clinical reasoning and biological data. Our interactive environments let models gather information, use tools and work through multi-step medical tasks, with feedback on both clinical performance and behaviour.. . Why medicine, why alignment. More capable models need to remain honest, reliable and responsive to human oversight in unfamiliar situations. Medicine is a perfect example of why this matters. Models must reason with incomplete information, recognise uncertainty, accept correction and avoid unsafe shortcuts. Strong benchmark scores alone don’t establish these behaviours.. We believe carefully designed medical and other beneficial data, environments and reward signals can improve clinical capability while helping aligned behaviours generalise beyond medicine. The literature shows this, and we’re building towards this across a broad range of models.. . . More varied data, fairer models. Models trained on a narrow slice of patients can perform unevenly across populations. We work with partners to build datasets spanning different people, conditions and care settings, including those underrepresented in existing data. Our aim is to make these gaps visible through evaluation and help models serve patients more fairly.. . A continuous data flywheel. Our partnerships support ongoing clinical data collection and give us settings to deploy and evaluate models on real clinical tasks. New data and findings feed back into our datasets, training environments and separately released benchmarks. As models improve, the capability and safety gaps we uncover shape the next round of data and evaluations.. . . Our team. Our team are engineers and researchers with backgrounds at Imperial College London and a joint Harvard–HKU lab. Our work spans AI safety, health AI, robot learning and data systems, with experience across Apple’s ML and data teams. We are committed to pushing the frontier forward, and dropped out of an AI safety for health PhD programme at the University of Oxford to do so.. .
