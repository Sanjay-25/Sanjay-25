Polymath is a data lab focused on increasing the reliability and autonomy of AI agents, in order to better serve people. We're heading towards a future where AI agents will be able to perform useful work over long horizons , with little or no human supervision.. To increase the performance of autonomous agents, they must be trained in environments that reflect the real world. Polymath builds simulation environments for agents to practice and learn through experience. We work with the leading model labs to push the frontier of agent capabilities.. We're a team of researchers, engineers, and operators focused on advancing safe, highly capable AI systems. Polymath is backed by Base10 , Cervin Ventures , Y Combinator , and others.. .
