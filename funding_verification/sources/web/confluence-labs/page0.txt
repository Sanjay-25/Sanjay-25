Confluence Labs is an AI research lab focused on learning efficiency – how quickly can you learn something given a limited amount of data / fewest number of experiments.. Many of humanity's biggest advances are locked behind a certain number of experiments. The strongest examples are drug and materials discovery.. The right drugs could essentially end human disease. The right materials could unlock fusion or help end global warming. These are constrained search problems where a certain number of experiments can unlock a breakthrough for humanity and change the world.. The mission of our company and of our research is to enable people to hit those breakthroughs in the fewest number of experiments possible, accelerating and enabling scientific technologies..
