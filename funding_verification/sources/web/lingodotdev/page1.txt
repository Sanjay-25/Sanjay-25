Lingo.dev is the localization engineering platform where teams measure translation quality, translate with LLMs, and proofread with native speakers. It’s built for teams accountable for quality in languages they can’t read, so they translate more with LLMs and trust the quality of every language.. View open positions . . . . . . Lingo.dev is the standard localization infrastructure for modern teams scaling into new markets – including Mistral, Solana, SoSafe, and Cal.com. . . . What we build. . On Lingo.dev, teams configure localization engines – stateful translation APIs that hold the glossary, brand voice, and per-locale model preferences – and the criteria a team defines score every translation before it goes live.. Retrieval augmented localization sends that context to the highest-scoring LLM per language pair, on the team’s own provider API key or through Lingo.dev without markup. An edit in the code repository or the CMS starts a pipeline, and the translation returns to the source of truth. Native-speaking proofreaders approve a translation where the team decides, and the rules Lingo.dev drafts from their corrections tune the engine.. We started at a hackathon back in December 2023, won best dev tool, and kept building localization tools that must exist but don’t yet. Today we have hundreds of real users, more than 5,000 GitHub stars, a CLI that runs more than 10,000 times a week, and over 300 million translated words. The work is the API and the CLI, the scorers and the localization engines, CI/CD and third-party integrations, AI agents, background workflows, and the dashboards where a team reads the quality of every language – and the team that owns localization runs the localization infrastructure..
