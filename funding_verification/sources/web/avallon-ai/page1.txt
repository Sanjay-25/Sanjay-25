We're transforming claims operations for teams that value efficiency and quality . . . Claims operations have long been bogged down by outdated systems and repetitive tasks. Adjusters are overwhelmed with administrative work, and an aging workforce adds to the strain as claim volumes rise. Inefficiencies in claim intake, subrogation, dispute resolutions, and after-hours operations slow down processing and reduce service quality.. Avallon offers a better solution. Our platform uses conversational AI and cutting edge machine learning to automate tedious tasks, seamlessly integrating with your systems. Whether you’re a carrier, MGA, or TPA, Avallon adapts to your workflows, boosting productivity and eliminating friction.. With our scalable context engine, every interaction is infused with the right information, freeing your team to focus on strategic decisions and creative problem‑solving. Avallon empowers operators to manage millions of claims workflows more efficiently, accurately, and with uninterrupted service, even after hours.
