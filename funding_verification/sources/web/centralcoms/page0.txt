What actually happens when a customer calls or messages us? + − CentralComs starts with the reason they reached out. For a new service request, that might mean asking what’s wrong, collecting the address, and finding out when the customer is available. Routine questions are answered using the information your shop provides, so the conversation can move toward a useful next step.. If booking is part of your setup, CentralComs checks your connected calendar before offering a visit. Questions about an unusual repair, a disputed charge, or a promise your team hasn’t approved need a person. The request and conversation context give your office something concrete to work from.. . We’ve tried AI before. How do we know it will handle our customers properly? + − Start with the conversations that worry you, rather than a perfectly scripted demo. Bring a caller who won’t explain the problem clearly, a request outside your service area, or someone asking for a repair price before a technician has seen the job. We’ll walk through how those requests should be handled using your instructions.. You can begin with a defined use case, such as overflow or after-hours calls. Agree on the answers CentralComs can give and the decisions your office needs to make before expanding the workflow. Your actual customer questions are the best starting point for that discussion.. . How does CentralComs use our prices, service area, and booking rules? + − Your shop provides the services you take on, the areas you cover, and the pricing information you want customers to receive. There’s a meaningful difference between explaining an approved service-call fee and quoting a repair that hasn’t been assessed. Those boundaries belong in the instructions.. Booking also needs an active connected calendar with availability configured, including working hours, visit duration, and notice requirements. CentralComs checks those details before offering a time and confirms after the booking succeeds. Exceptions, revised prices, and technical judgments stay with the people on your team who can approve them.. . How does this help our office staff during a busy day? + − It takes on routine conversations that otherwise interrupt whatever your office is doing: collecting a new caller’s details, answering a scheduling question, or asking whether a customer can still make their appointment. That gives staff more room to deal with the jobs and customers that need their attention.. Your team still owns the decisions that require experience or authority. During setup, we agree on which requests should come back to the office and what information should accompany them. A useful handoff includes what the customer asked, what has already been discussed, and what still needs an answer.. . Can it help with unsold estimates and customers we haven’t heard from in a while? + − Yes. The starting point is a relevant reason to get back in touch. For an open estimate, that could be asking whether the customer has questions about the work. For a past customer, it might be a reminder about a recurring service your shop offers. Your team supplies the customer context and decides the follow-up approach.. The reply matters as much as the first message. Someone who needs more time, has already hired another company, or wants to discuss a different scope needs a different next step. CentralComs can continue the conversation and help interested customers arrange a visit.. . How can CentralComs help us get more customer reviews? + − Make asking for a review part of the follow-up after a completed job. Your shop provides the review link and chooses when the request should reach the customer. A short message that mentions the visit and asks for an honest review gives them a straightforward way to share their experience.. The request shouldn’t depend on a customer first giving a positive answer. If they reply with a question or a concern, your team needs that context so it can respond. In your demo, we’ll confirm how completed-job information reaches the workflow and how review requests fit with the messages you already send.. . What do we need to have ready before getting started? + − Bring a few real customer questions, your service and booking rules, and a list of the tools your office uses. It also helps to choose one problem to start with, such as missed evening calls or estimates that aren’t getting a follow-up. That gives the demo a clear job to do.. We’ll check how customer information reaches CentralComs, what can be connected, and which steps your office or current software will handle.
