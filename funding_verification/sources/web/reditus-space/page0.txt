Humanity has dramatically advanced its ability to send hardware into orbit, but access to space has historically remained a one-way journey. . The future of the space economy will require more than frequent launch—it will require the ability to reliably return valuable payloads from orbit at scale. Just as every successful transportation network on Earth depends on both outbound and inbound logistics, the next generation of space infrastructure will depend on dedicated systems designed for routine return.. . Reditus Space is building the return systems and infrastructure required for the physical space economy to flourish. . . Reditus Space was created to redefine the costs and timeline of returning from space. . Reditus is transforming return from orbit into a reliable, repeatable, and ultimately routine capability. This foundation will enable a future where products, materials, and services move seamlessly between Earth and space—unlocking a new era of space-based manufacturing, research, and industrialization. . Founded in 2024 by Stef Crum and Will Sherman, and backed by world-class investors such as Y Combinator, Antler and Aera, Reditus Space is uniquely positioned to make this a reality, with leadership and staff from top institutions including SpaceX, Blue Origin, JPL, and the Department of Defense.. . . . . . . . . . . . . .
