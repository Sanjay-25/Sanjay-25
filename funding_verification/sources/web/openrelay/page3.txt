OpenRelay's production environment is now live on openrelay.inc . The marketing site runs at openrelay.inc, the dashboard at app.openrelay.inc , and the control-plane API at api.openrelay.inc: all serving with valid TLS on dedicated production infrastructure, separate from our beta environment.. Billing is live too. The platform is wired to Stripe in live mode: you can add a card and fund your account directly from the dashboard, and deposits are credited to your balance automatically via verified webhooks. The full GPU catalog: models, pricing, VM templates, and locations: is seeded in production with the same pricing as beta.. Under the hood, the control plane runs redundantly across multiple availability zones on AWS, backed by multi-AZ Redis with automatic failover and DynamoDB with point-in-time recovery: so the tier that manages your account, billing, and deployments is built to stay up..
