The story behind Burnt. . . . . Born into food. . Food has always been in our blood. JJ is a fourth-generation food entrepreneur who started his career on shrimp factory floors. Rhea comes from three generations of spice traders and grew up watching restaurant operations scale from a single pizza shop to a 20-location empire.. . . . . . . Growing up together. . Two families, one mission. JJ and Rhea met when they were 12—their families ran restaurants together in Dubai. They watched that business grow from nothing into a major restaurant group, seeing firsthand how hard operations become as you scale.. . . . . . . Seeing the problem everywhere. . An industry stuck in the past. Across JJ’s career—from restaurants, to food importers, to farms—he saw firsthand how deeply inefficient the food supply chain had become, and how much legacy software was holding the industry back. Teams were managing inventory across hundreds of Excel sheets, running factory production on pen and paper, and operating workflows decades behind where they needed to be.. . . . . . . Building the solution. . From problem to platform. In 2023, Rhea and JJ brought in Chandru as CTO and founded Burnt. Backed by some of the best AI investors, they're reinventing the technical infrastructure of of supply chain and building AI that the food supply chain has needed for decades.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
