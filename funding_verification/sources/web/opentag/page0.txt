Frequently asked questions. . What is OpenTag? . OpenTag is a model-agnostic AI teammate for Slack, and Microsoft Teams is coming soon too. Mention OpenTag in a thread, and the task is assigned to the best available model before the result returns to the same conversation.. . . . How is OpenTag different from Claude Tag? . Claude Tag is tied to Claude. OpenTag routes work to whichever of more than 40 models suits the task, including Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, Llama, and Mistral. It works in Slack today, with Microsoft Teams support coming soon, and OpenTag does not make money by selling you more model tokens.. . . . Which models can OpenTag use? . Over 40. The frontier families (Claude, GPT, and Gemini) plus the open and efficient ones behind them, reached through Conifer's catalogue. New releases land in the routing without your team changing anything.. . . . Where does the 70% saving come from? . Most tagged work does not need a frontier model. OpenTag routes each request to the cheapest model that can do the job, which targets up to a 70% cut in cost per tagged task against sending everything to a frontier model. It is a target rather than a customer average, and it moves with the mix of work your team tags.. . . . How do I get started? .
