Lead investor . Investor . Angels . Advisors . . . . We build in the gap the systems agree to ignore.. Every procurement team we spoke to had already bought the right systems. An ERP holding the transactions. A purchasing system holding the orders. A contract system holding the agreements. None of them were the problem.. The problem was everything those systems agree to ignore. A delivery date that changed in an email. A supplier that exists three times under three spellings. An invoice that does not match its order for a reason someone has to go and find. A renewal that arrives before anyone has assembled the evidence to negotiate it.. That work never appears in a system diagram. It appears as a team of experienced people spending the week searching, comparing, chasing, and re-entering. The happy path belongs to the system of record. The exceptions belong to the team.. The obvious fixes had all been tried. Integration moves records without making them agree. A dashboard names the exception and leaves the work exactly where it was. A transformation programme replaces systems that did not need replacing, and spends a year doing it.. So we build in the gap instead. We connect the sources one process depends on, reconcile the records that describe the same supplier or the same order, encode the rules and exceptions a team already applies by hand, and put the result into software those people can run. The accepted outcome goes back into the system that owns it.. Start with the process that costs the most repeated work. Measure it.
