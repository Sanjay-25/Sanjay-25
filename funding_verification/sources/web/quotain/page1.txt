Customers Pricing Blog Sign in Sign in Book a Demo . . . . . About Quotain. What we are. Quotain is an AI sales roleplay platform built by Eden AI, Inc., a Delaware corporation. We help revenue teams turn playbooks, call evidence, and buyer context into realistic practice before a live conversation is at risk.. Representatives run discovery, demo, negotiation, and objection-handling simulations against AI buyers generated from the company's product, ideal customer profile, and deal context. Each session produces a transcript, a rubric scorecard, likely objections, and a playbook the rep can take into the actual call.. The product is for sales enablement, managers, and representatives who need conversation-level readiness, not another LMS course or generic chatbot roleplay.. . Who we are.
