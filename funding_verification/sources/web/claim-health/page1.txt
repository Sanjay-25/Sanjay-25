Announcements. . . . . Today we're announcing our $4.4 million Seed round , led by Maverick Ventures , with participation from Peak XV, Y Combinator, and DHVP . The round also includes participation from CEOs and COOs at post-acute care agencies - operators who understand firsthand why this problem matters.. ‍. This funding accelerates our mission: eliminating the administrative chaos that comes with running a home-based care agency. . ‍. The invisible crisis in post-acute operations. Across the U.S., more than 68,000 post-acute providers are delivering critical patient care. But behind every admission is an operation held together by manual work: payer portals, faxed referrals, spreadsheet tracking, phone calls to verify information that should be automatic.. The result? Patients wait days to start care they've already been approved for. Referrals go to whichever agency answers first. Not because intake teams aren't good at their jobs, but because the information they need is scattered across faxes, portals, and inboxes — and the packet that finally arrives is often missing half the story.. ‍. A referral gets lost in an inbox.. Key clinical details never make it out of the referral packet.. A patient's coverage lapses and nobody notices until care is interrupted.. Coverage terminates without warning.. By the time these issues surface, the patient has already been waiting.. ‍. What's different about Claim Health. Most software in this category shows up after the fact, once a problem has already reached the patient. We're building something different: infrastructure that catches it upstream.. Claim Health manages the entire referral-to-discharge workflow. Our AI reads every referral packet, checks it against an agency's own clinical criteria, cites what it finds back to the source document, and keeps watching after admission — catching issues before they delay a patient's care.. We don't replace your EMR. We don't ask your team to learn another system. We plug into your existing infrastructure and operate invisibly, alerting teams only when action is needed.. The difference? Other platforms automate specific tasks. We coordinate the whole workflow, so problems that start at intake don't become delays at start of care.. ‍. What comes next. This Seed funding lets us accelerate on three fronts:. Product expansion - Deepening our AI capabilities across the admissions workflow to catch more issues before they reach a patient.. Team growth - Hiring engineers and operators who understand both AI and the operational reality of post-acute care.. Customer success - Scaling our implementation and support infrastructure to serve agencies of all sizes.. ‍. Building the future of home-based care operations. Our long-term vision is simple: care that starts the day it's approved.. A system where nothing sits between an approved patient and their first visit. Where intake teams spend their time on the judgment calls that actually require a human. That's the infrastructure home-based care deserves. And that's what we're building.. To our investors: thank you for believing we can rebuild this system from the ground up.. To our customers: thank you for trusting us with your admissions and pushing us to build something truly different.. And to every operator reading this: if you're ready to fix admissions for good, we'd be honored to build the future with you.. . . . Book a Demo with an Expert.. Stop chasing paperwork.
