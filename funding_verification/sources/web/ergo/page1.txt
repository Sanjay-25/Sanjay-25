Ergo’s story begins at Georgia Tech, where co-founders Ishan and Yash first met. Their early experiences taught them the challenges of building scalable solutions and the power of technology to transform industries. Ergo was born in the middle of a high-growth startup’s chaos. While crashing on a friend’s couch, Ishan and Yash saw firsthand how much time sales teams wasted on repetitive administrative tasks. Instead of focusing on relationships and deals, they were bogged down by inefficiencies.. . Determined to solve this, the duo built the first version of Ergo over a single weekend.
