What we do today Agentin operates Revenue (Quote-to-Cash) and Spend (Procure-to-Pay) workflows on top of SAP, Salesforce, NetSuite, ServiceNow, Workday and Dynamics.. Agentin is building the intelligence layer on top of those systems: one AI brain that understands your customers, vendors, orders and POs across tools, and runs core workflows end-to-end.. . . . .
