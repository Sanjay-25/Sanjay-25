Who is dreach for?. dreach is for staffing firm owners and operators with employer clients and room for another job order. It works the client side first and does not run broad candidate sourcing.. . Where do the warm intros come from?. dreach finds a way in through people your firm already knows: current and past clients, people you placed who now work at the hiring company, and contacts your team authorizes. It shows the path and why. Your team makes the introduction; dreach contacts no one.. . Where do the candidates come from?. dreach only looks at people your firm already knows: your ATS, past placements, and network you authorize. For a live role it ranks who would fit and shows why. No open-web search and no candidate database.. . How is dreach priced?. dreach is priced as a monthly minimum credited against a fee on results, only on orders that started with a dreach lead. The percentage and time window are agreed before launch.. . Does dreach guarantee revenue?. No. dreach gives timely proof and the right person to contact. The order, the placement, and the collection are up to your firm and the client, and dreach records what you report.. . Is dreach a CRM or an ATS?.
