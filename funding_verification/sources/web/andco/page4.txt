August 18, 2026 • 6 min read . In this article. We started Andco with medical records. . Connecting more of the work. . Agents that stay with the work. . More firms should be able to say yes. . . Today, we’re announcing Andco’s $4.8 million seed round , backed by Y Combinator and Robinhood, with participation from the founders of LangChain, Care.com, Fountain, Whatfix, Mixpanel, Eight Sleep, Grow Therapy, and others.. . We started Andco with medical records.. Getting them often means sending a request, calling to check on it, resending an authorization, and following up for weeks. Sometimes the records arrive incomplete. Sometimes a team finds out, after all that waiting, that the request went to the wrong place.. Behind that work is a person waiting for their case to move forward. They may be recovering from an injury, missing work, and trying to pay bills. The longer the process takes, the harder it can become to keep waiting.. We believe injustice should never be administrative. Someone’s ability to pursue a case should not depend on how many hours their legal team can spend on hold.. So we built agents to take on the work of getting medical records, from the first request through follow-up and collection. Andco has completed thousands of retrievals . We can serve firms in all 50 states .. . . Connecting more of the work.. We’re now expanding into intake workflows and demand drafting , connecting more of the work that goes into understanding a client’s situation and preparing their case.. These parts of the process depend on one another. What a client shares during intake informs which records to request. Those records can reveal additional treatment, missing bills, or questions to revisit with the client. Together, that information provides the foundation for a demand.. We want the understanding built along the way to carry through the whole process. . Every case has facts that must be gathered individually. What happened to this person? Where did they receive treatment? Are they still recovering? What coverage is available? How has their life changed?. The answers are spread across providers, insurers, agencies, and clients themselves. Getting them requires requests and conversations. It also requires knowing what to ask next.. That is the work we’re building Andco to do: gather case-specific information, follow through on what is missing, and help turn what has been learned into work the legal team can review and use .. . . Agents that stay with the work.. Our agents already retrieve records, bills, and police reports, open claims, verify coverage, and monitor outstanding work. They operate across phone, fax, email, physical mail, web portals, and SMS.. As we expand, we’re building toward agents that can stay with this work over the life of a case. An agent should remember what a provider said last week, notice when a promised response hasn’t arrived, and follow up. Information gathered during intake should inform the case workup. A demand draft should draw on the records and conversations collected along the way, with supporting sources available for review and gaps made visible.. Doing this well requires care. Clients’ circumstances change. Records can be incomplete or contradictory. Some questions need another phone call; others need an attorney’s judgment. Firms need to see what Andco has done, understand where information came from, and control when it acts. Attorneys and staff remain responsible for legal judgment, strategy, review, and approval. . This round will help us invest in the agents, infrastructure, and operations needed to connect that work reliably, from a client’s first conversation with a firm through preparation of their demand.. . . More firms should be able to say yes.. We’re grateful to the firms that trusted us with their first medical record requests and have helped shape what comes next. Their teams have shown us how much persistence goes into preparing a case, and how often capacity determines whether a firm can help another person.. We want more firms to be able to say yes to a client they can help. We want legal teams to have the information and time to prepare each case well. And we want fewer people to spend months waiting because a request went unanswered or a follow-up slipped through.. We started with medical records.
