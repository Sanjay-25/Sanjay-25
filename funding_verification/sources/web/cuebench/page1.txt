Security — Cuebench ← Back . Security at CueBench. Last updated: July 2026. Security is core to how CueBench is built. We are deliberate about handling as little sensitive data as possible — and about protecting what we do handle. This page summarizes our security practices. To report an issue or ask a question, email security@cuebench.dev .. Data minimization & privacy. CueBench builds and operates reinforcement-learning environments and benchmarks. We collect only the minimum needed to provide the Services — account details, the models or endpoints you ask us to evaluate, and the resulting scores and traces. We do not sell customer data, and we do not use it to train third-party models. See our Privacy Policy for details.. Encryption. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest within our infrastructure is encrypted using industry-standard algorithms (AES-256 or stronger) provided by our cloud platforms.. Infrastructure security. Our services run on reputable cloud infrastructure operating from SOC 2 / ISO 27001-certified data centers with physical, network, and environmental controls. Public endpoints are served behind managed TLS, DDoS protection, and a web application firewall. Production environments are logically separated from development and testing.. Access control. We follow the principle of least privilege. Access to production systems and customer data is limited to authorized personnel, protected by strong authentication and multi-factor authentication (MFA), and reviewed periodically. Administrative and privileged actions are logged.. Secure development. We use version control and peer code review, and we separate development and production environments. Secrets and credentials are stored in managed secret stores — never committed to source code. We monitor our dependencies for known vulnerabilities and apply patches promptly.. Monitoring & vulnerability management. We monitor our systems for availability and anomalous activity, and we triage and remediate vulnerabilities based on severity.. Incident response. We maintain an incident response process to detect, investigate, contain, and remediate security events, and to notify affected customers as required by applicable law and contractual commitments.. Compliance. We are pursuing SOC 2 Type II , with our observation period underway. We align our practices with the principles of GDPR and CCPA. As reports and certifications become available, we can share them with customers and prospects under NDA.. Responsible disclosure. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please email security@cuebench.dev . We welcome responsible disclosure and will work with you to validate and resolve legitimate issues promptly. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to remediate before any public disclosure..
