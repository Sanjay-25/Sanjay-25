What exactly is a "modular autonomous factory"? + A modular autonomous factory is a production line made up of self-contained, self-operating modules (welding, scanning, engraving, etc.) that can be reconfigured in software and relocated physically. Instead of building a new line for each product, you compose one from modules that run themselves.. . . Do you sell hardware directly? + Our first hardware product, the Welding Module, is on a waitlist.
