The ranges come from a Monte Carlo simulation: your exact portfolio is replayed through 2,000 possible futures, each one built by resampling year-long stretches of real market history since 2000. Crashes, recoveries, and the way your holdings move together all carry over. The band on the chart is where the middle 80% of those futures land. The method is judged by one test: walking back through 2008 to 2025, the 80% band had to contain what actually happened about 80% of the time. It predicts ranges, not winners: no stock picks, no news forecasts. Cash is modeled flat, and event confidence is capped at 95% because no honest model can promise certainty. .
