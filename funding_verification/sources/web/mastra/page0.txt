The team building . the TypeScript agent framework. Mastra is the leading open-source TypeScript agent framework. We started in 2024 and have grown to a team of more than 35, spread across the globe.. . Join the team . . . . Our story. Mastra started in 2024. Now, it's the leading open-source TypeScript agent framework, getting millions of downloads each month. We've raised $35m from some of the world's top venture firms, including Spark Capital, Gradient, basecase, and Y Combinator.. But this isn't a new thing. We've been building open-source JavaScript for over a decade. Previously, we were the team behind Gatsby, one of the earliest frontend frameworks for modern web apps.. We're based in San Francisco, but our team is spread across the globe. And we can't wait to see what you create with Mastra.. . Sam, Shane, Abhi. .
