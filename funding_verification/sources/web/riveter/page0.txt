Monitor listings, pricing shifts, and market activity across regions in real time.. . . . . . . . FAQs. Book a demo . What is Riveter?. Riveter finds and structures data from the web using AI agents. You describe what you need in plain language, and its agents search the live web, navigate to the data, and extract it, returning a finished, structured dataset through a single API. Riveter handles bot detection, proxies, and site changes, so pipelines keep working when layouts shift.. How is Riveter different from traditional web scrapers?. Traditional scrapers are written against a specific page structure, so they break whenever a site changes and need ongoing engineering time to maintain. Riveter uses AI agents that interpret pages the way a person would, so the same configuration keeps working after a redesign. Riveter also runs multi step workflows. It can find every dental practice in a city, then pull every dentist from each one, in a single request.. How current is the data Riveter returns?. Riveter fetches data from live websites at the moment you ask, rather than serving results from a pre-built index. That matters for pricing, inventory, job postings, funding announcements, and anything that changes daily or hourly, where a cached index can be weeks out of date. For fast moving data like scores or election results, you can refresh as often as every minute.. What sources can Riveter pull data from?. Riveter reads more than web pages. It reads PDFs and images, calls third party APIs as part of a workflow, and combines those results with data pulled from the web in a single output. Everything returns in the same structured format regardless of where it came from.. How is Riveter different from Exa, Parallel, or Firecrawl?. Those tools sell a search or scrape call that you assemble into a pipeline. Riveter runs the whole workflow end to end and returns a finished, structured dataset. Where they lean on a pre-built index, Riveter goes live and goes deep, reaching data, depth, and completeness they cannot.. How much does Riveter cost?. Riveter is priced per action in credits, not per seat. There is a free plan, pay as you go with automatic top ups, a self serve plan, and custom enterprise pricing.
