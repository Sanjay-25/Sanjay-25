RASPIRE was founded by security engineers who spent years building and breaking mobile apps for banks, fintechs, and high-risk teams. We kept seeing the same pattern: SDK integrations, weeks of configuration, audit delays, and protection that was already outdated by the time the app shipped.. We believe runtime protection should be part of the build, not another project plan.
