YC Raised Since 2021

Active YC companies from any batch (2005 to Summer 2026) whose latest funding round beyond YC's own investment was on or after 1 October 2021. Rounds come from SEC Form D filings, company announcements and press. Companies from batches that started after October 2021 count even when the round date is unknown. Total raised is not capped; companies over $200M are flagged. Open positions are the higher of the YC job board and the company's own job board. Checked 1 Oct 2026.
