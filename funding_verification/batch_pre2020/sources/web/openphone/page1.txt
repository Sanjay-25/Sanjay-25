Quo exists to give the 99.9% the tools they need to thrive.. . . . . . . . Our story. Mahyar and Daryna founded Quo (formerly OpenPhone) in 2018 with a single goal: Helping businesses grow through building stronger customer relationships. . Quo (formerly OpenPhone) was founded in 2018 by Mahyar Raissi and Daryna Kulya to solve a problem they saw firsthand: business owners relying on their personal numbers to run their companies. While building tools for trade contractors, Mahyar noticed that those who communicated better earned more — yet they lacked a professional, modern phone system.. . That insight led to the first version of Quo: a simple, smart business phone designed for the way people work today. With roots in small business families and a mission to help companies grow through better communication, Daryna and Mahyar took Quo through Y Combinator — and have been scaling it ever since.. . . . .
