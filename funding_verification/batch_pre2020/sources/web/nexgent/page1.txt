From Staff Sergeant. . to Founder. NGT Academy was founded by Terry Kim, a U.S. Air Force veteran who built his career the same way NGT students do today — through hands-on training, industry certifications, and relentless skill-building.. . . . Terry started as an Air Force Staff Sergeant earning $55,000 a year. Seven years later, he was a Cisco-certified consultant earning $350,000. He didn't take the traditional college path. He stacked certifications, worked at Keesler Federal, Cisco, Dimension Data, and Arista Networks , and eventually founded NGT to give others the same training shortcut he wished he'd had.. . NGT exists because Terry's path proved one thing: with the right structure, anyone willing to put in the work can build a serious career in IT — no four-year degree required..
