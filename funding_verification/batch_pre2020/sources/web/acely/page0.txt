When should I start studying for the SAT or ACT?. Most students start in the spring of sophomore year or the summer before junior year, which gives enough runway to build skills, take multiple practice tests, and leave room for 1-2 retakes if needed. Students who start 3 to 6 months before their test date tend to see the biggest score gains. Also, the sooner a student starts thinking about the SAT and ACT, the more time they have to figure out which test they may be stronger in as well.. . . . . .
