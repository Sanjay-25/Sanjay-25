We believe every student deserves the chance to reach their potential, regardless of their background. Acely was built to make world-class SAT and ACT prep personalized, affordable, and accessible to every student.. . . . . . . Why this matters. We are on a mission to expand access to test preparation for millions of students across the country. Research shows that 33% of students from the wealthiest families scored a 1300 or higher on the SAT compared to less than 5% of middle-class students. This has nothing to do with intelligence and everything to do with access. The students who score the highest are the ones whose families can afford to invest in preparation. Acely’s mission is to bridge that gap.. . . . . . . . . . How we're making a difference. Acely combines expert-developed content, AI personalization, and human insight to create a study plan tailored to each student. As students progress, the platform continuously adapts based on their performance, learning needs, and goals. By creating realistic plans that require as little as 20 minutes of studying per day, Acely makes personalized, expert-guided test preparation accessible, effective, and affordable for every student.. . . . .
