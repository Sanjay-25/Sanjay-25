Everybody hates it. But nobody does anything about it until.... . . . . The groundwork is laid for Rippling… 2016 . Parker's basement is unfinished. So is Rippling's first product. But we have started to build.. . . . . 2017 . . . Y Combinator taps Rippling. The famous accelerator behind Stripe, Airbnb, and Dropbox inducts Rippling. We raise $10M there.. . . . Rippling 1.0 is built. Hello, World! The first version of our product is here. It's not the platform it will be one day, but it's a start.. . . . A tale of two cities. We open a couple offices. One is in San Francisco, near our founders. The other is in Bengaluru, where products are being built.. . . . . . . . . . . 2018 . . . We go live with customers. Our first product is launched, getting into the hands of curious customers. Like magic, busy work starts to disappear.. . . . Product-market fit finds us. Closing tons of customers in our first year confirms what we suspected: There’s an appetite for Rippling.. . . . A compound startup is born. As part of Rippling’s strategy Parker commits to releasing great products regularly , instead of coasting on one.. . . . . . . . . . . 2019 . . . 10+ founders are hired. The team seeks out founders who can own products and lead teams. Many of them are Y Combinator alumni.. . . . CFO joins leadership team. Adil Syed joins Rippling from Snap, where he was Head of Strategy and Operations and helped lead Snap's IPO.. . . . So long, Shotwell Street. Rippling's San Francisco team outgrows its old office in the Mission District and relocates downtown.. . . . . . . . . . . 2020 . . . 260 employees onboard. Our recruiting team runs hard to hire tons of amazing Ripplers. Fifty percent of them are in product, engineering, and design.. . . . App Shop is launched. With over 200 integrations, Rippling launches the App Store. That's one giant leap for reducing admin work.. . . . Product gets its captain. After serving as Coinbase CPO, Jeremy Henrickson joins Rippling to lead product strategy across the company.. . . . . . . . . . . 2021 . . . Platform is launched. Rippling launches Platform, an overhaul that gives employees a host of new capabilities and changes how we build products.. . . . Payroll processes $10B. …in 2021 alone. Millions of employees receive Rippling's signature paystubs. Our infrastructure doesn't break a sweat.. . . . CTO rounds out engineering. Former founder and Amazon boss Gautam decides Rippling is headed places. He joins to help scale the engineering org.. . . . . . . . . . . 2022 . . . Engineering has new leadership. Albert joins as a CTO at Rippling after serving as Twilio Segment's VP Engineering. . . . Spend is launched. The first way to manage all of your spend in one place alongside payroll. It includes Rippling Corporate Cards, Expense Management, and Bill Pay.. . . . Rippling goes global. We launch Global Payroll , EOR , and globalized versions of nearly everything in Rippling’s product suite. Hello, World!. . . . . . . . . . . 2023 . . . Let's hear it for Talent. Launching Headcount Planning, Compensation Bands, Recruiting, and Performance Management, Rippling rounds out its talent products.. . . . Rippling raises $500M. Rippling weathers Silicon Valley Bank's collapse, raising $500M in a few short days to ensure companies will make payroll no matter what.. . . . . . . . . . . 2026 So what happens next? . We have a lot of exciting things planned for this year. But nothing is set in stone. Maybe you can help us write our story…. . . Come join us . . . . . . What's in a bar chart? .
