Every pipeline review in the world has this moment.. . Someone asks: "What's the backstory on this deal?" The room goes quiet. Someone opens a spreadsheet. Someone else checks their email. Twenty minutes later, you have a partial answer and three more questions. We built a product named after that moment - because solving it completely is our whole job.. . . . . . . What we believe about how revenue teams should work.. . Answers, not dashboards. . A dashboard tells you what happened. An answer tells you what to do. Revenue teams don't need more data to look at. They need someone to tell them what it means.. . . Your data, not generic benchmarks. . Every team sells differently. The patterns in your own deal history are more useful than industry averages. We built Backstory to learn how your business wins - not how everyone else does.. . . No new habits for your team. . Tools that require reps to change their behavior don't get used.
