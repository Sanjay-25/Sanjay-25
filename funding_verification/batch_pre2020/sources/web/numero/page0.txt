Frequently asked questions. — a / / can't legally wrap , so this uses the equivalent valid structure. --> What is Numero?. Numero is an all-in-one donor database and call time app for political campaigns and fundraising teams. It combines your donor lists, call time, texting, and payment tracking in one place, and surfaces each donor's giving history when you're making calls.. . How is Numero different from NGP VAN?. Numero is built for the person actually raising the money, not just for compliance staff. It automatically dedupes your list, shows giving history at call time, and includes texting and payment follow-up without the manual exports and VLOOKUPs NGP requires. Many teams switch from NGP VAN to Numero.. . Do I have to change how my team makes calls?. No. Numero works the way you already run call time — you open the app, see your call list, and make your calls. The difference is that each donor's giving history is right in front of you.. .
