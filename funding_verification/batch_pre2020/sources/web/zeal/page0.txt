Zeal is an embedded payroll and workforce payments platform purpose-built for staffing companies, labor marketplaces, EOR/PEO organizations, and workforce management tools.. Our platform provides the onboarding, payroll, payments, and billing infrastructure that powers modern workforce platforms. Companies use our API, embeddable components, white-label mobile app, and fully featured admin dashboard to deliver a branded payments experience.. Where traditional payroll systems were built for a single employer paying a fixed set of salaried employees, Zeal is built for the complexity of modern work: W-2 and 1099 workers, multiple pay frequencies, complex wage structures, distributed workforces, and industry-specific compliance requirements.. Zeal handles gross-to-net calculation, multi-jurisdiction tax withholding, payroll filing and reporting, remote I-9 with E-Verify, paycards, digital wallets, instant pay, and so much more. Workforce platforms partner with Zeal so they can focus on building great products instead of rebuilding payroll infrastructure from scratch.. . . . . . . . The problem we're solving. Despite its scale, payroll remains one of the most error-prone functions in business: the IRS estimates that more than 40% of small and medium-sized businesses pay at least one payroll penalty per year. For workforce platforms the problem is compounded. Legacy payroll systems aren't built to handle the shift-based, multi-employer, variable-schedule arrangements that define modern work.. . . . . . . $8.8 trillion. payroll processed annually in the United States. . 11,000+. tax jurisdictions. . 25,000+. income tax code changes per year. . . . . . The Zeal story. Zeal was founded in 2019 in San Francisco by Kirti Shenoy (CEO) and Pranab Krishnan (CTO). The company began as Puzzl, a gig staffing company. When regulation required Puzzl to reclassify thousands of 1099 contractors as W-2 employees, the founders went looking for infrastructure that could handle high-volume, dynamic payroll and couldn't find one that met their needs.. That experience became the foundation for Zeal: a payroll infrastructure company that gives other platforms the tools they struggled to find. Zeal was accepted into Y Combinator's S19 cohort.. In 2021, Zeal raised a $13 million Series A led by Spark Capital, with participation from Commerce Ventures and individual investors. In 2023, Zeal raised a $15 million series B led by Portage with continued support from Spark and Commerce.. . . . . . . . How we're different. We're not an all-in-one platform that does everything, but not well. We're also not a general purpose tool, that builds for everyone. We focus on building onboard, pay, and bill solutions for workforce platforms.. . . . . . . check. . Built for workforce complexity.
