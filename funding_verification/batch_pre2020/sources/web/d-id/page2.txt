About Us . Established in 2017 and backed by tier-1 venture investors, D-ID has been at the forefront of generative AI for nearly a decade—pioneering the technologies that now define the emerging era of Digital Humans. Our self-service studio and API have powered more than 200 million avatar videos and attracted a global community of over 280,000 developers building AI-driven solutions for customer experience, training, marketing, and communication. . . . . . . . . . . In 2025, D-ID expanded its leadership through the acquisition of simpleshow, uniting two innovators in AI video creation and interactive communication. This milestone accelerated our vision of delivering a unified Digital Human platform, combining enterprise-grade video creation with real-time, humanlike interaction. Today, leading organizations including PepsiCo, Fidelity, J.P. Morgan, SoftBank, NTT, Deutsche Telekom, PwC, Deloitte, Burda Media, AXA Insurance, and Gameloft rely on D-ID to create impactful, scalable digital experiences powered by expressive, intelligent Digital Humans. . . . . . . . . . .
