We work with the best banks so your integrations run smoothly.. . . . . Our thesis. For decades, banks ran on infrastructure built for the branch era. It moved the country's money reliably, and it still does.. Where banks grow has changed. Customer acquisition happens in software — apps, platforms, and marketplaces banks don't own.. Deposit growth follows the product, not the zip code.. Fee income increasingly comes from partnerships, not branch traffic.. $10B+ New deposits raised through bank–fintech partnerships . Running that work takes dozens of programs, shared oversight, and compliance evidence on demand: work the branch-era stack was never designed to do, because that era never required it.. We believe the fintech era should make banks stronger, not smaller.. . Stronger means the bank grows. Deposits, fee income, new customers. No core integration project. No risk the bank can't see or verify.. That takes two things. Infrastructure. The ledger, the rails, and bank-grade compliance — built to bank exam standards, not retrofitted to them.. . Demand. Bank-ready fintechs and enterprises, vetted before they ever reach the bank.. . . We build both. And we grow when the bank grows.. . The fintech era is missing its operating layer. The branch era had one. Core providers ran banking's back office for fifty years.. The fintech era never got its equivalent.. What's needed is a system banks trust with the business — not a vendor they endure.. . So we're building it.. See the platform → Talk to us . . . . How it's built. One network, built in layers. .
