We built GC GO Agent with a dial, not a switch—three levels of autonomy, and you decide where it’s set.. . . Assistant Mode: Agent Drafts + Human Approves. Your agent researches each constituent and drafts personalized outreach for every step of the sequence. Nothing sends without your review—you approve each message individually before it goes out.. . . Supervised Mode: Human Reviews + Agent Acts. Review a sample of your agent’s drafts, give feedback, and approve it to run the rest of the sequence the same way. Key moments—like a reply or a gift—still come to you before your agent acts.. . . Autonomous Mode: Agent Acts Independently. Your agent handles the full sequence—drafting, sending, and responding—on its own.
