Our Values. . . . Get to . the Problem. Earn Trust . Through Truth. Create . Clarity. Keep Finding . the Way. Own the . Outcome. . Get to the Problem. We believe you cannot truly solve problems from a distance. You have to go where the problem is; you have to be there. . Our edge isn't just the robots or the sensors or the software. It's what happens when we apply them from inside the problem, close enough to understand what's urgent and important, what's dangerous, what's been hidden, and what's been resistant to improvement for decades. . At Gecko, getting to the problem means being there, with presence, reliability, and care under pressure.. . . . . Earn Trust Through Truth. Gecko's work is too hard for low-trust teams. We move fast, give honest feedback that preserves dignity, ask for help, and assume that coworkers are trying to do the right thing. . We are genuinely curious people who care deeply about making things better for our customers, our users, and each other. We believe that trust accelerates execution and that performance without substance does not move people for long. . We believe that what does last is sincerity, humility, and authenticity.. . . . . Create Clarity. We believe that clarity is a scaling multiplier. . When our team understands the destination, the priorities, and what decisions they’re empowered to make, they generate initiative and move quickly. We fight for clarity across communication and product – for both our customers and for our teams – with the belief that this enables teams to be more decisive and maintain a bias for action. . Clarity is not certainty, and waiting for perfect information is how momentum dies.. . . . . Keep Finding the Way. We choose hard things on purpose. . We work in environments where failure has consequences, where the margin for error is thin, and where what we build affects safety, productivity, and national security. . We take risks, learn from failure, and improve fast. We relentlessly execute and carry a sense of urgency because our customers do not have the luxury of delay.. . . . . Own the Outcome. Ownership starts with responsibility, but it does not stop at role, title, function, or lane. A Gecko's role is the floor, not the ceiling. . When something important is at risk, owners do not wait for perfect clarity, hide behind process, or assume someone else will save it. They pull in the right people, create the path, and see the work through. . The best ownership at Gecko is shared ownership. It is teams coming together across functions to own the result, the tradeoffs, the mistakes, and the successes.. . . . . . . . Our People. Rust on our boots, dirt under our nails..
