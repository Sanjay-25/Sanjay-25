FightCamp enlisted top experts in nutrition, boxing, Muay Thai, and MMA to bring you an app series that will elevate your training to the next level. . Published: December 7, 2022. Topics: What's New, For Members. Author: Mollie McGurk . . . . . Ready to take your fitness training to the next level? FightCamp has brought the experts into the ring with an app series that will help you learn from the best.. On the FightCamp App now are tutorials and lessons from leading professionals in nutrition, boxing, MMA, and Muay Thai. You will have unique insight into how these professional fighters achieve their competitive edge, learning the tips and following the moves that have propelled them to the top of their field. Prepare to be inspired.. Here’s a peek at some of the high-level contenders kicking off FightCamp’s expert-led series.. Basic Nutrition with Tyler Minton. . . . Renowned nutritionist Tyler Minton leads us with an expert take on all things nutrition. A former professional MMA fighter, Tyler realized early on that there wasn’t much being done with nutrition in the sphere of combat sports. He stepped up to fill this void and is now recognized by MMA Junkie as one of the top nutritionists in the game. He has advised pro fighters including Khabib Nurmagomedov, Stephen “Wonderboy” Thompson, Max Holloway, and Daniel Corier.. Now he’s dropping his knowledge on the FightCamp crew, covering topics like different approaches to nutrition, energy balance, how calories are burned, how competitive fighters cut weight, and much more. Learn how to eat like an athlete today.. Advanced Footwork for Boxing Path with Christina Cruz. . . . A 7-time U.S. National Champion and record holder for winning the most NYC Golden Gloves Championships ever, professional boxer Christina Cruz is going to keep you on your toes with her series on advanced footwork. She will lead you through 6 chapters of tutorials, drills, and workouts based on her personal training method, showing you the step-by-step moves for increasingly complex footwork. Christina will help you build a solid foundation in footwork and get you moving like the pros.. FightCamp Co-Founder and Trainer Tommy Duquette has known Christina for over a decade and can attest that she has the best footwork in boxing. Now you can follow in the steps of the master.. 5-Week Boxing + Strength Program with Mike Rashid. . . . Powerlifter, entrepreneur, and professional boxer Mike Rashid teamed up with the FightCamp Trainers to give you this intense 5-week program. Mike will teach you how to combine weight training and boxing workouts into a weekly workout plan that will build muscle and burn fat. Mike knows that whatever your fitness goals are, strength training will help you achieve them. He will guide you through alternating strength training and boxing training days in this challenging, very effective program.. Mike Rashid’s fitness and motivational content on Instagram and YouTube have amassed a following of over 1 million people. He is also the co-founder of the supplement line, Ambrosia. You might need some extra supplements to make it through this challenge.. 4-Week Adapted MMA Fight Camp Path. . . . Professional MMA fighter and competitor in the welterweight division of the UFC Stephen “Wonderboy” Thompson is going to share how he structures his fight camps leading up to a big fight. This is 4 weeks of skills, conditioning, strength, and bag work, with an in-depth education on what makes a fighter’s training camp prep you to win. Stephen will teach you about several key concepts including road work, sparring, grappling, and more.. Stephen is also a former full-contact kickboxer with an undefeated kickboxing record of 57-0. Now regarded as one of the best strikers in MMA, he is sharing the secrets of success with FightCamp members. . Performance Nutrition Path with Chris Algieri. . . . Professional fighter, author, ringside analyst, and certified nutritionist Chris Algieri will teach you how to fuel your body for maximum performance. The author of “The Fighter’s Kitchen” and “So You Want to be a Fighter,” Chris brings expert insight on how to perform at your best in and out of the ring. He will go in-depth on topics like fasting, fueling, recovery, supplements, sleep optimization, and much more.. Chris won world titles in both boxing and kickboxing and even went the distance with the legend Manny Pacquiao. He was the Performance Nutrition Consultant to the Division I Stony Brook University Seawolves from 2016 to 2018 and has worked with top professional fighters including Danny Jacobs. Now he brings his extensive knowledge to FightCamp. . Intro to Muay Thai with Sean Fagan. . . . Sean Fagan , aka “The Muay Thai Guy,” will introduce you to ‘the art of 8 limbs’ with his intro to Muay Thai. Sean is a professional Muay Thai fighter, instructor, and entrepreneur who built an empire sharing workouts and instructional tutorials as “The Muay Thai Guy” persona. His YouTube videos have over 31 million views, and he has amassed around 300,000 subscribers. With Sean’s fighting record, it is no surprise. He fought on national Thai TV, competed on Lion Fight, won a title at Madison Square Garden, and won two amateur championships – one of which he fought with a broken arm.. “The Muay Thai Guy” Sean Fagan is going to show FightCamp the art of Muay Thai combat. .
