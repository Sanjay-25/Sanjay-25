Students who complete this course should be able to:. Explain how blockchains and cryptocurrencies work. Find data related to blockchain-based entity or transaction addresses. Follow a transaction across blockchains. Track attribution in relation to service providers, actors and entities. Identify and use open source methods of attribution. Understand smart contracts and tokens. Explain Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) and how they relate to blockchain. . . . . . 3. Delivery and pricing. .
