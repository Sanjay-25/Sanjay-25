We move fast, stay curious, and never lose sight of the customers we’re here to serve — because the engineers building tomorrow’s breakthroughs deserve a team that holds itself to the same standard.. . . . . . . . Great engineering changes the world.. We’re here to make it possible. . . . . . Customer-First Values. Rescale is here for, and because of, our customers. It takes GRIT.. . Growth Mindset Gets to Great. Our desire to learn and improve, combined with hard work, drives us to master our craft. We embrace challenges, start from first principles, and persist until we get to great. Like our customers, we push into the unfamiliar with curiosity.. . Run Fast, Own The Outcome. We execute with speed to keep our promises to customers and each other. We care about meaningful outcomes and real impact, not just a set of activities. Agreeing on first principles allows every person at Rescale to move with the autonomy to own results end-to-end.. . . Iterate to Innovate. We listen to customers intently, anticipating needs and keeping them genuinely happy. We move fast, deliver early, and improve continuously through feedback. We balance what customers ask for with the innovation they need us to envision.. . Team Over Self. How we accomplish our goals together is as important as what we achieve. We seek and give feedback generously, because it makes us all stronger.
