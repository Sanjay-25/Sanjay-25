Important Safety Information. ‍ The Spring Implant is indicated for men with obstructive urinary symptoms from BPH. It should not be used in patients who have had previous treatment to the prostate or pelvis; a current urinary tract or prostate infection; a known allergy to nickel, titanium, or stainless steel; a weak urinary sphincter; or certain other urinary tract or prostate conditions. The risks and benefits of treatment should be discussed with a doctor to determine candidacy. In the clinical study, side effects in a small number of men included urinary discomfort, ejaculation pain or changes, pelvic pain, back pain, and urethral narrowing. Individual results may vary. For the complete Important Safety Information, visit www.zenflow.com/ifu . Caution: U.S. Federal law restricts this device to sale by, or on the order of, a physician..
