If AI is going to make our work take half as long, maybe the most important question we should be asking is: What are we going to do with our newfound free time?. We’re building a new company focused on the fun that can be had with AI. It's called Weekend .. As a kid, I spent the entire week looking forward to the weekend. I think most people do.. At Weekend, our goal is simple: use AI to make people's lives more joyful. We make games you play with your friends and family—and we're starting with a device you already own: the television.. We've discovered that voice AI is the skeleton key that unlocks interactive television. For fifty years, the only input to a TV has been a janky remote. Voice removes that constraint. Meanwhile, generative AI completely collapses the cost of creating new content.. Together these shifts enable something new — interactive, personalized entertainment on the biggest screen in every home. Try it today on your Roku, Samsung, LG, or Fire TV..
