Who thrives at Just Appraised?. We're looking for teammates who:. . . 1. Care about solving real problems for customers. 2. Want to work with smart, motivated teammates. 3. Seek out environments where ownership and accountability matter. 4. Value meaningful impact over hierarchy. . Fit matters — for you and for us. So we think it’s just as important to share what we’re not as what we are.. . We’re not a traditional corporate environment. Roles here aren’t rigidly defined, and we’re not a ticket-taking culture. We expect people to think holistically about problems and focus on what’s best for the company and the mission.. . What you’ll find instead is a team of high-performing people who hold themselves to a high standard and enjoy working with others who do the same. We care about building something meaningful and creating real value in the world.. . And while we move quickly and expect a lot from one another, we also recognize that people are human beings first. We respect individuality, different working styles, and the fact that a great team is made up of people — not just roles on an org chart.. .
