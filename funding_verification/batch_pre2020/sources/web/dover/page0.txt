Dover is the all-in-one recruiting solution for startups — combining a marketplace of top fractional recruiters with a free ATS and sourcing toolkit built for early-stage teams.. What products does Dover offer? Dover offers a Recruiting Partner marketplace, a free ATS, LinkedIn sourcing extension, job board distribution, employee referrals, and an agency portal.. How much does Dover cost? The ATS and core hiring toolkit are free. Recruiting Partners are paid based on time worked — with transparent cost-per-hire data so you know what to expect.. How does Dover make money? Dover earns a platform fee when companies work with Recruiting Partners on the marketplace. Product tooling for startups remains free.. . . . . .
