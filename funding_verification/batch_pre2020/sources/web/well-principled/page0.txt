Dr. Anne Marie Knott Chair of Science Advisory Board & Professor of Strategy at Washington University in St. Louis . . Originally an engineer and later program manager for missile guidance systems at Hughes Aircraft Company, Anne Marie became a professor of business and revolutionized our understanding of innovation. She invented the first and only predictive measure of firms’ R&D productivity. After earning a masters and PhD from UCLA, Anne Marie’s work has been consistently published in top academic journals.
