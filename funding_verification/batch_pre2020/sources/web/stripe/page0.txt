View as Markdown Install tools . . . Your financial account can hold a stablecoin balance in OUSD or USDC, both US dollar-backed stablecoins. It supports funding and money movement, including in markets where a fiat balance isn’t available. With a stablecoin balance, you can receive , store , convert , and send funds.. Availability . Where you can send stablecoin funds depends on the destination:. Countries for stablecoin payouts to bank accounts . Countries for stablecoin payouts to crypto wallets . If your business is in an eligible country , you can enable a stablecoin balance by adding OUSD or USDC as a currency on your financial account. In the Dashboard, go to Balances and select OUSD or USDC .. How funds move . Funds can move in and out of a stablecoin balance in the following ways:. Inbound Stablecoin funds directly from an external USDC or USDC.e crypto wallet. Converted from a supported Stripe fiat balance, which you can fund from an external bank account or your Stripe payments balance. . Outbound Stablecoin funds directly to an external USDC or USDC.e crypto wallet. As fiat to a bank account (Stripe converts your stablecoin to the local currency). Converted to a supported Stripe fiat balance . To fund card spend. . You can’t move funds from an external fiat account directly to your Stripe stablecoin balance. You need to first move them to a Stripe fiat balance, then convert them to stablecoin. For how funds convert between OUSD and USDC, see Stablecoin balances .. . . . Stablecoin functionality .
