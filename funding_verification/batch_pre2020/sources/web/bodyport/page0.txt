At-home fluid status monitoring made effortless.. . . . . . . Introducing Bodyport. Bodyport is advancing the monitoring and management of patients with fluid management-related health conditions through simple daily measurements at home. The FDA-cleared Bodyport Cardiac Scale noninvasively measures weight, peripheral impedance, pulse rate, and balance. Bodyport’s connected software platform helps care teams interpret longitudinal patient trends, manage growing patient populations, and make more informed care decisions remotely.. . . . . Cardiac Scale: Simple design, big impact. . . With a single step, the FDA-cleared Bodyport Cardiac Scale measures hemodynamic signals through a patient’s feet and transmits the data over a cellular connection to the care team within minutes. Advanced sensors and proprietary algorithms non-invasively measure a wide range of metrics, providing a comprehensive view of heart health and fluid status from home.. . . . . . Congestion Index: Deeper insight into changes in congestion. The Bodyport Congestion Index combines multiple parameters captured by the Cardiac Scale into a single composite metric that provides insights into longitudinal changes associated with worsening congestion.* Presented alongside underlying measurements and trends, it helps healthcare professionals assess changes in clinical context, supporting more informed patient-management decisions.. . *Results from SCALE-HF 1, a prospective, observational, multicenter study of 329 patients with heart failure followed for 238 patient-years. Elevated Congestion Index preceded 70% of adjudicated worsening heart failure events, compared with 35% using traditional weight-based rules. . Fudim M, Egolum U, Haghighat A, Kottam A, Sauer AJ, Shah H, Kumar P, Rakita V, Lopes RD, Centen C, Ozonat K. Surveillance and alert-based multiparameter monitoring to reduce worsening heart failure events: results from SCALE-HF 1. Journal of Cardiac Failure. 2025 Apr 1;31(4):661-75. . . . . .
