Hightouch provides the control and governance capabilities you need to safely plug Reverse ETL into your tech stack.. SSO: Manage user authentication centrally with SAML SSO and keep access grants up-to-date with support for SCIM.. . Role-based access: Leverage fine-grained permissions and custom roles to control who has access to specific workspace resources (models, destinations, and syncs).. . Row-level access: Grant users access to data based on geography, business unit, or any other parameter.. .
