Doppler is on a mission to secure the world's secrets . . . . . . . . . 75B+ secrets read every month . . . . . . Our story. In 2018, our founder, Brian Vallelunga, hit a brick wall managing secrets while coding a personal project. Existing solutions felt clunky, designed for security teams rather than developers integrating into their workflows with ease. Out of this frustration, Doppler sprouted.. . The goal was to build a secrets management platform that developers, DevOps, and security teams could all love. Brian's product vision was clear: make security as appealing as developer productivity. We knew that if developers naturally loved using Doppler, the security benefits would follow, ultimately creating a safer internet for everyone.. . Meet our team . . . . Our investors .
