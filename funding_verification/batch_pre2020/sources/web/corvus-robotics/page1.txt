Jackie Wu and Mohammed Kabir started building autonomous drones together in 2017, with the first in-house build in Kabir's MIT dorm room. Today, Corvus One® drones fly day and night in warehouses all across North America while Corvus Trident™ records every pallet the forklifts move, keeping millions of inventory locations accurate for some of the largest companies in the world.. Backed by Tesla's cofounder, Y Combinator, execs from top robotics companies including Amazon's Kiva Systems, and leading multi-billion dollar funds in robotics and retail, Corvus Robotics is building the physical AI form factors to automate all material handling operations in the future.. . . . .
