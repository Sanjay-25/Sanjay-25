Below is an example of what you can typically expect from our interview process, though the number and order of interviews may vary depending on the position.. . Recruiter call. Our interview process begins with a conversation with one of our Recruiters. In this call, you'll learn more about the opportunity, dive into your experience, and discuss your career goals. We’ll also make sure to leave plenty of time for your questions.. . . Hiring Manager call. Next, you'll meet with the Hiring Manager for the position you’re interviewing for. They'll dive deeper into your experience and accomplishments and explore how your skills align with the needs for the role.. . . Practical assessment. In this stage, you'll showcase your skills in action through a practical exercise. Whether it be a coding challenge, a mock call, a case study, or something else entirely, these assessments are custom-designed to simulate the type work you would be doing on a daily basis.. . . Work History interview. Here, we'll review your past work and educational experiences in chronological order. This conversation helps us understand your professional journey, what motivates you, and how we could best support your growth at BioRender.. . . Leadership interview. In this last conversation, you'll meet with a cross-functional leader to explore how your approach aligns with our core values and competencies.. . . Reference checks. As a final step, we'll speak with people you've worked with previously to learn more about your working style and gather additional insights into how we could support your success.. . . .
