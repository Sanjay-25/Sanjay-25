Questions About the Event Operating System. . Event Operating System. What is the Event Operating System?. The Event Operating System is the part of Ticket Fairy your team uses to plan and run the event: planning, artists and advancing, travel and hotels, contracts and payments, budgets and costs, traders, exhibitors and sponsors, crew and volunteers, crew meals and safety, accreditation, registration and badges, show day, site and logistics, and your venue calendar. Fai can help with all of it.. . . . How do we get the Event Operating System?. Book a demo. We switch the Event Operating System on for your events and help your team set up the first one.. . . . . Does it work with our ticketing?. Yes. The Event Operating System works on your Ticket Fairy events, so your ticket sales, guest tickets, check-in and buyer emails are already connected. Settlements use your ticket sales, and your door team scans passes in the same ticket app they use for tickets.. . . . Is Fai included?. Yes. Fai chat comes free in your dashboard: ask it to build and change events, check live sales and make most of the changes you could make yourself. The AI event management plan goes further. Fai reads your event email, picks out actions and deadlines from documents and turns your meetings into follow-ups. Ask about the plan when you book your demo.. . . . . Does Fai act without asking?. No. Changes need your confirmation, and anything that reaches other people, such as an email, a text or a contract, waits for your approval. Fai can only do what the person asking can do.. . . . Who can apply for Ticket Fairy Capital?. If your event business is eligible, you can apply for up to $3M in the United States, up to €3M in Europe and up to £5M in the United Kingdom. A funding partner reviews your ticket sales history, event track record and projected sales, decides whether to approve your application and sets the terms.. . . . . Is Ticket Fairy a bank?. No. The Ticket Fairy, Inc. is a technology platform and is not a bank. Vault banking services are provided through regulated banking partners, and Ticket Fairy Capital financing is provided by third-party private credit partners, who make every funding decision.. . . . . . . . . . .
