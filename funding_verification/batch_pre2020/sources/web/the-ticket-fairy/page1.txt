Sell general admission, reserved seats, timed entry, payment plans, add-ons and merchandise from one checkout. You can put that checkout on your own website, sell at the box office and send tickets to buyers' phones.. See ticketing features . Referral marketing. When you turn referral rewards on, your buyers get a personal link and earn cash back when friends buy through it. Organisers see 20% more ticket sales on average, without extra advertising spend.. See how referrals work . Payments and payouts. Connect your own Stripe account and ticket money reaches your bank every day while you are still on sale. Fai is included free in every dashboard. Tell Fai about your event and Fai builds it, changes ticket types and answers questions about your sales and orders.. Meet Fai . Vault banking and Capital funding. Vault gives your team multi-currency accounts, expense cards and bank transfers for event money. Capital advances working capital against your ticket sales and takes repayment from those sales. Ticket Fairy is an event ticketing and marketing platform. Organisers use it to sell tickets, promote events through buyer referrals, get paid and check people in. Ticket buyers use ticketfairy.com to find events and buy tickets.. Is Ticket Fairy legit?. Yes. Ticket Fairy has sold tickets since 2013, has processed more than $300M in ticket sales and is backed by Y Combinator. See how we protect buyers and organisers .. Who founded Ticket Fairy?. Ritesh Patel and Jigar Patel founded Ticket Fairy in 2013. Ritesh ran underground music events for ten years while he worked as an engineer, and built Ticket Fairy to reduce the risk for event organisers. Jigar brought 17 years of building and scaling companies and an MBA from Warwick.. How much does Ticket Fairy cost?. Free events cost nothing. For paid tickets the buyer pays a service fee by default, so it does not come out of your ticket price, or you can choose to absorb it. Core ticketing has no monthly fee. See the fee for your country and ticket price .. How is Ticket Fairy different from Eventbrite?. Ticket Fairy can pay buyers cash back for bringing friends, offers daily payouts, payment plans and face-value resale, and can fund your event through Capital. Eventbrite has no payment plans or face-value resale marketplace and does not fund events.. Which buyers earn referral cash back?. Buyers of paid tickets earn cash back when you turn on referral rewards for your event. Free tickets do not earn cash back. You choose the rewards and how many friends a buyer needs to bring.. Who can get Capital funding?. Capital is for organisers with a history of selling tickets, on Ticket Fairy or any other platform. Third-party credit partners provide the funding and decide approval and terms, because Ticket Fairy is not a bank or lender. See how Capital works .. Who can open a Vault account?. Vault is available to businesses registered in the US, the UK, any EU country, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Canada, Australia and Singapore. It has its own eligibility check, separate from your ticketing account and from any subscription plan. Book a demo and our team will check whether your brand can open a Vault account.. What do door staff need to scan tickets?. Door staff need an iOS or Android phone with the Entry Fairy app and an internet connection at each entry point. Offline scanning is not available yet, so check the connection at every entrance before doors open.. When do organisers get paid?. Daily payouts need your own Stripe account, connected to Ticket Fairy and eligible for daily payouts. With it, payouts reach your bank every day. If Ticket Fairy processes the payments for you, you are paid 10 to 15 business days after the event.. Which countries does Ticket Fairy work in?. Ticket Fairy works for events and venues worldwide. Buyers pay in their local currency with local payment methods in countries including the US, UK, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, India, Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand, Japan, South Korea and Nigeria, and we add more regularly. If your country is not listed, ask our team.. How do I contact Ticket Fairy?. Organisers can email support, use live chat or ask their account manager.
