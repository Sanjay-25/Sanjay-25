Our team believes what you watch, read, and listen to matters. We’re here to help creators go independent and thrive —and we’re hiring.. See openings . . . . . . . Our Mission. At Substack, our mission is to build a new economic engine for culture — empowering writers, creators, and publishers to own their work, own their audience, and earn directly from their ideas. We provide simple, creator-first tools that make independent publishing sustainable, and shift value from platforms to the people who create. By linking great work with fair pay, we aim to strengthen independent culture and public discourse.. “ I enjoy working at Substack for three reasons: I get to work on and solve challenging problems every day; I am continually learning new things and developing myself; it may sound cliché but the people I work with are all awesome and if there's a common thread unifying us all, it’s a commitment to the mission and a good dose of humility. ”. Mike Cohen, Head of AI & ML Engineering. . . . . . . We’ve come far – and we’re only getting started. Substack’s successfully raised $100M in Series C funding in July of 2025. We are backed by Andreessen Horowitz, BOND, and The Chernin Group (TCG). Substack was part of the winter 2018 YC batch.. Our team is currently more than 150 people spread across the world.. Our HQ is in San Francisco, and we have offices in New York City and London.. . . . . . Support to do your best work. We offer robust benefits, though we know you’ll get those from many companies. What we we offer is an even more unique opportunity — the chance to work with and support independent creators around the world.. Remote work support. All employees receive a generous allowance to customize their home offices.. . Comprehensive health care. Substack covers 100% of premiums for employees and their dependents, as well as fully funded HRA plans.. . Parental leave. We want employees to be fully present for the new members of their family, so we offer generous parental leave.. . Flexible vacation and paid time off. Travel or recharge with our flexible vacation policy. We also observe an average of 12 paid holidays each year and have a company-wide winter break.. . 401(k) with employer match. Plan for tomorrow with a 401(k) that includes an employer match on 3% of eligible compensation, up to $10,000 per year.. . Internet stipends. Many of our employees work from home or on the go. We offer full time employees a monthly stipend to cover phone and internet costs.. . . . . . . . “ Substack is truly a mission-based company that cares deeply about the success of writers and creators. The entire company is dedicated to making it possible for publishers to earn a living doing what they love, and the ability to build their own space online that they control without advertising-based algorithms. I feel so lucky and fulfilled that I get to do this work every day. ”. Christina, Creator Marketing. . . See openings . . . . . Our Story. Substack’s story is purposeful reinvention. Launched as a subscription-first alternative to ad-driven media, we restore ownership and value to creators. Today, we’re a new media app that connects you with the creators, ideas, and communities you care about most. We provide publishing, payments, and analytics so writers, podcasters, and publishers can own their audiences and earn directly — and our leadership remains committed to shifting power back to creators..
