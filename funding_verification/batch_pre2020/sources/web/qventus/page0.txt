In 2012, two veterans of McKinsey and Stanford saw a crisis taking shape—and an opportunity to make a transformational impact on the healthcare system.. . . Even then, healthcare’s clinical, technological, and regulatory landscape was becoming increasingly complex—but healthcare operations were struggling to keep up. Today, doctors, nurses, and frontline managers are still struggling to close the gaps, and paying the price in surging levels of burnout.. . . . . For 12+ years, that challenge has been our inspiration. We’ve taken modern technologies and proven principles—AI, machine learning, behavioral science, and data science—and applied them to healthcare operations, with results that have made a revolutionary difference for countless healthcare providers and patients. . . And that’s just the start of our story. We’re continually innovating ways to simplify how healthcare operates—and what’s next will be even more transformative. . . . . . . . . . .
