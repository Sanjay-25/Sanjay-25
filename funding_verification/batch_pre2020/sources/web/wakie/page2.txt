The 70/30 Rule : Listen More, Win More. Research from Harvard University confirms that self-disclosure is intrinsically rewarding; talking about yourself triggers the same pleasure centers in the brain as food or money . Give them that "hit" by listening 70% of the time. Let them be the star of the show. . The "Why" Game : Skip the Surface. Never just ask what they do - ask why they love it. A Harvard Business School study found that people who ask more questions, particularly follow-up questions, are perceived as significantly more likable by their partners . This shift fast-tracks you from "Small Talk" to "Deep Talk." . The Mirroring Hack : Stay in Sync. Subtly mirroring their texting style, energy, or emoji usage. This is known as the "Chameleon Effect" - a documented psychological phenomenon where nonconscious mimicry of postures and mannerisms facilitates smoother social interactions and increases liking . . No Dead Ends : Kill the "Yes/No" Answers. Always ask open-ended questions. If they can answer with one word, you’ve lost.
