See Open Positions . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Who we are. At Snapdocs, our mission is to automate the home mortgage experience by connecting the people, processes, and technology that power the industry. . . Mortgages help borrowers become homeowners. However, the process of buying a home with a mortgage is long, stressful, and still mostly manual—not just for borrowers, but for the professionals who serve the industry. We are on a quest to transform how this impactful, multi-party transaction gets done. In turn, we aim to help millions of families experience simplicity and transparency during the biggest and most important financial decision they make: buying a home. . . To support our mission, we build teams that share our priorities—pragmatism, respect, and empathy for our clients—and create solutions that improve people’s lives.
