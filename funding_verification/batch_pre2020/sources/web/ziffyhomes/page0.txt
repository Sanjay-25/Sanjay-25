Is ZiffyHomes a marketplace like NoBroker? No. ZiffyHomes kicks in after you've found your tenant. We handle the full management lifecycle: agreements, rent, maintenance, communication. You can still make a property publicly listed on ziffyhomes.com when it's vacant, but that's a convenience, not our core product.. ii. Do you process rent payments? Not yet. We facilitate only. You enter the UPI ID or bank account, tenants see it on their dashboard, and we track when they pay. Direct payment processing lands in Phase 2 via Razorpay.. iii. How much does it cost? Free during the beta. Pricing is not finalised yet. What we know: a flat monthly fee per broker seat or per managed property, never a commission on your rent.. iv. Is this the same ZiffyHomes that was a co-living company? Same brand, new product.
