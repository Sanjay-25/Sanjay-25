Can’t find the answer?. . Contact us. . What is construction loan management software?. . . . Construction loan management software digitizes the full lending lifecycle, tracking draw requests, budgets, lien waivers, and inspections in one place so lenders can fund faster and reduce manual data entry. Rabbet Construction Lending is a construction loan management platform that unpacks draws, reconciles budget requests, verifies covenants, and surfaces risk early, giving lending teams one connected system for their entire portfolio.. . . How does construction loan management software work?. . . . The software centralizes every draw package, budget, and supporting document, then automates the review steps that lending teams usually do by hand. In Rabbet Construction Lending, incoming draw documents are read and reconciled against the budget automatically, and covenant and compliance checks run consistently on every draw, so draw reviews move faster.. . . What should lenders look for in construction loan management software?. . . . Start your evaluation with the loan types you manage, since not every platform supports every structure. Rabbet Construction Lending supports commercial, consumer, and homebuilder lines of credit. From there, weigh automated compliance and covenant checks, draw review speed, and integration with your existing lending systems.. . . What types of construction loans can Rabbet Construction Lending manage?. . . . Rabbet Construction Lending supports the three main construction loan types: commercial construction loans, consumer construction loans, and homebuilder lines of credit. Lenders with mixed portfolios administer every structure in one platform rather than maintaining a separate tool per loan type.. . . Does construction loan management software reduce draw turnaround time?. . . . Yes. Automating document intake and budget reconciliation removes the manual steps that slow draw reviews, which is where most turnaround time is lost. According to Rabbet's State of Construction Finance 2025 report, lenders using Rabbet Construction Lending report days-to-fund about 3 days faster than non-software users. Rabbet Construction Lending reads draw documents on arrival and reconciles them against the budget, so reviewers start from a complete, checked package instead of assembling one.. . . How do construction lenders reduce risk during the construction draw process?. . . . Lenders reduce draw risk by verifying every draw against the budget, tracking lien waivers, and catching budget overruns before funds go out. Rabbet Construction Lending runs automated compliance and covenant checks on each draw and surfaces exceptions and cost overruns early, so exposure stays visible while the loan is still in progress.. . . Can Rabbet Construction Lending monitor construction loan covenants and compliance?. . . . Yes. Rabbet Construction Lending verifies covenants and runs compliance checks automatically on every draw, applying consistent standards as the portfolio grows. This surfaces missing documents and exceptions early, so compliance holds up under audit without adding review headcount.. . . What reporting and audit capabilities does Rabbet Construction Lending give lenders?. . . . Rabbet Construction Lending gives leadership a higher-level view of the portfolio with the ability to drill down as needed. You can see where any project stands on a given budget line item, review how a borrower has billed to that line item over time, and pull the underlying invoices without leaving the platform, so examiner and investor questions get answered without reassembling files by hand.. . . Does Rabbet Construction Lending integrate with our CORE banking, LOS, and BI systems?. . . . Yes. Sync construction loan administration data across your CORE, LOS, and BI tools through secure, API-first integrations, so your portfolio stays in balance and your team stops rekeying data.. . .
