Like the joint we're named for, Rabbet turns scattered pieces of development into one solid financial foundation.. . . . . . . . Rabbet is a real estate software company with two products: Rabbet Development and Rabbet Construction Lending . . . Our mission is to take the friction out of real estate development execution through structured, connected financial data. We help real estate teams turn fragmented project information into trusted insights that can be shared, understood, and acted on throughout the development lifecycle.. Founded in 2017, accelerated in Y-Combinator, and backed by QED Investors and Camber Creek, Rabbet is the trusted real estate management platform for more than $160B in real estate capital.. . . . . . Our Foundational Values. What Drives Our Success. . . . Efficiency. In real estate, efficiency can be the difference between a successful project outcome and a budget gone bust. Solving daily inefficiencies and improving big-picture processes are part of Rabbet’s foundational mission.. . . . . . Transparency. We foster transparency internally, with our customers, and with our trusted partners. Relationships are at the core of why we operate—with data, with each other, with our customers, and for their customers. We work to be the best version of ourselves and strive to be known for our collaboration, reliability, and authenticity.. . . . . . Guidance. We’re committed to providing an intuitive and user-friendly platform for real estate professionals to use as their data foundation.
