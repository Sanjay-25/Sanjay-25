Chief Executive Officer. . Read more. . . . Tony Grimminck. Chief Executive Officer. . Tony Grimminck is the Chief Executive Officer of Scribd, Inc., where he leads the company’s vision, strategy, and operations. He joined Scribd, Inc. as Chief Financial Officer in 2019 and was appointed CEO in 2023, bringing deep experience in finance, strategy, and scaling consumer-focused businesses.. ‍. Prior to Scribd, Inc., Tony held executive roles at HotelTonight and StubHub and served as Vice President of Investment Banking at Goldman Sachs and J.P. Morgan. Earlier in his career, he was an Officer in the Australian Army. He holds a Bachelor of Engineering (Honors) and a Master’s in Engineering Science from the University of New South Wales, an MBA from Columbia University, and graduated from the Royal Military College, Duntroon, and the Australian Defence Force Academy.. . . . . . . . . . . . .
