Infrastructure for labs that can sense, trace, review, and improve. Regulated laboratories generate enormous amounts of physical evidence every day, but most of it is not captured in a governed, computable form. Monitoring reveals that something is happening. iLabService connects that awareness to accountability: what happened, where, which asset or sample was affected, who responded, whether the response followed procedure, and whether the evidence can survive an audit. . That is the logic behind the product breadth. Environment and safety, equipment utilization, inventory and reagents, samples, procurement, training, space, gas, compliance, and audit trails are not separate point tools. They are connected operating surfaces for one infrastructure layer that makes regulated labs safer, more compliant, and more efficient. . . Physical deployment depth Own the real-world signal record.
