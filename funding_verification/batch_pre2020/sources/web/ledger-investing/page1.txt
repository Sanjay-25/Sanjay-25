Ledger Investing, Inc. is a tech-driven financial services company pioneering casualty ILS since 2016. Recently named to CB Insights' Insurtech 50 (2023), it has raised over $90M from top VCs and strategic insurance investors. Its $75M Series B round, led by WestCap, closed in June 2022 with participation from Teachers' Venture Growth, Intact Ventures, SignalFire, MassMutual Ventures, Allegis Capital, and Accel. Ledger has securitized $1B in gross premium via primary and secondary transactions on its Ledger Marketplace for General Liability, Workers' Compensation, Commercial Auto, and Private Passenger Auto.. .
