Trusted by 30,000+ homes and businesses including. . . . . . . What is Community Phone. . Community Phone was founded in 2018 after James Graham helped his grandmother navigate phone service options and saw firsthand how major carriers relied on hidden fees and complicated contracts. That experience inspired us to build a phone service that is transparent, reliable, and truly customer-focused.. Today, we combine landline-level reliability with modern features such as 99%-effective spam blocking, voicemail-to-text, smart call routing, and more — all without internet or contracts.. We serve more than 30,000 homes and businesses, with over 20 million calls on our platform. Forbes ranked Community Phone the #1 Home Phone Service Provider of 2025 .. Founded. 2018. .
