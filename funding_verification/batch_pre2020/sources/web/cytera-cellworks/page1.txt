We noticed these therapies were showing they could cure Parkinson's, blindness, even spinal cord injury - and yet most patients wouldn't never be able to afford or get access to them, because they're produced manually. So we decided to fix that with automated production.. . Six years ago, we started automating production of stem cell derived cells such as heart and brain tissue for R&D use in the largest US Pharma companies (including 3 of top 20) - and we’ve taken our iDEM automated technology through 6 generations of refinement and iteration since.. . Now, we're building and operating fully automated cell manufacturing sites to scale production of Regenerative Medicines at low cost to millions of patients. . . . .
