Our Belief. Staffing firms deserve better tools. The staffing industry generates hundreds of billions of dollars in annual revenue globally. But much of its core infrastructure still runs on email, spreadsheets, and outdated workflows. Candidately exists because we believe staffing firms deserve the same caliber of digital tools that exist in e-commerce, fintech, and SaaS.. Not a replacement for the recruiter. An augmentation.
