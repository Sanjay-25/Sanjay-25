At Thunkable, we believe that anyone can be a creator of technology. Whether you're building apps to solve real-world problems, experimenting with ideas, or learning computer science fundamentals, Thunkable has always been about making app creation accessible and enjoyable for everyone. . Today, I'm thrilled to announce the relaunch of our Thunkable for Education offerings , designed to inspire, teach, and empower the next generation of creators.. Why Education Matters to Thunkable . Education has always been at the core of our mission . We know that the future of technology is in the hands of young learners, and we want to provide them with the tools, resources, and support they need to succeed. With the world becoming increasingly digital, understanding how technology works, creating apps, and bringing ideas to life is a critical skill. But, traditional coding education can be overwhelming, especially for beginners. That’s where Thunkable comes in.. We’ve always believed that anyone, regardless of age or technical background, should be able to bring their ideas to life through app development. Our classroom solutions aim to bridge the gap between creativity and technical knowledge. We want to equip students, educators, and lifelong learners with the skills to make a real impact through app creation.. Thunkable has a long history of being closely tied to classrooms, hackathons, and global challenges. After collecting feedback from teachers who use Thunkable in their classrooms, we focused on building features that give teachers the tools to easily implement mobile app building into any computer science curriculum. . Benefits of Thunkable in the Classroom . With Thunkable, you can teach your students everything from app design and development basics to more advanced topics like API integrations, app testing, and publishing apps on the App Store and Google Play. . Whether your students are building a mobile game, a productivity app, or a tool for social good, you'll get the chance to experiment and iterate on real-world projects.. For Teachers: Simple Classroom Management . With our admin dashboard, you can easily enroll students, access their projects, and manage permissions for your Computer Science students. You can quickly bring Computer Science to life and unleash creativity as your students build and learn. You’ll have total control to determine which features and functions you want your students to access, including publishing, monetization, geolocation, and more. . For Students: A Glimpse of the Future . Peer-to-peer learning is a powerful tool in education, and we’re fostering it by enabling learners to collaborate on projects, share resources, and learn from each other’s successes and challenges. Students will get hands-on experience creating a real, live mobile app they can share with friends, family, and the world. Budding entrepreneurs of all ages will get a taste of the skills they’ll need for a career in tech, science, and other industries. . What’s Included in Thunkable for Education . Shared Workspace. Easy enrollment . Security and Compliance . Admin capabilities . What's Next? . This is just the beginning. We’re committed to continually improving and expanding our offerings, providing even more opportunities for students, educators, and creators to thrive. So, stay tuned for more. . Join Us on This Journey . We’re incredibly excited about what’s ahead and want you to be a part of it. Whether you’re a student looking to learn app development, an educator eager to bring the power of creation into your classroom, or simply someone with a passion for technology, Thunkable’s new education offerings are here to help you succeed.. The world needs more creators, problem-solvers, and innovators. With Thunkable’s new educational resources, we hope to inspire and equip you with the skills to build the apps that will shape the future. So, what are you waiting for? Let’s start creating!. September 29, 2026. •. 5. min read. . . . How Vibe Coding Changed No-Code App Building. . Vibe coding, a term coined by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy in 2025, means building software by telling AI what you want and refining it through conversation. For no-code builders, that's a big shift. Instead of assembling your app one block at a time, you can start with a description and let AI generate the screens, design, and logic for you. That means faster iteration and a lower barrier to getting your idea in front of real users. You're still in charge, though. You decide what your app should do, whether it truly serves your users, and whether it works on a real device before you ship it. With Thunkable's AI Builder, you can vibe code a native mobile app from the start. You can refine it with prompts, blocks, or code, test it on your phone, and publish it to the App Store or Google Play.. Entrepreneur. . . . . . . . . Craig Renney.
