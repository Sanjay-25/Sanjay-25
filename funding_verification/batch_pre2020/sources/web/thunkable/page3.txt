We’re excited to share some big news for Thunkable and app-building education in Florida!. Thunkable has officially been added to Florida’s 2026–27 CAPE Industry Certification Funding List , creating a new state-supported pathway for eligible Florida schools and districts to bring hands-on app building to more students.. For us, this milestone reflects something we’ve believed from the beginning: students should have opportunities to create with technology.. With Thunkable, students can start with an idea and turn it into a real, working mobile or web app. Along the way, they’re solving problems, making design decisions, testing their ideas, and learning how technology works by actually building it.. Now, Florida schools have a new opportunity to make that experience part of their career and technical education programs.. We’re excited about what this means for educators across the state and even more excited to see what their students build.. Learn more about what the CAPE designation means for Florida schools and districts, including funding eligibility and how Thunkable can support hands-on technology education.. September 29, 2026. •. 5. min read. . . . How Vibe Coding Changed No-Code App Building. . Vibe coding, a term coined by AI researcher Andrej Karpathy in 2025, means building software by telling AI what you want and refining it through conversation. For no-code builders, that's a big shift. Instead of assembling your app one block at a time, you can start with a description and let AI generate the screens, design, and logic for you. That means faster iteration and a lower barrier to getting your idea in front of real users. You're still in charge, though. You decide what your app should do, whether it truly serves your users, and whether it works on a real device before you ship it. With Thunkable's AI Builder, you can vibe code a native mobile app from the start.
