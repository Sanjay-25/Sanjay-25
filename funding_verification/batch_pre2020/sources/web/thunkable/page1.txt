Short Description. Thunkable is the no code platform that lets users design, develop, and deploy powerful, native mobile apps without having to write a single line of code. We make it easy for anyone to quickly turn their ideas into apps.. . Boilerplate. Thunkable is the no code mobile application development platform that lets users design, develop, and deploy native mobile applications without having to write a single line of code. It’s the most robust platform in the no code mobile application market, empowering users to develop fully customized and integrated apps. From a single project, users can generate native Android, iOS, and Mobile Web files, then deploy directly to the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and Web.. ‍. The company was founded in 2016 by Arun Saigal and Weihua Li with the mission to make everyone, everywhere active participants in digital creation. Thunkable is headquartered in San Francisco, California and has a global workforce, helping them support a quarter of Fortune 500 companies and end users from over 181 countries. Backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners, NEA, Owl Ventures, and Y Combinator, Thunkable is changing the way the world builds mobile apps.. Mission Statement. Our belief is that people with great ideas are everywhere, but 99.5% of the world doesn't know how to write code. Everyone, everywhere should be able to get hands-on with digital creation.
