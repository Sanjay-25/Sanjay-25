CoinTracker was founded in 2017 by Jon Lerner and Chandan Lodha, who realized that mainstream cryptocurrency adoption was being held back by the tax implications of everyday crypto activities. In 2018, Jon and Chandan were accepted into Y Combinator, and since then CoinTracker has become the gold standard of crypto tracking and taxes.. . In 2026, we launched Nino, a financial planning service that brings the same clarity we built for crypto taxes to the rest of your financial life — investments, equity, cash, real estate, and retirement, modeled by AI and reviewed by a dedicated CPA and CFP.
