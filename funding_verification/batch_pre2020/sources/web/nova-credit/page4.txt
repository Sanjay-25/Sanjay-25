Nova Credit Announces Advanced Document Fraud Detection for Income Navigator. As document tampering becomes increasingly sophisticated, our enhanced capabilities help businesses identify falsified income verification documents with greater accuracy.. . . Share. . . . . . . . Announcements Company . . . . . . Akaash Gupta. Head of Tenant Screening Partnerships, Nova Credit. . . As the threat of fraud rises with the increasing use of generative AI, traditional methods of verifying consumer information have become inadequate. A recent study projects AI-driven fraud losses could reach $40 billion by 2027 , highlighting the urgent need for more sophisticated fraud prevention. This risk is particularly acute in document-based income verification, where fraudsters use falsified documents to gain approval for apartments, credit cards, loans, and other products—often leading to significant losses through non-payment.. Today, Nova Credit unveiled industry-leading fraud detection capabilities in Income Navigator , significantly strengthening our ability to identify falsified documents. Our enhanced platform improves our analysis of both PDFs and images, now detecting sophisticated tampering through more than 700 fraud indicators distilled into clear insights.. A Comprehensive Approach to Fraud Prevention . Income Navigator reduces reliance on documents by 60% through two key capabilities:. 1. Direct Data Access . Rather than solely collecting pay stubs and bank statements from consumers, Income Navigator connects directly to thousands of financial institutions covering 98% of U.S. consumers. This means businesses can instantly verify income from the source, eliminating the need to request, collect, and review traditional verification documents. The system intelligently selects optimal data sources for each verification, maximizing both security and conversion.. 2. Intelligent Income Analytics . While direct access to financial data is crucial, turning raw transaction data into accurate income verification requires sophisticated analysis. Our processing engine automatically analyzes transactions from financial institutions and payroll systems to accurately identify income, handling everything from traditional salaries to gig economy earnings. This automation is particularly valuable given that 36% of Americans (58 million) report independent work income.. When documents are necessary, our newly enhanced fraud detection provides:. 3. Advanced Document Analysis . The platform delivers a clear verdict for each document, flagging potential tampering with specific reason codes that help businesses understand verification outcomes. Unlike solutions limited to PDF analysis, Income Navigator's technology extends to image verification, providing broader protection and 10% increase in conversion when compared to pdf-only providers.. Rising to Today's Fraud Challenge . As generative AI makes document falsification increasingly sophisticated, the risks of relying on manual review have never been higher. Income Navigator's enhanced fraud detection capabilities provide businesses with the tools needed to identify advanced document manipulation while maintaining efficient operations.. Driving Efficiency and Security . Income Navigator's comprehensive approach not only strengthens fraud prevention but also delivers operational benefits, reducing manual review requirements by 70% while maintaining high accuracy. By combining direct data access, intelligent analytics, and enhanced fraud detection, businesses can streamline their verification processes while better protecting against increasingly sophisticated fraud attempts.. .
