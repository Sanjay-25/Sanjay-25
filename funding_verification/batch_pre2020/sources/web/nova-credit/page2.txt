At Nova Credit, we're working to power a fair and inclusive financial system for the world. Our products use reliable data and analytics to help represent people more fairly to financial institutions, going beyond traditional credit scores to see an applicant’s full financial picture. We originally built the company with cross-border credit data to address the needs of people immigrating to the U.S. Now we're building on that foundation, expanding to cash flow underwriting, income verification, and other innovative approaches to create additional paths to improve financial access.
