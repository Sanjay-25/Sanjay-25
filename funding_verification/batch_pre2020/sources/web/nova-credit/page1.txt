Nova Credit opens pathways to financial opportunity for millions of people otherwise misunderstood by the traditional credit system. About Us. . We’ve stitched together unique data sources and built comprehensive analytics so that businesses can grow responsibly and so that consumers can get fairer access to credit. We’re on a mission to bridge the universe of consumer-permissioned data to enable a more fair and inclusive financial system for millions of consumers around the world.. We founded Nova Credit in 2016 to build a better credit system, starting with serving one of the most misunderstood populations: newcomers to America. We built the Credit Passport®, the world's first and only cross-border credit reporting system, enabling newcomers to use their international credit history when applying for U.S. financial services. Today, leading institutions like Chase, HSBC, and Scotiabank use the Credit Passport to serve newcomers from around the world.. We then broadened our offering into the Nova Platform, combining new data sources in services of new consumer segments. Through Cash Atlas™, our cash flow underwriting platform, lenders gain real-time visibility into consumers' financial health through bank transaction data. With Income Navigator™, we've automated income and employment verification, reducing friction while improving accuracy. And through Eligibility Compass™, we're bringing the same data-driven approach to affordable housing access.. . . . . . . Our Mission. We’re Backed By some of the most notable names in the industry. . . . . Nova Credit 101. . Here are the quick details on who we are, what we’re building, and why.. . . What problem is Nova Credit solving? Our mission is to power a more fair and inclusive financial system for the world. Every year, millions of creditworthy individuals are denied access to financing because lenders can’t access the right data to assess the individual's financial responsibility. We are on our way to accomplishing this mission by rewiring the financial industry with better credit infrastructure, analytics, and workflows, helping more people get access to credit opportunities.. . . . What’s innovative about Nova Credit? Our cross-border credit product, Credit Passport®, cash flow underwriting product, Cash Atlas™, and income verification product, Income Navigator, are trusted by leading organizations like HSBC, SoFi, Scotiabank, and Yardi to help them reach valuable new applicants from traditionally credit excluded populations. . Learn more about partnering with Nova Credit . . Who can use Nova Credit? Our reach is always growing.. With our Credit Passport® solution, we serve consumers who have moved to the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, United Arab Emirates, or Singapore, and have a credit history in Australia, Austria, Brazil, Canada, Dominican Republic, Germany India, Kenya, Mexico, Nigeria, Philippines, South Korea, Spain, Switzerland, Ukraine, the US (for newcomers in non-US regions) or the UK.. Our Cash Atlas™ solution serves the 150 million U.S. consumers who are credit-eligible and want to receive a more accurate, up-to-date picture of their credit history. Traditional credit bureau reporting can lag ~2-3 months behind and doesn't always show a complete picture for credit applicants. Our leading cash-flow underwriting solution helps expand credit opportunities without increasing risk for lenders.. Our income verification tool, Income Navigator helps companies automate verification of income, significantly improving coverage, conversion, and user experience. Our income verification solution is already trusted by more than 4,000 customers, including property management software, like Yardi, and lenders, like SoFi.. . . . Who are Nova Credit’s partners? We work with credit card companies, lenders, and other credit product providers to extend their reach to underserved individuals worldwide. Key partners include HSBC and Scotiabank for credit cards; MPOWER, Prodigy Finance, and Earnest for student loans; and Yardi, Appfolio, Saferent, and First Advantage for tenant screening.
