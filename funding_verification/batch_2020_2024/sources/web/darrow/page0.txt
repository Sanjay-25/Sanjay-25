Legal Risk Moves Faster Than the Law.. . The world has changed. Information moves at a speed and scale no human was designed to process. Yet our legal system - built for a slower, simpler world - is still expected to govern this one. . . The signals of legal risk are everywhere: dispersed across regulatory filings, corporate disclosures, news reports, and vast streams of digital information. They exist long before exposure compounds. Long before harm reaches scale. Long before anyone knew to look.. . Darrow is building the intelligence infrastructure that changes that. Not by making existing legal processes faster - but by creating the awareness layer the legal system has always been missing. One that works upstream, surfaces what's hidden, and gives the people who shape legal outcomes the clarity to act before it's too late.. . . Read our Research. .
