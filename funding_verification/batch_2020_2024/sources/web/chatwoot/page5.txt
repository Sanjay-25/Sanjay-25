Hacktoberfest 2021 at Chatwoot. Pranav Raj S. Published on Oct 01, 2021 . . . . 1 minute read . . . . We are happy to announce that we are participating in Hacktoberfest 2021. For those who don't know about Hacktoberfest, it is an annual event conducted by DigitalOcean to promote open-source contributions and build awareness about open-source communities. This year it is supported by an array of great companies, as shown below.. You can now contribute to Chatwoot and participate in Hacktoberfest. Please see the guidelines below. Feel free to reach out to us if you need any help.. Code contributions. Look for the issues tagged with "hacktoberfest" to implement features or fix bugs.. Ask the team to assign the ticket to you if you are willing to pick it up.. Add test cases on RSpec/Jest/Cypress.. Documentation. Did you find something missing in the documentation? Contribute to our docs by raising a pull request at our docs repository.. Translation. We are huge proponents of making software available in native languages.. We support 30+ languages right now. We expect this to grow to 50+ languages by March 2022.. Use https://translate.chatwoot.com or Github repository for contributing translations.. If none of the above items suits your interest, you can try using the product and start reporting bugs or feedback.. We are very excited to see your contributions. Happy #Hacktoberfest.. . . .
