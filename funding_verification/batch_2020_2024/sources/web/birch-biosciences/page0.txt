We use generative AI and proprietary high-throughput screening techniques to redesign enzymes with transformational recycling properties. These high-performance enzymes are integrated into a proprietary recycling process that breaks down plastic polymers, enabling the production of 100% recycled content plastics. This eliminates the need for fossil fuels in plastic manufacturing.. . . . High Quality. Low Emissions. Better Economics.
