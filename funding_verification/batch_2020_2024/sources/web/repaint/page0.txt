See full pricing details . . . FAQ. What is Repaint? Repaint is an AI website builder that creates custom websites using your content as source material. It can use your existing site , social profiles , or files . Or it can generate a site from just a prompt . Once it builds your site, you can edit anything by chatting with AI. When you're happy with it, you can publish it directly from Repaint and connect a custom domain.. . . . . . Do I need an existing website? No. Rebuilding a live site is the easiest way to get started, but Repaint can also use your social profiles, files, or just a text prompt. It's designed to work with whatever you have, so you don't have to prepare anything.. . . . . . How is Repaint different from redesigning in my current platform? Most website builders were designed for manual editing. Their AI features have to work inside products that weren't built for AI. That makes them slower and limits what they can do. Repaint was built around AI from the beginning. It makes better use of your existing content and lets you edit anything by chatting with AI.. . . . . . Will my site look generic or obviously AI-made? Potentially, but it's easy to avoid. The reputation for generic AI websites comes from older models. Back in 2025, they produced similar designs no matter what you asked for. Newer models follow instructions much better, and Repaint designs around your existing content and brand. If you still don't like the style, one or two prompts can usually fix it.. . . . . . What platforms can Repaint import from? Repaint imports directly from your live website, so it works with any public site no matter how it was built. You don't need access to the original platform. All you need is the link. People commonly migrate from Wix, WordPress, Squarespace, GoDaddy, Webflow, and custom-coded sites.. . . . . . Can I keep my existing domain? Yes. You can connect a domain from any registrar to your Repaint website. If you ask in chat, Repaint can walk you through the process for your provider. Connecting a custom domain requires a paid Repaint plan .. . . . . . Will I lose my SEO rankings when I migrate? Not if you're careful. Protecting your rankings means keeping the same content at the same URLs. You can ask Repaint to compare the new site with the original. It can make sure no content went missing and no URLs changed. Any website migration carries some SEO risk, but Repaint makes this review much faster and more thorough. You can find our guide on SEO migrations here .. . . . . . Can Repaint handle large sites? There's no hard size limit for websites in Repaint. It can handle sites with thousands of pages. The practical constraint is your weekly AI usage limit. If you're creating hundreds of pages, you'll likely need a higher subscription tier and additional usage credits to finish your site, so budget accordingly.. . . . . . What if I don't like the site Repaint builds? It's only the first draft. You can keep making changes with AI until you like it. If you ask, Repaint can completely redo the style. It often works well to use screenshots or links to other websites as visual references. Because Repaint can make sweeping changes in minutes, it's much easier to iterate than in most website builders.. . . . . . Can I make an ecommerce store in Repaint? Repaint can build a storefront, but it doesn't include inventory management or payment processing. If you want to sell through your website, you'll need to integrate a checkout system or link to another platform that handles sales.. . . . . . How much does Repaint cost? It's free to generate a website, make edits, and publish on a Repaint subdomain without paying or entering a card. Paid plans start at $20/month billed annually, or $25 month to month. They let you connect a custom domain, remove Repaint branding, and raise your weekly AI usage limit. Most people start free and upgrade when they're ready to launch on their own domain. See the pricing page for full details.. . . . . . How do usage limits work? Each Repaint plan includes a weekly AI usage limit. Everything the AI does for you counts toward it, and the limit resets every seven days. Paid plans include more usage and let you buy additional credits if you run out.. . . . . . . . . . . .
