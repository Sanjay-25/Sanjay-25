Bring your own professionals, or work with ours.. . . . . . . . Our Story. . The story behind Elayne’s mission.. At Elayne, we talk about the one thing most people avoid: death. It’s universal, inevitable, and deeply personal. Yet the systems around it are cold, complex, and purposely difficult to navigate. ‍. . I know because I've been there. . . I launched Elayne after losing my mom, Ildikó. She was a trailblazer long before I even knew what that meant. She grew up in Hungary and moved to the U.S. in her twenties with $100 in her pocket, barely speaking English, and a dream of becoming a doctor. She became the kind of doctor who made her patients feel safe, seen, and deeply cared for.‍. . But even more than that, she became an extraordinary mother. In her early 50s, she was diagnosed with stage four lung cancer, despite being a lifelong non-smoker. During her five-year battle, all we cared about was keeping her comfortable and holding onto more time; estate planning wasn’t even on my radar.. . But after she passed, I found myself drowning in a maze of paperwork, phone calls, legal questions, and financial decisions. I was grieving and exhausted, and even with access to lawyers and savings, the post loss process was still completely overwhelming. . . I kept thinking: if this is what it’s like for me, how is anyone else supposed to manage? And why do I feel so alone in this process? . . . That was the moment I knew I had to build something better. . . At Elayne, we’re on a mission to create a new standard for how families everywhere prepare for and navigate loss. Elayne combines the latest AI with human compassion to make estate planning and settlements feel accessible, intuitive, and empowering – especially during the moments when clarity is hardest to find.. . ‍ We help you get organized before a crisis and walk alongside you after one.. .
