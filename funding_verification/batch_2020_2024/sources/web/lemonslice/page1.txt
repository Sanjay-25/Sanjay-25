We are building digital characters that you connect with.. . . As featured in. . . We're an AI research lab that built the world's first Character World Model. What is a Character World Model? A video world model is a new type of foundational AI model that generates video that is interactive, persistent, real-time, and physically accurate. For example, a playable videogame where every frame is generated in real-time by a diffusion model in response to the player's button clicks rather than pre-programmed within a 3D game engine.. LemonSlice trains video world models that can simulate the world, but are heavily optimized for interactive conversation. This is called a Character World Model: expressive, digital characters that talk, listen, and react in real-time.. LemonSlice released the world's first Character World Model in April 2025 with LemonSlice-1. Our latest model — LemonSlice-2.1 — is a 20B end-to-end diffusion transformer video model that renders every pixel at 20fps on a single GPU.. In contrast to deep-fake or generative approaches to avatars, Character World Models enable any type of character, hand-object interactions, movement within physical spaces, moving backgrounds, and more.
