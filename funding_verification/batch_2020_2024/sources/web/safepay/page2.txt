Safepay's unwavering commitment to innovation drives us to continually enhance payment experiences for businesses and consumers across Pakistan. We are dedicated to helping Pakistani businesses integrate into the global economy by simplifying payment acceptance. As a regulated Payment System Operator/Provider Service Provider (PSO/PSP) with a Commercial license from the State Bank and backed by Y Combinator and Stripe, Safepay collaborates with merchants to deliver a superior payments experience beyond what is currently available in the Pakistani market.. . At Safepay, we are more than just a payment service provider; we are the catalyst for a new era of digital payments in Pakistan. Join us in shaping the future of payments, one secure and seamless transaction at a time.. . . . . . . . . Company milestones. . . . . . . . 2025. Commercial License. Officially granted a Full Commercial License by the State Bank of Pakistan after rigorous evaluation and successful pilot operations.. . . . 2024. Payment Orchestration. First Pakistani fintech to build and offer payment orchestration allowing merchants to leverage their existing relationships with payment partners. . . . 2024. Embedded Checkout 2.0. Redesigned the UI and UX for embedded checkout, Introduced one-click checkout allowing for faster purchases for repeat customers. . . . 2024. Shopify Onsite. First and only Pakistani payment platform with an approved Shopify onsite payment app. No more redirects, no more lost orders.. . . . 2023. Innovative Product Development. We introduced key enhancements, such as Embedded Checkout, Quick Links, Tokenization and Subscriptions. . . . 2022. Live. Launched our proprietary technology and started onboarding merchants for live operations using its hosted checkout and plugins for e-commerce. . . . 2022. Approval for Pilot test granted. In August, 2022 State Bank of Pakistan approved Safepay for the 2nd stage of its Commercial license process. . . . 2021. Development. Safepay started hiring resources and building its own technology from the ground up to serve Merchants & Customers. . . . 2021. In Principle Approval. State Bank of Pakistan granted Safepay the initial approval to go ahead and start operations in June, 2021. . . . 2020. Funding.
