What does Ontra help transit agencies do?. Ontra combines travel demand modeling, optimization, and simulation to generate service options such as fixed routes, on-demand service zones, and frequency changes. Agencies can compare scenarios, understand tradeoffs, and build phased implementation plans while keeping planners in control of what moves forward.. What service decisions can Ontra help us make?. Ontra helps agencies generate and evaluate fixed routes, on-demand service zones, frequency, service span, and policy changes. For a given scenario, the platform generates service options, searches across various combinations, and recommends service changes based on the agency’s objectives and constraints. This helps planners compare the tradeoffs for each scenario, empowering them to make better decisions.. How does Ontra use AI?. Ontra uses travel-demand modeling, optimization, and simulation to generate and compare service scenarios. Agency staff set the budget, fleet, service standards, operational constraints, and policy goals. Ontra shows the projected impacts of each scenario, while planners decide what to refine, present, or implement.. . . . Start with one question. What would you change about your transit network?.
