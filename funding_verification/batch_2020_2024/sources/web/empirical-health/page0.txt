Get started . . . Heart health is a solved problem . Biomedically, we know how to prevent heart disease. We can measure the risk factors , we know which ones matter, and we have effective interventions for nearly all of them. Yet heart disease remains the #1 cause of death worldwide, killing more people than all cancers combined. We started Empirical as a heart health company because this gap between what medicine knows and what people actually do is the biggest solvable problem in healthcare. . . <5% lifetime risk . People who keep their risk factors controlled—blood pressure, ApoB , blood sugar, inflammation —have less than a 5% lifetime chance of developing heart disease. The science is clear. The gap is execution. . . 2,002 decisions per year . Heart health isn't one big choice. It's thousands of small ones. . 2 major decisions. What to test—the right biomarkers , at the right frequency. And what medications , if any, to take. These two decisions set the trajectory. . . 2,000 minor decisions. Every meal . Espresso vs. drip coffee. Every workout . The daily choices that compound into decades of heart health—or decades of risk. . . . . Value-based care and AI flip the economics of prevention . Value-based care and AI make it possible to build a heart health company that delivers comprehensive, personalized care to everyone, not just the wealthy. . .
