How can I ensure the AI does not make things up (“hallucinate”)?. . . . The Letter AI platform mitigates the risk of generating inaccurate or "hallucinated" content by anchoring its responses in your company's knowledge base, even declining to produce content when it cannot verify the necessary facts. This approach is underpinned by best-in-class AI engineering practices, a testament to the founding team's more than 20 years of experience in the field of artificial intelligence. On the very rare occasion that a hallucination slips through, the platform incorporates feedback loops that allow for the immediate reporting and correction of the issue, ensuring continual improvement and accuracy.. . . . . . .
