Frequently Asked Questions. . 01 We’ve had bad experiences with software developers overpromising and underdelivering. How is LaunchFlow different? Traditional developers often take a static requirements doc, build in isolation, and force you to accept whatever comes out, making future changes a battle. LaunchFlow works directly with your frontline staff, building one screen and one workflow at a time. We show progress early, listen without defensiveness, and keep refining the software until the details match how your team actually works.. 02 Are you going to force us to throw away our existing systems or replace our database? No. Your core databases and partner platforms (like SAP, Microsoft, etc.) remain the official sources of record. LaunchFlow layers over your existing stack, pulling disparate data sources into a single workspace so your team can work without switching between disconnected systems.. 03 What happens to all our historical data and side spreadsheets? We preserve your data history and bring those side processes into the core system. Rather than leaving staff to manage fragile Excel sheets or manual logs on shared servers, we turn those side tools into integrated calculators and automated checks directly inside your workspace.. 04 How do we transition without risking downtime or billing errors? We don’t do risky “cutover” launches. Old and new tools can run side by side in the LaunchFlow Workspace. Your team can test, compare results, and verify calculation accuracy on live work until everyone is completely confident before fully switching over.. 05 What is the LaunchFlow Workspace, and why do we need it instead of just another app? Most software forces staff to jump between docs, spreadsheets, shared drives, database forms, and other disconnected tools. The LaunchFlow Workspace keeps everything related to one piece of work, the notes, research, spreadsheets, images, and tools, grouped in a single tab. Your team never has to go somewhere else to complete a job.. . 06 How does LaunchFlow handle long-running cases or staff absences? When work is paused for months or a team member goes on leave, context is usually lost. LaunchFlow captures the breadcrumbs, the exact notes, files, research, and logic behind decisions, so anyone can pick up a case a year later or cover for a teammate without redoing their work from scratch.. 07 Do we need to be AI experts or change how our team operates to use this? Not at all. We build the software around your established processes. Our AI automations run in the background and on the side as a helper, speeding up search, generating reports, or catching data issues before errors happen.. 08 How much strain will this project place on our internal staff during development? We design the engagement around your team’s schedule and augment our work together using custom feedback tools that simplify sharing and iterating on feedback. Instead of demanding long, exhausting specification meetings, we embed with your team during normal operations to observe workflows, fix daily annoyances live, and validate improvements in low-stress check-ins at your preferred cadence.. 09 What happens if we need small bug fixes or tweaks after launch? Will we be locked out? You receive the same attention after launch as during the build. We aim to keep the same engineers who built your project on standby for support and more iterations.. 10 Is our data secure and compliant with local government and enterprise privacy standards? Yes. LaunchFlow is fully self-hosted in your cloud. Your data, customer records, and internal notes never leave your private network.. . . . We’ll help you launch your new workflow.
