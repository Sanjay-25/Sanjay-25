Cinder offers custom AI agents, hash-matching, and a configurable rules engine. Unlike off-the-shelf classifiers, Cinder's AI agents adapt to your specific policies and learn continuously from both their mistakes and successes. AI agents also adapt to your schema, so you can have them examine analytical units that actually make sense: an image, an entire conversation, a full user profile (with all its history), even a network of related entities.. . . . 02 How flexible is Cinder's data model? . Cinder's schema is very flexible. You define your schema in our UI, setting up entities, attributes, and the relationships between them. We use that structure to define how we render your entities in review settings and to enable appropriately-permissioned human decision-makers and AI agents to traverse that graph for the context necessary to make accurate decisions.. . . . 03 Will Cinder help me reduce my BPO spend? . Yes. Cinder customers improve their Trust & Safety outcomes and improve efficiency at the same time. Our policy-aligned AI agents allow more content to be actioned automatically and produce far fewer false positives than off-the-shelf classifiers. Our integrated platform improves average handle time by 75% over the legacy systems still widely used in industry, and we will serve as your BPO, pricing on outcomes, not by the hour.. . . . 04 Is integrating Cinder hard? . No. It's an API call to push data in and, for most customers, a webhook to get data out. Once integration is complete, a non-technical user can configure just about everything on their own: build queues, change policies, adjust workflows, shift actioning thresholds on AI agents, and more. All of that in the UI. The idea is to let your experts configure the system and free up your engineers to work on other problems.. . . . 05 Can I use my own Agents in Cinder? . Yes. Cinder orchestrates any agent. Bring your own agent, utilize ours, or a mix. Bring your in-house classifiers, custom LLM workflows, or third-party models, and run them alongside Cinder's pre-built agents in a single platform. You get unified case management, human-in-the-loop review, policy versioning, audit logs, and analytics across every agent, without rebuilding the workflow infrastructure around them every time you want to launch a new agent.. . . . 06 What languages does Cinder support? .
