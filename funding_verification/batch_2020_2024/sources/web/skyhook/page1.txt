About Us We're building the future of DevOps . Awesome developer experience like a PaaS, without the lock-in and limitations.. The power of Kubernetes, without the complexity.. . . . Our Mission. Skyhook was founded by engineers who lived the problem—at Google, eToro, Armis, and OneZero. We spent years building and scaling platforms, and kept seeing the same challenge: Kubernetes is incredibly powerful, but that power comes with complexity that slows teams down.. We built Skyhook to bridge the gap—giving developers the autonomy to ship fast, while giving Ops the guardrails and visibility they need.
