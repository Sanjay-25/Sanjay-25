Our mission More food-secure, less wasteful future. . Today, the world produces enough food to feed an extra 1 billion people, yet 13.5% of U.S. households are food insecure — and roughly 30% of all food produced is thrown out. Food waste has huge environmental repercussions: it accounts for about 8% of global greenhouse gas emissions. In grocery stores, billions of dollars worth of food is left unsold and thrown away, accounting for 15% of America's total food waste. Yet it's often treated as inevitable — demand for each item swings so much that overstocking can feel unavoidable. But what if we could forecast demand accurately, so stores know exactly how many items they need? What if we could build a future where more people can get the food they need — and far less food goes to waste? That's the future we're excited to bring to reality. . . . . . Our story It all started with guacamole. . Euro's (our CEO's) favorite guacamole was always out of stock at his local supermarket, and one day at a different store, he found a mountain of guacamole that was about to expire. Realizing that grocers were missing potential sales and throwing out tons of food at the same time, he mentioned the problem to his friend, Jack (our CTO), who was doing AI research on optimizing grocery logistics. They discovered how costly bad forecasting was for retailers' bottom lines, but also to food security, the environment, and animal welfare — causes that they both deeply care about. So, they decided to leverage Jack's AI research to solve this problem and help grocers solve food waste profitably. Today, Guac is working with some of the most forward-thinking grocery retailers to reimagine their fresh operations and cut food waste — and making sure the perfect amount of guacamole is stocked. . . . . . .
