Everything you need for press coverage, analyst reports, partnerships, or social mentions.. About BlueHill. BlueHill is a unified customer operations platform for customer-facing teams. Founded in 2022 and backed by Y Combinator, BlueHill replaces generic project-management tools (Monday.com, ClickUp, Asana, Notion) with customer-aware workflows: onboarding templates with relative-date scheduling, project boards, task tracking, native time tracking with billing reports, email integration, interaction timeline, internal notes, role-based access, and a branded customer portal.. BlueHill serves customer success teams, support teams, agencies, professional services firms, and SaaS implementation teams — typically 5–100 customer-facing people.. .
