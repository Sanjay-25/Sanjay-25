Find out more . . . . . . . . . . 01 What is AI sales? AI sales is the use of artificial intelligence to run and accelerate the sales process — from finding the right accounts to writing and sending personalized outreach. Instead of reps spending most of their day on manual research, list-building, and follow-up, AI handles the repetitive prospecting work and surfaces the conversations worth a human's time. The goal isn't to remove salespeople, but to let them focus on selling. See how it works in our sales AI guide .. . . 02 What is the difference between an AI sales platform and a sales engagement tool? A sales engagement tool automates execution — it sends the sequences and logs activity, but a human still decides who to target and what to say. An AI sales platform goes further: it makes decisions across the workflow, finding accounts based on real buying signals, qualifying them, and drafting context-aware messages. The shift is from "a tool that sends what I build" to "a system that builds and runs outbound with me." Topo combines both layers in one place.. . . 03 How does AI improve the outbound sales process? AI improves outbound by removing the manual bottlenecks that slow reps down and by improving timing and relevance. In practice it works across three stages: Targeting — surfacing accounts that match your ICP and show buying intent right now. Personalization — drafting messages based on real context instead of generic templates. Throughput — running follow-ups and enrichment continuously, without a rep doing data entry. The result is more qualified pipeline from the same headcount. Explore the tactics in our AI outbound prospecting guide .. . . 04 What should you look for in an AI sales platform? Look beyond automation and evaluate whether the platform actually drives qualified pipeline. The factors that matter most: Signal-based targeting — does it find in-market accounts, or just blast a static list?. Data quality — are contacts verified and enriched, or full of bounces?. Deliverability — is email infrastructure managed so messages reach the inbox?. Control — can you review and edit what the AI sends before it goes out?. Topo covers all four in a single platform, so you don't stitch together five separate tools.. . . 05 What is Topo? Topo is an all-in-one outbound platform for B2B sales teams. It brings prospecting, intent signals, multichannel sequences, verified data, and email infrastructure into one place — then layers an AI sales agent on top to handle the repetitive work. The agent monitors buying signals, builds and enriches lead lists, qualifies prospects, and feeds them into personalized sequences — while your team stays in control of strategy, messaging, and closing.. . . 06 What can Topo's AI sales agent do for my team? Topo's AI sales agent handles the repetitive parts of outbound so reps spend less time on admin and more time selling. It monitors intent signals, scrapes and enriches data, pre-qualifies leads, builds prospecting lists, and refills sequences automatically — then surfaces the hottest leads to your team with a clear reason to reach out. You set the ICP, messaging, and guardrails; the agent drafts within them, and reps review each message before it goes out.
