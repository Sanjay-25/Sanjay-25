Why does it look like my avatar is walking in slow motion in Playground?. . . This is usually a performance issue — your device or browser is struggling to render the Playground smoothly. Two things to try, in order:. Lower the Playground quality. Inside the Koala Go classroom, open the menu (the three horizontal lines in the top right) and go to Settings . In the Playground section, change Playground Quality to Low (square blocks + no shadows) . You'll be prompted to restart the Playground for the change to take effect.. Turn on hardware acceleration in your browser. This is on by default but sometimes gets disabled. In Chrome, paste chrome://settings/system into the address bar and toggle on "Use hardware acceleration when available" , then restart the browser.. If avatars still walk in slow motion after both changes, your device may be below our minimum requirements — please reach out to support@teachwithkoala.com .. . . . . I already have a Stripe account. Can I use this existing one or do I need to create a new one?. . . Unfortunately, you'll need to create a new Stripe account with us.. . . . . Why haven’t I received my payment yet on Stripe?. . . It can take up to a week for automatic payouts. You can request a payout immediately on your Stripe dashboard.. . . . . If I sell an activity on the marketplace, what's the total fee?. . . Koala takes a 15% fee from Koala Go marketplace sales and Stripe takes a flat fee of $0.30 and 2.9%.. Below is an example for an activity that creator A sells on the marketplace for $24.. The buyer pays $24 with a credit card, then:. Stripe will take 30 cents flat fee + 2.9% of $24 => Stripe fee = $1. Koala will take 15% of $24 which is $3.6. Creator A gets the rest which is 24-3.6-1 = $19.4. Note: If buyer is outside of the US, Stripe takes 3.9% instead of 2.9%. . . . . . .
