Stream Raises $5.3M Seed Round, Bringing Total Funding to $6.8M. August 14, 2025. . . Today, we’re excited to announce that Stream has raised a $5.3M seed round led by Spark Capital and joined by Acrew Capital and TTV Capital. The round also included participation from angel investors Sam Hodges, co-founder and CEO of Vouch Insurance and Mike Rosengarten, former co-founder and CTO of OpenGov. This new infusion of capital – combined with our $1.5M pre-seed backing from Y Combinator, NevCaut Ventures, TTV Capital, and Jonathan Crystal — brings the company’s total funding to $6.8M.. . Increased demand from major insurance carriers, IMEs, and Managed Care companies. Growing our team to scale with demand. ‍. Transforming Medical Document Management. Since our founding in 2022, Stream’s AI-powered platform has helped clients in workers’ compensation efficiently process massive medical records and extract crucial insights. With this latest round of funding, we’re poised to continue building the future of document understanding.. ‍. Stream offers three flagship products built on over a year of customer validation and testing:. Chrono – Real Time Document Processing Platform. Chrono is our AI-powered platform that allows clients to take full control of their document processing. Through an easy-to-use portal, users can upload medical records, segment documents, review AI-generated summaries, and finalize outputs according to their specific needs. Chrono gives power back to the client, allowing them to process their documents on their own terms.. Chrono+ – Full-Service Medical Chronologies & Summaries. . For clients looking for a hands-off approach, Chrono+ is our full-service offering. Simply upload PDFs and we take care of the rest. Our team of medical and insurance experts deliver curated chronologies within 72 hours, providing actionable summaries tailored to each medical professional’s specialty and needs.. Sift – Document Deduplication and Decluttering. . One of the biggest pain points in managing medical records is dealing with duplicate or irrelevant pages. Sift automatically detects and removes redundant information, drastically decreasing page count and leaving only the most pertinent data. This not only saves time, but also lowers the costs associated with processing excessive paperwork.. ‍. . Stream Co-Founders Eilam Levitov, CTO, and Eric Yen, CEO.
