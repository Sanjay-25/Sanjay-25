About Us. . . Meet the team transforming the construction industry. . . . Construction is a $12T industry that accounts for 13% of global GDP, but it’s been one of the slowest to adopt modern software and improve productivity.. . We founded Agave in 2021 with a clear mission: to fix the way data moves across the construction industry, so contractors can operate more efficiently.. . . . . . . . Our Values. . How we operate. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . We work backward from the customer. We’re customer obsessed, and avoid chasing shiny ideas by tying every roadmap decision to real customer commitment. . . How we do this?. . . Our roadmap is prioritized only by problems customers will pay to solve.. . We pre-sell new features, and build once N customers commit to paying.. . Engineers join customer calls regularly so they hear and understand customer needs first-hand.. . We treat internal teams as customers too — sometimes the highest ROI is making our own teammates’ work faster or easier.. . . . . We operate in the open. If you’ve ever wondered how to build a startup, you’ll get a front-row seat here. No matter your role, you’ll have access to everything — from financials to product plans to hiring decisions.. . How we do this?. . . We have thousands of internal docs that track nearly every decision since day one, and anyone can read, comment on, or challenge them.. . We set monthly goals and review them weekly, alongside sprint planning, sprint reviews, sales/post-sales recaps, recruiting updates, and team demos.. . Our KPI sheets and business metrics are open to the whole team, so anyone can see how we’re performing in real time.. . When we make a mistake or learn something new, we talk about it openly — sharing the tradeoffs and lessons learned with the broader team. . . . . . We take high. ownership. No one here is boxed in by their title. At Agave, you can wear multiple hats, shape priorities across the business, and lead high-impact initiatives from idea to launch.. . How we do this?. . . Teammates lead cross-functional projects outside their “core” role.. . Everyone owns monthly goals that ladder up to the company’s top priorities — if you’re hitting your goals, you’re moving the company forward in a visible way.. . No heavy hierarchy — everyone’s a hands-on builder with the autonomy to act and the freedom to build.. . . . . . . . . . . . . .
