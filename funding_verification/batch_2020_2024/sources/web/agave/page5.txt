San Francisco, CA — 12/16/2025 . ‍ Agave, the construction industry’s leading data-integration platform, is thrilled to announce an expanded partnership with Sage, the leader in accounting, financial, HR, and payroll technology for small and mid-sized businesses. This expansion makes Agave one of only two companies globally with access to exclusive APIs for securely moving critical business data in and out of Sage’s construction ERPs.. The new partnership significantly expands Agave’s access to APIs for Sage 100 Contractor, Sage 300 Construction and Real Estate (CRE), and Sage Intacct, enabling Agave to deliver deeper data integrations and offer even more connectivity for Sage’s growing customer base, Independent Software Vendors (ISVs), and Sage’s Value-Added Resellers (VARs). The expansion solidifies Agave as one of the most trusted and preferred integration partners across Sage’s entire suite of construction accounting tools.. ‍ Delivering More Options for Sage Customers and VARs . ‍ For Sage customers and VARs, Agave’s expanded partnership creates broader and deeper integrations,. Customers can now rely on Agave to connect more data from their Sage ERPs with popular applications they use in tandem, like project management systems and field service solutions. Agave’s expanded access spans core financials, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and workforce management data types, including APIs reserved exclusively for Sage’s most-trusted partners. Sage VARs can offer more tightly integrated product suites alongside Sage ERPs, giving customers simpler, more automated workflows out of the box from fewer trusted vendors.For contractors running Sage, the partnership directly improves the capabilities of Agave’s Sync product. With broader API access, Sync can automate more workflows (like syncing Prime Contracts, Owner Change Orders, and Timesheets), and ensure more accurate financial records between systems in real-time, including Job Costs, Vendors, Bills, Invoices, Commitments, and Payments.. ‍ Accelerating Innovation for Sage ISVs and Technology Partners . ‍ ISVs building on Sage’s platforms can now integrate with Sage’s construction solutions more quickly and easily through Agave’s unified API. Agave simplifies authentication, normalizes data, and abstracts the differences across each system it supports. By removing these barriers, the partnership makes it easier for ISVs to deliver high-quality, reliable integrations to their customers and creates more opportunities for innovation across the Sage technology ecosystem. . Quote From Agave . “Contractors rely on Sage ERPs every day to run their businesses, and reliable integrations are essential to keeping teams aligned and project financials under control. We’re thrilled to deepen our partnership with Sage and accelerate the delivery of the API access and integrations that ISVs, VARs, and contractors have long been asking for, and very much need.” . -- Tom Reno, CEO, Agave . Quote From Sage . "For more than 50 years, Sage has been a trusted technology partner for the construction industry. That trust is built on innovation and collaboration. We're proud to work with partners like Agave to deliver the seamless integrations our customers need to run their businesses more efficiently. This expanded partnership reflects our ongoing commitment to helping contractors connect their critical systems and focus on what they do best: building." . -- Dustin Stephens, VP of Construction and Real Estate, Sage . About Agave . Agave ( useagave.com ) is a leading data integration platform for the global construction industry. Thousands of contractors across North America use Agave’s Sync product to keep field and finance teams aligned, reducing manual work, improving financial accuracy, and enabling more profitable, efficient growth. Software vendors rely on Agave’s unified API to access normalized, high-quality ERP data and integrate with construction systems through a single, stable connection. Backed by YCombinator and Accel, Agave offers integrations with 13 ERPs in both cloud and on-premise environments. Agave’s products power millions of automated data flows every day.. . . . . .
