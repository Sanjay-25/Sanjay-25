Become the world's leading supplier of advanced electric motors by 2030 to:. enable commercial electric aviation and regional air mobility at scale. bolster national security . decarbonize the marine and industrial sectors. . . . . . . Values. . . At the core of our organization are principles that guide every action and decision we make. These values define our story, our problem-solving approach, and are the foundation for how we operate as a company.. 01. Be relentless.. . . . Never give up; persevere until the job is done. Be action-biased; run towards problems instead of away from them. Grit is key.. . 02. Gain insight through humility.. . . . Approach every challenge with a learner’s mindset. Be open to feedback, listen to others, and deepen your understanding through self-awareness. Put your ego aside and always seek truth in discussion with others. This is particularly crucial for effective leadership.. . 03. Distill and simplify.. . . . Eliminate unjustified complexity and simplify wherever possible, whether in requirements, design, or execution. This applies to communication and decision-making as well. Use first principles thinking and distill each facet of a problem to its essence. Less is more.. . 04. Find your flow.. . . . Engage fully and work with intention, finding the rhythm and deep focus that leads to doing your best work. Discover your Ikigai - where your strengths, passions, and purpose drive both personal fulfillment and team success. When you're "in the zone", that is when the magic happens.. . 05. SEND (Solve, Evolve, Nail it, Deliver). . . . Devise the best solution with the information at hand, test it, learn from it, iterate quickly, and then nail it. Don't expect it to work perfectly the first time, but you should never encounter the same issue twice. Laser-focus on solving customer problems and deliver solutions that provide real value for them.. . . . . . . . . Leadership. . . CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER. . . . Jason Sylvestre is an engineer, designer, and entrepreneur. As CEO, Jason leads the overall business and is responsible for supercharging growth. Under his leadership, H3X has launched seven industry-leading products, expanded to over 35 employees, and secured firm orders from the best aerospace and defense companies in the world. He has successfully raised over $30M in funding from top-tier investors including Lockheed Martin, Y Combinator, and Infinite Capital. He holds a BS and MS in Electrical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin-Madison with a specific focus on power electronics. As an avid outdoorsman, Jason spends his free time in the mountains of Colorado, skiing and hiking.. . . . . Max Liben. Chief Technology Officer. . . .
