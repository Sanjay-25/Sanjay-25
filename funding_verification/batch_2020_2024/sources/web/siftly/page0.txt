Set up a workspace with your brand name and domain, then create 3-5 prompts that reflect real customer questions. Our AI will analyze how your brand appears across multiple AI platforms to provide visibility insights.. . . How does Siftly generate GEO-optimized content? Siftly's AI analyzes what makes content rank well in AI responses, then generates high-performing content optimized for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). Our content generation workflow ensures your content is structured, cited, and formatted to maximize AI visibility.. . . How does Experimentation work for AI optimization? Run experiments to test different content strategies across AI platforms. Compare control vs optimized content performance, measure statistical significance, and identify which approaches drive the highest AI mention rates and visibility.. . . . Can I segment visibility by model, platform, and location? Yes. Filter your results by AI model (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), geographic location, and specific topics. This allows you to compare performance across different segments - for example, ChatGPT vs Perplexity responses, or US vs UK visibility.. . . What are brand visibility vs. source visibility metrics? Brand visibility tracks when AI responses directly mention your brand name. Source visibility shows when your website or content is cited as a source. Together, these metrics help you understand both brand recognition and content authority in AI responses.. . . Is there a free trial?
