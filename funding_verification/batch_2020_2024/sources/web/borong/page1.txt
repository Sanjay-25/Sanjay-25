Borong operates three interconnected layers of procurement infrastructure: Borong Procure for enterprise eProcurement workflows and compliance, Borong Marketplace for access to 37.9K vetted suppliers across core business categories, and Borong Credit for embedded trade financing backed by RM1.1B in credit facilities. Running across all three layers is MIDAS, our AI-powered price benchmarking and spend intelligence engine that ensures prices are within market benchmarks from the point of purchase.. . Borong Procure Borong Marketplace Borong Credit MIDAS . . . Built on Neutral Infrastructure. Borong is a platform facilitator. We connect buyers with suppliers and do not compete with sellers by buying or reselling products ourselves. This means your spend data, supplier relationships, and contract terms are never used to inform our own commercial decisions or shared with other parties on the platform.. This independence matters most in price benchmarking. Because Borong does not resell, MIDAS price intelligence reflects genuine market conditions - not a margin calculation.
