Andi built its own infrastructure from scratch — Trantora, a proprietary AI index that analyzes full page content for meaning and credibility. How does Andi AI work? + Andi uses Trantora — a proprietary AI index of billions of web pages that analyzes meaning and credibility, scoring 450+ data points per page.
