Our Vision: Modernizing India's Business Finance. . At Cashbook, we envision a future where managing business expenses in India is seamless, transparent, and instantly reconcilable. We're building the foundation for next-generation finance, making archaic methods like cash advances, paper receipts, and manual reconciliations a relic of the past.. We believe the backbone of India's business expense management won't be traditional cards or complex spreadsheets – it will be UPI. India's widely adopted payment infrastructure, reaching from tier-3 towns to enterprise vendors, offers unparalleled reach. Cashbook is built natively on this powerful system, reimagining expense management from the ground up for speed, interoperability, and scale.. . . . . . What We Do. . Cashbook is India's first UPI wallet platform purpose-built for efficient employee and vendor expense management. . . Instant Disbursements. . Disburse UPI wallets to staff instantly – eliminating the need for corporate cards or cash advances.. . . . . . . . Real-Time Control. . Set real-time spend limits, category controls, and approval flows for every transaction.. . . . . . . . Intelligent Dashboards. . Track every rupee spent with intelligent dashboards and granular insights for smarter financial decisions.. . . . . . . . Accounting Integrations. . Automate reconciliations with seamless accounting integrations, saving countless hours.. . . . . . . . . Reinforcing Statement: And all of this with no hidden fees, no MDR shocks, and no merchant limitations.. . . . Our Mission: Powering India's Business Growth. . We are on a mission to empower every Indian business – from startups to field-heavy enterprises – to manage expenses with the same simplicity, speed, and security as sending a UPI payment. Join us in shaping the future of business finance.. . . . Who We Are. .
