When you donate clothes to a charity shop, most of them travel to Pakistan, India, or Africa to be sorted by hand, one item at a time. The ones worth selling get shipped back to stock vintage shops in the UK and Europe. The ones that aren't get burned or buried or sold for pennies.. That double journey exists because the technology to sort clothing locally has never existed. Fleek is changing that. We're the #1 vintage wholesale marketplace connecting resellers and retailers with secondhand suppliers around the world. Our technology grades, prices, and categorises clothing from a single photo.. Since 2022 we've helped save nine million items from landfill. We're a small, fast-moving team. If you want to work on something that actually matters, you're in the right place.
