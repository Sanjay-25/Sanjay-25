We’re building the future of project management—AI-first, product-driven, and deeply technical. As an engineer at Dart, you’ll:. Work on challenging technical problems across AI, infra, performance, and UX. Move quickly and ship often—tight feedback loops, high autonomy. Use the latest LLMs and modern frontend/backend stacks. Get top of local market salary and equity that scales with ownership. Enjoy fully remote work with flexible hours and a home office stipend. Have Pro access to cutting-edge AI tools—for work and for fun. Join regular offsites, virtual events, and a playful, sharp team culture. Take the time you need with unlimited PTO. Engineering Culture. Lean and fast-moving team with high ownership and virtually no red tape. Collaborate directly with experienced leaders on core product decisions. Rapid iteration is our default—we ship fast learn fast and evolve constantly. We operate with high trust and high standards valuing autonomy, curiosity, and follow-through. We use Dart every day to shape and refine the product through real-world experience. We’re looking for engineers who are product-minded, pragmatic, and excited by hard work, speed, scale, and impact—not just optimizing tickets.. The Product. Dart is the only truly AI-native project management tool. We help teams:. Chat with their tasks and docs as if they were teammates. Use agents to automate work. Plan intelligently, break down tasks, auto-generate reports, detect duplicates, and much more. We eliminate routine project management work—saving teams 7+ hours/week—and help them focus on execution.. We use the latest LLMs, a modern Python/Django/Vue/TypeScript stack, and tight integrations across the toolchain to build an experience that feels magical and snappy. The product is already loved by tens of thousands of teams.. The Company. Dart was founded in 2022 in San Francisco and is growing quickly. Backed by strong funding and real revenue, we’ve assembled a tight-knit team of ~10 full-time employees, including five engineers distributed across the globe.. In 2025 alone, Dart has significantly expanded both its revenue and its team. It’s an exciting moment to come on board—our rapid growth means early team members will have real opportunities to lead as we scale..
