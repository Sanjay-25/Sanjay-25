Intrinsic value is typically calculated using discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis: project future free cash flows, discount them back to present value using an appropriate rate, and add terminal value. Stock Unlock's DCF tool automates the math, while you focus on the assumptions. . Alternative methods include earnings multiples (P/E compared to growth rate), dividend discount models for income stocks, or asset-based valuation for holding companies.
