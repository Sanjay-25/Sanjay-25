Shaped is backed by Y Combinator. We’re excited to announce that Shaped is backed by Y Combinator as part of its Winter 2022 batch!. . Mar 1, 2022. | . 2. min read. . by . Daniel Camilleri. . . . Y Combinator is the most renowned startup accelerator in the world and has backed companies like Airbnb, Stripe, Coinbase, Doordash, Dropbox and now Shaped! . We’re honored to be part of this community of incredible founders and would like to thank our group partners Michael Seibel , Brad Flora , Divya Bhat , and Ashutosh Desai for helping us on our journey.. Our mission is simple – we want to enable all companies to implement world class machine-learning into their products. It shouldn’t just be AI researchers at FAANG that have access to the latest technologies. We’re starting with ranking APIs to help companies increase engagement and retention.. If you need ranking in your product, contact us at hello@shaped.ai - Shaped works great no matter how messy, unorganized or how little data you have..
