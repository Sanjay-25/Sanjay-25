We empower climate-critical sectors to take data-driven action. . . . . . . Our mission. Transition the world’s supply chain to a net-zero economy. . . . CarbonChain measures what matters. Our software provides companies and financial institutions with precise accounting of the emissions caused by making, shipping and using critical commodities and products around the globe.. . . To create a net-zero global supply chain by 2050, companies need accurate emissions data to lower their carbon footprint at every step. In 2019, we set out to build technology that quantifies those greenhouse gas emissions, at scale.. . . We’re laser focused on the hardest-to-track emissions in the highest-emitting supply chains. We’re proud to be raising the standard for carbon accounting in key industries. That’s why we’re trusted by the world’s trade finance, commodities and manufacturing leaders, and backed by top climate-focused investors.. . . . . .
