Today, 3,000 companies process more than 1 trillion event lookups with GrowthBook. From the largest websites to the AI teams defining what’s next, GrowthBook is essential to the modern product tech stack.. . . GrowthBook began when our founders met at Education.com . With Graham as CTO and Jeremy as software architect, they unlocked an experimentation culture that led to 40% revenue growth year-over-year with flat traffic. . Graham and Jeremy established some foundational beliefs. Feature flags were the right way to release. Everything should be an experiment. Teams should be measured on KPIs, not features delivered. When they looked at the market, it was clear that experimentation products weren’t built with product teams in mind. They believed that had to change.. In 2020, Graham and Jeremy co-founded GrowthBook, and built the first warehouse-native, open-source platform for feature flags, experimentation, and product analytics. . . . . . . . . . . . .
