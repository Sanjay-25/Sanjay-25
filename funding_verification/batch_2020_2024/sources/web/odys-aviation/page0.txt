Timeline. . 08 21. Seed Funding Launches R&D. . Odys Aviation emerged from stealth and received $12.4 million in seed funding from a large group of early investors after completing Y Combinator in August 2021. This milestone provided the foundation to assemble a world-class team, accelerate early R&D efforts, and establish Odys as a serious player in the hybrid power generation and VTOL aircraft markets.. . . . 03 22. DoD Contract Wins. . Building on the momentum from the company’s early seed investment, Odys secured its first Phase II contract from the U.S. Department of Defense. This award marked a key validation of the company’s dual-use strategy, recognizing the potential of Odys’ hybrid-electric VTOL technology to serve both commercial and defense applications and accelerate real-world testing.. . . . 09 22. $9B in Early Orders. . The company secured over $9 billion in Letters of Intent and pre-orders for aircraft. This early commercial traction not only validated strong global demand for our dual-use technology but also positioned the company as one of the most commercially credible programs in advanced air mobility.. . . . 12 22. First Full-Scale Prototype Campaign. . Odys launched the first full-scale Laila prototype flight test campaign. This program provided critical real-world data on propulsion and flight characteristics, setting the stage for rapid iteration and proving the team’s ability to build and fly large-scale hardware within the first year of operation.. . . . 06 23. Second Prototype Built. . The second Laila prototype was completed, expanding our test fleet and enabling more rigorous validation of design improvements. Building a second aircraft so quickly underscored our execution velocity and allowed parallel testing, a crucial step toward accelerating certification and commercial readiness.. . . . 11 24. Transition Flight Validated. . Odys successfully completed its full transition flight test program, validating key performance metrics across vertical, transition, and cruise flight modes. This milestone marked the first time the Laila platform operated across its complete flight envelope, proving the technical feasibility of our hybrid-electric VTOL concept.. . . . 12 24. 1MW Generator Breakthrough. . The company also completed the first test of its Alta 1MW generator, a breakthrough in hybrid-electric propulsion. This milestone demonstrated our capability to develop and integrate bleeding edge power systems that scale beyond aviation to aerospace, nuclear power and clean technology, reinforcing its leadership in advanced aerospace and energy innovation.. . . . 09 25. Series A Fuels Global Expansion. . Odys Aviation closed its Series A funding round, raising $26m to accelerate full- scale flight testing, manufacturing readiness and global launch programs. The investment underscored growing market confidence in Odys' dual-use hybrid- electric VTOL technology and provided the resources to scale operations, expand the engineering team, and advance key international launch operations.. . . . 03 26. Honeywell Counter-UAS Aerial Platform. . Laila is selected to serve as the first airborne application of the Honeywell SAMURAI system, introducing a new defensive layer between ground-based sensors and high-end missile defense systems, reducing reliance on costly kinetic defenses while extending protection coverage across vast and remote areas. Laila’s hybrid propulsion system produces enough power to stay in flight for up to eight hours across a 450-mile range, making it the ideal aerial platform for ground-based cUAS systems.. . . . 07 26. ST Engineering Agreement. . Odys is selected by ST Engineering to develop a hybrid-electric propulsion system for their DrN-600 medium-lift cargo UAS. Under the MOU, Odys will serve as the hybrid architecture developer, initial prototype supplier, and scaled production partner for future tentative demands under the DrN-600 EHPS program.. . . . . . . . . . . 3280 Airflite Way. . . . . .
