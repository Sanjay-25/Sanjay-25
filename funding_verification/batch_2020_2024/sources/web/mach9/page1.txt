Mach9 makes AI CAD software for civil engineering, starting with surveying.. . . . What we believe. Before you can change the physical world, you have to know exactly what's already there. Most infrastructure projects start with a survey: a precise drawing of the site as it exists, so engineers can plan what comes next.. There is more infrastructure work than there are surveyors to do it. Firms struggle to hire enough experienced people, and current workflows limit what their teams can take on. The tools used to collect data have changed dramatically. The software used to turn that data into engineering drawings has not kept pace.. We don't think the answer is passing on big projects or asking the same experts to keep doing more by hand. Software should multiply what they can take on. . . . . . . Our story. Mach9 was founded in 2021 by Alex Baikovitz, Haowen Shi, Michael Mong, and Zachary Sussman, four researchers at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute.. Across our founding team, we worked on systems that perceived and navigated the physical world at Tesla, SpaceX, NASA, Apple and Google X.. We started by building reality-capture hardware, but customers taught us that collecting more data wasn't the problem. They were drowning in data they couldn't turn into engineering drawings fast enough.. In 2024, we went all in on software. Today, we're headquartered in San Francisco.. . . MACH9 SF OFFICE. . SCANNED WITH NAVVIS MLX. . . . . . What we do. Mach9 automatically drafts features from raw scans at centimeter-level accuracy and infrastructure scale. Customers upload scans and imagery collected with equipment they already use. Mach9 produces a first draft that surveyors and engineers review, refine, and finalize using our editing tools.. The expert still owns the result. Mach9 changes the starting point. Instead of drawing each object from scratch, the team spends its time on judgment, quality and the details that make each site different.. . . . . . . Mach9 today.
