Four things that have happened since our $17.5M Series A. . . . . . . . . . Everyone is adding AI to the marketing stack. We're building the marketing stack for AI.. . Not just content. Not just personalization. Not just experimentation. Not just delivery. The whole thing.. It's an unconventional bet. But a truly agentic future isn't going to come from bolting AI onto a dozen tools built for a different era. And nobody has time for more SaaS. Pun intended.. More tabs. More plugins. More configuration. More learning curves. More humans stitching together workflows that should just work. The more we build, the more obvious it becomes: AI isn't another layer in the stack.. It's a chance to rethink the stack entirely. From the infrastructure our agents run on to how teams create, decide, personalize, orchestrate, and grow, AI first, end to end. We're building the F1-grade revenue engine for the AI era, and handing marketers the keys.. October 1st. Shhh. . Book a demo → . Found a new home. 🐙. . We have a new office! Always a little nostalgic to look back at how it all started: the chaos in an IKEA shoebox on Market Street, and now, the next chapter in the heart of FiDi. We have kombucha and sunlight to fuel the hustle and ambition, a wall where every visitor draws their own face, and significantly more room to grow into.. The whole move got caught on camera, and it's worth the 80 seconds.. Watch the office move short → . "The people behind all of it.". We're growing. And hiring the best of the best.. . People often ask us, "Am I too early for this role?" Our answer: depends. Do you want to be part of the rocket ship? We're defying what "early" looks like, and looking for people who want to build with us, in person, in San Francisco, across engineering and go-to-market.. Right now that means engineers who want to build the infrastructure this new stack runs on, and go-to-market folks who love talking to marketers as much as we do. If that's you, or someone brilliant you know, we'd love to talk.. Come build with us → . Giving back to the community that got us here.. . Everything we know about lifecycle came from marketers being generous with what they learned. Time to return the favor.. And our CEO Neha's going deeper with the people actually doing this work every week on The AI Marketer Podcast .. But the thing we're most excited about: this fall, we're bringing SF's sharpest founders, lifecycle and growth leaders, and rising talent into one room. The Growth & AI Summit.
