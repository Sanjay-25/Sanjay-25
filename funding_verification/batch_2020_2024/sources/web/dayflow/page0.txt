Local AI keeps analysis on-device. BYO cloud providers and Dayflow Pro can receive selected screenshots for analysis.. . . . Frequently asked questions. Is Dayflow a time tracker? Dayflow does what people want from an automatic time tracker for Mac: a truthful record of where your time went, with zero manual timers or tags. But instead of charts of app usage, you get a readable story of your day. Think of it as a time tracker that grew into a work journal. It’s free and open source (MIT), and full recordings stay on your Mac.. How is Dayflow different from other time tracking apps? Traditional trackers log app names and show you charts. Dayflow understands what you were actually working on - it can tell “researching on YouTube” from “watching cat videos” - and writes your day into a visual timeline with summaries, so you never tag or categorize anything by hand. Full recordings stay stored on your Mac, and you choose local or cloud AI for analysis.. Is my data private? What about the recordings? You have full control. Full recordings and databases stay stored locally on your Mac. Local Mode with Ollama or LM Studio keeps analysis on-device. If you choose a BYO Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude provider, or use Dayflow Pro, selected screenshots can be sent to that service for analysis. The open-source Mac client shows local storage and outbound network behavior; the privacy policy covers cloud retention.. Will it slow down my computer? The recording process is extremely lightweight (around 100MB RAM, under 1% CPU). If you use Cloud AI (Gemini/ChatGPT/Claude), the impact remains minimal. Local AI inference is more demanding on your GPU and battery, so we recommend plugging in during long sessions if you go fully offline.. Who is Dayflow for? It’s for developers, freelancers, and knowledge workers who want a truthful, automated log of their day. If you want to own your data and stop guessing where your time went, Dayflow is for you.. Can I pause or disable recording? Absolutely. You can start/stop recording instantly from the menu bar. You can also automate it using Shortcuts (via deeplinks). You have full access to delete recordings and data from your local folder at any time.. How accurate is the AI at understanding my work? It depends on the model you choose. Gemini and ChatGPT/Claude generally offer the highest accuracy and narrative quality. Local models are improving rapidly but may be slightly less detailed. You can switch providers at any time to find the best balance for your needs.. Is there a free version? Dayflow is free and open-source (MIT Licensed). You can use it for free with Local models or Gemini’s free tier. Using ChatGPT or Claude requires their respective paid subscriptions (Plus/Pro) to access the API via CLI.. What platforms are supported? Dayflow is a native macOS app built with SwiftUI. It requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.. . . . Try Dayflow Today. . Download for MacOS . Dayflow.
