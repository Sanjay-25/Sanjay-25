Mar 19, 2026. . written by Adrian Ziegler. . . . . ← back. . . . Healthcare software was designed for humans. Prior auth submissions, EHR note creation, eligibility verification: these are multi-step, complex workflows that can't be reduced to an API call. That's what AI agents in healthcare are now being asked to automate.. Every agent that needs to do real work inside a payer portal or EHR has to navigate it through a browser, exactly the way a human would. Building that access reliably is hard. Keeping it working as portals change is harder.. CloudCruise is the infrastructure layer that handles it: a coding agent to build the automations, fully managed infrastructure to run them at scale, and a maintenance agent to keep them working as systems change. Today, we're announcing our $5M seed round, backed by Floating Point, Meridian Street Capital, Twine, Refract Ventures and angels like Zack Lipton (CTO, Abridge) and David Singleton (fmr. CTO, Stripe). If you're building AI agents that need to operate payer portals or EHRs, we'd love to talk. And we're hiring!. . Our engineering team is more productive than ever.
