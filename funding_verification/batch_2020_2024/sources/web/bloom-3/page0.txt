Disclosure: We offer an automated investment strategy (the “Auto Portfolio”) that seeks to construct and rebalance client portfolios based, in whole or in part, on publicly available information derived from Form 13F filings submitted to the SEC by certain institutional investment managers and other market participants. Form 13F filings are inherently delayed and may be filed up to 45 days after the end of each calendar quarter. As a result, there may be a significant lag between the positions disclosed in a 13F filing and the execution of transactions in client accounts. Securities reflected in the Auto Portfolio may no longer be held, or may have been materially changed, by the applicable filer at the time client trades are executed. Client portfolios will not be identical to the actual holdings or strategies of any 13F filer. Form 13F filings do not disclose all asset classes, derivatives, short positions, hedging activity, cash positions, or intraperiod trading, and therefore do not reflect the full investment strategy or risk management practices of any filer.
