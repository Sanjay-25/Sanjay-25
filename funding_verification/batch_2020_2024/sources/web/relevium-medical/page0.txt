Our team & partners . . . . . . . . . . . . . A PIONEERING APPROACH . . . . . . Pharmaceutical Excellence . . . . . . Chronic pain is our therapeutic pillar - validated as an urgent clinical need by key stakeholders. . Our science is based on TRPV1-targeting therapeutics to treat pain and other conditions involving hyperexcitability of the peripheral nervous system. Our novel prodrug therapeutic was developed to overcome the burning and tolerability issues that have limited the widespread use of TRPV1 agonist drugs until now. Our therapeutics are highly tolerable, with superior efficacy compared to conventional TRPV1 agonist drugs, no systemic side effects, and without the addiction potential and side effects of opioids..
