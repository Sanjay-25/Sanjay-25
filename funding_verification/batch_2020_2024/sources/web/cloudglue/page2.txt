We’re building the infrastructure that lets AI understand video. . Cloudglue is a small, fast-moving team building developer APIs that turn video and audio into structured, searchable data. We handle the hard infrastructure - transcription, visual analysis, search, extraction - so developers can build on top of video without managing ML pipelines themselves.. If you like shipping real systems, owning problems end-to-end, and working close to both the product and the infra, you'll probably feel at home here.. Open Roles.
