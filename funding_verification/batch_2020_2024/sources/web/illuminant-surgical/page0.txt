THE MOST POWERFUL TECHNOLOGY IS THE KIND YOU DON'T HAVE TO THINK ABOUT USING. . . Eliminate Spatial Uncertainty . Traditional navigation systems create a fundamental disconnect between the imaging you see and the intervention you’re performing. Surgeons must constantly shift attention between screens and patient, mentally translating 2D scans into 3D reality, introducing cognitive burden and uncertainty during critical moments. Skylight™ eliminates this challenge by projecting anatomical information directly onto the patient, creating a seamless integration of digital guidance and physical reality. . . Simplify Your Workflow . Traditional navigation systems add significant complexity to spine procedures, with setup times often exceeding 30 minutes and requiring specialized technical support. This disruption extends beyond time: it requires invasive patient prep, fragments the surgical workflow, increases OR costs, and limits the number of patients that can be treated each day. Skylight™ has been designed for non-invasive setup within minutes. . . Scale Beyond the Operating Room . Advanced navigation has been traditionally reserved for major medical centers due to prohibitive costs and space requirements. Skylight™ changes this paradigm with its compact footprint and wide applicability to pain management and interventional radiology. Skylight™ brings cutting edge visualization to facilities of all sizes, from university hospitals to ambulatory surgical centers and private specialty clinics, ensuring that practice size never limits surgical capability.. . .
