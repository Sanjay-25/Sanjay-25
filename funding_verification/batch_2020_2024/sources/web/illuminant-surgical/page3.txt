Announcing Illuminant’s $8.4M Seed Round. . . By Illuminant Surgical . | April 30, 2026. | 1 min read. . . . . . Illuminant Surgical is excited to announce our $8.4M Series Seed, featured in Fortune by Allie Garfinkle.. With this funding, our team has already been able to accelerate our engineering, manufacturing, and clinical efforts. We’ve recently opened the doors to our new 12,000 sq ft headquarters in Los Angeles. By housing our R&D labs, pilot manufacturing lines, and dedicated BSL-2 cadaver lab all under one roof, we’ve drastically reduced time from clinical insight to product.. We’re excited to roll-out our first production-equivalent Skylight systems from our manufacturing line in the next few weeks.. Our mission is simple: we want doctors to never miss..
