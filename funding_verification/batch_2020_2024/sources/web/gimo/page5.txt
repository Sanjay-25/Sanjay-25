FinTech startup GIMO hits first close of Series A at $5.1 million . 14 February, 2023. Posted by: GIMO TEAM. . . . . . . . . Hanoi, Vietnam – Vietnam’s fintech start-up GIMO today has announced the first close of its Series A funding with a US$5.1 million investment led by TNB Aura. . Other investment firms joining th e round are returning investors including Integra Partners, Resolution Ventures, Blauwpark Partners , ThinkZone Ventures, a nd Y Combinator. . GIMO’s latest financing follows a year of remarkable growth. With a solid 24x year-on-year revenue growth and an 11x year-on-year transaction volume increase, the company has delivered one of the favourite financial apps for Vietnamese financially underserved workers . .
