( fig. 01 ). . . . . . / Our story. From the waiting room to the clinic floor.. . I entered healthcare when people I love became critically sick. Overnight I was managing referrals, coverage questions, and endless phone trees, and I learned how much of getting care is spent fighting the process instead of getting better.. That experience pushed me to the clinical side. I started my own physical therapy practice and ran its operations end to end: scheduling, eligibility, authorizations, claims. The same bureaucracy that had worn my family down was burying the providers too.. Shasta is how we fight back. It's a platform for running a modern practice, with AI agents that take on the mounting admin burden from insurance companies.
