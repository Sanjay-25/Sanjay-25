Everything you need to know about Coldreach and AI-powered sales outreach.. . What is Coldreach? + . Coldreach is an AI SDR (sales development representative) that finds leads who need your product now, conducts real research on each one to understand why, and automates personalized outreach across email and LinkedIn. It is built for B2B sales teams that want research-driven outbound at scale, without hiring more SDRs.. . . What is an AI SDR? + . An AI SDR is software that automates top-of-funnel sales development work: identifying in-market leads, researching them, writing personalized outreach, and following up. Unlike traditional sales tools, a real AI SDR can independently decide who to reach out to and what to say — without a human writing each email. Coldreach is one of the leading AI SDR tools used by B2B sales teams today.. . . How is Coldreach different from other AI SDRs? + . Most AI SDR tools personalize emails using surface-level data like job title or company size. Coldreach is the research-driven AI SDR: for every lead, it reads the company's website, 10-K filings, hiring pages, news, and LinkedIn activity before writing a single email. Each message opens with a specific, verified reason for the outreach — not a generic icebreaker.. . . How does Coldreach compare to Apollo, Clay, 11x, or Artisan? + . Apollo and ZoomInfo are contact databases — they help you find leads but do not research or write outreach. Clay requires building custom automations with a credit system that burns fast. 11x and Artisan are enterprise-priced AI SDRs with limited customization. Coldreach replaces the Clay + Apollo + Instantly stack with one tool, starting at $899/month, and is the only one that researches every lead deeply before outreach.. . . How much does Coldreach cost? + . Coldreach self-service plans start at $899/month (Starter) and scale through Growth ($1,099/mo), Pro ($1,499/mo), and Enterprise ($1,999/mo). A fully managed Done-For-You plan runs $3,000/month. All tiers include signal research, deep lead enrichment, multi-channel outreach across email and LinkedIn, and full deliverability setup. By comparison, hiring one human SDR typically costs $60,000–$80,000 per year before tools.. . . What outreach channels does Coldreach support? + . Coldreach runs outreach across both email and LinkedIn. LinkedIn outreach is paced like a human to avoid account flags. Email sending includes the full deliverability stack: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, mailbox warmup, send pacing, and reputation monitoring — so messages reach the primary inbox, not the spam folder.. . . What buying signals does Coldreach monitor? + . Any signal you can describe in plain English. Coldreach monitors 113M+ companies across 5+ data sources — job postings, company news, website content, LinkedIn posts, employee changes, SEC filings, and more — and surfaces only the leads that match the signals you define. There are no preset categories or rigid filter boxes.. . . How does Coldreach research each lead? + . Once Coldreach matches a lead to one of your buying signals, it conducts a deep read on that company: website, 10-K, hiring page, recent news, LinkedIn activity. It extracts the specific reasons that company is likely in-market for your product — the pain point, the status quo, the urgency — and uses those as the foundation for personalized outreach. Every email references something the AI actually found.. . . Does Coldreach integrate with my CRM? + . Yes. Coldreach syncs natively with Salesforce, HubSpot, Apollo, and Outreach. CRM integration is included on all plans from Growth ($1,099/month) and above. No workflow changes are needed — Coldreach pushes researched leads, sent activity, and replies into your existing CRM.. . . What results do Coldreach customers see? + .
