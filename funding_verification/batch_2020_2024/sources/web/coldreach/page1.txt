What is an AI SDR and how does it work? + An AI SDR (Sales Development Representative) is software that uses artificial intelligence to automate top-of-funnel sales tasks - prospecting, lead research, personalized outreach, and follow-ups. Unlike basic email automation that relies on static templates, an AI SDR like Coldreach researches each lead before reaching out, analyzing buying signals across 5+ data sources including job postings, company news, LinkedIn activity, website changes, and SEC filings. It then writes personalized emails and LinkedIn messages that reference verified, timely reasons to reach out - driving significantly higher reply rates than generic cold outreach.. . . What are buying signals in B2B sales? + Buying signals are observable actions or events that indicate a company is likely in-market for a product or service. Common B2B buying signals include new job postings that suggest a growing need, leadership changes, funding rounds, technology stack changes, regulatory shifts, and relevant LinkedIn posts. Coldreach lets you define custom buying signals in plain English - for example, 'companies hiring 3+ engineers with Next.js experience' or 'recently mentioned data compliance challenges on their website' - and monitors 97M+ accounts in real time to surface only the leads that match your criteria.. . . How is Coldreach different from other AI SDR tools? + Most AI SDR tools personalize outreach using surface-level data like a prospect's name, title, and company description. Coldreach takes a research-first approach: before writing a single message, the AI researches each lead's specific pain points, tech stack, recent company events, and in-market timing across 5+ data sources. This deep research drives a 3.8% average reply rate across 500,000+ AI-sent emails - roughly 10x the industry average of 0.3–0.5%. Coldreach also includes private email deliverability infrastructure with dedicated mailbox warmup, so your messages actually reach the primary inbox.. . . What reply rate can I expect from AI cold outreach? + The industry average reply rate for cold outreach is 0.3–0.5%. Most AI SDR tools improve this marginally because they still rely on basic personalization. Coldreach customers see an average 3.8% reply rate because every email references a real, verified buying signal - not just a name and company. The key factors that drive reply rates are signal quality (reaching out at the right time), message relevance (referencing something the prospect actually cares about), and deliverability (making sure the email lands in the primary inbox, not spam).. . . Coldreach goes beyond this by combining multiple signal types - job postings, company news, website content, LinkedIn activity, employee changes, 10-K reports, and more - into a unified view of buying intent.
