Yes, Constructable is a more modern all-in-one construction platform designed to fully replace Procore or Autodesk Construction Cloud.. . Does Constructable have all the features I need to run my general contracting (or subcontracting) business?. Yes, Constructable is an all-in-one project management software designed for small-to-mid sized construction companies looking to streamline their operations. Constructable replaces the need for multiple systems and puts everything in one place. We have all of the features you need, and none of the ones you don't—so you'll never pay for features you don't actually use.. . Does Constructable have mobile apps?. Yes, Constructable has both an iPhone app and Android app. You can find them in the Apple App Store or the Google Play store.. . Download the Constructable app for iOS and Android . . We hear your product has Bluebeam Sessions-style commenting, markup and measurement features, is that true?. Yes, Constructable is designed to keep all your work together in one place, including commenting, markups, and measurements right on the plans. Plus, it's all stored in the cloud so there's no messy setup, it just works on any device. Group all markup together in Constructable and decide who has access. Resolve and archive when done. No more screenshots, no more lengthy emails—everything is unified in one place, trackable, and easy to use.. . I care a lot about ease of use for my field team, is your product easy to use? . Does Constructable price their software by my company's ACV (Annual Construction Volume)?. No, we charge a flat fee based on the plan you choose, billed monthly or annually depending on your preference. We know every project involves risk, and we want to give you peace of mind and predictable pricing. We will never audit your books or charge you overage fees.. . Do I have to pay per user?. No, with Constructable you get unlimited users for your team, owners, consultants, subcontractors, and vendors with no extra cost. We don't believe in nickel-and-diming our customers.
