Svix is the webhooks-as-a-service platform . We enable companies of all sizes to send webhooks easily and reliably, and our mission is to make webhooks easy and ubiquitous.. Webhooks are a key part of any modern API, and they unlock a variety of new possibilities for interacting with your services. They transform software products from useful applications into critical, real-time data infrastructure.. We also co-created Standard Webhooks , the open specification for secure, reliable webhook delivery, now governed by a technical steering committee and adopted across the industry. Making webhooks ubiquitous means making them consistent everywhere, not just for Svix customers..
