While working in private equity, our CEO, Austin, met with dozens of car wash operators. He was excited about the future of the industry and the growth of the subscription model, but recognized the lack of tools to support the evolving needs of the next generation of car washes. . . Seeing an opportunity to make a meaningful impact in the car wash industry, Austin set out to create a platform that would help operators manage every aspect of the customer journey. He brought in his co-founders, Sam and Nic, to develop The Car Wash CRM platform, and the rest is history!. . . . . How it's Going . Today, Rinsed is even more than just The Car Wash CRM. In May of 2024, we acquired Ignite Wash, expanding our product set to include Salespath, The Car Wash Salesperson Platform.. . Rinsed is currently installed at over 3,000 car washes nationally, helping to manage more than 8 million car wash memberships. . . What we've built so far is just the beginning. Frequently asked questions . . . . What does the interview process look like?. There are typically 5 steps to our interview process:. ‍. (1) Application Review: Our recruiting team intentionally sorts through resumes and applications and schedules interviews accordingly. ‍. (2) Recruiter Phone Screen: Your first stop in the interview process! Here you’ll learn more about the story of Rinsed while we learn about your background and experience. ‍. (3) Hiring Manager Interview: The deep dive into your specific experience from the person you’ll report to and how that translates into the world of Rinsed . ‍. (4) Peer Panel Discussion: Your chance to meet more of the Rinsed team, gain insights into our daily workflows, and picture yourself in the role from the experts themselves. ‍. (5) Final Round : The finish line! As a finalist, you can expect to meet our CEO either virtually or onsite in our NY office, typically with a presentation that showcases your unique skill set. . . .
