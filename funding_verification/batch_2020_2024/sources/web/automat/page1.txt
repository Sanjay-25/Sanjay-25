Building the Modern Enterprise Automation Platform. . . Traditional automation software is slow & expensive, that’s why we built Automat. It’s 10X faster and 1/10th the price. It lets companies automate their work without a massive team or budget.. . . . . . . . . . Our Story. . Founded by two ex-Googlers who realized the potential of using AI techniques from robotics and applying them to software automations.. At Google, their team was tasked with finding product applications of LaMDA in early 2020, & they built over 50+ prototypes integrating LaMDA across Google’s products and services. Eventually Gautam (Automat CTO) would go on to implement multiple demos for Google’s first public Demonstration of LaMDA, the AI test kitchen.. Our team of robotics/ai engineers and operators at Automat work out of a former historic brewery, in the sunny San Francisco Mission District.. . . . . . . . . . . We believe:. . . .
