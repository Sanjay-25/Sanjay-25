Billing shouldn ' t suck. . Getting paid should be the easy part. But billing systems make it hard. For the longest time, you had to buy a third-party black box or waste time constructing your own billing. Lago fixes that.. It ' s not a problem that goes viral on social media, but it ' s ubiquitous in tech companies. At one of Europe ' s biggest fintechs, we saw the “billing nightmare”: 10+ full-time engineers worked on billing alone. Product grew frustrated because pricing took months. Finance labored in spreadsheets they shouldn ' t need.. Nobody talked about this common issue: Bad billing systems block projects, frustrate teams and devour resources better spent on innovation.. But what options are there? Building your own system is a nightmare. Third-party systems become black boxes that cause more work than they save.. We wanted to solve billing, and open-source became the obvious choice. You should have full transparency and control your financial infrastructure without sacrificing the feature set of a market-leading vendor. That ' s how Lago was born..
