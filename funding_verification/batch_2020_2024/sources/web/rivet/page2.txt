About Rivet. Rivet is the open-source orchestrator for agentic workloads: infrastructure for the agentic era. Everything it runs is an Actor : a long-lived process with its own SQLite database and filesystem that hibernates when idle and wakes on demand, scheduled by one control plane on workers you already run. . Agents , Workflows , agentOS , and Dynamic Apps are Actor types built on that primitive. Secure Exec is a library that runs inside Actors. . Everything we build emphasizes self-hosting, flexibility, and performance. Most of Rivet is Apache 2.0, self-hostable, and can run fully air-gapped in your VPC. Rivet Cloud runs it for you, or BYOC runs it inside your own cloud account. . We believe no good engineering happens in a vacuum, so we work closely with customers to make sure Rivet fits their use case and is flexible enough for their roadmap. . Our mission-critical “off-rails” code uses no or limited AI. Our routine “on-rails” SDK and frontend code uses reviewed AI. For the website, we just test the output. . Where we can hire depends on local employment and tax rules, partner coverage, and U.S. trade and economic sanctions. As a U.S. company, we can’t employ or contract with someone when doing so would violate those requirements. . Today, we can’t support employment or contracting in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, the Crimea region of Ukraine, or other regions of Ukraine covered by U.S. sanctions. Separately, operational and compliance constraints currently prevent us from hiring in mainland China. . . . How eligibility works. These programs can address human rights abuses, terrorism, international narcotics trafficking, genocide, and other sanctioned activity. .
