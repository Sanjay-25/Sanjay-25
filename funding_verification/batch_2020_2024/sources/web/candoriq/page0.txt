As former execs, we were drawn to the challenges of building the modern workforce, because we'd lived the pain ourselves. After leading large R&D teams from early startups through hypergrowth through IPO, we saw how compensation and headcount planning still relied on spreadsheets and manual work, no matter how many tools were in the stack.. We also love solving multi-stakeholder problems. Workforce planning requires real collaboration across HR, Talent, and Finance, yet most tools are built for a single persona and break down when teams need to plan together. We wanted to build a platform that brings these teams into one system, with shared data and shared decisions.. Finally, our perspective on pay transparency is personal. As first-generation immigrants, we struggled to understand our own market value while navigating work, visas, and new systems. That experience shaped our belief that fairness and clarity in pay drive opportunity and innovation.. . Today, CandorIQ is evolving into an AI-powered platform for designing and managing the workforce of the future—where humans and AI work together across compensation, headcount, and spend.. .
