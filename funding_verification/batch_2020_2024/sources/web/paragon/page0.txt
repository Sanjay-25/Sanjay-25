Our Company Values are intended to provide a framework for decision-making and are directly applicable to our daily work at Paragon. When assessing a project, initiative, or approach - we consider if it aligns with these values.. Customer-centric . We believe that our laser focus on customers allows us to create the most innovative product and solutions. . We believe that customer voices matter more than ideals. . We deepen our empathy by listening to customers and consider the most direct solution to their problems. . High ownership . We align on objectives and give individuals the ownership to determine the best approach and execute. . We bias towards action - identifying problems is not enough, we expect individuals to take ownership to solve it. . We believe ownership promotes accountability - we own the results, success or fail. . We believe in a results-oriented approach to everything; we value output over input. . Confidence without ego . “Strong convictions, weakly held”. . We execute on our beliefs with determination, but remain open to our assumptions being challenged. . We can disagree and commit - if the owner has high conviction, we don’t need consensus to move forward. . Iteration . “If it’s perfect, you shipped too late”. . We believe in a minimum-viable approach to everything. . We approach every opportunity as an experiment and find the fastest path to feedback. . We are not afraid of unknown territory – we have the freedom to fail and learn from our failures. . We believe everything at Paragon is a work in progress and should be improved over time. . Transparency . We believe in creating a culture of mutual respect that promotes honest and direct communication. . We can respectfully disagree and work together to reach the best solution, or disagree & commit. . Startups are hard; being transparent about our challenges helps us build a more cohesive and efficient team. . We believe that candid feedback accelerates learning and career development. . . .
