Our story. idemeum started in 2021, joined Y Combinator’s S21 batch, and initially focused on endpoint security. Our first product, Endpoint Control, helps IT and security teams remove admin rights and control what runs on their devices. As we worked with those teams, we kept seeing the same thing: skilled people buried in routine IT work, such as password resets, device problems, and onboarding checklists.. As AI moved to the forefront of everyday technology, it became clear that AI could finally do that work. However, most AI tools were simply answering questions and making suggestions. We wanted agents that could reason about issues, fix things on their own, and act safely, so that IT teams would trust them on every laptop and in every cloud system.. . . Our mission. .
