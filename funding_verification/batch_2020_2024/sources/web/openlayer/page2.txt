Why This Matters Now . . . . . . We’ve Raised a $14.5M Series A to Build the AI Reliability Platform. Published May 14, 2025 5 min read . . The pace of AI development has never been faster. Every day brings new tools, new models, and new ideas. But in this rush to ship, one thing keeps getting left behind: confidence that the system will actually work when it matters.. That’s why we built Openlayer. We believe the future of AI depends on reliability, and reliability depends on testing. Not just once, but continuously, before and after deployment. Today, we’re announcing our Series A. But more importantly, we’re sharing why we believe reliability is the key to unlocking AI’s next era.. . When AI Breaks, the Costs Are Real. It’s easy to overlook risk when a product is new and the stakes are low. But for the enterprises building mission-critical AI systems, failure isn’t theoretical. It’s expensive. When Zillow’s Zestimate model couldn’t adjust to pandemic era housing trends, it reportedly cost the company over $500 million and forced widespread layoffs [1] .. That wasn’t a bug in the code. It was a failure of validation, a breakdown in trust between the system and its creators. And it’s not isolated. We’ve seen AI driven losses, hallucinations, and data leakage affect industries from finance to telecom to e-commerce.. Most companies aren’t equipped to catch these problems early. That’s where we come in.. . The Platform for AI Testing and Governance. Openlayer is a platform for ensuring AI systems behave the way you intend. Whether you’re building traditional ML models or deploying LLMs, we help you define what “good” looks like, then test against it consistently, repeatedly, and at scale.. Our customers use Openlayer to:. Speed up iteration. Catch edge cases and regressions before launch.. Improve accuracy and performance. Run behavioral tests tied to business outcomes.. Reduce downstream costs. Eliminate hours of debugging and failed experiments.. Collaborate across teams. From engineers to product to compliance.. Most importantly, we help teams move from guesswork to guarantees, replacing “I hope this works” with “we’ve already tested that.”. . From Point Solutions to a Complete Stack. The tooling landscape for AI development is fragmented. One tool for training, another for prompting, a third for observability. None of them talk to each other, and most weren’t built with reliability in mind.. Openlayer is a unified platform designed from the ground up for end-to-end AI testing and governance. It works across use cases and model types. It supports real-world deployment patterns. And it’s intuitive enough for any team to use.. For technical teams: A flexible, powerful interface for creating and running tests.. For business stakeholders: A clear view into system performance and risk.. For leadership: Peace of mind that what’s shipped reflects company values.. . Why This Matters Now. AI is no longer just an experiment. It’s infrastructure. It’s embedded in customer experiences, financial decisions, and real world outcomes. The companies that succeed won’t be the ones who move the fastest, they’ll be the ones who move fastest with confidence.. We believe reliability is what unlocks scale. That’s what we’re building at Openlayer. And with the backing of our investors and early customers, we’re just getting started..
