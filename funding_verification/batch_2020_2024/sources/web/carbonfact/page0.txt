What it's like working at Carbonfact. . . Play video . . . . . . . . . . . Principled from Day One . There are many ways to build a team and grow a company. From the very beginning, we’ve been clear about what we stand for. Carbonfact Principles guide our daily behaviors and the design of our operating system. . . . Idealist pragmatists . . We exist to help fashion and textile companies cut carbon, and everything starts with our customers. We partner closely with them, solve their biggest pains, and do the work to turn data into real reductions. Climate action demands both ambition and pragmatism: we hold a clear ideal while working in practical ways that make impact tangible. . . . . Own it . . Carbonauts are owners and shareholders. We take responsibility for outcomes, deliver our work end-to-end, and consider the impact we have on each other. We share context broadly and openly so everyone can make good decisions. No permission is needed to do what’s right for Carbonfact.. . . . Simplify . . The carbon problem is complex, and we make it simple. We choose the most straightforward path, avoid unnecessary complications, and trust the efficiency of small teams. Our communication is clear, concise, and easy to understand cross contexts. . . . . Low ego . . We embrace feedback and personal growth. We’re a stronger team when we stay open to change, new ideas, and learning from each other. We give and welcome feedback regularly, treat setbacks as opportunities, and use 360 reviews to identify strengths and areas to improve. . . . . . . . . . . . Join the team . . . . . . . . .
