We built Datasaur to ensure that AI strengthens institutions rather than undermining them.. ‍. AI is fundamentally different from previous software. Its power does not come from the tool alone, but from the data it learns from and improves upon. Most of that data is private—and for the companies that own it, it is a key source of long-term competitive strength. . . By enabling organizations to protect sensitive data while deploying AI responsibly, we help regulated enterprises modernize with confidence—so innovation, privacy, and accountability can thrive side by side. . . That’s why the future of AI is private AI. It is our mission to help critical sectors like healthcare, finance, and public services transform static records into living intelligence to unleash the full power of artificial intelligence and deliver real-world benefits for all. . . Datasaur has raised $8m in venture funding from top-tier investors such as Initialized Capital, Greg Brockman (President, OpenAI) and Calvin French-Owen (CTO, Segment) and serves companies such as Google, Netflix, Qualtrics, Spotify, the FBI and more.. . Meet the founder. Ivan Lee. Ivan leads the company’s mission to help regulated and data-sensitive organizations adopt AI without sacrificing control, privacy, or accountability. . . With deep experience spanning machine learning, data infrastructure, and enterprise AI systems, he has worked closely with some of the world’s most demanding organizations to operationalize AI in environments where accuracy, governance, and trust are non-negotiable.
