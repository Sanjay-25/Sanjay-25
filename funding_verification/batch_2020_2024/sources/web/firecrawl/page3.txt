[ 01 / 04 ]. ·. Origin. . . . . Why we exist . Firecrawl was built on a simple insight: large language models are powerful, but hard to feed well. Developers shouldn't have to duct-tape proxies, scrapers, orchestrators, and post-processing logic to give an LLM the data it needs. Today, Firecrawl is one of the fastest growing open source projects in the world.. The web holds nearly everything humans know — the most complete, real-time record of our knowledge. But this knowledge is trapped, scattered across millions of domains, locked behind JavaScript, and constantly changing. AI needs this data to be useful, to answer questions accurately, to take actions confidently, and to understand the world as it exists right now.. Firecrawl changes that. We provide the infrastructure to make the web fully LLM-ready: structured, searchable, and usable in production. We're building the eyes for AGI.. . [ OUR MISSION ] Our mission is to make web data work for AI — cleanly, scalably, and in real time.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [ 02 / 04 ]. ·. Capabilities. . . . . What we make possible . One API to turn the web into structured, LLM-ready data. No boilerplate, no infrastructure — just the building blocks for AI-native software.. [ 01 ] Search The web as a database . Search the web and get full page content from the results — not just links. Feed real-time context and fresh knowledge into your agents and RAG pipelines.. . . [ 02 ] Scrape Any page, any format . Convert any URL into clean markdown, structured JSON, screenshots, or semantic text. Describe what data you need in natural language and get back structured results — from any page, at any scale.. . . [ 03 ] Interact Cloud sandboxes for agents . Take actions on any page — click buttons, fill forms, and navigate dynamic content. Describe what you want in natural language, or write code for full control.. . . . We're not a toolkit.
