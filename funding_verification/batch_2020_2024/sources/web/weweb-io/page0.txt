How it all began. Inspired by the rise of component based frontend frameworks (React, Vue.js, and Angular) and decoupled architecture, we saw an opportunity to help individual business owners build their websites using their mobile phones. . . . . . . Our first funding. Led by 42 CAP and Aglae Ventures we raised our first round of funding. . . Energized, we put our heads together to build a product that customers would love and want.. . . . . . YC Experience. We joined the best startup accelerator, Y Combinator, and learned from the collective wisdom of YC partners and alums. . . After conversations with hundreds of customers, we noticed a growing trend: many were using WeWeb to create web applications, not just websites.. . . . . . Finding our true North. We listened to our customers and launched WeWeb 2.0 focused on building web applications. . . The new WeWeb attracted thousands of users, who were impressed with the new experience.. . . . . . Focusing on growth. Our mantra has always been: talk to customers, build, ship, iterate .. . In 2023, we made WeWeb easier to use by refining the editor’s interface, introducing components, and adding more data sources.. . We also introduced the WeWeb Academy to help users learn and succeed.. . . . . . Experimenting what's next. This year was all about pushing the boundaries.. ‍. We introduced a Figma plugin for seamless design imports, made WeWeb apps 50x faster, and launched the WeWeb Marketplace.. ‍. We also explored how AI can transform the building process in WeWeb.. . . . . . . Redefining no-code with AI. With WeWeb 3.0, we’re taking no-code web development to the next level by embedding AI at its core, making powerful tools accessible to everyone, regardless of their skill level. . . The future of building is here, with WeWeb.. . . . . . . Evolving into full-stack. This year we’re evolving WeWeb into a full-stack platform : unifying frontend, backend, and AI into a cohesive building experience. . . Now non-coders can build and deploy complete applications, not just frontends, from one place.. . . . . . . . . . . WeWeb values. Our team is the soul of our company.
