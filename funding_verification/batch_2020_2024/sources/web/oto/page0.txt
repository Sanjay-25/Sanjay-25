What is Oto?. Oto is a tinnitus program that combines expert care with a daily mobile app. You're matched with an audiologist who specializes in tinnitus. You use the Oto app for daily sessions and soothing soundscapes. It's built around the approaches that leading health authorities recommend for people living with tinnitus.. . . Soothing soundscapes. A library of calming sounds and soundscapes to help you relax and switch off.. . . Guided coping exercises. Short daily exercises using cognitive behavioral techniques to help you build coping skills.. . . Better sleep. Wind-down routines and sounds designed to help you fall asleep more easily.. . . Calm and focus. Breathing and mindfulness exercises to lower stress and help you concentrate.. . . Progress, shared with your provider. Track how you're doing over time.
