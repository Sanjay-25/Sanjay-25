Any model Model routing across frontier, open-source, and proprietary models means no single vendor's pricing ever holds you hostage. . Your cloud Deploy Gumloop in your own VPC. . Your database Through VPC, you own storage outright. That means owning every agent trace, forever. . Your choice of intelligence Frontier, open-source, or your own proprietary models. You decide what powers your agents. . Every action is logged and accounted for, or blocked if it crosses a line your admins drew. No black boxes.. How we make it real:. One gateway A single MCP gateway is the one place to give every agent access to the tools you use. . Total tracing Every tool call made, logged, and aggregated for humans or analytics agents to review. . Audit everything Every action to create, use, or edit an agent, diligently on the record. .
