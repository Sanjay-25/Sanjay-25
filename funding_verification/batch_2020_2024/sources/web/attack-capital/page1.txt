We Build It.. Attack Capital was born from a simple observation: the traditional venture capital model is broken for early-stage AI companies. Great ideas are lost in pitch decks, and brilliant founders are forced to spend more time fundraising than building. We knew there had to be a better way. As a YC-backed AI venture studio , we are redefining how AI companies are created, funded, and scaled.. . . Our Story: From YC to Venture Studio. Our journey began as founders in Y Combinator's Winter 2022 batch . Surrounded by the brightest minds in technology, we experienced firsthand the power of intense focus, rapid iteration, and a community dedicated to building great things. We saw how the YC model transformed promising ideas into world-changing companies.. However, we also saw a critical gap in the ecosystem. While YC excels at accelerating existing startups, many brilliant founders and transformative AI ideas never even reach the starting line. The initial phase of company creation, validating an idea, building an MVP, and securing the first customers, remains the most challenging and underserved stage.. We founded Attack Capital to solve this problem. We combined the operational intensity of Y Combinator with the strategic resources of a venture fund to create a new kind of organization: an AI venture studio that systematically builds companies from the ground up. . . . . . Our Mission. . To Build the Foundational AI Companies for Every Industry. Our mission is to build the next generation of market-defining vertical SaaS companies , powered by artificial intelligence. We believe that AI is a general-purpose technology that will reshape every industry on the planet. By focusing on specific verticals, from healthcare and legal to finance and manufacturing, we can build targeted, high-impact AI solutions that solve real-world problems.. We are not passive investors. We are builders, operators, and co-founders who are deeply involved in every aspect of company creation.
