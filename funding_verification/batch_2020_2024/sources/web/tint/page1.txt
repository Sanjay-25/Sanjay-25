Principles, not perks. . These are the operating principles that shape how we work every day.. . Remote-First. Our team is distributed across the US by design. We believe the best talent shouldn't have to relocate, and the best work shouldn't require everyone in the same room. . . . Performance and Rewards Based on Outcomes. We don't measure activity. We measure impact. Your contributions are recognized by what you deliver, not where or when you deliver it. . . . Stage-Appropriate Workload. We're a growth-stage company. That means we move fast, stay focused, and scope our work to what matters most right now — not what a Fortune 500 might do with 10x the resources. . . . . . What We Look For . The traits we see in our most successful people. . At Tint, we've thought carefully about what makes someone thrive here. These traits aren't a replacement for our values — they're how our best people live them.. . Collaborative and Inclusive. You bring people in. You share context freely, welcome different perspectives, and make the team stronger by including others in the work. . . . Positive, Optimistic, and Curious. You default to possibility. Hard problems excite you. Ambiguity is an invitation to explore, not a reason to stall. . . . Proactive, Adaptable General Athlete. You don't wait to be told. You spot what needs to happen and you make it happen — even when it's outside your lane. . . . Good Communicators. You write clearly, speak directly, and make sure the right people have the right information at the right time. . . . Smart Decision Makers and Risk Takers. You make calls with the information you have. You don't let perfect be the enemy of good. And you own the outcomes. . . . Accountable, Reliable, and Focused on Results. You do what you say you'll do. You follow through. And you care about whether it actually worked. . . . . .
