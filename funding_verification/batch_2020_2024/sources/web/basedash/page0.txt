Social X , LinkedIn , GitHub , YouTube , Product Hunt , Y Combinator , Crunchbase , G2 . . . . Frequently asked questions. . Who founded Basedash, and when?. Max Musing founded Basedash in 2020 as a solo founder, and the company joined Y Combinator's Summer 2020 batch that year. Max leads the company as founder and CEO, and Kris Lachance is president, leading go-to-market and product growth. Basedash began as a collaborative interface for production databases and relaunched in 2025 as an AI-native business intelligence platform.. . Where is Basedash headquartered?. Basedash is headquartered in San Francisco, Toronto, and Montreal. The company's legal entity is BaseDash Inc., a Delaware corporation. Basedash serves customers worldwide, and its interface is available in English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese. Organizations with data residency requirements can self-host Basedash or run it in a private VPC instead of the managed cloud.. . How is Basedash funded?. Basedash is a privately held, venture-backed company. Y Combinator invested when Basedash joined its Summer 2020 batch, and in May 2023 the company announced a $4.4 million seed round led by Matrix, with participation from Y Combinator, Form Capital, and Worklife. Angel investors include the founders of Figma, Notion, and Bloom Tech. The company is growing and hiring across engineering and go-to-market.. . Is Basedash the same company that made the Basedash admin panel?. Yes. Basedash launched in 2020 as a collaborative interface for viewing and editing production databases, which many teams used as an internal admin panel. In 2025 the same company relaunched Basedash as an AI-native business intelligence platform for questions, dashboards, semantic metrics, and automated reporting. Teams still using the original product can sign in through the legacy login, and new teams start on the BI platform.. . How many teams use Basedash?. Basedash powers analytics for more than 5,000 teams worldwide, from early-stage startups to established companies. Customers include AI companies such as Exa, Gumloop, and Composio; developer-tool and data companies such as Tiger Data and Hookdeck; financial services firms such as Purpose and Taxfyle; and healthcare companies such as Fullscript.
