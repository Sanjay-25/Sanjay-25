Hi, we're Frigade fri-GADE · like “brigade”, with an F . . We help people understand your software and get their work done.. . . . . . . . . What we believe 01 Care about the defaults.. Frigade becomes part of your product. Its components should look and feel like they belong there from the start. We put care into the defaults, including the empty states and the way a checklist closes when you're done.. . . 02 Use what we build.. We use Frigade to onboard and support our own customers. Working with the same product our customers use helps us spot the awkward steps and decide what to improve.. . . 03 Work directly with customers.. Customers talk directly with our engineers, designers, and founders. The people building a feature also help customers put it to use, so questions and feedback reach the people who can act on them.. . . 04 Improve what's already in use.. We keep working on features after they launch. Customer feedback helps us decide where a fix or a simpler workflow will do more good than another feature.. . . . . . . How we got here It started with onboarding.. At LinkedIn, an internal system helped product teams build onboarding flows without starting from scratch. We wanted to make that kind of tooling available to other companies.. That became Frigade Engage: React components for checklists, tours, and announcements that developers could make their own.. Working with customers also showed us how much effort goes into keeping guidance current as a product changes. Moving a button or changing a workflow can leave a tour pointing people to the wrong place.. With Frigade Assistant, we took a different approach to that problem. It learns a product by using it, then helps people navigate the screen they're on and complete tasks. Engage gives teams control over the flows they design; Assistant helps with the questions and tasks that come up along the way.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
