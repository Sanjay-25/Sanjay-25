We’re lowering prices to make Recall.ai accessible to more startups and companies building products around conversation data. Recall.ai is an essential infrastructure layer for companies that work with meeting data, spanning industries like sales, notetaking, healthcare, legal tech, incident management, and more. Ultimately, lower pricing helps more teams test, launch, and scale products built on top of Recall.ai.. . . . Breakdown of Recall.ai’s updated pricing . Recall.ai now charges $0.50 per recording hour. As of early 2026, Recall.ai has dropped prices for the Pay As You Go Plan from $0.70 per recording hour to $0.50 per recording hour. This change applies to both our Meeting Bot API and Desktop Recording SDK , which are priced at the same rate.. Recall.ai has no platform fee. We no longer charge a monthly platform fee – our usage-based pricing model means our customers only pay when they’re actively using Recall.ai. This change makes it easier for startups to build a proof of concept without a financial commitment.. Calendar API included for free. Unlike other companies, we don’t charge for our Calendar API , which connects Recall.ai with your users’ Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook accounts. This lets you automatically send meeting bots to select events on your users’ calendars, while enriching meeting recordings with calendar information like participant emails and meeting titles.. Flexible transcription pricing. To get meeting transcripts , you can use Recall.ai’s built-in transcription engine for $0.15 per recording hour. Based on your preferred balance of cost, language support, accuracy, and latency, you can also pick from one of the many transcription providers we partner with and pay them directly.. Storage and playback. You can store meeting audio, video, and transcripts for up to 7 days for free on Recall.ai’s platform. If you need to store your data for longer, we charge $0.05 per recording media hour for every additional 30 days of storage.. . . Frequently Asked Questions . Can I try Recall.ai for free? . Yes! We give complimentary credits to every user who signs up for a Recall.ai account. This lets you try recording and transcribing meetings with our Meeting Bot API or Desktop Recording SDK at no cost. . . Is Recall.ai pricing competitive compared to other meeting bot APIs? . Yes, Recall.ai’s pricing is competitive compared to other meeting recording products. Our platform integrates with the most video conferencing platforms while having the simplest set-up process, making it the most cost-effective option when factoring in engineering resources. . . Does Recall.ai have platform fees? . No, Recall.ai does not require customers to pay any platform fees. You will only pay for the meeting recording hours you use, with no hidden monthly costs.. . Is Recall.ai pricing friendly for startups? . Yes, Recall.ai offers a Pay As You Go plan as a flexible and affordable option for early-stage startups . Supporting our full range of video conferencing platforms for $0.50 an hour, the Pay As You Go plan allows your team to easily build and test a proof of concept with zero commitment.. . Is calendar integration included in Recall.ai’s pricing plan? . Yes, our Calendar Integration API is included in all of our pricing plans at no additional cost. . . How does Recall.ai’s pricing scale with usage? . We want companies to build successful products with Recall.ai, so our pricing gets cheaper the more you use. We offer volume discounts to our customers on our Launch and Enterprise plans – the more hours you record, the cheaper each hour will be.. . Is Recall.ai’s pricing sustainable at scale? . Yes! We have many enterprise customers, including Hubspot, Calendly, and Brighthire, that cost-effectively use Recall.ai as part of their product infrastructure. . .
