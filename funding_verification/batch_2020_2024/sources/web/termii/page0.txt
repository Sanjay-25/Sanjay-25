Our mission Every day, billions of people interact with financial systems that depend on small but critical transaction signals: a one-time password, a fraud check, a transaction confirmation. When those signals fail, people can't access their money, authenticate their identity, or trust that a payment went through. . Termii exists to solve that. We built the intelligence layer that sits between financial platforms and the channels they communicate through, scoring, routing, and confirming every transaction signal before it fails silently. . . . The problem we solve An OTP that never arrived. A fraud alert that fired too late. A transaction confirmation that got lost. These failures are preventable, and preventing them is what we do. . . What makes us different We don't just deliver messages. We predict delivery success before the signal fires, route intelligently in real time, and learn from every outcome, so the system gets smarter with every transaction. . . . . . 2017 to 2025 Our story The road so far . . How we work The values that shape everything we build . . Reliability is the product. We don't just build features. We build infrastructure that financial systems depend on. Every decision we make is evaluated against whether it makes the platform more reliable.. . . Intelligence over configuration. The best systems don't require constant tuning. We build intelligence into the platform so that routing, fraud detection, and delivery confidence improve automatically, without your engineers maintaining rules.. . . Built for emerging markets. We started in Africa because the gap was biggest there. Our models understand carrier quality, network conditions, and delivery behaviour across emerging markets in a way generic platforms never will.. . . Security without compromise.
