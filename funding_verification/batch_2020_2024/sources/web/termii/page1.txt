The hiring process What to expect when you apply to Termii . . 01 Application review. We read every application. We're looking for clear evidence of craft, curiosity, and a track record of making things that work. Response within 5 business days.. . . . 02 Introductory call. 30 minutes with a member of the hiring team. We want to understand what you've built, what you care about, and why Termii is the right next step for you.. . . . 03 Technical assessment / case study. A practical exercise relevant to the role, either a short take-home or live session. We design these to be fair, respectful of your time, and genuinely reflective of the work.. . . . 04 Team interviews. Two conversations with people you'd work closely with. We assess for genuine technical depth, cultural alignment, and whether we'd both be excited to work together.. . . . 05 Offer. A competitive offer with transparent equity information and clear total compensation. We aim for the whole process to take under three weeks.. . . . . . . . . . . What success looks like .
