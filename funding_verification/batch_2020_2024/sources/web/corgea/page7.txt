Corgea Security Team Research & Product Security . . . . We’re thrilled to announce the release of our new integrations for JIRA, Slack, Zapier, and webhooks! These powerful integrations are designed to seamlessly integrate Corgea into your existing workflows, making it easier than ever to manage and fix security vulnerabilities in your codebase.. JIRA Integration. For teams that rely on JIRA for issue tracking and project management, our JIRA integration is a game-changer. Corgea can automatically create JIRA tickets for detected vulnerabilities, complete with detailed information and suggested fixes. This streamlines the remediation process and ensures that security issues are properly tracked and addressed within your existing workflows.. . For more info, checkout our docs .. Slack Integration. Stay informed about new vulnerabilities and fixes directly in your Slack channels. With the Slack integration, you can receive real-time notifications whenever a scan is complete with an entire scan report. This integration ensures that your team stays up-to-date and can quickly address any security concerns. Additionally, Slack customers can use Slack Workflows to drive further automation.. . For more info, checkout our docs .. Zapier Integration. Zapier is a powerful automation tool that allows you to connect various apps and services. With our Zapier integration, you can create custom workflows and automate actions based on events in Corgea. For example, you can automatically create a GitHub issue, or send an email notification when a critical finding is found. .. . For more info, checkout our docs .. Webhooks Integration. For teams that prefer a more customized approach, our webhooks integration allows you to receive notifications and data from Corgea in real-time. You can configure webhooks to send information to your preferred destination, such as a custom application or a third-party service, enabling seamless integration with your existing tools and processes.. For more info, checkout our docs .. Conclusion. These new integrations are designed to make your life easier and help you maintain a secure codebase without disrupting your existing workflows. Whether you prefer to work within Slack, JIRA, Zapier, or your own custom tools, Corgea has you covered.. To get started with these integrations, simply navigate to the “Integrations” section in your Corgea dashboard and follow the step-by-step instructions. If you have any questions or need assistance, our dedicated team is always ready to help.. Stay tuned for more exciting updates and features from Corgea as we continue to enhance our platform and provide you with the best tools for securing your codebase.. . . On this page.
