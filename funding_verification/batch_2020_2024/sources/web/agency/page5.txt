How is ISO 27001 different from SOC 2? SOC 2 is a U.S.-rooted attestation report describing your controls; ISO 27001 is an international certification of a management system (an ISMS) that buyers recognize by name. The underlying controls overlap heavily, but ISO leans more on governance and continual improvement. Many startups end up pursuing both.. What are Stage 1 and Stage 2? Certification happens in stages. Stage 1 is a documentation review that confirms your ISMS is ready. Stage 2 examines whether it's actually implemented and effective — pass it and you're certified. Lighter annual surveillance audits and a recertification around the three-year mark keep the certificate active.. Do I need a GRC platform like Vanta or Drata? A GRC platform automates evidence collection and control mapping, but it won't run your risk workshop, make the judgment calls in your Statement of Applicability, conduct your internal audit, or sit across from the certification body.
