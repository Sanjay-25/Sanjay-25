Nine categories spanning the mandatory ISMS clauses and all four Annex A control themes: ISMS scope and foundations, leadership and governance, risk assessment and treatment, Annex A organizational, people, physical, and technological controls, operating the ISMS, and certification prep.. How is ISO 27001 different from SOC 2? SOC 2 is a U.S.-rooted attestation report; ISO 27001 is an international certification of a management system (an ISMS) that buyers recognize by name. The underlying controls overlap heavily, but ISO leans more on governance and continual improvement — risk reviews, internal audits, and management reviews..
