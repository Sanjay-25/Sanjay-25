What's the difference between SOC 2 Type I and Type II? SOC 2 Type I confirms your controls are designed properly at a single point in time. Type II observes your controls operating over a period of time (commonly 3 to 12 months), which is stronger evidence. Most enterprise buyers ultimately want Type II. The guide explains when each one makes sense.. Do I need a GRC platform like Vanta or Drata? A GRC platform automates evidence collection and monitoring, but it doesn't write your policies, remediate failing controls, or face the auditor for you. The guide breaks down the three common paths — DIY with spreadsheets, buying a platform and running it yourself, or having the program operated for you.. How long does a first SOC 2 take? A Type I can be achieved quickly to unblock an urgent deal; a Type II adds an observation window during which your controls must operate. The single biggest predictor of a smooth audit is how well your day-to-day security operation runs during that window. The guide explains the timeline in plain terms..
