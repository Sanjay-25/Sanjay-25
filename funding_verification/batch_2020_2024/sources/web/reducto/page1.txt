Introducing r-1: Reducto’s new SOTA document parsing model Back to blog . Announcements. . . . Reducto Announces New York City Expansion. Bringing our team closer to the enterprises putting document intelligence into production.. Today, we announced the opening of our New York City office. The office will serve as the center of our East Coast presence and support our continued growth with enterprise customers across the region.. Since announcing more than $108 million in total funding last year, we’ve grown to process billions of pages for the world’s largest enterprises and AI teams. Our platform powers production use cases where extracting accurate, structured data from complex documents is critical. We recently released r-1 , the first in a new generation of unified parsing models, and we’re expanding our physical presence to better serve our growing customer base.. Getting closer to our customers. Bringing document intelligence into production requires a detailed understanding of each customer’s documents, systems, accuracy requirements, and existing workflows. We work closely with customers from initial evaluations through architecture and deployment, using their most challenging documents to ensure the system performs reliably in production.. “New York is home to many of the enterprises shaping how AI is deployed across major industries. Expanding our presence there brings us closer to those customers and gives us access to an exceptional pool of commercial and technical talent. Together, that will help more enterprises move document intelligence from evaluation into production.” — Adit Abraham, CEO Many of our most valuable customer conversations have happened in person. Our New York office builds on our growing relationships across the region, creating more opportunities to collaborate directly with customers and better understand the workflows they’re bringing into production.. We’re growing quickly. We’re actively hiring across functions for our New York team.
