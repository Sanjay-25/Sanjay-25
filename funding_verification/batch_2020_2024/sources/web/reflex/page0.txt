For decades, businesses have had to choose between generic SaaS that doesn’t fit how they work and expensive custom development that requires a large software team. AI has changed that equation.. Reflex is the complete platform for building, deploying, and managing custom software. It brings together AI-powered development, company data integrations, secure deployment, and governance on one standardized stack.. Your team can build and maintain software themselves, with Reflex providing the platform and support. Or our forward-deployed engineers can work alongside your team to identify and deliver high-value systems. Either way, every application is real, editable code that you control and can run in the cloud or fully on-premises.. The result is software designed around your business: tools, workflows, portals, dashboards, and AI systems that help you move faster, reduce costs, and adopt AI without building a large internal software organization..
