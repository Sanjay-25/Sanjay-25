We're here to ease any concerns, feel free to reach out anytime.. . . . . . . . What treatments do Legion Health offer?. . . . . . Our board-certified psychiatrists offer medication management, psychiatric consultations, psychiatric evaluations, and ongoing support. We specialize in treating anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, and various other mental health conditions.. . . . . . Does Legion Health accept insurance?. . . . . . Yes, Legion Health accepts most major insurance plans including Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, Magellan, Oscar, UnitedHealthcare, and many others. We also welcome self-pay patients with transparent pricing.. . . . . . How soon can I see a provider?. . . . . . Most patients can schedule appointments within days. Our providers have a variety of availability including early mornings, evenings, and weekends to fit your schedule.. . . . . . Are the appointments in-person or virtual?. . . . . . All appointments are virtual (telehealth), allowing you to meet with your provider through secure video from the comfort of your home or wherever you are.. . . . . . How long are the appointments?. . . . . . Initial consultations are typically 30-45 minutes, while follow-up appointments are 15-20 minutes. The duration may vary based on your specific needs and treatment plan.. . . . . . What happens in the first appointment?. . . . . . In your first appointment, your provider will discuss your symptoms, medical history, and goals to create an effective, personalized treatment approach. This helps determine if medication would benefit your treatment plan.. . . . . . How do I schedule an appointment?. . . . . . You can schedule directly through our website by selecting an available provider and time slot that works for you. The entire booking process takes just a few minutes.. . . . . . What if I need to cancel or reschedule?. . . . . . You can cancel or reschedule your appointment through your patient portal. We request at least 24 hours notice for any changes to your scheduled appointment.. . . . . . Do you prescribe controlled substances like Adderall?. . . . . . Yes, our providers can prescribe controlled substances when clinically appropriate. We follow all federal and state regulations regarding prescribing controlled substances. Our providers will work with you to determine the most effective treatment plan based on your specific needs.. . . . . . How do I get my prescriptions?. . . . . . After your appointment, your provider will send your prescriptions electronically to your preferred pharmacy. For controlled substances, you may need to present identification when picking up your medication.. . . . . . What conditions do you treat?. . . . . . Our providers specialize in treating a wide range of mental health conditions, including anxiety disorders, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, PTSD, OCD, insomnia, and more. If you're unsure if we treat your specific condition, please contact us.. . . . . . Is my information kept confidential?. . . . . . Absolutely.
