Open roles . . . . . Our story An energy startup that became an AI workforce for the trades. . Elyos began in London in 2023 as Elyos Energy , a climate tech company backed by Y Combinator, building smart demand response software that helped commercial buildings use energy more intelligently.. Working that closely with the built environment, the founding team kept meeting the same people: the electricians, engineers and field service businesses who actually keep buildings running. They were drowning in admin, missed calls and lost jobs, and almost no one was building software that truly understood their world.. . So Elyos made a bold pivot: from optimising energy to building an AI workforce for the trades . Today we build AI agents that answer calls, book jobs and handle the busywork, plugging directly into tools like Simpro, Joblogic, Commusoft and ServiceTitan.. In January 2026 we raised a $13M Series A led by Blackbird , alongside Y Combinator and Pi Labs, fuelling our mission to give every trade and field service business an unfair advantage.. . . .
