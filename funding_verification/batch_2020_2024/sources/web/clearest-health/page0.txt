Our Mission Leveling the Playing Field for Independent Practices . Payers have the data. Now you do too.. . . . Why We Started Clearest. We started Clearest because we saw something broken.. The No Surprises Act created IDR to give providers a fair shot at appropriate reimbursement. But payers adapted fast. They built teams, bought data, and turned disputes into a numbers game they were designed to win.. Independent practices got left holding the bag. They were told to "just file disputes" without the pricing intelligence to know what to ask for. Payers were counting on them not knowing what was possible.. We decided to change that.. We built the largest database of real IDR outcomes in the country. We trained AI models on what actually wins. And we made it all available to the practices that need it most—the independent ones fighting to stay independent.. The difference is not effort. It is information. And now you have it.. . . . . .
