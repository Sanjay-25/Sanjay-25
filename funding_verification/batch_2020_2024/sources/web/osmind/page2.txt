See open roles. . . . . . . A mission . that matters. . . Today, mental health disorders are one of the leading causes of death worldwide. Osmind empowers clinicians and researchers to bring innovative mental health treatments to patients who need it most.. . Osmind is a San Francisco–based public benefit corporation led by scientists, technologists, and psychiatrists to advance new evidence-based medicine that helps people living with moderate to severe mental health conditions.. . . . . . Competitive salary and equity package, and company-sponsored 401k plan to support your plans for today and tomorrow.. .
