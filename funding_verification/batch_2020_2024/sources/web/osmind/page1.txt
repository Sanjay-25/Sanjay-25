Real-world evidence can ”accelerate medical product development and bring new innovations and advances faster and more efficiently to the patients who need them.” . . US FDA . . . . . Our mission. . To empower clinicians and researchers to bring innovative mental health treatments to patients who need it most.. . We are scientists, technologists, psychiatrists, and other mission-driven professionals passionate about generating new evidence-based medicine to improve the lives of people with moderate to severe mental health conditions. As a Public Benefit Corporation , Osmind is committed to supporting the development of new, effective treatments for moderate to severe mental health conditions, and to ensure access to care for patients of all backgrounds. We achieve this through rigorous research, advocacy, and modern technology that will most effectively help the medical community advance breakthrough therapies.. . .
