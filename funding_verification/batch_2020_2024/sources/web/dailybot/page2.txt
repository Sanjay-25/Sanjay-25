Dailybot launched in 2017 with one obsession: async-first coordination for distributed teams. What started as automated standups grew into a full platform for keeping work visible at every level.. In 2021, we joined Y Combinator (S21) — accelerating our growth and connecting us with a network of founders who move fast. Thousands of teams across the world started running daily workflows through Dailybot..
