This Series A was led by Creandum , with participation from Y Combinator , Point Nine , Amino Collective , and Inflect Health . With this funding, we’re scaling our platform, growing our team, and expanding our API network to help healthcare organizations deliver smarter, more connected patient care. Alongside this milestone, we’re unveiling our new brand: Junction—a name that reflects our role in modernizing healthcare’s foundational infrastructure and bridging gaps between fragmented systems. Healthcare’s Infrastructure Problem The future of healthcare is in the home, not the hospital—but today’s infrastructure wasn’t built to support it. Every healthcare company delivering care outside traditional settings needs hospital-grade biometrics and physiological data. Yet, the systems managing this data rely on outdated infrastructure like manual lab integrations, faxes, and siloed devices—leading to:. Delayed care – It takes 6+ months to integrate labs and devices, slowing down new service launches.. Missed diagnoses – 1 in 5 lab orders require manual follow-ups, and 1 in 4 go unfulfilled due to inconvenient patient access.. Operational strain – Care teams waste hours on administrative work instead of focusing on patients.. High costs, low adherence – Most providers up-charge lab tests, while outdated workflows make testing inefficient. The healthcare industry is overdue for modern infrastructure—one that powers automation, improves patient access, and enables data-driven insights at scale. Junction: The Infrastructure for Patient-Centered Care Junction provides modern infrastructure for healthcare organizations, making patient data accessible, actionable, and automated across lab testing and device integrations. What Junction Does. Seamless Lab Ordering – Order lab tests across all 50 states, with results from 10+ labs (including LabCorp & Quest) via in-person or at-home testing.. Automated Workflows – Reduce manual work for clinical teams, keeping patient data flowing without admin burden.. Real-Time Patient Updates – Patients receive status updates & reminders, improving adherence while eliminating uncertainty. Device & Wearable Connectivity – 500+ wearables & medical devices, integrated via a single API to create a longitudinal view of patient health. “Junction is transforming healthcare by addressing its most critical pain points: scalability, convenience, and data-driven decision-making.” - Maitham Dib, Founder and CEO of Junction. Record Growth → Market Opportunity In the past year, Junction has seen exponential growth. We’ve expanded our wearable and lab integrations to serve more than 140 healthcare organizations, including digital health leaders like Found, Parsley Health, and Evidation. Across these organizations and others, we’ve supported 500K+ lab tests annually and over 2 million connected devices. But this is just the beginning. The healthcare industry is at a pivotal moment, and the demand for scalable technology infrastructure has never been greater. A Growing Need for Unified Health Data The opportunity for transformation is immense: a 2020 McKinsey report estimates that $250B of U.S. healthcare spending could shift to virtual care delivery, while the global virtual healthcare market is expected to grow to $450B by 2030 . Junction is uniquely positioned to make this a reality by bridging the gap between data collection, automation, and data-driven preventative care. Sabina Wizander, partner at Creandum, said: “Data is the bottleneck to unleashing the potential of new technologies like AI in healthcare. Patient data often sits in silos in legacy systems, captured using outdated methods like fax and pen and paper. Junction solves this problem, unifying and integrating healthcare data so that it can become actionable for preventative care.” As healthcare shifts toward automation, predictive analytics, and real-time decision-making, organizations need a modern infrastructure that can collect, connect, and activate patient data at scale. Why We Rebranded to Junction “Vital was always about helping healthcare systems operate more effectively. As Junction, our new brand reflects the pivotal role we play in connecting systems, data, and patient care seamlessly.” — Maitham Dib, CEO Junction represents the infrastructure layer that powers the next generation of healthcare innovation. Just as clearinghouses and practice management systems streamline administrative workflows, Junction is the connective layer for diagnostic data between devices, labs, and data-driven decision making. The future of healthcare is real-time, automated, and predictive but without the right infrastructure, these advancements remain out of reach. Junction represents our commitment to closing that gap.
