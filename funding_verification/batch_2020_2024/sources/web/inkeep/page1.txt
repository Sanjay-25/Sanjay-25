We're excited to announce $13M in seed funding to empower any customer-facing team to create and collaborate with agents.. Today, we're excited to announce $13M in seed funding, led by Khosla Ventures, GreatPoint Ventures, and Y Combinator with participation from Guillermo Rauch (CEO of Vercel), Dan Pinto (CEO of Fingerprint), and Colin Sidoti (CEO of Clerk), Rowan Brewer (Founder of Paymentology), Eli Brown (Founder of Guilded), Coho VC, and Myelin VC. The best teams invest in building elite Support, Product, Content, Sales, and Marketing teams dedicated to making every step in the prospect and customer journey delightful.. What if you could empower each of these teams to be 10x more effective, and have a customer experience to match? Leading companies like Anthropic, Midjourney, Pinecone, Clay, PostHog, and Solana use Inkeep today to empower their teams, supercharge their customer experience, and automate time-consuming workflows ( learn more ).. Agents Accessible to Any Team . Our mission is to empower any customer-facing team to create and collaborate with agents to get work done in one, unified place.. With Inkeep:. Support teams can deploy AI assistants as the first line of support. Product teams can create in-app copilots that guide users. Docs teams can use agents to turn support tickets into docs updates. Marketing teams can create blog posts out of frequently asked questions. Sales teams can create AI SDRs that build pipeline and accelerate sales. With Inkeep's No-Code Visual Builder, all these teams can create and manage agents visually or in plain English.. . . . . . . A Developer Platform to Match . Engineering teams work hand-in-hand with customer experience teams to ship AI systems, so Inkeep Agents can also be written and edited as code using our first-class Agent SDKs.. Our platform includes everything developers need to build conversational multi-agent systems: MCP and tools support, RAG and data connectors, a UI component library, and more.. Inkeep Agents transparently admit when they don't know or can't help, and every conversation is labeled and analyzed to determine if it warrants review.. With that peace of mind, teams can spend their time where it counts: using Inkeep's AI Content Writer to maintain an up-to-date knowledge base and AI CX Intelligence Agent to identify feature gaps and develop product roadmaps..
