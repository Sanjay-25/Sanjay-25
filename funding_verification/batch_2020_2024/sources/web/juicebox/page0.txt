About Juicebox. . . About Juicebox. . . About Juicebox. . . Juicebox is an AI recruiting platform built around PeopleGPT, a natural language search engine that replaces Boolean strings with plain-English queries. Recruiters describe who they need, and it searches 800M+ profiles across 30+ data sources, ranking candidates by fit in seconds.. . Beyond sourcing, Juicebox automates personalized multi-step outreach, tracks engagement, and syncs with 40+ ATS platforms like Greenhouse and Ashby — while AI agents run searches autonomously to keep pipelines full 24/7.. . . . . . .
