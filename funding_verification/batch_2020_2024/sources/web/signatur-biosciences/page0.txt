Our molecular technology makes diagnosing complex diseases simple. No need for laborious tests and expensive machines, our kits bring molecular intelligence to widely accessible PCR equipment. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Traditional PCR can’t keep up with modern medicine. . Conventional PCR tests detect one or two biomarkers — ideal for infections, but not for cancers or chronic diseases that depend on complex molecular patterns. . To capture these patterns today, samples must be sent to specialised central laboratories, where analysis depends on sophisticated equipment, complex workflows, expert staff, and multi-step data processing. . The result: high costs, long turnaround times, and limited access to precision diagnostics outside major reference centres — creating a real bottleneck for precision medicine adoption and leading to underserved disease areas. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . We simplify precision molecular diagnostics — without compromise. . Signatur Biosciences’ platform transforms standard qPCR machines into powerful precision diagnostic tools. . By condensing complex RNA signatures into a single PCR assay, we make precision diagnostics decentralised, faster, and more affordable — while maintaining the same clinical utility as reference-lab genomic assays. . Built for compatibility with standard qPCR machines and workflows, our kits integrate seamlessly into existing hospital infrastructure, enabling clinicians to deliver precision insights within days, not weeks. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
