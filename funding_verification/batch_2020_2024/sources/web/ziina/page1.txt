The story behind Ziina. In 2020, siblings Faisal, Sarah and Talal Toukan kept running into the same problem. Splitting dinner meant exchanging 23-digit IBANs. Paying a freelancer took days. Sending money to family required multiple apps and a maze of fees.. They saw the gap between the Arab World's ambition and its outdated financial tools. So they left Silicon Valley, joined Y Combinator and moved back to Dubai to build what should have existed all along.. . What started as the region's first social payments app evolved with the people who used it—entrepreneurs getting paid, restaurants running daily operations, families supporting each other instantly.. Today, Ziina powers money movement across the UAE, from individuals to fast-growing startups to some of the region's most innovative businesses.. . . . . The Ziina Vision.
