Join our Microschool Network . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Frequently Asked Questions . . . . . . . What is a microschool, and how is it different from a traditional school? . Microschools are small learning communities that prioritize personalized instruction, strong relationships, and flexibility—often with multi-age groups and project-based learning. . . . How does KaiPod help educators start a microschool? . In KaiPod’s Catalyst program, we provide guidance, tools, and hands-on support across planning, legal setup, operations, enrollment, and long-term sustainability, so you’re not figuring it out alone. . . . . Is KaiPod a franchise? . No. Each microschool is independently owned and operated. KaiPod supports founders while honoring their unique vision and local community. . . . . Do microschools in the KaiPod network follow a specific curriculum or teaching model? . No. KaiPod does not require a specific curriculum or teaching model. Each microschool is independently designed and led by its educator-founder, who chooses the curriculum, learning approach, and instructional methods that best serve their students. . What unites KaiPod-supported microschools isn’t a single program—it’s a shared commitment to small learning environments, strong relationships, and student-centered learning. We support educators with guidance, tools, and best practices, while preserving the freedom to teach in ways that align with their vision and community. . . . . How much does a microschool cost? . Tuition varies widely depending on location and program. Some families use education savings accounts (ESAs), scholarships, or other funding options where available. . . . . How do I know if starting a microschool is the right path for me? . Deciding whether to start a microschool takes reflection, research, and clarity around your goals. We’ve created a short 5-question Founder Assessment to see where you are on your journey to starting a microschool — and find out exactly what you should do next. . . . . . . . . . . . .
