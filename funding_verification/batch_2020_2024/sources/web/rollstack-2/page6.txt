December 16, 2025. . . 5. min read. . . Share on. . . . . . . . . Table of contents. . Link. . . . . ". . . Bridging the Gap. In the modern data stack, speed and trust are everything. But for too many data teams, the "speed" hits a wall the moment it’s time to present findings. You spend hours building the perfect data model and exploring metrics, only to spend even more hours taking screenshots and copy-pasting charts into slide decks.. Today, we are removing that bottleneck. Rollstack now integrates seamlessly with Omni. . This integration bridges the gap between BI dashboards and executive decision-making. By connecting Omni’s governed, AI analytics platform directly to Rollstack, teams can automate the creation and delivery of their most critical data driven presentations—from Weekly Business Reviews to QBRs—without ever worrying about stale data or manual errors.. Why This Matters. Omni has rapidly become a favorite for teams looking to accelerate AI adoption and self-service due to its built-in semantic layer and flexible UI. Rollstack completes that journey by handling the "Last Mile" of data delivery and insight creation.. Together, we enable a workflow where:. Omni acts as the source of truth: All metrics and business logic are defined and governed within the built-in semantic layer, then used to build verified visualizations and dashboards.. Rollstack acts as the data delivery platform: Rollstack transforms insight creation and data storytelling by automatically populating your Google Slides, PowerPoint presentations, and Google Docs with live, governed data from Omni. Serving as a comprehensive data delivery platform, Rollstack handles deck formatting, scheduling, and scaling, ensuring that accurate and current insights seamlessly integrate into existing workflows to drive critical business decisions.. What the Team at Omni Says. “Teams use Omni to build trusted metrics, visualizations, and dashboards that surface what the business needs to know. With Rollstack, those insights flow directly into docs and slides, giving stakeholders live data and clear context straight from the source.” — Jamie Davidson, co-founder & President The Result: Data Delivery at Scale. With this integration, you remove the manual friction of communicating data. Instead of spending hours on the tedious assembly of decks, your team can focus on the strategic work that matters: supporting teams with insights that move the business forward..
