What's the difference between Gauge Chat and Gauge Agents? Gauge Chat helps your brand get recommended when people ask AI for answers. It tracks your visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and other AI search platforms, shows how you compare to competitors, and helps you create content that earns mentions and citations.. Gauge Agents helps developer tools get selected and implemented by coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor. It shows how agents discover your product, read your docs, and attempt integrations, so you can identify what makes them choose your tool or abandon it.. Use Gauge Chat to improve how AI answers describe and recommend your brand. Use Gauge Agents to improve whether coding agents choose your product and successfully build with it. You can explore either product using the Chat / Agents switch at the top of this page.. . . . . . How does optimizing for AI compare to SEO? Many people are incorrectly saying that "SEO is dead". This is objectively false, and we see so in the data.. SEO and AI optimization are highly correlated. This is because AI is using search tools to retrieve data from the internet. This means we're still optimizing for search.. That being said, there are unique aspects of how AI searches the web that creates opportunities for brands. AI does not suffer the same sharp attention span dropoff that humans do; it also cares significantly less about things like domain authority. Instead, AI is solely focused on the relevancy of the search result and corresponding content to the original user query.. Because of this, AI will typically pull the top ~50 results for a given search, and distribute attributions much more evenly than Google. Humans almost never click past the first page; AI does every time. This means we can target the long tail for as many keywords as possible, rather than fight tooth-and-nail for the #1 position on a SERP.. . . . . . How should I think about measuring AI search and traffic? There are several key metrics that are important to think about with respect to AI search:. Mention Rate: What % of tracked AI answers is my brand mentioned in?. Citation Rate: What % of tracked AI answers cite my website?. Referral Traffic: How much traffic is AI sending directly to my website? What URLs are seeing the most traffic?. It's important to note that all of these measurements are imperfect in some way. Citation and Mention rate are only as good as the prompts configured. This is where Gauge's detailed research that finds real usecases and pain points becomes critical. Referral traffic also only represents a tiny fraction of your true visibility across AI answers, as users infrequently actually click on citations in generative engines. Estimates show that you may see as much as 100x the number of sessions where your brand is referenced when compared to the number of clickthroughs that will register to your web properties.. . . . . .
