Craftsmanship. . . We maintain an incredibly high bar for ourselves. Every product decision, every line of code, every customer interaction—in everything we do we strive for world class. We obsess over the details and constantly aim to raise the bar on quality.. . . . Velocity. . . Speed matters but speed without the right direction becomes wasted effort. Life at Terminal. . The resources to do the best work of your career. . . Equity and ownership. . . . We refresh grants as your scope expands. . . . . . Company offsites. . . . Twice a year we step out of the day-to-day for a multi-day company offsite. Office culture. . . . We work in person four days a week. It is not about keeping an eye on anyone, since everyone here is self-driven. It is that an early-stage company moves much faster in the same room: the relationship building, the context sharing, the decision making. It also builds camaraderie and is how the team becomes a team. Remote can feel like logging into the Matrix every morning, and we would much rather build something people feel part of.. . . . .
