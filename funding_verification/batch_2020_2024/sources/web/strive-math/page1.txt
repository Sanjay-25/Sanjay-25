Strive Math is a Singapore-based coding and math education provider on a mission to make learning more joyful and meaningful. We teach coding, math and custom STEM courses to kids aged 8 to 16, and build the tools to support teachers to deliver engaging learning experiences.. . . . . Our Story. Strive Math was founded by Stanford alumnus and Forbes 30 Under 30 lister Pulkit Agarwal and backed by Y Combinator. We started by teaching math through code, letting students grasp mathematical concepts by building fun games and visual simulations, and shifted to teaching coding as a standalone course when parents kept asking for coding as the primary focus. Strive's most popular offering is 1-1 coding classes, since coding remains one of the best ways to develop logic, problem solving, critical thinking, resilience, and creativity. Strive Math students engage with math, coding, and technology as something meaningful and creative, not something to be endured.. . In the press.
