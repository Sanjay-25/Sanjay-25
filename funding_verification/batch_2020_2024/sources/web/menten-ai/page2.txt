Menten AI is pioneering generative AI to design cyclic peptide therapeutics for drug targets beyond the reach of small molecules and biologics. Our proprietary platform, MAUD 1.0 , enables the rapid creation of macrocycles with drug-like properties such as oral bioavailability and cell permeability.. . We partner with top-10 pharma leaders (e.g., Bristol Myers Squibb) to accelerate preclinical discovery, and we are backed by world-class investors including Social Impact Capital, Uncork Capital, Khosla Ventures, and Y Combinator .. MAUD 1.0 . MAUD 1.0 ( M ulti-parametric A I for U nbiased D esign ) integrates generative AI , physics-based modeling , and quantum chemistry simulations to design novel peptide macrocycles from scratch—bypassing the limitations of traditional library screening.. . Unlike traditional machine learning approaches, MAUD 1.0 leverages physics-informed generative AI, eliminating the need for large training datasets while enabling efficient peptide design with atomic precision.. . Its multi-parametetric approach optimizes multiple properties simultaneously (e.g. potency, permeability, stability), creating a shortcut to the right chemical space and enabling faster, more effective therapeutic discovery.. .
