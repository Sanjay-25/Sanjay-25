MindsHub gives everyone the freedom to choose the models and providers that fit their work—whether they use AI to complete projects or build it into products. . . Our story Where we started.. . UC Berkeley · CA, 2018 Jorge Torres and Adam Carrigan released the first version of MindsDB in Berkeley in 2018. The name came from the “Minds” in Iain M. Banks’ Culture novels: intelligent systems that work alongside people. . The first product was an open-source query engine that brought machine-learning models to data inside databases. It later expanded to documents, knowledge bases, semantic search, and federated data access. . Today, MindsHub works toward the same goal in two ways. MindsHub Agents is an agent workspace for doing complex work with AI, from research and analysis to building software. MindsHub Inference provides one way to put intelligence into agents and products while remaining free to choose models and providers. The original MindsDB query engine remains available on GitHub and is maintained by the community. See how the product has evolved → . . . Our mission Give people the freedom to choose.. Access to intelligence should not depend on one model, one provider, or one closed platform. . . Freedom to choose. Choose the model and provider that fit the work. Change either without rebuilding everything around them.. . Open by design. We publish core agent technology as open source so people can inspect it, adapt it, and help shape what comes next.. . Help people do the work. MindsHub Agents helps people research, analyze, create, and build software instead of stopping at an answer in a chat window.. . Help developers build with intelligence. MindsHub Inference gives developers one way to use leading models in their own products while keeping providers interchangeable.. . . . . FAQ Questions about MindsHub and MindsDB.. . What is MindsHub? MindsHub gives everyone access to leading AI models without locking them into one model or provider. MindsHub Agents, an agent workspace, applies that choice to projects ranging from research and analysis to software development. MindsHub Inference brings it to agents and products built through an API. . Is MindsHub the same as MindsDB? MindsHub is the product; MindsDB is the company behind it. The product was renamed in 2026 because it had grown beyond databases. The company and team did not change. . Is MindsHub open source? Core MindsHub technology is open source, including Anton, the agent harness behind MindsHub Agents. We believe people should be able to inspect, adapt, and contribute to the systems they rely on. . Who is MindsHub for? MindsHub Agents is for anyone working on research, analysis, reporting, software development, and other complex projects. MindsHub Inference is for building AI into agents and products. . Who builds MindsHub? MindsHub is built by MindsDB, founded in 2018 in Berkeley by Jorge Torres and Adam Carrigan. MindsDB is backed by Benchmark, Mayfield, Y Combinator, and NVIDIA, with a global open-source community of 800+ contributors. . . Team A distributed team.. Team members work across five regions.
