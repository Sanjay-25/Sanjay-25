Security Visit our security portal . . Book a demo . . . . . . . About us . We build for the companies that move the world’s commodities.. . An operating system for the physical commodity trading industry, built by people who have lived the work.. . Join us . . . . Why we care. . Physical commodities feed, build, and power the world.. . They are the hidden operating system of the global economy, and almost everything around you passed through a trade that someone negotiated, financed, and moved.. It is one of the oldest and largest industries on earth, and one of the least served by modern software. While the rest of finance and logistics has been rebuilt around technology, physical commodity trading still runs on phone calls, PDFs, and systems built decades ago.. We think that is backwards. The people who move the physical world deserve the best software in it. That is what we are building.. . . . . . . Culture. . What we value. . 01 Earn the desk’s trust. Our customers move real cargo under real pressure, and they only trust software that earns it. We learn the work as well as they do, answer before they have to chase us, and take their problems on as our own. We win when they win.. . . 02 Move at market speed. Markets do not wait, and neither do we. The industry has gone decades without software that fits, so we ship, learn, and improve in days, not quarters. A small team that moves fast makes an outsized difference.. . . 03 Kill the busywork. “That is how it has always been done” is where we start, not where we stop. We hunt down the workaround, the re-keyed PDF, and the spreadsheet everyone tolerates, and replace them with something better.. . . 04 Own the outcome. We do what we say and own the result the customer feels, not just the task that produced it. No one here points at someone else’s lane.. . . . . . . Who we are. . The founding team. .
