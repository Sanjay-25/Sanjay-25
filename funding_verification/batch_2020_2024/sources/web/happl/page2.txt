What’s wrong with benefits. . Global benefits . need reimagining. AI is changing the way we all work but the benefits industry is still running on old tech. HR teams are left stuck in manual processes, overloaded with admin and unable to offer their people the support they really need.. . . . . Limited flexibility. Top-down solutions fail to recognise the individuality of every employee.. . . . . Not built for global complexity. Complicated tax laws across countries require bloated local tech resulting in time-consuming management.. . . . . Outdated systems. Legacy platform and dated tech simply means more manual work for you.. . . . . . . . . . We do things differently. . A global, connected system to run benefits. . Better benefits strategy starts when your platform can handle the admin itself. Your benefits connect to the systems, people and workflows you already have, running as one operating system instead of one more platform, with AI built in to do the heavy lifting.. . . . One cohesive global system. You can’t work well when your data sits across fragmented tools that aren’t even the same across all your entities.. . Happl integrates with 100+ HRIS and payroll platforms and automatically syncs your data. So you end up with one system that understands your people, everywhere.. . . . An operating system you can build on top of. Happl isn’t another benefits platform. It’s the infrastructure layer that lets you build a benefits system around how your organisation actually works.. . Like the operating system on your phone or laptop, Happl provides the foundation so you can add the tools, workflows and connections you need on top.. . It’s an adaptable system that helps benefits work your way, now and as your organisation scales.. . . . Personalised plans for everyone. Why we do this. . Benefits fit for the next generation of work. Happl began with a simple belief: benefits should make people feel like they belong at work, not excluded.. And it's personal. Our founder, Ben Towers MBE, started Happl after seeing people close to him struggle with their mental health and witnessed outdated systems let employees down. So he set out to build something better. Since then our work at Happl hasn't gone unrecognised and he is now an adviser on AI and tax as well as receiving an MBE for services to business and innovation.. The mission uncovered a deeper truth: benefits are one of the most powerful levers of inclusivity and appreciation at work. And with AI fully integrated into the process they can be easy to manage, access and personalise.. Today, Happl is a global platform helping HR and finance teams deliver flexibility, compliance and simplicity at scale, with inclusivity at the core.. . . . “I started Happl because I believe everyone deserves to feel a true sense of belonging at work. Inclusivity isn’t a nice to have, it’s the foundation of how we build benefits and culture.”. Ben Towers. CEO & Founder at Happl. . . . . . . . . . . Built by a truly global team.
