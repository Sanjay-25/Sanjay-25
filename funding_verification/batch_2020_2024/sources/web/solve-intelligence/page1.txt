Careers Open roles.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . FAQs.. . . . . . . . Do I need to know patent law coming in? For most roles, no. We’ve brought in excellent engineers and researchers with no legal background who’ve gone deep on the domain quickly. For some product and customer-facing roles it accelerates your contribution, but we’ll be explicit in the listing if it matters. . . . . . . Are roles in office? Yes. We have offices in London, New York, and Munich. We think being in the room together matters, particularly when building. . . . . . . Do you offer visa sponsorship? Yes. . . . . . . Can I reapply if I wasn’t selected? Yes. We make decisions based on timing, team needs, and fit at a specific moment, not a permanent verdict on someone’s ability. If you weren’t selected, we’d encourage you to reapply when a relevant role opens, particularly if your experience has developed in the meantime. . . . . . . Why don’t your job titles have seniority levels? Because we think they create more confusion than clarity, both internally and in hiring. What matters at Solve is scope of impact and ownership, not where you sit on a ladder. We’d rather talk honestly about what a role involves than have a title do that work imprecisely. . . . . . . Why don’t you list your benefits publicly? Partly because they vary by location, and a single list would be misleading. Partly because we’d rather have a real conversation about what matters to you than have you filter us out based on a line item. What we can say: we pay competitively, offer meaningful equity, and take the overall package seriously. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . FAQs. . . . . . . . Where is Solve based? We are headquartered in London, with offices in New York and Munich and colleagues working across the US and Europe. . . . . . . Do you support remote work? The answer depends on the role and location. Each role page states its working pattern and eligible locations clearly. . . . . . . What is the interview process? A direct initial conversation, a focused practical exercise, and a final conversation with founders and relevant team leads. . . . . . . Can I apply for more than one role? Yes. Apply to the role that best fits your experience and mention other relevant interests in your application. . . . . . . What benefits do you offer? We offer competitive salary, meaningful equity, and benefits appropriate to each hiring location. The details are shared during the process. . . . . . . What if I do not see the right role?
