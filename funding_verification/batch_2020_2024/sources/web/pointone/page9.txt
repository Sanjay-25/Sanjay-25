NEW YORK, September 29, 2026 — Baker Sterchi Cowden & Rice , a nationally recognized defense litigation firm, deployed PointOne across approximately 100 attorneys to bring automated time capture and more consistent billing workflows directly into the firm's SurePoint environment.. The deployment is part of Baker Sterchi's broader investment in the operational infrastructure needed to support a growing, multi-office firm: reducing administrative work for attorneys, capturing a more complete record of the work already being performed, and creating more consistent processes across the firm.. "We've actually captured more time overall and written off less time at the point of billing. We're also starting to see fewer write-offs from the clients too. Our time entries now are more descriptive of the work our firm is doing, and our clients appreciate that." . — Doug Doerfler, Executive Director. Baker Sterchi worked with PointOne to consolidate nearly 100 individual timekeeping rules into a consistent set of firmwide billing standards. What previously depended on individual knowledge and manual review could now be applied more systematically as time entries were created.. The result is a more connected workflow from the moment work happens through pre-bill review: less time spent reconstructing activity, greater consistency in time entries, and less administrative work for attorneys and operational teams.. . . . . . .
