They're in a thread from four years ago, a calendar invite from a job you've left, a follow you barely remember. The problem was never that you know nobody, it was that no tool could answer a question about the people you already know.. Happenstance answers it. How it works. . You connect the accounts. Happenstance reads who you email and how often, who sits in meetings with you, who you're connected to, who you follow, and who's in your Google Contacts. Email is read as headers only, which is enough to tell how well two people know each other.. It scores the relationships. Every tie carries evidence, so the search can rank an old colleague you speak to weekly above someone whose name you accepted once.. You ask in plain English. Every answer names the people, why they matched, and the path to each one: direct, through a friend, or through a group.. Friends and groups widen the reach. Your own accounts are the first degree. Add a friend and each of you can search the other's network, because sharing is always mutual. Join a group and every member opens their network to the rest, which turns a fund, a team or a community into one pool. What you can reach stops being the people you know, and becomes everyone known to anybody willing to help you.. . . . Seed investors in my network who back AI. Example . Every result is somebody you know, or somebody a friend or group can introduce you to.. How does Happenstance work? You connect your accounts. Headers only: who wrote to whom, and when. Message content and attachments never reach Happenstance servers. That's enough to tell how well you know someone, which is what the relationship score is built from.. Is Happenstance secure? Happenstance is SOC 2 compliant and DTI Trust Level 2. Your connections are searchable only by you, plus the friends you accept and the groups you join. The security page lists what is stored and for how long.. . . How people use it. What are friends and groups? A friend is someone who agreed to share networks with you, in both directions. A group is a team, fund, cohort or community whose members pool their networks into one searchable place. Groups are free and unlimited, and the largest make 380,000+ people searchable.. Is Happenstance a CRM? The kind you search. It does the half of a personal CRM most people buy one for, knowing who you know and finding the right person, from your accounts rather than from data entry. Reminders, per-person notes and a pipeline live in a CRM you maintain; a team running deals runs one beside Happenstance. That is why we call it a personal CRM you search instead of maintain.. Can I use Happenstance from ChatGPT or Claude? Yes. There's a public API, an MCP server, a ChatGPT app, Claude and Claude Code integrations, and a CLI, so the same question works inside the assistant you already use.. Is there a Happenstance iPhone app? Yes. Happenstance AI is on the App Store for iPhone, free, on iOS 26.2 or later.
