Aiming to Improve the Lives of Patients Undergoing Vascular Surgery. . . . . . About VenoStent. A Clinical-Stage Therapeutic Medical Device Company. We’re a clinical-stage therapeutic medical device company that’s developed finely-tuned, bioabsorbable wraps intended to improve outcomes for the 5 million vascular surgeries performed each year. . . . . . . . . . Technology. A Novel Technology with High Potential. We’re developing a bioabsorbable perivascular wrap, SelfWrap, that goes around arteriovenous (AV) access sites at the time of AV creation surgery. The wrap is intended to improve the usability (i.e. maturation) and durability (i.e. patency) of these sites for chronic kidney disease (CKD) patients requiring hemodialysis. If successful, this could improve the quality of life for CKD patients requiring hemodialysis. . . . . . . . Bioabsorbable. Materials are absorbed by the body, unlike alloy-based products. . . . .
