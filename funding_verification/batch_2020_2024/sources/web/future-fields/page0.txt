Founded in 2018, Future Fields is on a mission to change how we do science for humanity and the planet.. Our biomanufacturing platform, the EntoEngine ® , is the first synthetic biology system in the world to use fruit flies for recombinant protein production. Powered by people and fly by design, we’re equipping industries with the biomolecular tools they need to sustainably, ethically, and economically conduct great science.. As of 2023, our lab has been Green-certified by My Green Lab, and our FGF2 are the first growth factors in the world to receive the ACT Environmental Impact Factor Label. Future Fields is a proud business member of 1% for the Planet and the United Nations Global Compact initiative. Learn more about how we’re transforming science at futurefields.io.. .
