Cloudanix Joins AWS ISV Accelerate Program. Cloudanix, a YCombinator-backed cloud security platform, is delighted to announce it has joined the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Independent Software Vendor (ISV) Accelerate Program. This co-sell initiative connects independent ISVs with the AWS Sales organization, helping drive new business and accelerate sales cycles. Cloudanix safeguards critical business assets with proactive threat detection, real-time monitoring, and automated remediation. By joining the AWS ISV Accelerate Program, Cloudanix underscores its commitment to delivering advanced cloud security solutions that complement AWS offerings and protect organizations across industries. The partnership empowers businesses to leverage AWS’s infrastructure while benefiting from Cloudanix’s expertise, optimizing cloud environments and ensuring top-notch protection against cyber threats. Testimonial: “We are delighted to be recognized as an AWS ISV Accelerate Partner. This underscores our dedication to providing best-in-class cloud security solutions to our customers. With the unparalleled support from AWS, we are excited to further enhance our cloud security platform and help businesses thrive in the digital landscape securely.” — Sujay Maheshwari, Co-founder & CEO . . . A Comprehensive Cloud Security Platform. About Cloudanix. Cloudanix is a Y Combinator-backed cloud security platform that empowers organizations to identify and remediate security gaps across code, cloud, and workloads from a single dashboard. By consolidating multiple security solutions, providing smart remediation workflows, and offering IAM Just-In-Time access, Cloudanix increases productivity, saves time, and ensures business continuity. Available as both SaaS and CloudPrem deployments with data residency options, Cloudanix combines real-time monitoring, threat detection, and automated remediation in an intuitive interface backed by advanced analytics. Its founders and teams bring extensive experience in infrastructure and IAM, ensuring a solution designed to solve real-world security challenges.
