Unthread is an AI-native helpdesk that turns conversations in Slack and Microsoft Teams into trackable tickets, answers common questions from connected knowledge, runs support workflows, and gives teams one place to manage requests across channels.. . Questions, answered. Who is Unthread for? + Unthread supports IT, HR, customer support, employee support, and RevOps teams that manage requests in Slack, Microsoft Teams, email, or shared support channels.. What makes Unthread different? + Unthread combines conversational ticketing, agentic AI, workflow automation, knowledge management, analytics, and integrations instead of treating Slack as a notification layer for a separate helpdesk.. Is Unthread a Slack helpdesk? + Yes. Unthread is built to create, manage, and resolve support tickets directly from Slack conversations, while also supporting Microsoft Teams, email, portals, and connected channels.. Can Unthread support IT service management? + Yes. Unthread includes ITIL-ready capabilities such as CMDB, asset tracking, change management, problem management, CAB approvals, SLAs, knowledge, and omnichannel intake.. Does Unthread use a knowledge base to answer questions? + Yes. Unthread uses connected knowledge to answer requests and can capture resolved conversations to propose or update documentation so future answers stay current.. Can Unthread automate work in other tools? + Yes. Unthread can trigger workflows and actions across connected systems such as ticketing tools, CRMs, HR systems, identity tools, and other business applications.. How do teams evaluate Unthread? + Unthread does not offer self-serve signup.
