Importers and customs brokers are confronting a rare but massive compliance opportunity: after the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariffs were struck down by the Supreme Court, billions in previously paid duties may now be refundable. Yet without proper tooling, most teams are still trying to calculate refunds manually, leaving money on the table and deadlines at risk.. Introducing Reform’s Tariff Refund Calculator : a fast, accurate way to quantify duty refund eligibility from IEEPA-related tariffs in minutes and turn compliance change into recoverable cash.. Refund exposure is real, but hard to measure . Assessing refund eligibility means reconstructing what was paid versus what should have been paid under current law. That requires identifying entries assessed under IEEPA authority, mapping historical tariff treatments, and recalculating duties across thousands or millions of lines. Most teams still do this with spreadsheets, manual rate tables, and ad-hoc models. It’s slow, error-prone, and typically only attempted reactively once a suspected case is raised, often too late to protest or correct.. From historical data to refund estimate in minutes . Reform’s Tariff Refund Calculator automates what would otherwise take weeks:. Users upload historical entry summaries and duty payment data. Accepts variety of input formats (e.g. spreadsheet, PDF, XML). The tool identifies entries subject to IEEPA tariffs. It reconstructs alternative duty treatment and calculates overpayments. Output includes line-by-line refund estimates with exportable detail. Results arrive in minutes, not weeks, with structured outputs ready for PSCs, protests, or legal follow-up.. Timing matters more than ever . The Supreme Court ruling created real refund potential, but recovery windows are procedural and diminishing. Importers without scalable tools risk missing protest windows and leaving money unclaimed. Brokers can unlock value for clients by surfacing refunds early, turning compliance into a revenue driver.. Quantifying just a small percentage of historical duty spend often translates into six- or seven-figure recoveries. That’s immediate cash flow improvement with minimal operational change.. The Tariff Refund Calculator works standalone, requires no complex integrations, and addresses a challenge every importer and broker now faces, unlocking refunds before regulators or competitors do. As tariff authority and legal interpretations continue to evolve, those who quantify their exposure today will capture the value tomorrow.
