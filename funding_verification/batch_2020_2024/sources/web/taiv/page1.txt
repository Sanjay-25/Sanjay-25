It started over drinks at a bar . . Like most good ideas, Taiv started over drinks at a bar. The game cut to a commercial break and the screen started promoting a rival spot down the street. That sparked a question between the founders: if you could rebuild TV from the ground up, how would you make it better for the people who own the room and the people in it? . Years later, that question still drives us. Our AI streamlines operations for thousands of managers and makes the room more fun for millions of people every month.
