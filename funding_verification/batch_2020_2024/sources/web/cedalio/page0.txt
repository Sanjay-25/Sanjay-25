Cedalio gets vendor invoices read, matched and validated before they enter the ERP; the finance team only touches the exceptions. Its AI agents read each invoice, match it against the purchase order and receipt, catch duplicates, validate taxes with ARCA, SAT, DIAN, SII and Receita Federal, calculate withholdings and post it to the ERP. Cedalio also validates utility bills (electricity, gas, water, telecom) against the regulated tariff. It serves finance and administration teams at companies with 200 to 5,000 employees across Latin America. Customers include Galicia, Ambev, La Segunda, Cavalieri, ACA, 47 Street, Lartirigoyen and Supervielle.
