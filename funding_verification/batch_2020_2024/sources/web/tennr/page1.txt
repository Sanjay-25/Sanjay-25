Solving the pre-visit patient processing problem will massively reduce patient delays and denials across the U.S. healthcare system. And the problem is completely solvable.. . . NUMBER 1. No patient should fall through the cracks.. The auth that expired. The patient who went dark. The chart note that never came back. Patients leak out of the system at every step. Practices lose patients to these every day, adding up to people who never got care. We built a platform to own them, so every patient keeps moving and everyone (practice, referrer, patient) can see exactly where things stand.. . . NUMBER 2. We believe healthcare technology companies need to meet providers where they’re at.. Providers are drowning in back-and-forths, ever-changing payor guidelines, and 45 minute phone tree wait times. We meet providers where they’re at, providing visibility and fetching missing information automatically, without forcing referring or receiving providers to change their processes.. . . NUMBER 3. AI isn’t the point.. The point is a patient who receives care and a claim that doesn’t come back denied. We use purpose-built models where precision matters and we publish how they benchmark, but the model isn’t the product. We’re opinionated about the workflows and how we deploy them.
