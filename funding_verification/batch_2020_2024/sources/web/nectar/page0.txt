About us. We're engineering data infrastructure to power the clean energy economy.. GHG levels are rising and so is the demand for energy. We've previously built ad-hoc software tools for sustainability and energy teams. From helping them, we learned that accurate and reliable utility data collection blocked most organizations from taking effective action against climate change.. Solving climate change requires several major steps, and it boils down to how we use and harness energy. Cleaner energy sources have already disrupted electricity supply and lowered prices in recent years. However, many companies are not well-positioned to benefit from this change.. The core challenge: companies barely understand their energy usage enough to start optimizing. Prices fluctuate every 15 minutes, and without data, companies can't find the best rates and take advantage of cleaner energy.. That's where Nectar comes in. Our first product is a utility intelligence platform that aggregates data from utility bills and online portals. We're building the data infra to power the future clean energy economy, and we're just getting started.. . 1M+ Hours saved for customers . 150+ Companies tracked . 100k+ Measurements tracked . . . .
