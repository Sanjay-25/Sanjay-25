The name is ironic. The product is not.. Bland is supposed to be bland. The best infrastructure is the kind you forget is there. Like a flight where nothing goes wrong. No turbulence, no announcements, no incidents. That is the goal for every call.. . . We bet $1 million on GPUs with $400,000 in the bank.. In 2023, we pivoted mid-YC into a category nobody believed in. A hundred and eighty investors rejected the pitch with the same line: phone calls won't exist a year from now. Isaiah slept on a closet floor in San Francisco.. We spent every dollar we had on GPUs, then $600,000 more, because the only way to build voice AI that actually works is to build the whole stack. Wrappers around someone else's models were never going to be enterprise-grade. Latency would always lose to the layers underneath.. We were right. A $60,000 billboard later, Bland hit $2 million in ARR in four months.. . . . . We don't wrap anyone's models. We built our own.. Voice, LLM, text-to-speech, speech-to-text. All in-house. The bet on GPUs was the bet that you can't shortcut this. Every API call you don't own is latency. Every model you license is a model you can't fix. Our stack is the stack we're accountable for.. . . . “Humans simply can't work 24/7, handle millions of phone calls simultaneously, or be trained to a company's exact liking down to its voice and behavior.
