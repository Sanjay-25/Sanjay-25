Verifiable Announces Data & AI Specialist Brett Dooies as Head of Product. . . . . July 22, 2025 (Austin, Texas). . SHARE THIS ARTICLE. . . . . . . . . . . . . With a proven track record of bringing AI-enabled software to market, Dooies will be instrumental in driving the company’s next phase of growth. . Verifiable , the industry-leading healthcare credentialing and monitoring platform, today announced the appointment of Brett Dooies as Head of Product. In this role, Dooies will lead the continued development of Verifiable’s industry-leading software and verification platform, leveraging the latest advancements in AI to streamline provider credentialing and network monitoring. . ‍. Dooies brings over a decade of experience to the company, with a demonstrated history of developing transformative enterprise software for highly regulated industries. Most recently, he led Data and AI product development at nCino, Inc. —a global technology company and top Salesforce partner—where he applied advanced AI and analytics to help financial institutions enhance decision-making, manage risk, and elevate the customer experience.. ‍. During his tenure, Dooies played a pivotal role in scaling nCino from startup to global leader, serving more than 2700 customers and generating over $500 million in annual revenue. Committed to pushing the industry forward, his team led product innovation on the Salesforce platform and spearheaded critical integrations with key technology partners. His teams launched groundbreaking AI solutions that streamlined operations and delivered real-time, data-driven insights across the financial services ecosystem.. ‍. “Similar to when I joined nCino nearly a decade ago, I’m coming to Verifiable at a transformative moment for both the company and the industry, right as technology development reaches a critical inflection point,” said Brett Dooies, Head of Product. “The company’s API-first infrastructure, industry-leading primary source verification automation, and seamless Salesforce integration will enable us to fully leverage the latest advancements in AI – setting us up to address a critical market need. Drawing on my experience transforming cloud banking through artificial intelligence and automation, I am excited about advancing innovations that drive operational excellence, uphold regulatory compliance, and improve data accuracy across the healthcare ecosystem.”. ‍. Today’s news follows several years of strong growth for Verifiable, including $47 million raised from leading investors and securing high-profile customers such as Humana, Modern Health, LifeStance Health, Midi Health, Grow Therapy, and more. The company’s momentum has been recognized with multiple industry honors, including Newsweek’s Greatest Startup Workplaces (2025), Salesforce Partner Innovation Award (2024), CB Insights’ Digital Health 50 (2023), and Forbes’ Next Billion-Dollar Startups (2023), underscoring Verifiable’s growing impact on the future of healthcare operations.. ‍. "From day one, Verifiable has been driven by innovation, delivering a platform that has helped customers reduce credentialing turnaround times by up to 98% and realize immediate seven-figure cost savings,” said Nick Macario, CEO of Verifiable. “Whether through our enterprise-grade software enabling organizations to manage credentialing in-house or our NCQA-certified CVO, our data-rich foundation now powers a new generation of automation at scale. I'm excited for Brett to lead this next chapter of product, pushing beyond the limits of traditional automation to reduce administrative costs and unlock greater efficiency with auditable precision.". ‍.
