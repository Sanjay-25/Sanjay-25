How Greenboard and Kroll Help Investment Advisors Unify Compliance. . Katie Tumurbat. Director of Marketing. . . Please share on. . . . . . . . All Articles. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Compliance teams don’t struggle because they lack guidance. They struggle because that guidance is hard to execute at volume, in realtime, under ever-changing regulatory updates and scrutiny. . Firms can work with experienced advisors, define strong supervisory frameworks, and document clear policies. But once that work is delivered, it often lives in static reports. The actual execution gets distributed across disconnected systems. Supervision happens in one platform, attestations in another, and vendor oversight somewhere else entirely.. The guidance is sound. The execution is fragmented. When an exam arrives, proving how the two connect becomes a scramble.. That’s the gap Greenboard was built to address. It’s also why we’re proud to announce our partnership with Kroll .. Greenboard and Kroll partner to connect regulatory guidance directly to execution, giving compliance teams a single system to apply, track, and document decisions in real time.. The gap between guidance and execution. Most compliance tools focus on storing activity. They archive communications, track certifications, or log approvals. What they don’t do well is connect regulatory guidance to the day-to-day work of running a compliance program.. As a result, compliance officers spend time translating policies into workflows, chasing documentation across systems, and reconstructing decisions after the fact.. This is where breakdowns happen. Not because the policy is unclear, but because the execution is inconsistent or hard to trace.. A unified approach means the guidance doesn’t sit outside the system. It shapes how the work is actually performed.. Why Kroll. Kroll has spent nearly 100 years advising organizations on risk, governance, and regulatory complexity. Their Financial Services Compliance and Regulation practice works directly with firms to interpret SEC and FINRA requirements, design supervisory frameworks, and prepare for examinations.. What sets Kroll apart is how closely their work reflects real operational environments. Their guidance is grounded in how compliance programs function day to day, not just how they are documented.. That makes them a natural partner for Greenboard . Kroll understands what good compliance looks like in practice. Greenboard is built to make it operational. . What this partnership changes. Through this partnership , Kroll’s advisory team and Greenboard’s AI-native platform operate in the same environment.. Kroll helps firms design or refine their compliance programs. Greenboard provides the infrastructure to run those programs across communications supervision, marketing review, employee certifications, and vendor oversight.. For compliance teams, this changes how work moves.. Guidance is no longer delivered as a separate report. It is reflected directly in how workflows are configured, reviewed, and documented. Policies are applied consistently. Reviews and escalations are captured as they happen. Decisions are recorded with the context needed to explain them later.. When examiners ask how a decision was made, the answer already exists in the system.. A shift toward a system of action. More than 500 financial institutions use Greenboard to manage compliance operations. This partnership extends that model by connecting advisory guidance with execution in a way that is structured and auditable.. Greenboard is also part of Kroll’s broader RegTech ecosystem, which brings together providers across risk and compliance. Within that ecosystem, Greenboard is where compliance work is carried out and documented.. For firms facing increasing regulatory pressure, this shift matters.. Compliance has always been about demonstrating that a program works. This partnership makes that easier to prove.. ‍. ‍ Learn more .
