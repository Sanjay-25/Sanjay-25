Question & Answers. . What is Docsum?. . . . . . . Docsum is an AI-powered contract review and negotiation platform that helps in-house legal, finance, and operations teams accelerate pre-signature contracting. Using Docsum, teams can review, redline, and analyze agreements directly in Microsoft Word through our secure add-in, or via email agents that automate redlines and clause suggestions during negotiation. Every document processed through Docsum stays under your control — it is encrypted, processed securely, and never used to train AI models. The platform integrates with tools like DocuSign, Google Drive, and Outlook, enabling fast and compliant collaboration without exposing sensitive data.. . . . .
