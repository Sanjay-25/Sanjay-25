Milestone. 1,827,264 . . Interactions successfully automated across Yuma-powered support experiences.. . Yuma's Origin Story. In November 2022, just days before ChatGPT's debut, our founder Guillaume launched one of the first AI support apps on the Gorgias Store. The idea was simple: use generative AI to handle e-commerce support with real accuracy.. Dozens of merchants reached out overnight to test the prototype. The feedback was immediate and clear: generative AI was going to change customer support, and Yuma was born.. . . What is Yuma?. Yuma AI is an AI customer experience platform that resolves support, sales, and social conversations end to end for ecommerce brands, inside the helpdesk they already use. Its AI agents read live order, product, and customer data, then take real actions like refunds, reships, order edits, and subscription changes, so conversations are fully resolved rather than deflected. Brands pay only for the tickets Yuma fully resolves.. . Who uses Yuma. More than 100 ecommerce brands across North America and Europe run their customer experience on Yuma, including:. Shopify and DTC brands handling 1,000+ support tickets a month. CX teams on Gorgias, Zendesk, Kustomer, Gladly, or Salesforce Service Cloud who want automation without switching helpdesks. Subscription brands running Recharge, Skio, Stay AI, or Loop. Brands selling in several countries that need support in multiple languages. CX agencies and BPOs running support for several brands at once. Subscription and B2B commerce platforms with complex account and order workflows. . . . . What Yuma does. Its AI agents resolve support, sales, social, chat, and voice conversations end to end, inside the helpdesk the brand already uses.. . . . . Who founded Yuma AI, and when? Yuma was founded in 2022 by Guillaume Luccisano, a three-time Y Combinator founder. It started as one of the first generative AI support apps on the Gorgias App Store, and it joined Y Combinator in 2023.. . . . . Where is Yuma AI based? Yuma is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, with a second office in Barcelona, Spain. The team is around 30 people across both offices and remote.. . . . . How much does Yuma AI cost? Yuma uses performance-based pricing: you pay only for tickets the AI fully resolves, with plans starting at $14,400 per year. Contracts are annual and can be paid monthly or annually, and every contract starts with a free 30-day trial.. . . . . How long does it take to go live with Yuma? Most brands go live within one to two weeks. Yuma's onboarding team connects your helpdesk and store, imports your knowledge, and rolls out automation gradually so your team can review quality.. . . . . Do I need to switch helpdesks to use Yuma? No. Yuma installs inside Gorgias, Zendesk, Kustomer, Salesforce Service Cloud, Gladly, Re:amaze, Front, Sprinklr, and Zoho Desk, so your team keeps working where it already does.. . . . . How is Yuma different from Gorgias AI, Zendesk AI, or Siena? Yuma is helpdesk-agnostic, charges only for fully resolved tickets, and takes real order and subscription actions across 50+ integrations.
