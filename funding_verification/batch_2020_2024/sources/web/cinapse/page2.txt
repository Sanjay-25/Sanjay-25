Cinapse builds modern production scheduling software for the media and entertainment industry.. . Founded in 2020 by assistant directors, producers, and technologists frustrated by the outdated, archaic tools still used to schedule high-budget films and television series, Cinapse was created to bring clarity, speed, and collaboration to the heart of physical production.. . The schedule is the foundation of every project. Without it, there’s no accurate budget, no real plan, no path forward. It’s the map that takes us from idea to image.. . ‍ Cinapse helps filmmakers draw that map better, faster, and together.. . Today, Cinapse powers scheduling for productions of every size — from independent films to major studio series. Thousands of ADs, UPMs, and producers use Cinapse to collaborate more effectively, stay aligned, and keep their productions on time and on budget.. . Our mission is to equip filmmakers with intuitive tools to build better schedules, collaborate seamlessly, and bring their stories to life with less friction. . . By streamlining scheduling, Cinapse helps productions spend less time on software and more time on storytelling. ‍ . Cinapse + Wrapbook. . ‍ In 2025, Cinapse was acquired by Wrapbook , the fastest-growing payroll and production finance platform in Hollywood. Wrapbook is backed by Andreessen Horowitz, Bessemer Venture Partners, Tiger Global, and Jeffrey Katzenberg’s WndrCo, having raised over $150M and most recently valued at $750M .. . Together, we’re laying the foundation for the first truly connected production system, one we plan to bring to life in the coming years. A future where:. . - The schedule and budget stay in sync automatically. - Hot costs update in real time as the plan evolves. - Payroll and real cash-flow needs are informed directly by the actual schedule. - Production and finance teams share a single, living source of truth. .
