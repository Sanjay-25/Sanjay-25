Sep 28, 2026 # In the Salesforce app, you can now change the entitlement on an amendment offer directly from its form — a Change link reopens the entitlement picker — and Suger skips the picker entirely when a Cloud Partner Selection mapping already names the base entitlement. The account widget gained a List and Board view with a search box on every tab that has rows (Marketplace, Co-Sell, Funding and Contacts), each remembering its own choice, and Link Opportunity now offers entitlement and offer lookup filters. Salesforce app offers . AWS and Azure co-sell auto-accepts now run as their own workflow, visible in Logs with the time each was accepted, and are sent from the stored referral. Manual Link Offer and Unlink Offer now work at any stage once a referral is approved, and the AWS referral API accepts partial updates with merge=true, so an integration can patch a single field without resending the whole referral. Co-sell configuration Referral API . Console GCP offer forms gained an Internal Notes field for your team and now block an expiry date more than three months out — and past start or end dates — right in the date picker, and GCP offer history names the person who started each change. When a marketplace rejects a usage report downstream, the usage record group now shows a Report Failed status with the reason explained in plain language, instead of the failure living only in internal logs. Create a GCP private offer Usage metering . AWS fund-request records in Salesforce now carry the cash-request PO and payee, the claim dates and additional report fields. The Azure Marketplace integration card gained an Update Credential action, and Suger now warns you 30, 7 and 1 days before an Azure client secret is due to expire. . On a partner record, the Partner Manager and Partner Champion fields are now searchable by name or email, and you can register a deal on a partner's behalf before the relationship type is set — Suger prompts you to set it first. Work a partner record . Fixes this week: offer EULA attachments no longer duplicate or mismatch, and AWS offer creation catches more validation problems up front instead of failing at AWS; AWS resale authorizations can pick a currency and SaaS resale commit terms run up to 144 months; HubSpot offers preserve CRM-mapped commit terms and dimension rates; and the multi-channel Revenue dashboards resolved a set of UI and data issues. Email notification settings were consolidated into a single searchable catalog with a per-scope sender and on-demand Cc and Bcc. . . . . Sep 21, 2026 # Partners can now reach you without an invitation. A public deal-registration link lets a partner with no Suger account register a deal through a form, and every ISV now has a default partner sign-up form. The branded partner login asks for a work email and sends the visitor the right way — to sign in, to apply, or to join their company's partner organization, where join requests are now approved automatically. An invitation and an application for the same company can no longer cross, and each application expands to show everything the partner submitted. Public deal registration link Partner portal login page . Co-sell and commission lifecycle events now post to the seller's Slack channel alongside the email. Skilljar course completions now flow back into Suger, so a journey step pointed at a Skilljar course completes on its own. Every number in the partner portal has a definition tooltip, the co-sell metrics on a partner's page are recalculated hourly, and the partner's Co-Sells table now pages and searches across all of that partner's co-sells. Work a partner record . Funding requests from HubSpot's deal card now cover all seven AWS funding programs: MDF, POC, MAP, PIF GenAI POC, PIF GenAI MDF, ISV Workload Migration and MPOPP Grow. Funding records show up in Salesforce global search, the fund-request progress stepper shows each application's actual AWS stage in the console, Salesforce and HubSpot, and the Market Development Funds attachment types now match AWS's own list. In co-sell, the Partners & Status table shows your own side's status next to your partners', and Auto-Link again links accepted offers to ACE opportunities that are already Launched. Co-sell funding . Each Suger organization now gets its own AWS Marketplace EventBridge rule, connection and role, so two organizations that share one AWS account both receive their events. AWS renewal configuration accepts up to 86 installments, matching AWS, and reads the contract term and renewal schedule from the actual dates in the console, Salesforce and HubSpot. Offers that expire in AWS Marketplace are now closed in Suger by the hourly sync, and an AI-API OpenAPI spec is checked against AWS's rules when you upload it rather than failing later in AWS. AWS Marketplace integration Pre-authorized auto-renewal . Google Cloud private offers created through Google's API are more dependable: synced offers cancel and extend through the right channel, resubmitting a failed offer keeps your edits, the expiry date must fall within three months, and cloning a synced offer starts with its customer, notes, deal type and billing cadence filled in. The Professional Services offer form gained the Proration toggle. Snowflake no longer creates a duplicate private offer, and Snowflake marketplace revenue now syncs into Revenue Records. In Salesforce, you can choose a lookup field whose related record holds the attachments to offer as the EULA. Create a GCP private offer . The received-usage log in Metering can now be searched by the invoice ID your billing platform assigned, so usage reconciles against an external invoice. Chargebee metered overage can now be reported to the marketplace automatically every hour. Analytics moved to Superset 6.1: pinned dashboards appear as tabs, a public dashboard shared by another organization can be pinned, and a dashboard left open renews its session instead of expiring. Metering Analytics quick guide . . . . Sep 14, 2026 # An organization can now connect more than one AWS Marketplace seller account. Add the next account from the AWS Marketplace card on the Integrations page, choose which account is the default, and pick the account when you list a product; each product, offer and entitlement stays with the account it was listed or sold under. With EventBridge events connected, offers released outside Suger — from the AWS Marketplace Management Portal, the Catalog API, or as renewals AWS generates — now sync as soon as the event arrives instead of waiting for the hourly sync. AWS Marketplace integration . Pre-authorized auto-renewals with a percentage-range uplift can now be adjusted before the adjustment deadline — in the console, Salesforce and HubSpot. Set one uplift or price each dimension separately, preview the renewal prices, then save a draft or publish the renewal offer to the buyer; the renewal details show the Uplift status, and Suger can email a reminder once per renewal cycle, from 30 days before the deadline, while the uplift is still pending. Usage dimensions no longer rule out renewal terms: an offer with product commits qualifies, subject to AWS's own validation. Pre-authorized auto-renewal . Bookings on the Revenue overview now measures contract value: the value of entitlement terms that start in the period, where a plan or quantity change adds only its change. Revenue Recognition was redesigned around four summary cards, a period summary that rolls the deferred balance forward channel by channel, revenue trends with a 12-month recognition forecast, the schedule table, and a queue of revenue records that still need a schedule. Revenue Recognition Revenue overview . Partners and sellers can act on a co-sell straight from the email: the Accept co-sell, Mark Closed Won, Mark Closed Lost and Approve commission change buttons are personal to the recipient, work once, and expire after 24 hours, and co-sell and commission emails can now go out from the acting user's own connected Gmail. Partner management also gained an Applications list of every request to partner with you, with Approve and Decline on pending ones; links to your NDA or partner agreement in invitation emails; and a Marketplace connection tab that links a CPPO to its co-sell. Track and respond to deals Collaborations . More offer options across the marketplaces. Azure resale authorizations can now be granted from the Salesforce and HubSpot CPPO forms, which leave out the customer markets Microsoft rejects for resale; Snowflake private offers accept up to five PDF attachments from the console or Salesforce; GCP private offers gained a Proration switch for monthly billing; and Oracle offers now set a billing start — on acceptance or on a date you choose — and a billing end, with durations sent to Oracle in days. CPPO in Salesforce GCP private offers . Failures now surface where you work instead of passing silently. When AWS rejects a change to a live offer, the offer shows Update Offer Failed with AWS's reason, and History explains the rejection; on co-sells, an AWS rejection of a CRM edit, a failed invitation accept or decline, and a CRM Closed Won that could not close the co-sell each appear on the referral with the reason. Co-sell update emails now carry a What Changed table, failure emails the newest reason with an Open in CRM link, and email templates can be duplicated. Co-sell status Email notifications . . . . Sep 7, 2026 # AWS Marketplace pre-authorized auto-renewal is now supported end to end. Sellers configure a renewal count, a lockout window, a fixed or ranged uplift, and an optional renewal payment schedule; synchronized offers and agreements then carry the authoritative renewal state and the renewal chain that produced it, with lifecycle notifications on change. The same configuration and read-back is available in the console, Salesforce and HubSpot. Private offers on AWS . Partner Co-Sell gains a board view beside the existing list — the same query, the same search and filters, rendered as columns drawn from the real co-sell stages. Dragging a card between stages persists the move with an optimistic-concurrency check, and the closing stages are not patched blind: they open the same dialogs the deal page uses, so a close-won or close-lost still collects what it needs. Referral lists can now also be sorted on partner timestamps. Co-sell docs . Partner training can now run in an external LMS. A course can point at content hosted outside Suger, version-pinned the same way a SCORM package is, with Suger acting as the identity provider in front of it and a per-enrollment ledger recording each open. Partners sign in automatically when they open an external course. Build a training course Take a training course . Product tagging is live on the console. Settings › Private Offer gains a switch and one row per cloud showing tag coverage, with a dialog for tagging that cloud's products; the Products list and each product's detail page show the tag. Turning contact routing on starts a backfill that routes every product already tagged. Private offers . A partner can now be removed from an accepted deal registration, and the entitlement list can be filtered by external ID. Multi-channel revenue operations and reporting were improved across the board. Partner management Revenue . Two speed-ups you should feel on larger accounts: AWS agreements now sync incrementally by update time rather than re-reading the whole set, and the entitlement list's sort order is indexed with a bounded pagination count, so paging through a long list no longer slows down as it goes. . . . . Aug 31, 2026 # Unlinked AWS co-sell referrals can now be matched to CRM opportunities in bulk, straight from the co-sell list. A match pass compares each opportunity’s account website and name against the referral’s customer, sorts what survives into high match, needs review, and no match — keeping a failed lookup distinct from a genuine no-match — and links the ones you approve in a single background job. This was previously a support request, worked one referral at a time. Co-sell docs . The Partner Co-Sell surfaces in Salesforce and HubSpot were rebuilt to match the console. Both now carry attribution-role tags, a partner-by-partner status roll-up, exit reasons on a declined or closed-lost deal, an accept-by deadline warning, a shared conversation thread, and per-partner commission detail — so a rep can accept an inbound referral or close a multi-partner deal Won on the record itself. Accept and Reject appear only on inbound copies now, which removes the error a seller hit after sharing an outbound one. Integrations Co-sell docs . Co-sell deal ownership is a rule now, rather than a required field somebody fills in at accept time — which is how the owner so often ended up being whoever accepted. One rule pre-fills the owner from the partnership’s partner manager, or from a person you name, on auto-accept, manual accept, and outbound share. A second, off by default, copies the owner across from a Salesforce opportunity or HubSpot deal once one is linked. Partner management . Azure subscription auto-activation, already available in the console, is now on the Salesforce and HubSpot offer forms, so all three offer-creation surfaces behave alike. Self-managed organizations get an offer-wide toggle, off by default, that applies to every pricing plan on the offer — and it restores correctly from a saved draft or a cloned offer instead of carrying a stale value. Private offers . Added sales-tax audit and reconciliation. It reconciles your marketplace and billing revenue against the tax records committed in Avalara or Stripe Tax, classifies every revenue record into exactly one match status with the evidence behind it, and surfaces an ambiguous case for review rather than guessing an allocation. Editors and admins can remediate a finding from the console, and each remediation is written back to the tax provider rather than resolved only inside Suger. . Money renders in its own currency everywhere now. Several surfaces still had US dollars hard-coded — the usage-dimension rows on AWS and GCP offer emails, and the HTML half of the invoice-issued email, which left a euro invoice disagreeing with its own plain-text version. The console’s money rendering was audited alongside them. Billing & metering . . . . Aug 24, 2026 # Completed multi-channel revenue with bank reconciliation and ASC 606 revenue recognition.
