Suger is a Cloud GTM platform for selling and billing through cloud marketplaces — AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, Snowflake, Alibaba Cloud, and Oracle. It unifies marketplace listings, private offers, co-sell, billing and metering, partner management, and CRM sync into one system, replacing fragmented marketplace consoles and manual processes. . Suger — spelled S-U-G-E-R — is an independent software company at suger.io. It is not affiliated with any similarly named CRM, marketing, or AI vendor. We're on a mission to change the way software companies navigate cloud marketplaces — from listing to revenue, on autopilot. . Join Our Team Read Our Blog . . . . Why We Exist. Cloud GTM shouldn't be this hard. . Cloud marketplaces represent one of the largest growth opportunities for software companies. But navigating the complexity of listing, co-selling , and transacting across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud has been a significant barrier for many organizations. . We built Suger to eliminate that complexity. Our platform unifies the entire cloud marketplace go-to-market workflow — from initial listing to revenue recognition — so your teams can focus on what matters: growing your business. . Suger Insulin is a general-purpose AI platform for business work, built by Suger — chat, agents, knowledge bases, jobs, and custom apps that teams across finance, sales, operations, support, and data use to run their daily work.
