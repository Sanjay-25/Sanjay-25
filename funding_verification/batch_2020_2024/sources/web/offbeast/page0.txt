It all started at Harvard University with a simple question: what if nature could teach us how to replicate the texture of animal tissue? Here's our story.. learn more . . 2022. . Inspired by natural fibers and our founder Insa’s background in pharma and biotech, we began experimenting with plant-based building blocks and, after being accepted into Y Combinator, launched Mooji Meats from a small lab in Baltimore.. . 2023. . We launched our first whole-cut steak (our infamous "ribeye") as a restaurant pilot in Baltimore City. Religiously focusing on customer feedback, we moved into a shared commercial kitchen and refined both the product while scaling the process.. . . . 2024. . We rebrand into Offbeast Foods and expand into restaurants across the East Coast. Our Cross-Fiber Assembly platform launches giving rise to our filet mignons and our steak bites, the latter designed for cost-effective, high-volume foodservice.. . 2025. . Listening to customers asking to cook our products at home, we launch our e-commerce store www.offbeast.shop. Demand further accelerated and we launch our first holiday bundles and collaborations.
