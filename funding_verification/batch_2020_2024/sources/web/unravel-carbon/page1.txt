We perform our work with the highest integrity and humility.. . . . . . . . A letter from our co-founders. . Sustainability teams are driving one of the most important transformations of our time—but they’re held back by manual processes, fragmented data, and limited resources.. AI changes that. With Unravel AI, we’re combining AI-powered agents with our enterprise climate platform to help teams measure, reduce, and report emissions—faster, smarter, and with less effort.. We combine AI-powered automation with enterprise-grade carbon management to help organizations embed sustainability into decision-making, scale their impact, and drive real progress.. This isn’t about replacing people—it’s about amplifying their impact.. AI is here to unlock a new era of sustainability. And we’re just getting started.. . . Grace Sai, Marc Allen, and Kevin Teng. .
