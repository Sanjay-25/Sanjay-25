Supercharging Contact Center Operations with AI: Retell AI's Journey and Vision . At Retell AI, our vision is clear: to be the leading platform for building and managing AI voice agents that revolutionize contact center operations. As we drive forward on this mission, we are thrilled to share that Retell AI has secured a $4.6 million seed funding round. This round is led by Alt Capital and includes contributions from Y Combinator , Carya Venture , as well as over 20 founders, executives, and operators. We are privileged to have participation from industry leaders like Rajat Suri, co-founder of Lyft&Presto; Aaron Levie, co-founder & CEO of Box; Siqi Chen, founder & CEO of Runway; Alex Levin, co-founder & CEO of Regal; Michael Seibel, co-founder of Twitch and YC group partner; and Tyler Bosmeny, co-founder of Clever and YC group partner.. . Building conversational AI agents today is radically different from the past, largely due to the paradigm shift towards LLM-based conversational AI. This evolution introduces a unique set of challenges in creating production-ready AI voice agents that are both reliable and user-friendly. Previous tools allowed for the creation of extremely detailed but rigid conversational flows. It would take months to design the dialogs so that the virtual agent would have enough knowledge to handle situations in a way that is user-friendly and not overly stiff. With LLMs, the challenge is almost the opposite, as they can be overly 'creative' with the conversation. Our focus is on ensuring that LLMs are reliable, properly guardrailed, and can properly execute actions while maintaining their intelligence and creativity . Other crucial aspects with LLM-based voice agents include achieving human-level latency (<500ms), mastering turn-taking, knowing when to speak and when to listen, and handling interruptions to deliver a sentient-like experience.. Contrary to some opinions, building a robust AI voice agent isn't a quick, simple process—it involves more than just inputting a prompt template into GPT for a cool demo. Developing a production-ready agent that can handle edge cases, execute actions reliably, and allow for iterative improvements through monitoring and A/B testing requires significant effort. However, at Retell AI, we are committed to simplifying this process as much as possible, from building and testing to deploying and monitoring agents. We understand the intricacies of creating production-ready AI voice agents and have integrated that knowledge into our products so that you can achieve production-readiness as fast as possible.. . Since our initial launch in February, Retell AI has experienced substantial growth, achieving an annualized revenue of more than $3M with customers including leading BPOs.. . Our customers have witnessed the tangible benefits of deploying our production-ready AI voice agents, which streamline operations and enhance efficiency.. . Looking ahead, we remain committed to enhancing the process of building AI voice agents, making it simpler and more robust. Our goal is to enable companies to develop AI voice agents (and eventually multi-channel conversational AI) that effectively complement their human teams, boosting overall efficiency.. ‍.
