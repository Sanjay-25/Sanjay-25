What Are E-Transfer Payday Loans?. E-transfer payday loans are short-term loans, usually $100 to $1,500, sent to your bank account by Interac e-Transfer and meant to be repaid by your next payday, typically within 14 to 62 days. They are marketed on speed: apply online, get approved in minutes, and receive the money the same day.. . How an e-transfer payday loan is defined in this guide: small, fast, and due by your next payday. The "no documents" part refers to instant bank verification (IBV). Instead of emailing pay stubs or bank statements, you log into your online banking through a secure portal. The lender reads your recent transactions to confirm you have steady income and an active account. Your banking password is never stored, and the connection is read-only.. Interac is the rail, not the lender. Interac e-Transfer is only the delivery method that moves the money into your account. The loan, the fees, and the terms all come from the payday lender, not from Interac or your bank. A fast e-Transfer says nothing about whether the loan itself is a fair deal.. How Much Do E-Transfer Payday Loans Cost in Canada?. E-transfer payday loans cost $14 for every $100 you borrow . This is the maximum a licensed payday lender can charge under federal rules that took effect on January 1, 2025, and it applies across every province with an approved payday-lending regime, according to the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada . On a standard 14-day loan, that fee is the same as an annual interest rate of roughly 365% .. Dishonoured payment fee: If your pre-authorized repayment bounces, the lender may charge a one-time fee of up to $20 .. Bank NSF fee: Your own bank may add a non-sufficient-funds fee. Are "No Refusal" or "Guaranteed Approval" Loans Real?. No. No licensed lender in Canada can legally guarantee approval for everyone, and "no refusal payday loans" is a marketing phrase, not a real product. Every legitimate lender has to assess whether you can repay, which is why they run instant bank verification in the first place.. When you see "guaranteed approval," "everyone approved," or "no refusal," treat it as a warning sign rather than a feature. Lenders that lean on that language are often unlicensed, charge above the legal maximum, or bury fees you only discover after you sign. The honest version of "easy to get" is a lender or app that does not require a credit check and accepts more income types, not one that pretends risk does not exist. For a full breakdown of what to use instead, see our guide to safer alternatives to no-refusal payday loans .. What Are the Risks of E-Transfer Payday Loans?. E-transfer payday loans carry real financial risk, mostly because the cost is high and the repayment window is short. The main dangers are:. High effective interest. At $14 per $100 for a 14-day term, the effective annual rate reaches about 365%. Borrowing $500 costs $70 in fees, money that could have gone to groceries or a bill.. Stacked fees when a payment fails. A bounced repayment can trigger a dishonoured-payment fee of up to $20 from the lender plus an NSF fee from your bank. The bank fee is capped at $10 at federally regulated banks since March 12, 2026, but a missed payment still adds cost on top of a loan that was already expensive.. The repeat-borrowing trap. Because the term is short and the cost is high, many borrowers cannot repay in full and still cover the next two weeks, so they borrow again. Borrow $500, pay $70 in fees, then come up short and re-borrow $500, and you have paid $140 in fees on the same $500. Debt-help organizations like the Credit Counselling Society describe this cycle as the single biggest harm from payday lending.. Lower financial wellbeing over time. Research from the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada found that payday-loan use is linked to lower financial wellbeing: 88% of payday-loan users report having no emergency savings, compared to 50% of non-users. The convenience of instant cash can hide a budget gap that a one-time loan will not fix.. Cheaper Alternatives to Payday Loans. Payday lending is regulated province by province, though the federal $14-per-$100 maximum now applies everywhere licensed payday loans are allowed. The key provincial rules:. Ontario. The maximum cost is $14 per $100 borrowed. Borrowers get a two-business-day cooling-off period to cancel without penalty, extended payment plans after three loans in 63 days, and protection from rollover loans, per the Government of Ontario . Licensed lenders must display their licence number.. British Columbia. The cost cap is $14 per $100, and a single loan cannot exceed 50% of your net pay . The maximum amount is $1,500, the maximum term is 62 days, and lenders cannot roll over a loan or issue you more than one at a time, according to Consumer Protection BC .. Nova Scotia. Nova Scotia lowered its cap in stages, from $19 per $100 down to $17, then $15, and finally to $14 by January 1, 2025 . The Province of Nova Scotia also caps the default fee at $20 and requires full disclosure of all fees and terms.. Quebec and the territories. Quebec effectively caps consumer lending at 35% APR, which leaves no room for a payday-loan market, so licensed e-transfer payday lenders do not operate there. Yukon, the Northwest Territories, and Nunavut have no dedicated payday regime, so lenders there must stay under the federal 35% criminal-interest ceiling.
