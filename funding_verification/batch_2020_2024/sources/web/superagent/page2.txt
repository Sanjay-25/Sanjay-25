AI-native development moves faster than manual review. Code is written by agents, merged by default, and run with more access than anyone can watch. The security tools most teams have were built for a slower, human-written world.. Superagent exists to close that gap: security that lives where you already work, so you can trust what you merge, control what your agents do, and show your users it is safe.. . . ──[ the team ] ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── . Alan Zabihi (CEO) and Ismail Pelaseyed (CTO) are the co-founders of Superagent.. They have built several companies together during 15-plus years of working together. They built one of the first open source AI agent frameworks, which was adopted by enterprises building agentic experiences.
