Conveo is a consumer understanding platform that conducts AI-moderated video, voice and text interviews with real consumers in more than 50 languages. Founded by Dieter De Mesmaeker (previously founder & CTO of DataCamp) and Hendrik Van Hove (formerly McKinsey & Company), Conveo went through Y Combinator (S24). Its StoryLines product turns one-off research studies into continuous consumer understanding programs. Conveo works with consumer enterprises including Unilever, AB InBev, Google and Canva.. . . . . 02 / Fast facts. Conveo at a glance. . Interviews in 50+ languages: video, voice and text. . Data from Conveo’s own published studies, available for citation with attribution to Niels Schillewaert, PhD, Head of Research & Methods at Conveo.. . The say-do gap, measured. In a study of 91 AI-ethnographies (real households on camera), video AI detected brands in 85% of interviews while only 70% of participants mentioned them verbally: a 20-point gap between reported and observed behavior.. .
