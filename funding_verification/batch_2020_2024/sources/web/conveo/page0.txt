It uses voice and video interviews, not avatars, to capture authentic, human responses. Conveo’s AI then analyzes these multimodal inputs to extract themes, quotes, and actionable insights. Ideal for marketing, product, CX, and innovation teams who want real qualitative data without slow, manual analysis.. . . . . How is Conveo different from other qualitative research tools? . Unlike traditional qual platforms or AI copilots, Conveo delivers researcher-grade insights at scale.
