We're assessing you, and you're assessing us. The exact steps depend on the role and the team.. . . . . Step 01. Application. We review every application with care. Timelines can vary, but we'll keep you informed along the way.. . . . . . Step 02. Talent Conversation. A first conversation with our Talent team to get to know you, your experience, motivations, and what you're looking for. You'll also get a better sense of Conveo and the role.. . . . . . Step 03. Hiring Manager Conversation. A deeper dive with the hiring manager into your experience and how you think about your work. This is a chance to explore how you'd contribute to the team.. . . . . . Step 04. Assessment. Depending on the role, this may include a take-home task or case, often followed by a live walkthrough. We focus on your approach and reasoning, not just the outcome.. . . . . . Step 05. Meet the Founders. A candid conversation with one of our founders. We'll share our vision, ambitions, and the standards we hold ourselves to. We're looking for people who engage, challenge, and think alongside us, not just answer questions.. . . . . . Step 06. Reference Checks. We'll ask for a couple of references and keep this step simple and efficient.. . . . . Step 07. Offer & Onboarding. If it's a strong match, we'll move forward quickly. This may include an office visit, followed by a smooth onboarding to set you up for success.. . . . . . . . . . Do the best work of your career..
