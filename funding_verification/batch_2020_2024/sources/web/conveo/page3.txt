Conveo was founded in 2024 by Dieter De Mesmaeker and Hendrik Van Hove after graduating from Y Combinator. The company is headquartered in New York with engineering in Belgium, and offices in London and San Francisco. Conveo is the platform pioneering always-on consumer understanding through AI-moderated qualitative interviews, multimodal analysis (speech, tone, facial expression, and on-screen context) and a compounding consumer-context layer that the whole enterprise can query in natural language.
