We’ve got answers.. . Doesn’t the cloud already have disaster recovery? There are a lot of backup and recovery features native to cloud providers, but assembling these into a disaster recovery capability is your responsibility. For example, it’s part of AWS’s “shared responsibility model,” which you can read about here .. . . How will using Arpio impact my cloud provider bill? Arpio replicates data and infrastructure into a recovery environment that you control, so you’ll be responsible for the cost of data storage and transfer. But we use the most cost-effective techniques available in the cloud, and our customers often report that their DR environment costs just 1-2% of their production environment.. . . Can I use Arpio to recover from a ransomware attack? Yes! Arpio protects your backups so they can’t be destroyed by an attacker. When you recover, Arpio quarantines your systems and automates your forensics so that you can safely return to service.. . . Will there be data loss when I failover with Arpio? That depends on your recovery point objective (your RPO), and which cloud services you’re using. For many services, Arpio can replicate your data as it is updated in production, so there’s nothing lost (or close to it!) if you need to failover.. . . How does failback work with Arpio? Arpio’s failback process takes a snapshot of the data in your recovery environment and restores it to the original infrastructure in your production environment. You can test the failback process as many times as you’d like so you’ll be confident that it will work when it’s finally time to return to your primary environment.. . . Do I have to have infrastructure automation in place? Nope! It’s great if you do, but Arpio is driven by what you’ve got in your production environment, not what’s written in your automation. Our customers span the spectrum from no automation to full automation, and everything in between.. . . How do I test my DR solution with Arpio? Arpio makes DR testing easy and non-disruptive for your production workloads. Arpio allows you to easily spin up your recovery environment in test mode, giving you an opportunity to validate that your applications would work in a failover scenario. While in test mode, your production system remains online and processing production traffic. And, because of Arpio’s sophisticated automation, the full testing process can often be completed in minutes.
