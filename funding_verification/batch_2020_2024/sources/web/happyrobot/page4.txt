From First Customer to 70+ Enterprises . Building the AI Operating System for the Real Economy . Accelerating with Series B . We’re Just Getting Started . . We’re excited to share some big news: HappyRobot has raised a $44 million Series B , led by Base10 Partners with participation from a16z, Array Ventures, Avra, Samsara Ventures, Tokio Marine, WaVe-X, WiL, YC, and other strategic investors .. This milestone comes just 10 months after our $15.6M Series A , bringing our total funding to $62 million .. From First Customer to 70+ Enterprises. Just a year and a half ago, we went into production with our very first customer. Today, we’re proud to be powering operations for more than 70 enterprise customers , including DHL, Ryder, Schneider, and Werner .. These companies are already seeing what’s possible when AI workers step in to take on complex, high-volume tasks:. Appointment scheduling reduced from a week to under 30 minutes. Collections delivering returns of more than 100x. Outbound sales producing ROI north of 19x. Carrier sales driving 5x returns. The early results are clear: AI workers aren’t just cutting costs — they’re unlocking new revenue opportunities, increasing visibility, and freeing teams to focus on strategic, relationship-driven work.. Building the AI Operating System for the Real Economy. Our mission is ambitious: to build the AI operating system for the real economy .. The AI OS is a system that closes the circuit between intelligence and action. By combining real-time truth, specialized AI workers, and an orchestrating intelligence, HappyRobot manages complex, mission-critical work with true autonomy. Starting with supply chain and industrial-scale operations, we empower enterprises to operate with speed and continuous improvement—while humans focus on higher-value work.. This is just the beginning. Impact is exponential with an intelligence that learns & improves in real time.. Accelerating with Series B. This new round of funding will help us accelerate towards our mission by. Hiring world-class talent to join our team. Continuing to advance the product and make our product even more powerful. Expanding into more enterprise deployments. It’s fuel to scale what we’ve built so far — and to keep pushing toward the operating system that the world’s most impactful companies will run on.. We’re Just Getting Started. We’re deeply grateful to our team, customers, partners, and investors who believe in this mission & support us in getting there.. 👉 We’re hiring.
