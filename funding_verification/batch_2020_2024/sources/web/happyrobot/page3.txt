Tokio Marine’s partnership helps accelerate HappyRobot’s growth strategy and expansion across Asia and beyond.. . Carlos Becker. Deployment Lead. . . Updated Sep 22, 2026 1 min read . . . . We’re proud to highlight Tokio Marine Future Fund as a critical partner in our Series B round.. This partnership strengthens our mission to build the AI operating system for the real economy—powering the logisticians, manufacturers, builders, retailers, and operators who keep the world moving. With Tokio Marine’s support, we are accelerating our expansion into Japan and Asia, a region that represents one of the most vital hubs of global supply chain and industrial operations. Establishing trust and impact early in these markets is central to HappyRobot’s long-term growth strategy.. “We are excited to partner with HappyRobot. We believe that their AI workforce has the potential to redefine enterprise operations by reducing inefficiencies, improving visibility, and mitigating execution risk across the global supply chain.”. — Yuto Nash Nakanishi, Managing Partner, Tokio Marine Future Fund. As we enter this next stage of growth, we’re excited to work together to bring fully autonomous operations closer to reality.. “From the very beginning, working with Tokio Marine has been both insightful and invaluable,” said Javi Palafox, co-founder of HappyRobot. “We’re honored to continue partnering with their team on this shared mission.”.
