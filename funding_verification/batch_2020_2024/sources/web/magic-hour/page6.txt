What is Seedance 2.0? . Seedance 2.0 is ByteDance Seed's multimodal video model. It is designed for text, image, audio, and video-conditioned creation, with a strong emphasis on motion stability, cinematic control, and continuity.. . . . . What is Seedance 2.0 best for? . Seedance 2.0 is best for narrative short-form, structured reference workflows, cinematic continuity, and projects where lighting, framing, and identity need to stay consistent.. . . . . What are Seedance 2.0's specs? . Inside Magic Hour, Seedance 2.0 supports 480p, 720p, 1080p, and 4K output, 9:16, 16:9, and 1:1 aspect ratios, and durations up to 15 seconds.. . . . . Does Seedance 2.0 generate audio? . Can I use Seedance 2.0 for image to video? . Yes. Seedance 2.0 works well in reference-led workflows and is one of the more useful options when you want controlled image-to-video behavior.. . . . .
