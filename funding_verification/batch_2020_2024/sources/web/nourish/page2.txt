People are struggling to eat well, and the cost of healthcare continues to rise. We want to change that with Nourish. We’re on a mission to help everyone live a healthier, longer, and happier life. . . Nourish pairs evidence-based nutrition counseling with technology that supports you on your health journey.. . We connect you with a Registered Dietitian for telehealth appointments, and they’ll help you reach your goals with our app between sessions.. . No matter who you are or where you’re from, Nourish is for you. 94% of our patients pay $0 out of pocket — it’s fully covered by insurance — and we’re available in all 50 states.. . . . . . . . The Nourish Clinical Advisory Board brings together leading physicians and researchers with a wealth of clinical and research experience, to provide guidance on Nourish's care protocols and clinical standards, helping to ensure our patients receive the highest quality, evidence-based care.. . Dr. Ajay Haryani. MD MBA. . Dr. Haryani is the founder of Seen Medical, a modern concierge primary care practice, and currently serves as an Assistant Clinical Professor at Mount Sinai. Dr. Haryani brings extensive experience building and scaling tech-enabled care models and has worked closely with patients managing obesity, diabetes, and related conditions.. . Dr. Amanda Powell. MD. . Dr. Powell is a physician and weight-loss specialist, currently serving as the Chair of Lifestyle Health for Beth Israel Lahey Health and Assistant Professor of Medicine at the University of Massachusetts Chan Medical School. Dr. Powell has extensive experience in obesity medicine, having run a large obesity program in Boston for 15 years.. . Dr. Monica Berner. MD. . Dr. Berner is a board-certified family physician and senior health plan executive with more than a decade of direct clinical practice. She previously served as Chief Clinical Officer at HCSC and now leads Group and Individual/Family Markets as Senior Vice President. Dr. Berner brings deep expertise in population health, care management, and improving access and outcomes for large, diverse member populations.. . Dr. Dariush Mozaffarian. MD MPH Dr.PH. .
