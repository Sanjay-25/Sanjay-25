Join over 200 of the top OEMs, VARs, and Distributors selling to the U.S. Federal & SLED markets. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Meet our customers. . . . . . How Govly got started. Govly was born out of firsthand frustration with the complexity of selling to the U.S. government. Founders Mike and Nick Weiland had launched a company called Telescope to pursue high-value government contracts. But what began as a focus on finding the best opportunities quickly turned into a battle with red tape. . . They were forced to build an entire internal department just to manage compliance, certifications, and endless federal acquisition rules — long before a single contract was won.. . The problem wasn’t just finding opportunities. It was the fragmented, opaque process that made collaboration nearly impossible and stalled momentum across the board.Realizing there had to be a better way, the brothers teamed up with entrepreneur Jon Wright to build Govly — a unified platform where vendors could discover, evaluate, and act on public sector opportunities with speed and confidence.. . . . . .
