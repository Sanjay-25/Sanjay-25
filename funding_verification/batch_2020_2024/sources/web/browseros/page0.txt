Everything you need to know about BrowserOS neo.. . What is BrowserOS neo?. . BrowserOS neo is a free, open-source browser your AI agents drive using your logged-in accounts. Your AI opens its own tabs inside it, and you watch, approve, and audit every step from a dashboard on your new-tab page.. . . Is BrowserOS neo free?. . Yes. BrowserOS neo is completely free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. You bring your own AI provider keys.. . . Which AI does it work with?. . Any AI agent that supports MCP, which is essentially every serious AI coding tool today. Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, VS Code, Zed, and GitHub Copilot install with one click. Anything else connects with a URL.. . . Does it work with Claude Code?. . Yes. Open BrowserOS neo , click Connect on the Claude Code row in the MCP install board, and Claude Code has a browser.. . . Does it work with Codex?. . Yes. Codex is supported through the same one-click install as every other harness. No config file editing.. . . Does BrowserOS neo work with Claude Cowork?. . Yes. Claude Cowork connects through the same one-click MCP install as Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex. Any agent that speaks MCP can drive BrowserOS neo .. . . Is BrowserOS neo a cloud browser or does it run locally?. . BrowserOS neo runs entirely on your own machine. It is not a cloud browser and not a headless driver. Your logins, sessions, screenshots, and replays are local files, and nothing goes to a server we own.. . . How is BrowserOS neo different from a headless browser?. . A headless browser has no real logins, so agents never touch your actual accounts. BrowserOS neo is a full browser signed in as you, so agents do real work, and you can watch every step live and replay it afterward.. . . I downloaded BrowserClaw. Is this the same thing?. . Yes. BrowserClaw is now BrowserOS neo — same browser, same project, new name. Nothing you installed stops working, and you do not need to download it again. You will still see the old name in a few places we have not caught up with yet, like the GitHub repository and the MCP server name.. . . Do I still need the original BrowserOS?. . Not required. BrowserOS neo is a standalone browser you install on macOS or Windows. If you also use BrowserOS as your daily human browser, the two products live side by side.. . . Is my data safe? Does anything go to the cloud?. . Everything runs on your machine. Session history, screenshots, replays, and settings are files on your disk. Your logins stay in your browser profile, same as any browser you use. Nothing goes to a dashboard we own. Open source, top to bottom.. . . Can I run two AIs at the same time?. . Yes. Every agent gets its own set of tabs, tracked separately. Codex and Claude Code can each work on their own tasks at the same time without stepping on each other.. . . Will my AI interrupt my work?. . No. Every agent opens its own tabs to work in and never touches the ones you already have open. It cannot close the doc you are writing or steer the tab you are actively using. You keep working while it works.. . . Does my AI share my logins?. . Yes, that is the point. Agents drive BrowserOS neo using the sessions you already have, so they automate your real work instead of poking a sandbox with no context. Per-agent profile isolation is on the roadmap if you want to keep them apart.. . . Can I replay what my AI did?. . Yes. Every session is saved as a scrubbable video. Rewind, spot the moment things went sideways, and give the agent a better instruction next time.. . . What operating systems are supported?. . macOS and Windows.
