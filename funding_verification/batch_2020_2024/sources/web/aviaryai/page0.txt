So what exactly does AviaryAI do? AviaryAI is an AI voice agent platform built for credit unions, banks, and insurance companies. It automates outbound member calls and gives staff instant access to institutional knowledge through a compliance-grade AI built on a private large language model trained on financial services data. AviaryAI helps financial institutions increase contact rates, reduce operational costs, and improve member experience.. . Which financial institutions is AviaryAI designed for? AviaryAI is purpose-built for credit unions, community banks, regional banks, and insurance companies — specifically those that need scalable AI-powered member outreach and internal knowledge tools with TCPA compliance, SOC 2 security, and full regulatory auditability built in.. . Why not just use ChatGPT or another AI tool we already have? Unlike general-purpose AI platforms, AviaryAI runs on a proprietary large language model trained on financial services data. It includes TCPA-compliant outbound calling, SOC 2 certified data security, compliance guardrails, and 100% post-call auditing — purpose-built for the regulatory environment banks, credit unions, and insurance companies operate in.. . . The AI Knowledge Base converts your institution's internal policy documents, product guides, and procedural materials into an instantly searchable resource that front-line staff can query in plain language during a live member interaction — without putting the member on hold.
