Distr is an open-source software distribution platform for vendors who ship their software into customer-controlled environments. It delivers Docker Compose and Helm applications from one platform, with guided installation, customer entitlements, offline license keys, logs and metrics.. Distr is built by Glasskube, a company backed by Y Combinator and based in Vienna, Austria and Chicago, US. The platform and both deployment agents are open source under the Apache 2.0 license on GitHub .. . . . 200+. Organizations. . . . 15k+. Agent Downloads. . . . 1200+. GitHub Stars. . . . 20+. Contributors. . . . . Why we built Distr. We started with an open-source package manager for Kubernetes that grew to nearly 3,500 GitHub stars. Soon, startups in our network asked for help getting their applications into large enterprise environments, so we spent a long time writing Helm charts and containerizing applications for them.. We learned quickly that a Helm chart or a Compose file is a great start, but not enough to ship software reliably. The hard part comes after the first install: maintaining, observing, updating and securing every customer environment over time. Solving that is why we built Distr.. What we believe. Keep it as simple as possible, both for you and your customers.. A distribution platform should be open source and self-hostable.. Every enterprise environment is different, so the platform needs to be as flexible as possible.. Application design matters: architecting for a self-hostable product is half the struggle.. Standards over lock-in.. Who uses Distr. Teams at Artifact, Basedash, Lerian, Ozgar AI, Sophris.ai and Weave use Distr to ship their software to self-hosted customers. Read how in our case studies ..
