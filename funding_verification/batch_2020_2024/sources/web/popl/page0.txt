How does Popl's universal lead scanner work? . Popl’s universal lead scanner first uses AI-powered OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology to scan event badges, paper business cards, and QR codes, extracting contact details in real-time.. Once our OCR vision technology identifies the information in an image, our proprietary Popl AI enrichment system and trained LLMs enrich the lead into a full contact.. With an industry-leading 90% enrichment success rate, Popl AI eliminates the need for reliance on conference lead capture badge kit APIs, giving customers a seamless and superior solution.. . . . How does Popl AI enrichment work? . Popl AI captures a contact's name and company, then leverages our proprietary AI algorithms and intelligent search methods to gather the rest of their contact details.. Our enrichment process achieves a 90% successful email or LinkedIn profile match rate—significantly above the industry standard.. Beyond providing personal contact details, our enrichment goes further, providing valuable insights into a contact's company as well. . . . Does Popl require an event badge API kit to scan badges? .
