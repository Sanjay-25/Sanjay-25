What happens after I answer the questionnaire? + You get an instant recommendation — which tests answer your question, which ones you can skip, and what each costs. It's free, there's no account, and you can refine it by chatting with Mito Concierge. Buy the tests if and when you want them.. . Do I have to be a member to buy? + No. Anyone can order at the non-member price, no membership required. For $9/mo, membership unlocks member pricing plus the personalized action plan, longitudinal tracking, and AI health chat through Mito Concierge.. . Is there a lab draw fee? + Yes — a per-visit draw fee that varies by provider and is shown at checkout. It's charged per visit, not per test, so drawing several tests in one visit keeps it low. We pass it through at cost rather than averaging it.. . Will you really tell me I need less? + Yes — when your answers say so. If a single marker retest answers your question, that's the recommendation, not a whole panel. Recommending less is the point of the model — we don't earn margin on the tests, so there's no reason to pad the order.. . Why do the prices look so much lower than what I usually pay? + Most retail lab pricing reflects insurance-billing dynamics — list prices are inflated so the negotiated payout looks like a discount. We bypass that. Our non-member price is already among the lowest in the category, and members pay our cost. We don't profit from your tests; Mito makes its money on the $9 membership.. . Do I need a doctor's order? + No. Every order is reviewed and signed by a Mito-network physician. You don't need an existing PCP, a referral, or a separate consult.. . What does the $9/mo membership actually buy? + A health team that holds your whole picture and can act on it — a personalized action plan, longitudinal tracking, AI health chat, retests ordered from inside the product, and the judgment to tell you when to stop.
