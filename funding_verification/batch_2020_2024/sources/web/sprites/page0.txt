Everything teams ask before they hand Sprites their ad accounts. Still deciding? Talk it through with us.. Book a demo → → . What is Sprites? Sprites is an AI ads agent built for startup marketing teams. It plans, launches and optimizes campaigns across Google, Meta, Microsoft, LinkedIn, TikTok and Reddit, and keeps you cited when buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini about your category, so two or three people cover the ground a full channel team would.. Do we still need to hire a channel specialist? Not for the execution. Sprites does the work you would otherwise hire or outsource: audits, campaign builds, creative, budget moves, weekly reporting, across every channel at once. Your marketers set the goals, read the plans and approve the changes, and the headcount goes into product and positioning instead.. What does "autopilot" actually mean? You set a goal, such as a CPL or ROAS target, and Sprites runs the account toward it around the clock: launching tests, shifting budget to what works, pausing what does not, and reporting back. Every change is staged for your approval before it reaches the account.. Which channels does Sprites run? Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max), Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn, TikTok and Reddit, all through official partner APIs. The same agent runs AEO, so when a buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Gemini who to use in your category, you are in the answer.. Can I use Sprites from Claude, ChatGPT or Grok? Yes. Sprites ships an official MCP server, so you can drive it from Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or any MCP-compatible assistant, or work in the Sprites app. Either way it uses the same official partner integrations and the same approval gates.. How does Sprites improve ROI? It starts with a wasted-spend audit, then keeps optimizing: budget reallocation, creative refreshes, negative keywords, audience and bid changes. Teams typically see lower CPL and CAC and higher ROAS within the first month, and the plan costs a fraction of the channel hire it stands in for.. Who is Sprites for? Startup marketing teams: the one to five people expected to cover paid, creative and AI search at once, and the founders doing it themselves before that first marketing hire lands.. How does Sprites work? Connect your ad accounts and site, then describe the outcome in plain English, the way you would brief a marketer: "Launch a Meta lead gen campaign with 3 audiences and 5 creatives, then hold CPL under $40." Sprites plans it, shows you the plan, and once approved runs and optimizes it.. How is Sprites different from ChatGPT or Claude? ChatGPT and Claude can write a plan; Sprites executes it. It is connected to your ad accounts and CMS through official partner APIs, ships campaigns and content, and keeps optimizing them. If you already live in Claude or ChatGPT, connect Sprites there through MCP and it becomes the hands.. Do I still approve changes? Yes. Sprites stages every launch, budget move and edit for approval before it touches an account, and shows the expected impact. Teams that trust a workflow can set an Autopilot to run it on a schedule, such as a weekly wasted-spend audit or a Monday performance report.. Do I need to be technical to use Sprites? No. If you can describe what you want the way you would to a teammate, you can run Sprites. Audits, competitor research and creative teardowns come back in minutes. Campaign launches take hours instead of weeks.
