Learn about our Project Based curriculum . . . What your child actually builds. One project at a time.. A project is a real-world problem that children work as a team to solve. The projects typically last two to four weeks and are designed so that children have to learn and apply the essential underlying skills and concepts.. . . ELA · Media · Research Become an investigative journalist. Your child picks a real-world issue and builds a case strong enough to convince Qrious-AI. Along the way they learn to skim long articles for what matters, tell fact from opinion, judge whether a source can be trusted, and turn solid evidence into an argument that holds up.. . . . Math · Science · Health Become a nutrition influencer. Your child audits what they actually eat, then puts decimals, percentages and unit rates to work on real food labels. They discover what carbs, fats and protein do in the body, and learn to see through the marketing tricks that make junk food feel like a smart choice.. . . . Engineering · Physics · History Build a bridge that holds. Your child designs, tests and rebuilds popsicle-stick bridges until they understand the forces (tension, compression, why a triangle won't budge) that keep a structure standing. The build sits inside a bigger question: how humans went from small tribes to whole countries, and why good design starts with understanding people.. . . . Math · Business · Design Run a cloud kitchen. Running Granny's cloud kitchen turns fractions into something your child reaches for: scaling recipes, measuring ingredients, comparing and simplifying as they cook. They finish by costing the menu, working out profit margins, and pitching the whole business to investors.. . . . . . The proof parents notice.
