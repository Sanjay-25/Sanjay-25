Milestones. . From our early days building tools for homeowners to becoming a trusted growth partner for thousands of service pros nationwide, every step in our journey has been shaped by one mission—empowering skilled tradespeople to succeed in a digital world. Here’s a look at the moments that have defined who we are today.. . . . . . . . . Dwelling Days. August, 2020. . . . . Before becoming Topline Pro, Nick and Shannon built Dwelling Management. The platform provided tools for organizing home maintenance tasks, connecting with trusted service pros, and staying ahead of repairs. This early venture laid the groundwork for their deep understanding of homeowner needs and contractor relationships.. . . . . ProPhone. May, 2020. . . . . As they spoke with more contractors, Nick and Shannon realized pros needed better tools to grow their own businesses, not just connect with homeowners. That insight led to ProPhone, a mobile-first platform that helped home service pros get discovered, manage leads, and win more jobs.. . . . . Expanding The Product. November , 2020-. . . . . Building on ProPhone, we started shifted towards offering an AI-powered platform that helps pros get discovered, trusted, and booked online. It combines everything from websites to customer messaging, reviews, and payments—built to save time and grow their business.. . . . . Enter Topline Pro. February, 2022. . . . . The shift from ProPhone to Topline Pro marked a turning point—from offering basic digital tools to becoming a true partner in pro growth. The new name reflects a bigger mission: helping every home service business reach their full potential and grow their topline.. . . . . Expanding The Team. January, 2023. . . . . In 2023, Topline Pro began rapidly expanding its team to meet growing demand. With strong traction in the home services space, the company brought on talent across engineering, marketing, and customer success—all united by the mission to support pros with AI that works as hard as they do.. . . . . Someone Say SMB Tech 50?. February, 2023. . . . . Topline Pro was named to CNBC’s inaugural SMB Tech 50 list, recognizing the top startups serving small and medium-sized businesses.
