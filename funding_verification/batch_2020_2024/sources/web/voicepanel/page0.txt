Voicepanel is an AI-native feedback platform for modern research & product teams. More than an AI moderator, we support a wide range of methods and modalities, from voice & video to surveys & tasks, and integrate directly in the tools where products get built via MCP. Provide your objectives & assets; we handle delivery, a wide range of recruitment options, and analysis grounded in your responses.. . . . How do you handle security and compliance?. . Voicepanel is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant. We do not train on customer data. Controls, subprocessors, and agreements are in our Trust Center . Enterprise includes SSO and other controls where procurement requires them.. . . . Can we customize methodology, AI behavior, and branding?. . Yes. You govern study design, stimuli, languages, and how AI assists with moderation, translation, and analysis, including custom prompts and workflows. Branding and deployment options are built for teams that need a consistent, professional respondent experience.. . . . How is Voicepanel different from surveys and traditional qual?. . Surveys optimize for fixed questionnaires; traditional qual optimizes for depth but not throughput. Voicepanel combines depth (voice and video, adaptive follow-up) with structured protocols so you get richer signal than typed open-ends.. . . . How does recruitment work, and can we use our own audiences?. . Use Voicepanel's integrated access to large consumer and professional panels with screeners and quotas, share a link to your own customers or community, or route from third-party panels via standard redirects. Thematic summaries and quantified themes with evidence (quotes, audio, and video), highlight reels, and “ask your data” workflows tied to underlying responses.
