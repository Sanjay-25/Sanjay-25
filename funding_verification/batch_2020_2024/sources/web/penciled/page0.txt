Waitlist . + There are four ways patients can join the waitlist. First, when patients are initially prompted to schedule their plan of care, they can opt into the waitlist for any visits that don’t have available times available to them. Or, patients can simply reply to any text received from Penciled and ask to join the waitlist or update their waitlist status. Additionally, if the front office has printed out the waitlist QR code, patients can scan that QR code and join the waitlist. Lastly, the front office can add patients to the waitlist at any time.. .
