FOR DEALERS Frequently Asked Questions. . Can I start with just one product? Absolutely. Ekho is built to be modular — start with the product that solves your biggest problem right now, whether that's AI Sales Agent, your Website, or the Transaction Engine. Most customers start with the AI Sales Agent since it's the fastest to get integrated. The Transaction Engine may have a waitlist depending on integration scheduling. You can add the rest when you're ready, and most dealers expand within the first 90 days.. . . . How does pricing actually work? Ekho offers modular pricing — you pay only for the products you use. Some products are a flat monthly fee per rooftop, others are usage-based (per sale). Talk to our team for a quote tailored to your business.. . . . Do the products work better together? Yes. While each product works standalone, they share data and context when combined — so your AI Sales Agent knows your inventory, your Website reflects real-time pricing, and your Transaction Engine pulls from the same lead record. The result is a faster, more seamless experience for both your team and your customers.. . . . Are there setup fees? Setup fees vary depending on the products and scope of your implementation. Some configurations include onboarding and setup at no additional cost, while others may involve a one-time implementation fee. Your account team will walk you through the details during the quoting process.. . . . How fast can I get started? AI Sales Agent can be up and running in 1-2 days. Transaction Engine typically takes about 30 days for full onboarding, since it depends on your integration scheduling.. . . . Does Ekho actually drive incremental sales? Yes — and the data backs it up. 70% of Ekho buyers say they probably or definitely wouldn't have bought without the online option. 40% are purchasing that vehicle type for the first time. And 88% are younger than the industry average. These aren't existing customers shifting channels — they're net-new buyers your dealership wouldn't have reached otherwise.. . . . What if I have multiple locations? Ekho is built for multi-rooftop operations. You get a unified dashboard across all locations, shared inventory visibility, and consistent branding — while still allowing per-location customization where it matters.. . . . Does Ekho support F&I products? Yes. Ekho supports embedded F&I directly in the checkout flow — including extended warranties, GAP insurance, tire and wheel protection, and other aftermarket products. Buyers can compare coverage tiers and add protection during checkout, with the cost rolled into their financing terms. Dealers retain full control over which products are offered and at what margin.. . . . How does Ekho handle lender relationships and points? Ekho integrates with multiple lenders and supports lender-specific rate sheets, reserve structures, and dealer participation. Your existing lender relationships carry over — Ekho surfaces offers from your lender panel and lets buyers compare terms. Dealer points and reserve income are managed through your standard lender agreements, not through Ekho.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Product Newsletter. Stay up to date with Ekho's product team .
