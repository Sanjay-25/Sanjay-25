Try another team. . . . . . . . . . . . CAREERS Frequently Asked Questions. . Where is Ekho based? Ekho is headquartered in New York City. We work in-person from our NYC office — we believe the best products are built by teams that work closely together.. . . . What's the interview process like? It depends on the role, but generally involves two to three pre-onsite calls followed by a full day onsite with working sessions and limited pre-work. We'll also ask for references. We move fast and respect your time.. . . . What kind of people thrive at Ekho? People who own outcomes, move quickly, and care deeply about craft. We're a small team tackling a massive market — you'll have outsized impact from day one.. . . . Do you offer visa sponsorship? Yes — we're very open to sponsorship. One of our co-founders is from South Africa and is on an O-1 visa. We believe international candidates are often the grittiest, hardest-working people, and we'll go to lengths to make sure you get what you need to stay in the US.. . . . What benefits does Ekho offer? Competitive salary and equity, comprehensive health/dental/vision, unlimited PTO, 401(k), office equipment stipend (new laptop), free lunch and dinners, an annual team offsite, and the energy of building something transformative with a world-class team.. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
