Everything you need to know before you apply.. . Will checking eligibility affect my credit score? No — checking eligibility is a soft search that's only visible to you. A hard search happens only if you formally accept an offer.. . . How much does it cost? Bits is a monthly subscription. The card has no hidden fees and no FX fees when you spend abroad.. . . What credit limit will I get? You get two lines of credit. Your credit card starts between £50 and £100 and can grow over time as you show you can manage it. Your store card limit depends on the plan you choose.. . . How does it actually build my credit? When you sign up you get two lines of credit — a credit card and a store card — and both are reported to the credit bureaus. Every payment, whether missed or on time, is reported. Having these lines of credit and making regular repayments on them is what contributes to your score. A score increase isn't guaranteed, and there are also many free ways to build your credit.. . . Is it a real Mastercard? Yes. It's a real Mastercard you can use anywhere Mastercard is accepted — in store, online and abroad.. . . Who can apply? UK residents aged 18+ with valid ID. Bits isn't suitable if you're struggling with debt, and you shouldn't apply if you've recently entered an IVA or Debt Management Plan (DMP).. . . . . . . . . Start your credit journey today. .
