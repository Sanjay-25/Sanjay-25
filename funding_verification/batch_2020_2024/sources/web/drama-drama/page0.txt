Drama Drama is an all-in-one monetization platform for AI character and drama creators. Creators can monetize their fandom through gated episode access, Live AI Chat, personalized requests, tips, and other paid fan experiences.. Creators can keep using social platforms for discovery while using Drama Drama for direct fan monetization.. . Frequently asked questions. What is Drama Drama? Drama Drama is an all-in-one monetization platform for AI character and drama creators. It gives creators paid fan experiences around their characters and stories, including gated episode access, Live AI Chat, personalized requests, and tips.. What fees apply to creator earnings? Drama Drama’s revenue share depends on the product. Creators receive 80% for creator-provided paid episodes and series, creator-generated personalized requests and digital gifts; 50% for Live AI Chat and personalized content generated and funded by Drama Drama. Co-produced content uses the agreed 40% creator / 60% Drama Drama split. These shares are before separately deducted payment-processing and payout fees. Payouts carry a 1% Drama Drama service fee plus the actual payout-provider cost.. Do I have to stop posting on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook or X? No. Social platforms are where creators already build reach and discovery. Drama Drama is designed to add direct fan monetization around the characters and stories audiences want more from.. Can I monetize an AI character even if I do not have a full drama series? Yes. Drama Drama is designed for monetization around AI characters as well as serialized AI dramas, including paid character interactions and personalized fan experiences.. Does Drama Drama create the series for me? The main platform is for creators who already have characters or content to monetize. For selected creators, Drama Drama also offers optional co-production support covering writing, production, and production costs.. Can agencies manage multiple creators, characters or series on Drama Drama? Yes. Agencies and management teams can manage multiple creators, AI characters, drama series and social accounts under one organization. Each property can keep its own content, monetization and performance data while the agency manages the portfolio centrally.. Can creators and agencies outside the U.S. use Drama Drama? Yes. Drama Drama is designed for creators and agencies around the world. Our payment infrastructure supports international creator payouts, subject to country availability, identity or business verification, and the payout methods available in each market.. When and how are creators paid? Payouts are monthly once eligible cleared earnings reach US$10 before payout fees. Smaller balances carry forward. Each payout is reduced by a 1% Drama Drama payout service fee plus the actual payout-provider cost. Stripe and stablecoin payouts are offered where supported; available methods are confirmed during onboarding.. How long does a paid episode or series unlock last? 30 days from the unlock—not from the credit-pack purchase. A full-series unlock also includes episodes added to that same series during your active access period. New episodes do not extend the original expiry date.. How do credits work? The current model is 100 credits for US$1, with US$5, US$10, US$15 and US$20 packs. Creator earnings are recorded when paid credits are spent, not when the pack is purchased. The credit model may evolve.. Do I need to own the rights to the characters, voices and content I monetize? Yes. Creators and agencies must have the rights or permissions needed to monetize the characters, likenesses, voices and content they bring to Drama Drama. Agencies representing creators or talent must have authority to act on their behalf.. How do I join Drama Drama? Join the creator waitlist at dramadrama.ai. Drama Drama reviews creator profiles and follows up with selected applicants as access becomes available.. .
