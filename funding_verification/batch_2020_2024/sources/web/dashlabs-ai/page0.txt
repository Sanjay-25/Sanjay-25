Dashlabs is an end-to-end clinic management platform that covers patient intake, electronic medical records (EMR), results encoding, PDF certificate generation, machine integrations, and financial reporting — all in one system.. . . . . . . Is there really a free tier?. . . . . Yes. You can create an account and start processing patients immediately at no cost. The free tier covers the core EMR, basic result templates, patient registration, and 24-hour reports. Paid plans unlock custom templates, emailing, full reporting history, and more.. . . . . . . How long does setup take?. . . . . Most clinics are live within a day. The free tier requires no setup call — just register and go. Pro and Enterprise onboarding typically takes 2–5 business days depending on the number of custom templates and machine integrations needed.. . . . . . . Which countries is Dashlabs available in?. . . . . Dashlabs is currently live in the Philippines, Indonesia, and Malaysia.
